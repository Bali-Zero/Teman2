## Rubrica 1: KBLI Decoded

**Schedule:** Weekly, Monday 09:00 WITA
**Format:** Long-form article (balizero.com) + 5-tweet promotional thread on X
**Length:** 1,500--2,000 words
**Target audience:** Foreign entrepreneurs researching business setup in Indonesia

### Tagline

> *Decoding Indonesia's business classification system, one code at a time.*

### Structure Template

Every installment follows this exact order:

1. **Hook** (100--150 words)
   Open with a real scenario or common mistake. "You found the perfect spot in Canggu for your restaurant. You registered your PT PMA. Then OSS rejected your license application. Here's why."

2. **The Code** (200--300 words)
   Official name (Indonesian + English translation). Full uraian summary in plain English. What businesses fall under this code. What does NOT fall under this code.

3. **Foreign Ownership Status** (150--200 words)
   PMA status (TERBUKA / TERBATAS / TERTUTUP). Maximum foreign ownership percentage. Conditions, if any. Source regulation (Perpres number).

4. **Licensing & Risk** (250--350 words)
   OSS-RBA risk classification per business scale (Micro/Small/Medium/Large). Required permits and certificates. Timeline for issuance. Obligations and compliance requirements.

5. **What Changed in 2025** (150--200 words)
   Differences from KBLI 2020. Merged codes, split codes, or new codes. Migration implications if your company already uses the old code.

6. **Bali Context** (200--300 words)
   Bali-specific insights. Saturation by area. Local government requirements. Practical tips from real experience.

7. **Codes You Might Confuse It With** (150--200 words)
   Related KBLI codes. When to use which. Common misclassification mistakes.

8. **TKA (Foreign Worker) Positions** (100--150 words)
   Eligible positions under Kepmen 228/2019. RPTKA requirement threshold. Director/Commissioner exception.

9. **FAQ** (3 questions, 200--300 words total)
   The three questions every client asks about this code.

10. **Next Week Preview** (50 words)
    Tease next week's code with a one-line hook.

## Rubrica 2: Bali Zero Data Brief

**Schedule:** Biweekly, Wednesday 09:00 WITA (1st and 3rd Wednesday of each month)
**Format:** Long-form article (balizero.com) + 5-tweet promotional thread on X
**Length:** 2,000--2,500 words
**Target audience:** Existing business owners in Bali, potential investors, journalists

### Tagline

> *The numbers behind doing business in Indonesia -- stripped of spin.*

### Structure Template

1. **Data Point** (100 words)
   Lead with the number. Bold, isolated, impossible to ignore. "47% of PT PMA companies in Bali registered under the wrong KBLI code in 2025."

2. **Context** (300--400 words)
   Where this number comes from. Methodology. Why it matters now. Historical comparison if available.

3. **Analysis** (500--700 words)
   What the data actually tells us. Segment by business type, region, or industry. Counter-intuitive findings. Comparison with competitor markets (Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines).

4. **So What** (300--400 words)
   Actionable implications. What businesses should do differently. Who wins, who loses. Timeline for impact.

5. **Methodology Note** (100--150 words)
   Transparent about data sources, sample sizes, limitations. Builds credibility.

6. **FAQ** (2 questions, 150 words total)

7. **Next Brief Preview** (50 words)

### Visual: Header Image Template

- Dark canvas (`#0c0c0e`)
- The key data point displayed huge: "47%" or "IDR 10B" in `#d4845a`, 160pt
- Contextual label below in `#edeae4`, 20pt: "of Bali PT PMAs used the wrong KBLI code"
- "BALI ZERO DATA BRIEF" in `#c9a96e`, 14pt, uppercase, top-left
- Issue number: "// DB-007" in `#8c8884`, top-right
- Minimal chart element (single bar, trend line, or dot plot) in `#5e7fb5` (batik indigo)
- Bottom bar: `#d4845a` 3px line with "balizero.com" in `#575350`
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (1200x675)


### Thread Format (5 tweets)

**Tweet 1 (The Number):**
The data point, naked. No preamble. "47% of PT PMA companies in Bali are registered under the wrong KBLI code. We checked 1,200 active companies. Here's what we found:"

**Tweet 2 (Why It Matters):**
The consequence. What happens when this number is wrong. Real business impact.

**Tweet 3 (The Breakdown):**
Segment the data. By area, by industry, by company age. The most interesting slice.

**Tweet 4 (The Fix):**
What to do about it. Concrete steps. Timeline. Cost estimate if available.

**Tweet 5 (CTA):**
Link to full brief. Mention the KBLI Navigator for self-audit. Soft CTA.

**Formatting rules:**
- Lead with the number, always
- Use the em dash for impact: "1,200 companies -- 47% wrong"
- Maximum 2 hashtags on last tweet: `#IndonesiaBusiness` and `#BaliData`

## Rubrica 3: Indonesia Business Intelligence

**Schedule:** Monthly, 1st Thursday 09:00 WITA
**Format:** Long-form article (balizero.com) + 8--10 tweet promotional thread on X
**Length:** 3,000+ words
**Target audience:** Serious investors, C-suite, policy watchers, journalists

### Tagline

> *Monthly strategic intelligence on Indonesia's business landscape -- for people who make decisions.*

### Structure Template

1. **Signal** (200--300 words)
   The regulatory change, market shift, or emerging trend that triggered this edition. Lead with the "so what" -- why should a busy executive stop scrolling.

2. **Evidence** (500--700 words)
   Data, regulations, official sources. Quote the specific Perpres, Perda, or Permenkumham number. Cross-reference with OSS changes, BPS data, or BKPM investment reports.

3. **Scenarios** (600--800 words)
   Three scenarios: base case, bull case, bear case. For each: what happens, who benefits, who gets hurt, and estimated probability. Time horizon: 6--18 months.

4. **Implications** (400--500 words)
   By sector: visa/immigration, company setup, tax, property. What changes for each. Concrete examples.

5. **Action Required** (300--400 words)
   Prioritized list of actions. By urgency: "Do this now" vs. "Watch this" vs. "Prepare for this." Include estimated cost and timeline where possible.

6. **Source Index** (100--200 words)
   Numbered list of every regulation, data source, and official document cited. Full references for credibility.

7. **Next Month Preview** (50--100 words)

### Visual: Header Image Template

- Dark canvas (`#0c0c0e`) with subtle batik pattern at 3% opacity
- "INDONESIA BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE" in `#c9a96e`, 18pt, uppercase, top-left
- Issue identifier: "MARCH 2026" in `#8c8884`, top-right
- Headline text in `#edeae4`, 36pt, bold: the core signal (e.g., "KBLI 2025 Migration Deadline: 82 Days")
- Subheadline in `#d4845a`, 20pt: the most provocative finding
- Left vertical accent bar in `#d4845a`, 4px, full height
- "BALI ZERO" wordmark in `#575350`, bottom-right, 12pt
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (1200x675)

### Thread Format (8--10 tweets)

**Tweet 1 (The Signal):**
One sentence. The most important thing that happened. "Indonesia just quietly changed the rules for [X]. Here's what it means for your business:"

**Tweet 2 (The Evidence):**
The regulation number. The data point. The official source. Build credibility immediately.

**Tweet 3 (Who's Affected):**
Specific business types, visa holders, or industries. Name them.

**Tweet 4 (Scenario: Bull):**
The optimistic interpretation. What happens if this plays out well.

**Tweet 5 (Scenario: Bear):**
The pessimistic interpretation. The risk. The downside.

**Tweet 6 (Base Case):**
The most likely outcome. What we actually expect.

**Tweet 7 (Sector Impact):**
Break it down by sector. Immigration. Company. Tax. Property.

**Tweet 8 (Action Items):**
"Do this now. Watch this. Prepare for this." Prioritized.

**Tweet 9 (Sources):**
Key regulation numbers and official links for the skeptics.

**Tweet 10 (CTA):**
Full report link. Subscribe to newsletter. Soft CTA to Bali Zero consultation.

**Formatting rules:**
- This is the prestige series. Every word earns its place.
- No emoji in the first 3 tweets. Authority first.
- Maximum 3 hashtags on the last tweet: `#IndonesiaBusiness` `#KBLI2025` `#BaliInvestment`
- Thread should feel like reading the Economist, not a Twitter influencer

## Quality Standards

**Every article must:**
- Cite at least one official regulation by number (Perpres, PP, Perda, Kepmen, or BPS number)
- Include at least one data point from our own systems (KBLI Navigator, CRM, KG)
- Be factually verifiable against source_documents/KBLI_2025_FINAL_CLEAN.json
- Never include pricing (prices come from PricingTool and change; articles should link to /services)
- Include a "Last verified" date
- Link to the relevant KBLI Navigator page: balizero.com/kbli/[code]

**Every thread must:**
- Be written in English (primary X audience is English-speaking expats and investors)
- Not exceed 280 characters per tweet
- Include the article link in the final tweet
- Be proofread for regulatory accuracy before posting
