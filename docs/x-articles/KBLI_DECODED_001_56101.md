## The Code: What 56101 Actually Covers

**Official name:** Aktivitas Penyediaan Makanan di Bangunan Tetap
**English:** Food Service Activities in Permanent Buildings

This code covers any business that serves food to customers inside a permanent, non-movable building structure. The building must be fixed -- it cannot be dismantled, relocated, or transported. Food can be consumed on-site, taken away, or delivered.

**Specifically, 56101 includes:**

- Restaurants (sit-down, full service)
- Rumah makan (Indonesian-style eateries)
- Canteens and cafeterias
- Fast food restaurants
- Take-away restaurants
- Restaurants inside transportation facilities (airports, train stations, cruise ships) -- but only if the restaurant is a separate business unit from the transport operator
- Restaurants inside hotels -- again, only if the restaurant is a separate unit from the hotel
- Take-away operations inside supermarkets, if operated by an independent unit
- Fine dining and gourmet restaurants (adiboga)
- Ice cream and yoghurt shops

**What 56101 does NOT cover:**

- Food stalls, tents, food trucks, or mobile vendors -- that is now **56102** (non-permanent buildings)
- Bars and alcohol-focused venues -- that is **56301**
- Cafes and coffee shops -- that is **56303**
- Catering services -- that falls under **56210**
- Beverage kiosks (boba, juice bars) -- that is **56304**

The distinction is structural: if the building is permanent and the primary activity is serving prepared food, it is 56101.

## Foreign Ownership: 100% Open

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **PMA Status** | TERBUKA (Open) |
| **Maximum Foreign Ownership** | 100% |
| **Conditions** | None |
| **Priority Sector** | No |
| **Source Regulation** | Perpres 10/2021, amended by Perpres 49/2021 and Perpres 14/2024 |

This is the good news: KBLI 56101 is fully open to foreign investment. There is no maximum foreign ownership cap, no local partner requirement, and no special conditions. A foreigner can own 100% of a PT PMA operating a restaurant in Indonesia.

This has been the case since the 2021 Positive Investment List (Perpres 10/2021) replaced the old Negative Investment List (DNI). Before 2021, restaurants had various ownership restrictions depending on scale and type. Those restrictions are gone.

**However, "open" does not mean "easy."** You still need:
- A PT PMA with minimum IDR 10 billion total investment commitment (per PP 5/2021)
- Paid-up capital of at least IDR 2.5 billion per shareholder
- Proper licensing through OSS-RBA
- Multiple sector-specific permits (detailed below)

The investment minimum is the barrier, not the ownership structure.

## Licensing and Risk Classification

KBLI 56101 has different risk classifications depending on your business scale. This is critical because the risk level determines what permits you need, how long they take, and who supervises you.

### Micro Scale

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Risk Classification** | Menengah Rendah (Medium-Low) |
| **Required Permits** | NIB + Sertifikat Standar |
| **Issuance Timeline** | Automatic |
| **Authority** | Bupati/Walikota (Regent/Mayor) |

**Obligations:**
- Hygiene Sanitation Label (HSP)
- Periodic activity reports
- Standard implementation documentation

Micro-scale operations get the lightest treatment. Your NIB and Standard Certificate are issued automatically through OSS. No inspection, no waiting period.

### Small, Medium, and Large Scale

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Risk Classification** | Menengah Tinggi (Medium-High) |
| **Required Permits** | NIB + Sertifikat Standar |
| **Issuance Timeline** | 14 working days |
| **Authority** | Gubernur (Governor) |

**Obligations:**
- Self-Assessment of Standard Readiness (Penilaian Mandiri Kesiapan Penerapan Standar)
- Certified standard from an accredited certification body (LSPr)
- Hygiene Sanitation Feasibility Certificate (SLHS) from the local health department
- If employing foreign workers (TKA): approved RPTKA document

This is where it gets real. The SLHS alone requires an on-site inspection by Dinas Kesehatan (the regional health department). They check kitchen hygiene, water quality, food storage, waste management, and staff health documentation. In Bali, budget 2--4 weeks for the inspection and certificate issuance.

**Additional permits you will likely need:**

- **SKPL A, B, or C** (Surat Keterangan Penjualan Langsung Minuman Beralkohol) -- if you serve alcohol. This is a separate license, and 56101 alone does not authorize alcohol sales.
- **Sertifikasi Nomor Kontrol Veteriner** -- if you serve meat products, required for food safety traceability.
- **Sertifikat Halal** -- mandatory for certain food categories under the 2024 Halal Product Assurance law (UU JPH). Timeline and enforcement vary.

### The Fiktif Positif Rule

Both tiers benefit from *fiktif positif*: if the government fails to respond within the stated timeline, your application is automatically approved. This is a feature of the OSS-RBA system designed to prevent bureaucratic bottlenecks. In practice, it works -- but keep documentation of your submission dates.

## What Changed in KBLI 2025

The 2025 reclassification brought the most significant change to restaurant codes in over a decade.

**Four codes merged into one.** Under KBLI 2020, restaurant-type businesses were split across:

| Old Code (KBLI 2020) | Old Name | Status in 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| 56101 | Restoran | Absorbed into new 56101 |
| 56103 | Warung tenda / food stalls | **Merged into 56101** |
| 56104 | Self-service restaurants | **Merged into 56101** |
| 56109 | Other fixed-building food service | **Merged into 56101** |

The new 56101 is an aggregation of all four. If your company was registered under 56103, 56104, or 56109 before, your code no longer exists as a standalone category. You need to update to 56101 before the **June 18, 2026** migration deadline.

**What this means practically:**

If you registered a cafeteria-style restaurant under 56104, or a food court stall under 56103, those codes now map to 56101. Your NIB will need updating. The licensing requirements of 56101 (including the SLHS for non-micro scale) now apply to you, even if they did not before.

The consolidation makes logical sense -- a restaurant is a restaurant regardless of service style -- but it creates migration work for companies that used the old codes.

## Bali Context: Ground Truth

KBLI 56101 is the single most common business code for foreigners in Bali. Our CRM data confirms what anyone walking through Canggu already knows: the F&B sector is saturated in certain areas and still growing in others.

**Area saturation (as of Q1 2026):**

- **Canggu / Batu Bolong / Berawa:** Extremely saturated. Landlords charge premium rents because they can. New concepts struggle to differentiate. Turnover is high -- we see restaurants change hands every 12--18 months.
- **Seminyak / Petitenget:** Mature market. Established players dominate. Entry costs are the highest on the island.
- **Ubud:** Growing but niche. Wellness-focused dining does well. Casual concepts face less competition than coastal areas.
- **Sanur:** Underserved relative to demand. The older expat demographic is stable and loyal. Lower rents.
- **Uluwatu / Bingin:** Growing rapidly. Still room for new concepts, especially mid-range. Access logistics are a challenge.
- **Tabanan / North Bali:** Virtually untapped. Low rent, low foot traffic. Destination dining only.

**Practical timeline for opening a restaurant in Bali with a PT PMA:**

| Step | Duration |
|------|----------|
| PT PMA registration + NIB | 2--4 weeks |
| Location lease signing | 1--2 weeks |
| SLHS inspection and certificate | 2--4 weeks |
| Interior build-out and kitchen fit | 4--12 weeks |
| Alcohol license (SKPL) if applicable | 2--6 weeks |
| **Total** | **3--6 months** |

The biggest variable is the SLHS inspection. Dinas Kesehatan in Badung (covering Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta) processes more applications than other regencies and can have backlogs.

## Codes You Might Confuse It With

### 56102 -- Food Service in Non-Permanent Buildings

This is the code for food trucks, warung tenda (tent stalls), mobile vendors, and gerobak dorong (push cart sellers). If your structure is not a permanent building, you need 56102, not 56101. The legal test is whether the building can be dismantled or relocated.

**Common mistake:** Beach clubs and open-air restaurants with permanent foundations sometimes get mis-classified as 56102 because they "feel" temporary. If the foundation is permanent, it is 56101.

### 56301 -- Bar

If your primary activity is serving alcoholic beverages rather than food, you need 56301. Many restaurants in Bali derive significant revenue from cocktails and wine -- but as long as food is the primary activity, 56101 is correct. If in doubt, register both codes in your PT PMA. You can have multiple KBLI codes on a single NIB.

### 56303 -- Cafe / Coffee Shop (Rumah Minum / Kafe)

Coffee shops and cafes have their own code. If you serve food alongside coffee, the primary activity determines the code. A brunch spot that also serves excellent coffee is 56101. A specialty coffee roaster with a pastry display is 56303.

### 56210 -- Event Catering (Jasaboga / Katering)

If you prepare food for events, offices, or delivery-only operations without a dine-in component, that is catering under 56210, not 56101.

## TKA (Foreign Worker) Eligible Positions

Under Kepmen 228/2019, KBLI 56101 falls in Category 8 (Accommodation & Food/Beverage). There are 12 TKA-eligible positions in this category, of which 7 are directly relevant to restaurant operations:

| Position (EN) | Position (ID) | ISCO Code |
|---|---|---|
| Operations Manager | Manajer Operasional | 1439 |
| Marketing Manager | Manajer Pemasaran | 1221 |
| Finance Manager | Manajer Keuangan | 1211 |
| Technical Manager | Manajer Teknik | 1439 |
| Guest Relation Manager | Manajer Hubungan Tamu | 1439 |
| Hotel Manager | Manajer Hotel | 1411 |
| Country Club Manager | Manajer Country Club | 1439 |

**Important:** If your restaurant is Small, Medium, or Large scale and employs foreign workers, you must obtain an approved RPTKA (Rencana Penggunaan Tenaga Kerja Asing) before hiring.

**Director/Commissioner exception:** Directors and Commissioners who do not manage personnel (personalia) can work without being listed in the RPTKA jabatan list. This is the standard exception under the Kedua note of the regulation.

## FAQ

### 1. Can I run a restaurant in Bali as a foreigner without a PT PMA?

No. There is no legal structure that allows a foreigner to directly own and operate a restaurant in Indonesia without a PT PMA (or PT PMDN with Indonesian partners). Operating under a nominee arrangement -- where an Indonesian partner "holds" the business on your behalf -- is illegal under Indonesian law and unenforceable in court. Many people do it. It is still illegal.

The PT PMA route is the only fully legal path for 100% foreign ownership of a restaurant.

### 2. Do I need to update my KBLI code if I was registered under 56103, 56104, or 56109?

Yes. Those codes were merged into 56101 under KBLI 2025 (BPS Regulation 7/2025). You have until **June 18, 2026** to update your company's KBLI classification through OSS. After that date, the old codes will not be recognized, and any license application or renewal referencing them will be rejected.

The update process is done through your OSS-RBA account. It requires submitting a company amendment (perubahan akta) through a notaris if the KBLI code change affects your deed of establishment. Budget IDR 2--5 million for the notarial amendment and 1--2 weeks for processing.

### 3. Can I serve alcohol with just a 56101 code?

No. KBLI 56101 authorizes food service, not alcohol sales. To serve alcoholic beverages, you need an additional SKPL (Surat Keterangan Penjualan Langsung Minuman Beralkohol):

- **SKPL-A:** For beer and beverages up to 5% ABV
- **SKPL-B:** For wine and beverages 5--20% ABV
- **SKPL-C:** For spirits and beverages above 20% ABV

Most Bali restaurants serving cocktails need SKPL-B and SKPL-C. You should also add KBLI **56301** (Bar) to your company's registered codes for complete coverage.

The alcohol license is issued by the provincial government (Gubernur) and requires a separate application. Processing time: 2--6 weeks in Bali, depending on backlog.
