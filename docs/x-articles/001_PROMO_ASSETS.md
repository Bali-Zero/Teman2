## 3 Reply Templates

### Template 1: Someone asks "What is KBLI?"

```
KBLI = Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia. It's Indonesia's standard business classification --- think of it as the DNA of your company's legal identity.

Every Indonesian company has KBLI codes in its deed that determine: what you can do, what licenses you need, who supervises you, and whether foreigners can own it.

KBLI 2025 (BPS Regulation 7/2025) replaced the old 2020 system. 1,563 codes, 87 sectors. Compliance deadline: June 18, 2026.
```

### Template 2: Someone asks "Does this affect my PT PMA?"

```
Yes, directly. Your PT PMA's NIB and akta reference KBLI codes. After June 18, 2026, only KBLI 2025 codes are recognized by OSS-RBA.

What to do:
1. Pull your current akta --- list all KBLI codes
2. Map each to the 2025 equivalent
3. Check if foreign ownership conditions changed
4. Amend your notarial deed (IDR 3-7M, 2-4 weeks)
5. Update NIB through OSS

Budget 8-12 weeks if you have multiple codes or restricted activities.
```

### Template 3: Someone asks about a specific code

```
Happy to help with specifics. Each KBLI 2025 code maps to:

- Risk level (Rendah/Menengah Rendah/Menengah Tinggi/Tinggi)
- License type (NIB only, NIB + Sertifikat Standar, or NIB + Izin)
- Processing time (Automatic, 7 days, 15 days, or 30+ days)
- Authority (Bupati, Gubernur, or Minister)
- Foreign ownership status (Terbuka/Terbatas/Tertutup)

Which code are you looking at? The full dataset has 1,563 codes with complete licensing metadata from BPS 7/2025 and PP 28/2024.
```
