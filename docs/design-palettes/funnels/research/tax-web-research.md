### Holiday Shift Rule

## 2. NPWP/NIK Reform (Post-2024)

- **PMK-112**: NIK (16-digit National ID) replaces NPWP for Indonesian individual taxpayers
- **Foreigners**: Get a separate 16-digit NPWP (zero prefix added to old number), validated via passport through Immigration
- **Coretax DJP** system launched January 2025: all monthly compliance through Coretax; annual SPT still via DJP Online
- **NIK = NPWP** applies to Indonesian citizens; foreigners still get a distinct NPWP
- Source: bizindo.com, mamsolutions.net, cekindo.com

## 4. Expat-Specific Concerns (from web research)

### Tax Residency (183-day Rule)
- Stay > 183 days in 12-month period = tax resident
- KITAS holders: tax resident from **Day 1** (not 183 days)
- Immigration now syncs data with DJP — automatic flagging
- Source: sevenstonesindonesia.com, wearesynergypro.com

### Penalties
- Late individual SPT: **IDR 100,000** administrative fine
- Late corporate SPT: **IDR 1,000,000** fine + potential criminal liability
- Not registering NPWP: up to **6 years imprisonment** + 4x tax owed
- Monthly late payment: interest at **benchmark rate + 5%**
- Source: jcss.co.id, lmiconsultancy.com

### Common Expat Mistakes
1. Delaying NPWP registration (think "nobody will notice")
2. Ignoring worldwide income obligation
3. Assuming holidays = no deadlines (deadlines shift, don't disappear)
4. Confusing PB1 with VAT (they're mutually exclusive for hospitality)
5. Not filing even when "dormant" company (still required)

## 5. Compliance Calendar UX — Products Research

### SOTA Products (2025-2026)
1. **Rippling** — unified compliance timeline, proactive alerts, entity-specific filtering
2. **Deel** — country-specific compliance cards, plain-English summaries
3. **Vanta** — real-time progress rings, checklist breakdowns
4. **Remote.com** — visual tax calendar with "handled" toggle per deadline
5. **Carta** — investor reporting timelines with done/upcoming segregation
6. **JCSS Indonesia** — tax compliance calendar with risk matrix, penalty tracking

### Calendar UX Patterns (Cron/Notion/Linear)
- **Cron/Notion Calendar**: Dot indicators under dates, color-coded by calendar, faded past events, keyboard navigation
- **Notion Timeline**: Collapsible rows, filter by property, gantt-style duration bars
- **Linear**: Issue grouping by cycle, status pills (Backlog/Next/Done), hover-to-peek, keyboard-first
- **GitHub Contribution Heatmap**: Density encoding (more activity = darker cell), year-at-a-glance
- **Apple Fitness Rings**: Conic progress, completion motivation, daily/weekly tracking

### Key UX Takeaways
- Dense calendar data → use **dot density** (more dots = busier month)
- **Hover reveals detail**, don't show everything upfront
- **Group similar deadlines** (e.g., "Monthly Withholding" as one dot with count)
- **Collapse/fade past months** to reduce noise
- **Filter is king** — show only what applies to the user's profile
