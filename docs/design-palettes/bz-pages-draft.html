Frequently asked questions

Can I still apply for a B211 visa?
No — B211 and B211A no longer exist as of 2024. They were replaced by e-Visa (e-Visa On Arrival, Visit Visa single/multiple entry, KITAS for longer stays). We'll map your case to the right current vehicle.
How long does a KITAS renewal take?
Typical renewal timeline is 14–21 working days from complete document submission. Start 30 days before expiry. Overstay penalty is IDR 1M/day — we file well in advance.
Do I need to be in Indonesia during the process?
For investor KITAS: you can start remotely. For working KITAS, biometric and interview require in-person presence in Indonesia. We schedule everything to minimize your travel.
What happens if my visa is rejected?
Full refund of our service fee minus government charges already paid. Rejection rate on properly prepared cases is under 2% — we pre-screen eligibility before filing.
Can my family join on the same visa?
Yes — spouse and children under 18 qualify for dependent KITAS (IDR 12M each). Included in the Standard and Premium packages at no extra service fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreigner fully own a restaurant in Bali?
Yes. KBLI 56101 is classified as Open with 100% foreign ownership allowed via PT PMA structure. Minimum authorized capital is IDR 10B with 25% paid-up (IDR 2.5B). No local partner required.
What is the minimum investment for a PT PMA restaurant?
Regulation requires IDR 10,000,000,000 authorized capital minimum, excluding land and buildings. Of this, at least 25% must be paid-up (IDR 2.5B deposited in an Indonesian bank account before registration).
Do I need a separate license to serve alcohol?
Yes. The base KBLI 56101 covers food only. For alcohol service, you need Izin Miras (alcohol permit, ~IDR 5–15M depending on category A/B/C) issued by the local regent or Dinas Pariwisata.
Can I use a leased property for the restaurant?
Yes. Property can be leased (Hak Pakai or long-term rental). You will need the owner's SPPT (land tax certificate) and a notarized lease agreement registered for the operating period.
