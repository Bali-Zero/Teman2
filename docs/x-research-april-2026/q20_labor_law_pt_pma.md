Maximum total duration (including all extensions/renewals) is 5 years. No limit on number of renewals as long as the total does not exceed 5 years. Must be in writing (Bahasa Indonesia primary; bilingual allowed), specify start/end dates, and be registered with the Manpower Office. If limits are breached or used for permanent roles, it automatically becomes PKWTT. No probation period allowed. \naniday.com +1\n  \nPKWTT (permanent / Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tidak Tertentu): For ongoing roles. Probation period maximum 3 months (must be stated in contract).
