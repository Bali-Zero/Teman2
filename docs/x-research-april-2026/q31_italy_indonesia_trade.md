Full-year 2024 machinery exports were ~USD 565 million. \noec.world +1\nIndonesia’s main exports to Italy (2025-Q2): Base metals & products (€479M), chemicals (€416M), and textiles & apparel (€326M — the fashion/textiles sector). Both are part of the Italian export-credit and internationalization support system (under Ministry of Foreign Affairs/MAECI).\nSIMEST: Offers subsidized soft loans, non-repayable grants (up to 20% in some cases for southern Italy/start-ups/women-led firms), and equity financing for internationalization projects, including foreign investments, production facilities, e-commerce, trade fairs, and competitiveness upgrades. Available for non-EU markets like Indonesia (general programs apply; no specific exclusion).\nSACE: Provides guarantees, export-credit insurance, and political/commercial risk coverage for loans/investments, including buyer credit and project finance.\nItalian SMEs/entrepreneurs investing in Indonesia (e.g., machinery, energy, manufacturing) can access these. Check current country-risk ratings, calls for applications, and eligibility on simest.it and sace.it (programs evolve; energy/defense sectors often prioritized). These cover promotion, matchmaking, and training. Check ice.it for open calls (many are competitive and co-financed).
