# Q46+48+49+50+52: Creative Economy, Crypto, Payments, Insurance, Retirement — Bali 2026
**Source:** Grok on X, 2026-04-06

## 1. Creative Economy (Design/Fashion/Arts)
- KBLI 2025 codes: **74130** (graphic design/visual communication), **74100** (specialized design), plus 16 total creative-economy codes per Permen Ekonomi Kreatif No. 7/2025 (fashion, crafts, arts manufacturing, 32xxx textiles/jewelry/apparel)
- Balinese crafts CAN be exported by PT PMA in open Positive Investment List sectors, subject to customs/export compliance + potential local UMKM partnership checks
- **IP protection via DJKI**: foreigners must appoint local Indonesian IP consultant. Faster 2026 processing: 3 months for clean applications (Ministry of Law Reg. No. 5/2026)
- **Italian advantage**: strong competitive edge in Bali's creative hub — international aesthetics + local craftsmanship = fashion, interiors, artisanal exports

## 2. Crypto/Blockchain
- **Legal as regulated digital financial asset** — OJK oversight since Jan 2025 (transferred from Bappebti). Not legal tender per Bank Indonesia
- Primary KBLI: **62014** (blockchain technology dev). Crypto exchanges need specific OJK license (PAKD/CPAKD), NOT simple KBLI 66113
- PT PMA CAN pursue crypto exchange but needs OJK licensing, strict capital/AML/consumer-protection rules. Most licensed platforms remain Indonesian-majority
- **Tax**: 0.21% final income tax on transaction value (sellers, up from 0.1% since Aug 2025). No VAT on crypto sales/transfers (buyers exempt)
- **Bali community**: Coinfest Asia (Aug 2026), Bali Blockchain Week, regular Bitcoin/Canggu meetups

## 3. Digital Payments for PT PMA
- **QRIS**: mandatory/strongly incentivized (57M+ users, 38M+ merchants)
- **Top gateways for PT PMA**:
  - **Xendit** — developer-friendly API, 100+ methods, no setup fees, BEST for foreign/tech businesses
  - **Midtrans** (Goto) — strong SME/local coverage
  - **Doku** — 20+ years, excellent retail reach
- **Stripe NOT available** in Indonesia
- International collections: **Wise Business**, **Payoneer**, direct SWIFT corporate accounts

## 4. Insurance
- **Business property/fire**: IDR 2-10M/year (property is Indonesia's largest general insurance line, ~29% of premiums)
- **Health**: BPJS Kesehatan mandatory (≈5% salary, employer/employee split). Private top-up: **Allianz, AXA Mandiri, Cigna** IDR 5-15M/year for expat plans. New co-payment/waiting-period rules in 2026
- **Workers comp**: mandatory BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (social security for employment injury)

## 5. Retirement Visa (ITAS Lansia) vs Investor KITAS
| | Retirement ITAS | Investor KITAS |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 55+ required | No age limit |
| Income proof | USD 1,500-3,000+/month pension | IDR 2.5B paid-up capital |
| Can work? | NO | YES |
| Duration | 1 year renewable | 2 years renewable |
| Best for | Retirees, passive income | Active entrepreneurs |

- **Italian INPS pension**: transfers via SWIFT to Indonesian bank account. Annual proof-of-life certification via Citibank for overseas recipients — standard procedure, no barriers for Bali residents
