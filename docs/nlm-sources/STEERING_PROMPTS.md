# NotebookLM Video Steering Prompts — KBLI 2025

## How to Use

1. Upload `KBLI_2025_VIDEO_SOURCE.md` as a source to your NotebookLM notebook.
2. Open the Audio Overview / Video generation feature.
3. Paste ONE of the variations below into the custom instructions field.
4. Generate. Download the MP4.
5. Follow `POST_PRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md` for branding and export.

---

## Variation A — Authority Briefing (Recommended for LinkedIn, X main post)

**Target length:** ~3 minutes
**Tone:** Calm, professional, consultative
**Audience:** Foreign business owners already operating in Indonesia

```
Create an authoritative 3-minute briefing for foreign business owners in Indonesia about KBLI 2025 changes. Tone: calm, professional, consultative — like a senior advisor explaining to a board. Focus on three things: (1) what actually changed in the classification system and why it matters for their licenses, (2) who is most affected — especially PT PMA companies and multi-activity businesses, and (3) what they need to do before the June 18, 2026 deadline. Use specific numbers from the source: 1,563 codes, 234 new, 928 unchanged, 266 renumbered. Mention specific codes like 56101 for restaurants and 70209 for consulting as concrete examples. Skip basic definitions of what KBLI is — assume the audience already operates in Indonesia and knows the basics. End with the recommended timeline: audit now, assess in April, prepare in May, execute before June 18.
```

## Variation B — Urgent Alert (Recommended for X short-form, Instagram Reels)

**Target length:** ~2 minutes
**Tone:** Urgent but measured
**Audience:** Entrepreneurs and investors considering or starting businesses in Indonesia

```
Create a concise 2-minute alert for entrepreneurs and investors about the KBLI 2025 compliance deadline. Tone: urgent but measured — not alarmist, but clear that this requires action. Open with the deadline: June 18, 2026. Then cover three critical consequences of non-compliance: license renewals blocked, tax filing misalignment triggering audit flags, and new business activity registration becoming impossible. Then give exactly 3 actions to take now: (1) commission a KBLI code audit mapping all current codes to 2025 equivalents, (2) check if any risk classifications changed — which could mean new permits are needed, and (3) coordinate with both your notary and tax consultant because this is cross-functional, not just an accounting task. Mention that 460 codes — nearly a third — involve mergers, renumbering, or new classifications. Close with: the OSS system hasn't fully integrated KBLI 2025 yet, so prepare now but expect to execute the actual update closer to the deadline.
```

## Variation C — Educational Explainer (Recommended for YouTube, website embed)

**Target length:** ~4 minutes
**Tone:** Approachable expert explaining to a smart non-expert
**Audience:** Prospective investors, digital nomads, entrepreneurs exploring Indonesia

```
Create a clear 4-minute educational overview of Indonesia's business classification system and the 2025 update. Tone: approachable expert — think a knowledgeable friend who happens to be a regulatory specialist. Start by explaining what KBLI is in one sentence: it's the code system that determines what your Indonesian company is legally allowed to do, how it's taxed, and whether foreigners can own it. Then explain why 2025 matters: Indonesia completely revised the system, adding 234 new codes for digital economy and green sectors, consolidating 194 redundant categories, and renumbering 266 others. Use real examples: code 56101 is what you need if you're opening a restaurant in Bali, code 70209 is the go-to for management consultants, and code 62019 covers software development — all three allow 100% foreign ownership. Explain that 96.7% of all 1,563 codes are now open to foreign investment, which is a significant liberalization. Then explain the risk-based licensing system: low risk gets automatic approval, high risk needs full environmental review. Cover the June 18, 2026 deadline and the fact that missing it means your licenses can't be renewed. End by walking through the three-step migration process: audit your codes, assess the impact, then update in the OSS system.
```

## Notes for Production

- All three variations reference the same source document. NLM will pull data from the source.
- **Do not mention any company or brand name** in the steering prompts. Brand overlay is added in post-production.
- After generating, listen to the full output before starting post-production. NLM occasionally paraphrases numbers — verify accuracy of: 1,563 total codes, 234 new, June 18, 2026 deadline.
- If NLM produces content longer than target length, it can be trimmed in DaVinci Resolve (see post-production checklist for cut points).
