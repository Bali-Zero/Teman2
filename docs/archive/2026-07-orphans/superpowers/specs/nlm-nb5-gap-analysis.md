## PART I — LIFECYCLE GAPS (DeepSeek analysis)

### STAGE 1 — Pre-Acquisition

#### PROP-108 — Due Diligence Procedural Checklist

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (mentioned conceptually, no sourced procedure)
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- Step-by-step: which documents, from which offices, in what order
- BPN certificate: check at local Kantor Pertanahan (not online)
- SPPT PBB: 5 years arrears check via dispenda.badungkab.go.id or local BPKPD office
- Land block map (peta bidang tanah): available via BPN BHUMI app or direct at Kantor Pertanahan
- Zoning certificate: via GISTARU or RDTR map at local BPKPD + Tarubali cross-check
- PPJB review: before signing any reservation or deposit
- Temple exclusion zones: NOT on any government GIS — must be verified locally with banjar/desa adat

#### PROP-109 — PBB Arrears as Transaction Blocker

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (PBB mentioned as tax, not as blocker)
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- Outstanding PBB arrears PREVENT BPN registration transfer (AJB-to-SHM/HP)
- PPAT legally required to verify SPPT payment history before notarizing AJB
- Hidden arrears from absentee owners: common in Bali (non-resident WNA sellers)
- Solution: buyer requests 5-year SPPT receipts as condition of PPJB
- Check via: local BPKPD office with parcel certificate number

#### PROP-110 — BPN Dispute Register Verification

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- Pre-existing sengketa (disputes) on a parcel are registered at BPN
- Request: "Surat Keterangan Bebas Sengketa" from local Kantor Pertanahan
- Common Bali dispute types: inheritance disputes, duplicate certificates, adat vs. state law conflicts
- BPN has endemic duplicate SHM/SHGB due to incomplete digitization of legacy paper records

#### PROP-111 — Tanah Girik: Risks and Conversion

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- Girik (Letter C/D, petuk D) is NOT legal proof of ownership — only historical tax payment record
- ATR/BPN confirmed girik no longer accepted as ownership evidence from 2026 (PP 18/2021 basis)
- Price discount: 10–40% below equivalent SHM land (Prof. Mardiman Sane, Universitas Mpu Tantular)
- Conversion timeline: 6–18 months (village history cert → BPN measurement → 14-day public notice → SHM)
- 60-day public notice period: window for third-party claims — multiple family members may emerge
- Foreigners cannot hold HM (Hak Milik) — girik conversion produces SHM → foreign buyer needs HP/HGB
- Fee formula (PP 13/2010): Tu = (L/500 × HSBKu) + Rp 100,000; Registration: Rp 50,000
- Fraud precedent: Jimbaran — investor SN lost Rp 24.7B (July–Oct 2024, police: LP/B/165/III/2025)
- Girik risk: easily forged, purchase history undocumented, unclear boundaries, multiple inheritance claims
- **Sources:** detik.com/properti/d-8218287 (T3, SVS 0.88), bfi.co.id/en/blog/cara-mengubah-tanah-girik-ke-SHM (T2, SVS 0.82), nusabali.com/berita/215837 (T3, SVS 0.92)

#### PROP-112 — KKPR/RDTR Spatial Verification in Practice

**Status:** THIN IN NB-5 (KKPR defined but not how to verify)
**Priority:** HIGH

- GISTARU (gistaru.atrbpn.go.id): national zoning map — check parcel zone BEFORE any investment
- Bali temple exclusion zones: NOT on GISTARU or any government GIS — verify locally with banjar/desa adat
- Green zone (kawasan hijau, HP/hutan): ANY structure = demolition risk, no compensation
- Tarubali (tarubali.baliprov.go.id): provincial spatial planning portal — most current Bali RDTR data
- **Source:** tarubali.baliprov.go.id/implementasi-kebijakan... (T0, SVS 0.95)

#### PROP-113 — Awig-Awig Due Diligence

**Status:** PARTIALLY IN NB-5 (flagged as "known gap" — understated)
**Priority:** HIGH

- Awig-awig: customary law of each desa adat — not digitized, not standardized, varies by village
- Tanah ayahan desa: land with communal service obligations attached — WILL follow the buyer
- Ngampel rights: desa adat claim on land performance/duties even after private sale
- Palemahan: spatial/boundary rights of the village affecting land use even with valid BPN cert
- Due diligence: must visit the desa adat head (bendesa adat) and review written awig-awig if available
- PTSL program (mass certification) risk: girik land under individual name being certified without desa adat consultation

### STAGE 2 — Transaction

#### PROP-114 — PPJB Protective Clauses

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (PPJB mentioned, no clause detail)
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- PPJB (Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli) = binding sale-purchase agreement BEFORE AJB
- Specific performance clause: buyer can force sale even if seller refuses
- Down payment forfeiture: MUST be explicit — Indonesian courts default to returning 2× down if absent
- Seller warranty: clear title, no encumbrances, no disputes, PBG compliant, SLF current
- Force majeure definition: avoid broad clauses that excuse seller from regulatory delays
- PPJB is notarial — must be by a licensed PPAT (not just any notaris)

#### PROP-115 — Notaris vs PPAT Distinction

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- A notaris who is NOT also a PPAT cannot execute an AJB (Akta Jual Beli) — the title transfer deed
- AJB signed by notaris-only (not PPAT) = VOID for land title transfer purposes
- PPAT authorization is territory-specific: PPAT authorized in Denpasar CANNOT notarize a deal in Gianyar
- Check PPAT authorization: verify at local BPN Kantor Pertanahan before signing
- Common client confusion: "I signed with a notary" ≠ title has transferred

#### PROP-116 — Foreign Currency Transaction Restriction

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- UU 7/2011 (Currency Law): ALL domestic property transactions must settle in IDR
- Bali villa prices quoted in USD universally — legally non-compliant as transaction currency
- Compliant workaround: USD-quoted price converted to IDR at spot rate in the AJB
- Risk: USD-denominated purchase without IDR AJB is technically voidable; BPN only registers IDR transactions
- Withholding at source: PPh is calculated on IDR AJB value — underdeclared IDR = tax exposure

#### PROP-117 — Surat Kuasa Mutlak (Irrevocable POA) — Prohibited

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- "Surat Kuasa Mutlak" (irrevocable/unconditional power of attorney) is explicitly PROHIBITED
- Legal basis: Instruksi Mendagri No. 14/1982 — creates voidable title
- Common agent scheme: sell property via irrevocable POA to avoid formal AJB process
- Risk: if BPN discovers irrevocable POA basis, registration can be challenged
- Valid POA for AJB: "Surat Kuasa Memasang Hak Tanggungan" (limited purpose, revocable)

### STAGE 3 — Ownership

#### PROP-118 — PBB Registration for Non-Resident WNA Hak Pakai Holders

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- SPPT PBB (annual tax notice) delivered BY PAPER to registered address
- Non-resident WNA owners routinely miss paper delivery → silent PBB arrears accumulate
- Arrears block: refinancing, title transfer, permit renewals
- Solution: register local representative (kuasa) with BPKPD for paper receipt
- Online check: via dispenda.badungkab.go.id or local BPKPD portal (not yet unified nationally)
- NJOP shock 2025: Badung commercial land Rp 28,774/m² (2024) → Rp 1,027,225/m² (2025) = **+3,569%** (UU 1/2022 HKPD 3-year revaluation cycle)
- Gianyar commercial PBB ~+700%; Gianyar 2026 policy: free for Gianyar residents
- Buleleng: 90% discount offered; Klungkung: no increase
- **Sources:** balipost.com/news/2025/08/18/481362 (T3, SVS 0.88), wartabalionline.com/2025/08/16/kenaikan-pajak-pbb-badung-3500 (T3, SVS 0.85)

#### PROP-119 — PBG on Leasehold: Lessee or Landowner Applies?

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (genuine legal ambiguity)
**Priority:** CRITICAL — genuine legal ambiguity (flag explicitly)

- PP 16/2021 is unclear on this point; Bali kabupaten practice is inconsistent
- Badung DPMPTSP practice: lessee applies with explicit "right to build" clause in lease contract
- Gianyar practice: some cases require landowner co-application
- Lease contract MUST explicitly grant "right to build" (hak mendirikan bangunan) — not assumed from Hak Sewa alone
- Without PBG: SLF cannot issue → NIB operational license cannot issue → commercial operation illegal
- **NB-5 must flag as genuine ambiguity — verify at local DPMPTSP before signing lease**

#### PROP-120 — SLF (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi): Full Process

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (mentioned but no process or validity periods)
**Priority:** HIGH

- IMB officially replaced by PBG (PP 16/2021) — any guide mentioning IMB is outdated
- SLF = occupancy certificate required AFTER construction completion
- SLF validity by type:
  - Residential villa: **20 years** (then renewal required)
  - Commercial property (hotel, resort, restaurant): **5 years** (mandatory renewal — expensive)
- SLF inspection triggered at 90–100% construction completion
- SIMBG (Sistem Informasi Manajemen Bangunan Gedung): national permit portal for PBG/SLF applications
- **Without SLF:** insurance policies considered VOID by most Bali insurers; hospitality licensing blocked
- **Sources:** kingswoodbalivillas.com/pbg-slf (T2, SVS 0.89), cptcorporate.com/villa-pbg-slf (T2, SVS 0.87)

#### PROP-121 — Bali AMDAL/UKL-UPL/SPPL Thresholds

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- Bali provincial thresholds (Pergub) are STRICTER than national PP 22/2021 standards
- Environmental approval tier depends on project scale AND location sensitivity
  - AMDAL: large/environmentally sensitive projects (major resorts, >5ha)
  - UKL-UPL: moderate impact (most commercial villas)
  - SPPL: small villas (self-declaration for minor works)
- Subak zone (Tabanan, Gianyar, Ubud surroundings): AMDAL mandatory regardless of scale
- Without environmental approval: PBG stalls → SLF cannot issue (even with valid title)
- Permit chain: KKPR → Environmental Approval → PBG → construction → SLF → NIB operational

### STAGE 4 — Monetization

#### PROP-122 — Can WNA Hak Pakai Holder Commercially Rent?

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (genuine legal ambiguity)
**Priority:** CRITICAL — genuine legal ambiguity (flag explicitly)

- HP is granted for residential use; commercial rental arguably triggers PP 18/2021 Art.60 cancellation grounds
- **Reality:** zero enforcement cases documented; widespread WNA Airbnb operation via HP
- **Legal risk:** HP can theoretically be revoked for unauthorized commercial use
- **Correct structure:** PT PMA + HGB for commercial rental (no ambiguity)
- **NB-5 must flag: HP commercial rental = unenforced legal risk. PT PMA = clean structure.**

#### PROP-123 — Resident vs Non-Resident WNA PPh Tax Rate

**Status:** THIN IN NB-5 (10% mentioned, 20% not)
**Priority:** HIGH

- Resident WNA (KITAS/KITAP holder): PPh Pasal 4(2) = **10%** of gross rental income
- Non-resident WNA (no valid Indonesian residence permit): PPh Pasal 26 = **20%** of gross rental income
- Withholding mechanism: OTA platforms (Airbnb, Agoda) withhold if NPWP not provided
- PMK 48/2020: OTA platforms must verify NPWP — without NPWP, highest rate applies
- DGT enforcement trajectory: increasing scrutiny of WNA rental income

#### PROP-124 — OTA Platform Tax Compliance

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- PMK 48/2020: OTA platforms collect and remit PPh on WNA property rental income
- NPWP required to receive correct withholding rate (10% vs 20%)
- **DEADLINE TODAY (March 31, 2026):** All OTA-listed properties must have verified NIB (Permenpar 6/2025)
- OTA badge system: "Terdaftar dan Berizin" badge = verified NIB → unlisted properties hidden from April 1
- **Source:** balipropertyrules.com/guides/bali-villa-licensing-foreigners (T2, SVS 0.97)

#### PROP-125 — Villa Licensing: Pondok Wisata vs PT PMA + KBLI 55193

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5 (bare mention, no category breakdown)
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- KBLI 55191: Pondok Wisata — **RESTRICTED to Indonesian citizens** (Permenpar 18/2016)
- KBLI 55193: Villa — PT PMA eligible (foreign investment allowed)
- KBLI 55110: Hotel — PT PMA eligible
- Foreigners operating as Pondok Wisata: IDR 50M+ fines, deportation, **1–6 year blacklist**
- 48 illegal structures demolished Bingin Beach, July 2025 (Morabito Art Clift precedent)
- Tourism accommodation MUST be in pink/tourism-zoned land (Green = demolition risk)

### STAGE 5 — Exit

#### PROP-126 — Capital Gains: Mechanics for Foreign Sellers

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- PPAT withholds PPh at AJB signing (source withholding — seller cannot "forget" to pay)
- Resident WNA seller: PPh Final Pasal 4(2) = **2.5%** of gross transaction value (AJB price)
- Non-resident WNA seller: PPh Pasal 26 = **20%** of gross gain (or 2.5% if tax treaty applies)
- Underdeclared AJB value (below market): DGT audits common; penalty 2× unpaid tax
- Developer/first sale: VAT (PPN 11%) may also apply if seller is PKP-registered entity
- BPHTB buyer's duty: 5% of (AJB value − NJOPTKP threshold)

#### PROP-127 — Forced Divestment Procedure

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- PP 18/2021 Art.52: WNA who loses KITAS/KITAP has **1 year** to transfer HP to eligible party
- Options within 1 year: sell to Indonesian citizen, transfer to Indonesian entity, convert to another eligible foreigner with valid permit
- If nothing done in 1 year: **property reverts to State with ZERO compensation**
- Step-by-step procedure: notify BPN within 3 months of permit loss → BPN issues grace period notice → sale or transfer by notarial AJB within 1-year window
- PT PMA avoidance: company holds HGB → individual KITAS loss does not trigger divestment (shares transfer separately)
- WNA death without eligible heirs: same 1-year rule, public administrator may be needed

#### PROP-128 — PPJB Default Remedies

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** MEDIUM

- If seller defaults: buyer can seek specific performance (Indonesian courts) but litigation takes 3–5 years
- Down payment forfeiture by seller: only if explicitly stated in PPJB — not automatic under Indonesian civil law (KUHPerdata)
- If buyer defaults: seller keeps deposit ONLY if PPJB explicitly states forfeiture clause
- Practical resolution: most disputes settled via negotiation + notarized agreement rather than litigation

#### PROP-129 — WNA Inheritance: 1-Year Rule

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- PP 18/2021 Art.56: foreign heirs WITHOUT valid KITAS/KITAP have **1 year** to transfer or sell
- If neither obtained nor sold: property reverts to State with ZERO compensation
- Hak Milik (SHM) heirs: same rule — HP heir must have valid residence permit
- PT PMA inheritance: shares transfer to heirs — no land title divestment triggered (preferred for estate planning)
- Hak Sewa inheritance: governed by lease contract succession clause — must be explicit
- Apostilled documents required: death certificate + inheritance proof (will/probate order)
- Cross-reference: NB-2 for which visa qualifies heirs as eligible holders

### CROSS-CUTTING CLAIMS

#### PROP-130 — Hak Tanggungan Mortgage for WNA

**Status:** THIN IN NB-5
**Priority:** MEDIUM

- Hak Pakai (individual) and HGB (PT PMA) can serve as Hak Tanggungan (mortgage collateral) — ATR/BPN Regulation 18/2021 Art.186
- **Hak Sewa (leasehold): CANNOT serve as Hak Tanggungan** — not a registered land title, no collateral value
- Banks offering WNA KPR (2025–2026): Permata Bank iB IMBT WNA (launched June 2025), Commonwealth Bank, J-Trust Bank, HSBC Indonesia
- LTV for foreigners: 50–70% (30–50% down payment); locals: 70–80%
- Minimum property value Bali: IDR 5 billion (≈ USD 305K) — highest threshold in Indonesia
- Max tenor: 15–25 years for foreigners (vs 30 for locals)
- KITAS/KITAP required: all banks
- PT PMA route: company borrows against HGB → better LTV terms, no personal KITAS dependency
- **Critical negative claim for NB-5:** Leasehold cannot be mortgaged — clients building leasehold villa cannot leverage against it
- **Sources:** ilotpropertybali.com/kpr-foreigners (T3, SVS 0.91), rumavi.com/foreigners-mortgage (T2, SVS 0.91)

#### PROP-131 — BPN BHUMI Digital Certificate Verification

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- ATR/BPN: all certificates transitioning to digital from 2026 (gradual rollout)
- Physical certificates remain legally valid until individual conversion process completed
- **DEBUNK:** viral claim "not converting by 2026 = state confiscation" is FALSE (officially debunked by ATR/BPN)
- BHUMI app: verify certificate authenticity, ownership history, encumbrances
- Electronic SHM/HGB cannot be physically forged → stronger fraud protection
- Risk: if seller's certificate not yet in digital system, BPN transfer process may be slower
- **Source:** detik.com/properti/d-7769213 (T3, SVS 0.72)

#### PROP-132 — Construction Moratorium Scope (Updated 2026)

**Status:** IN NB-5 but needs update (Perda 4/2026 not yet incorporated)
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- **Active moratorium** — no formal end date set by Governor Koster
- **6 restricted districts (formal ban, new tourism accommodation):** Tabanan, Jembrana, Buleleng, Bangli, Karangasem, Klungkung (including Nusa Penida)
- **Exception districts (NOT in 6-district ban):** Badung (Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta, Uluwatu), Gianyar (Ubud, Sanur north), Denpasar
- **Province-wide agricultural land ban (applies EVERYWHERE incl. Badung/Gianyar/Denpasar):** productive sawah/rice paddies cannot be commercially developed
- **Perda 4/2026 (enacted Feb 24, 2026):** rice field conversion to commercial = CRIMINAL OFFENSE (jail time, not just fines); LP2B area fallen from 70,996 ha (2019) to 64,474 ha (2026)
- **Perda 4/2026 retroactivity:** structures established before Feb 24, 2026 not retroactively criminal; but ONGOING maintenance of nominee structures = possible continuing offense
- What is NOT restricted: existing approved construction (pre-moratorium PBG valid), residential construction for personal use, renovation of existing structures
- **Sources:** tarubali.baliprov.go.id/moratorium... (T0, SVS 0.93), emerhub.com/news/bali-criminalizes-rice-field-conversions/ (T1, SVS 0.92), balipropertyrules.com/guides/bali-construction-moratorium-foreigners/ (T2, SVS 0.89)

## PART II — ADDITIONAL TOPIC GAPS (xAI + Exa + Gemini analysis)

### PROP-133 — Hak Sewa: Full Contract Guide

**Status:** THIN IN NB-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL

- Extension clause: "jaminan perpanjangan" (binding guarantee) vs. "prioritas perpanjangan" (first refusal only) — always insist on guarantee language
- Extension pricing mechanism: fixed / CPI-indexed / independent appraisal — "to be agreed later" = no protection
- Improvement reversion: ALL permanent structures (villas, pools) revert to landowner at expiry with ZERO compensation (default Indonesian civil law) — must have explicit compensation clause
- **Maximum market structures:** 25+20+15 = 60 yrs (standard), 30+20+30 = 80 yrs (maximum)
- No visa required: unlike Hak Pakai — valid passport sufficient for Hak Sewa
- BPN registration of lease: not mandatory but possible for stronger protection
- **Mortgage-on-underlying-title risk:** if freehold owner mortgaged their SHM, lease is VOID upon foreclosure — check SHM for Hak Tanggungan encumbrance before signing
- PPAT territory rule: PPAT authorized in Denpasar CANNOT notarize Gianyar deal — verify PPAT authorization
- Landowner death risk: heirs are bound but may not cooperate with permit renewals
- Landowner sale risk: new owner bound by lease but relationship must be rebuilt
- **Sources:** balipropertyrules.com/guides/leasehold-hak-sewa-bali-guide/ (T2, SVS 0.91), ppbali.com/guide-to-leasehold... (T2, SVS 0.93), sanurvillalandhunter.com/hak-sewa (T2, SVS 0.81)

### PROP-134, 135 — Adat Land / Tanah Ulayat

**Status:** PARTIALLY IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- Tanah druwe desa: communally-held land managed for benefit of kahyangan tiga temple complex — NOT individually owned
- PTSL (Programa Tanah Sistematis Lengkap) gap: mass certification can privatize communal girik land without desa adat consultation — active dispute risk in Tabanan, Gianyar
- Foreigners leasing adat land: technically possible but disputed title risk + awig-awig restrictions
- Subak zones (UNESCO World Heritage): Perda 4/2026 specifically targets anti-conversion enforcement
- Tanah ulayat (PP 18/2021 update): community rights recognized but not uniformly implemented
- Due diligence red flags: location in subak zone, very recent SHM on historically girik land, landowner is village official or temple administrator
- **Sources:** kanalbali.id/awas-sertifikasi... (T0, SVS 0.85), tarubali.baliprov.go.id/implementasi... (T0, SVS 0.95)

### PROP-136–138 — Strata Title (HMSRS) Paradox

**Status:** NOT IN NB-5
**Priority:** HIGH

- HMSRS = Hak Milik atas Satuan Rumah Susun — foreigners CAN hold apartment unit title (PP 18/2021 Art.141–144)
- **HGB paradox:** unit title can be in foreigner's name BUT underlying land is HGB held by developer — if developer fails to renew HGB, entire structure's legal status is at risk
- Conditions for foreigner HMSRS: commercial zone only, KITAS/KITAP required, min unit value IDR 5B (Bali), max 1 unit per individual, ready-built only (no off-plan)
- **Practical Bali situation 2026:** very few Bali developments structured as true HMSRS — most use Hak Pakai or leasehold instead
- Effective duration: same 80-year maximum as Hak Pakai (30+20+30) because dependent on underlying HGB renewal
- **Sources:** balipropertyrules.com/guides/pp-28-2025-foreign-property-bali/ (T2, SVS 0.78), bambooroutes.com/blogs/news/foreigners-buy-apartment-indonesia (T3, SVS 0.75)
