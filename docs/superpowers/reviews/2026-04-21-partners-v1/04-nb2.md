# NB-2 Immigration Review — Partners Module v1

**Notebook:** NB-2 Immigration & Visa Indonesia 2025 (`cff93ab0-...`)
**Queried:** 2026-04-21 post-implementation, full diff review.

## Verdict (immigration-law angle)

**SHIP WITH 2 CRITICAL FIXES** before onboarding the first foreign partner.

## CRITICAL

### C-IMM-1 — `entity_type="foreign"` is unsafe as-is

Paying commission to a foreign partner with any standard KITAS violates the KITAS conditions directly:

| KITAS type | Violation |
|---|---|
| E23 (work) | "autorizza il lavoro esclusivamente per il datore di lavoro indicato nel RPTKA" — commission from Bali Zero = unauthorized employer. Violation "indipendentemente dalla natura (part-time, consulenza occasionale, prestazione professionale)". |
| E33G (digital nomad) | "VIETATO generare qualsiasi reddito da clienti o fonti locali indonesiane". |
| E33F/E33E (pensioner/silver hair) | "Ricezione di compensi, stipendi o onorari da fonti indonesiane" tassativamente vietata. |

**Consequences per NB-2:** KITAS revoked, administrative detention, deportation, *cekal* (re-entry ban).

**Only safe foreign partner:** KITAP holder or foreign entity domiciled abroad (paid in IDR or FX to a bank abroad, documented as arm's-length service to a non-resident supplier — separate fiscal question, out of NB-2 scope).

**Fix before v1.1:**
1. Rename/refine `entity_type="foreign"` → split into:
   - `foreign_kitap` (permanent resident — compensation allowed)
   - `foreign_offshore` (entity domiciled abroad — paid via international wire)
   - Remove the generic "foreign" as ambiguous.
2. If `entity_type ∈ {foreign_kitap, foreign_offshore}`, require explicit admin override + a documented legal basis note stored on the partner record.
3. Add an NB-2-sourced warning on the partner creation form when entity_type is either of these two values.

### C-IMM-2 — Soft warning on "sponsor|garante|penjamin" is insufficient

Per NB-2: **hard block required**. *Penjamin* has legal/criminal liability under PP 31/2013 Pasal 1 Ayat 20, and a CRM field that mislabels a referrer as sponsor risks data-mismatch with SIMKIM submissions (which have a "One Sponsor Policy" per Kemnaker SE 3/836/PK.04/I/2026 — ITK and RPTKA sponsors must match).

**Fix:**
1. Promote the warning to an error: if `work_role` matches `/sponsor|garante|penjamin/i`, **block activation** (not creation — save as draft) until either (a) the work_role is corrected or (b) admin overrides with a documented exception reason.
2. Add a CHECK constraint or service-layer guard on `onboarding_status='active'` transition.

## IMPORTANT

### I-IMM-1 — No "licensed intermediary" requirement found

Referral fees to third parties who merely direct clients to Bali Zero (with Bali Zero as sole *Penjamin* on SIMKIM) are **not classified or sanctioned** by Permenkumham 11/2024 or Permenimipas 5/2025 as "illegal intermediation of visa services." The immigration regime only constrains formal sponsors (agenzie autorizzate Kemenimipas for E33F, impresariati for C7/C8).

This confirms the v1 business model is compliant on the immigration side — but only for the non-sponsor referral path.

### Out of scope (NB-2 cannot answer)

- Fiscal distinction between one-time referral fee vs continuous business-partnership compensation (potentially OSS/NIB-triggering): **consult accountant**, not NB-2.
- UU PDP classification of partner data as PII: out of NB-2 scope.
- Whether the 10% default commission is a "service fee" (PPh 23 @ 2%) or "employment-like compensation" (PPh 21 progressive): out of NB-2 scope.

## One-sentence summary

Ship v1 for Indonesian-national partners only; block `entity_type="foreign"` onboarding and harden the sponsor/garante check to error-level before the first foreign partner is created.
