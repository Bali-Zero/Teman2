# Zantara S01E13 camera-probe execution plan

## Goal

Generate and evaluate an adaptive wave of identity-preserving Flow/Veo probes for **What Your Residency Permit Does Not Come With**, starting with six genuinely different camera families and never exceeding 24 attempts, without entering factual scripting or publishing.

## Inputs

- `docs/wr3/factory/episodes/s01e13-residency-permit/context-snapshot.json`
- `docs/wr3/factory/episodes/s01e13-residency-permit/creative-lock.json`
- `docs/wr3/factory/episodes/s01e13-residency-permit/probes/probe-plan.json`
- A007 identity anchor on Pro
- six runtime `shot-pack.json` files, four shots each

## Execution

1. Validate all JSON artifacts and resolved prompt word counts.
2. Run FlowKit health and credit preflight on Pro immediately before generation.
3. Record the scoped operator spend decision for at most 24 probe generations and 240 measured credits.
4. Copy only the runtime probe packs to Pro; keep source edits in the isolated Air-M5 worktree.
5. Render the first declared variant from each of the six families. This frontier compares six different ideas before comparing small variations of one idea.
6. Verify every output with `ffprobe`; record missing, corrupt, wrong-aspect, or short clips as failures rather than silently retrying the whole wave.
7. Extract start, middle, and end keyframes on Pro and build the frontier contact sheet.
8. Run deterministic SHA-256/perceptual duplicate checks, the existing cliché gate, identity verification, and one local VLM contact-sheet review.
9. Review visual continuity, edit surfaces, native sound, and narrative fitness. Select or reject each frontier probe with one concrete reason.
10. Open additional variants only for a recorded ambiguity, technical failure, or close contest. Use the already-authored axis; do not invoke a new creative reasoning pass.
11. Persist the generation receipt, review ledger, selected grammar, and updated factory state. Leave publication and deployment switches at zero.

## Stop conditions

- FlowKit loses extension or authentication twice after the standard reconnect retry.
- The A007 identity anchor cannot be verified on Pro.
- A billing call returns without durable job/spend evidence.
- A family produces no valid artifact after its declared four variants.
- Any request would require a legal claim not present in the context snapshot.

## Definition of done

- every submitted attempt accounted for, with six frontier probes and no more than 24 attempts;
- valid media and job lineage preserved;
- contact sheets and QA reports generated;
- one winning camera grammar selected or a precise failure report produced;
- no final script, upload, publication, deployment, or outward message.
