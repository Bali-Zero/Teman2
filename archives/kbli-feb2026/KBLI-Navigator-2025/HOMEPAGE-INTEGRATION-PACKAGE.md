```markdown
Every KBLI code in the 2025 system carries a risk classification: Low (L), Medium-Low (ML), Medium-High (MH), or High (H). This four-tier system, established under PP 5/2021, determines regulatory intensity for your business operations.

Low-risk codes (430 total) represent minimal regulatory oversight—think digital services, consulting, and e-commerce platforms. These businesses face streamlined permit processes and fewer compliance requirements. On the opposite end, High-risk codes (375 total) include manufacturing, construction, and resource extraction sectors requiring enhanced safety protocols and stricter foreign worker limitations.

The practical implications are immediate. A restaurant (code 56101, Medium-High risk) faces different foreign worker quota calculations than an IT services company (code 62191, Medium-Low risk). Understanding your code's risk level is essential for accurate workforce planning and permit applications.
```
