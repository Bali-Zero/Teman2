## A1 - Visa-Free Tourism

**Purpose:**
For tourism, visiting family, attending meetings, incentives, conventions, and exhibitions (MICE), or similar events, as well as transit in Indonesia to another country.

**Visa Type:**
Visa-Free Facility is a category intended for nationals of certain countries for a single entry into Indonesia with a maximum stay permit of 30 days, calculated from the date of arrival. This stay permit cannot be extended and cannot be converted into another type of stay permit.

**Duration of Stay:**
You can stay in Indonesia for a maximum of 30 days, calculated from the date of arrival. This stay permit cannot be extended.

**Sponsor:**
You do not need a Sponsor to apply for this visa-free facility.

**Visa Application:**
*   **Upon Arrival:** You must present:
    *   A passport valid for at least 6 months before its expiration date.
    *   A return ticket or onward ticket to another country (except for transport crew members joining their ship to continue their journey).
    *   The immigration officer at the counter will stamp an entry permit which also serves as your stay permit.
*   **Pre-Arrival (Electronic):**
    *   You must fill out the electronic form at evisa.immigration.go.id.
    *   You will receive an electronic entry stamp which also serves as your stay permit and can be used to use the autogate at immigration checkpoints.

**Other Provisions:**
*   Stateless foreigners and foreigners holding travel documents other than a national passport (such as temporary passports, emergency passports, titre du voyage, certificates of identity, laissez-passer, or similar documents) are not eligible for the Visa-Free Facility.
*   Foreigners holding a Visa-Free Facility stay permit are prohibited from selling goods or services or receiving compensation, wages, or similar payment for work/services from individuals or corporations in Indonesia.

## B1 - Tourism Visit Visa (Visa on Arrival)

**Purpose:**
Tourism, visiting family, attending meetings, incentives, conventions, and exhibitions (MICE), or transit.

**Visa Type:**
B1 is a Visit Visa Upon Arrival (Visa on Arrival / VoA) for eligible nationals. Single entry, initial stay of 30 days.
*   Can be extended once.
*   Can be converted to another stay permit type via the bridging visa mechanism.

**Duration of Stay:**
*   Initial: 30 days.
*   Extension: Once, for up to 30 additional days (Total max 60 days).

**Extension Process:**
*   **e-VoA:** Online via evisa.immigration.go.id or indonesiavoa.vfsevisa.id.
*   **Regular VoA:** At the nearest Immigration Office.

**Fees:**
*   Visa Fee: Rp 500,000.

**Application:**
*   **Pre-Arrival:** Apply and pay online.
*   **Upon Arrival:** Apply and pay at the designated bank counter in the immigration area. Requirements: Passport (valid 6 months), return ticket.

**Other Provisions:**
*   Holders of temporary/emergency passports are allowed to apply for VoA (must be valid for 12 months in some cases/require scrutiny).
*   Prohibited from working or selling goods/services.

## C1 - Tourism Visit Visa (Pre-Arranged)

**Purpose:**
Tourism, personal development, studying tourism attractions, cruise ship travel. Also attending MICE as a participant, conducting business discussions/negotiations, signing agreements, field surveys (offices/factories/mining sites), or medical-related activities.

**Visa Type:**
Visit Visa (Single Entry).
*   **Initial Stay:** 60 days.
*   **Extensions:** Can be extended multiple times up to a total of 180 days.
*   **Conversion:** Can be converted to a Limited Stay Permit (ITAS).

**Cost:**
Rp 1,000,000 (plus verification fees if applicable).

**Requirements:**
*   Passport valid for at least 6 months.
*   Proof of living costs (bank statement, last 3 months) min. USD 2,000.
*   Return ticket.
*   Recent color photo.
*   No sponsor required (unless stateless or from specific calling-visa countries).
