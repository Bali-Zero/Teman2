- Knowledge base covers: KBLI 2025, visa types (KITAS/KITAP/B211A), PT PMA setup, tax (PPh/PPN/NPWP)

1. **KBLI 2025 impact analysis**: What sectors have been opened/closed to foreign investment since the KBLI 2025 update? Which KBLI codes are most requested by expats in Bali? New restrictions or opportunities for digital/tech businesses.

5. **PT PMA regulatory changes**: Minimum capital requirements (real enforcement vs written law), negative investment list changes, OSS (Online Single Submission) system status 2026, local director requirements.

6. **Digital nomad ecosystem**: Is Indonesia officially recognizing digital nomads? Comparison of Bali's positioning vs Chiang Mai, Lisbon, Dubai, Medellín. Regulatory framework gaps.

## Output Format

For each topic: current state (March 2026), recent changes (last 6 months), predicted direction (next 12 months), and actionable intelligence for a consulting firm.
