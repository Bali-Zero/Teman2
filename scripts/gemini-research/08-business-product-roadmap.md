# Deep Research: Product Roadmap & Feature Prioritization — Zantara AI Platform 2026

You are researching for Bali Zero / Zantara AI — an AI-powered business intelligence platform for Indonesia.

## Current Product Features

- Zantara AI chat (24/7, multilingual: EN/IT/ID)
- Client portal (visa tracking, documents, messaging)
- CRM with 5000+ clients
- KBLI 2025 Navigator (1,563 business codes with AI explanations)

- Intel pipeline (automated news → enrichment → publishing)
- 6 subdomains: kita, mail, calendar, drive, knowledge, zantara
- WhatsApp + Telegram integrations
- Prime Intelligence (spatial data / zoning maps — early stage)

## Research Questions

1. **Feature prioritization frameworks**: For a 2-person team with AI leverage, what's the best framework to decide what to build next? RICE, ICE, Kano, or something designed for AI-native products? How to measure AI feature impact.

2. **Client portal benchmarks**: What do the best client portals in professional services look like in 2026? (Deel, Rippling, Gusto for reference). Must-have features for immigration consulting portals. Real-time visa status tracking — how do competitors implement it?

3. **WhatsApp Business API opportunities**: Beyond chat — WhatsApp Flows, product catalogs, payment integration (in Indonesia context). What's possible with the 2026 API? Examples of consulting firms using WhatsApp as a full-service channel.

4. **Spatial intelligence / PropTech**: Our Prime Intelligence feature does zoning lookups. What's the opportunity for property intelligence in Bali? Foreign ownership rules, green zone mapping, price intelligence. Who else does this?

5. **Document automation**: Contract generation, visa application pre-filling, tax form auto-completion. What tools exist? (Docassemble, Juro, PandaDoc) — what can we build with AI to reduce manual document handling by 80%?

6. **Revenue diversification**: Beyond consulting fees — SaaS subscriptions, API access, white-label platform, training/certification, insurance partnerships. What's realistic for a Bali-based firm?

## Output Format

For each area: opportunity sizing, effort estimate, competitive landscape, and a recommended 12-month roadmap with quarterly milestones.
