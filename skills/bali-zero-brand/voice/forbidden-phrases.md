# Forbidden phrases — closed set

> Hard fail in `wr2-critic` rubric 3 (Copy). Soft-match case-insensitive substring.

## A. AI-tell phrases (LLM giveaway)

- `delve into`
- `let's dive in` / `dive into`
- `at the end of the day`
- `it's worth noting`
- `it's important to note`
- `in today's fast-paced world`
- `in today's [adjective] [noun]`
- `unlock`
- `seamless`
- `synergy`
- `game-changer`
- `cutting-edge`
- `innovative` (without specific referent)
- `leveraging`
- `streamline`
- `holistic`
- `paradigm`

## B. Marketing-stock phrases

- `make Bali your home`
- `live the dream`
- `paradise`
- `your Bali adventure`
- `your Bali journey`
- `tropical paradise`
- `island life`
- `your dream life`
- `book now`
- `limited offer`
- `act now`
- `don't miss out`
- `DM us`
- `link in bio` (acceptable in IG caption only, never on slide)
- `swipe to` / `swipe for` (carousel mechanic, not editorial copy)
- `tap the link`
- `follow for more`
- `like and subscribe`

## C. Empty metaphors

- `landscape` (metaphorical — "regulatory landscape" → use "regulatory perimeter" or "regulatory framework")
- `tapestry`
- `realm`
- `journey` (boilerplate sense)
- `ecosystem` (marketing sense — engineering sense OK e.g. "Indonesian fintech ecosystem" if there's a real network being described)
- `arena`
- `world of` ("the world of Bali real estate")

## D. Engagement-bait questions

- `are you thinking of moving to Bali?`
- `did you know that...?`
- `have you ever wondered...?`
- `what if I told you...?`
- `wondering how to...?`

## E. Disclaimer corporate-legalese

- `this is not legal advice`
- `consult a professional`
- `we are not lawyers`
- `please verify with your accountant`
- `subject to change`
- `terms and conditions apply`

> Bali Zero IS the konsultan pajak + PPJK + immigration consultancy. Disclaimers undermine authority.

## F. Title case violations

- Any title in Sentence case (must be UPPERCASE) → hard fail
- Any title with question mark → hard fail (engagement bait pattern D)

## G. Numbers vague

- `a lot of` / `lots of`
- `many expats`
- `several investors`
- `a significant portion`
- `considerable interest`
- `growing demand`

> Replace with concrete number from brief's `key_facts`. If no concrete number is available, restructure sentence to remove the quantifier.

## H. Emoji

ALL emoji are banned in titles and body. Hard fail on any emoji character in slide content. Exception: separator `➖` reserved for IG bio only (NOT slides).
