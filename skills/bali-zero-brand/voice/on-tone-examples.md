# On-tone examples — voice that worked

> Few-shot grounding for the wr2-design-architect copy step. 5 examples per register, drawn verbatim from WR2 reference carousels (set 2025-2026) + balizero.com homepage. Each example tagged with its register.

---

## Register: rituale (solemn, ceremonial)

### Ex 1 — closing slide, regulatory tightening dossier
```
EVERY QUARTER, THE PERIMETER TIGHTENS.
```
Why it works: single sentence, no verb tense ambiguity, "perimeter" is a Bali Zero signature term replacing the banned "landscape". Implicit warning + brand promise.

### Ex 2 — closing, Marina developer dossier
```
PERMITS ARE PERMISSIONS. THEY CAN BE RESCINDED.
```
Why: two short sentences = call-and-response rhythm. Word "rescinded" carries legal weight. No "we" / "you" — speaks as principle.

### Ex 3 — frame, anti-impunity carousel
```
THIS IS NOT ANTI-INVESTMENT. THIS IS ANTI-IMPUNITY.
```
Why: setup-pivot structure. The pivot word ("impunity") is unexpected and reframes the whole conversation away from "are foreigners welcome" toward "do laws apply equally".

## Register: analitico (fact-dense, citation-heavy)

### Ex 4 — body, KEP-71/PJ/2026 SPT extension
```
BIMO WIJAYANTO, DIRJEN PAJAK, SIGNED KEP-71/PJ/2026 ON 30 APRIL 2026.
SPT TAHUNAN PPH BADAN TP2025 EXTENDED FROM 30 APR TO 31 MAY 2026.
ONLY BADAN. WAJIB PAJAK ORANG PRIBADI EXCLUDED.
AUTOMATIC. NO APPLICATION REQUIRED.
NO DENDA, NO BUNGA ON PPH 29 IN THE WINDOW.
```
Why: 5 sentences, each carries one verifiable fact. Acronyms verbatim. Bilingual lexicon (Wajib Pajak Orang Pribadi). Numbers concrete. No filler.

### Ex 5 — body, Jl Tuka Tibubeneng KBLI dossier
```
SEVEN KBLI CODES SUBMITTED.
ONE DUPLICATE (62019 ×2) → SIX UNIQUE.
KBLI 79902 IS NOT TRAVEL AGENCY. IT IS TOURISM CONTENT / INFLUENCER.
RISK CLASSIFICATION: RENDAH UNDER PP 28/2025.
TDUP ABOLISHED — RBA REGIME APPLIES.
```
Why: structural surprise (the duplicate, the misclassification correction). Reads as forensic.

## Register: militante (sentence-bomb, punctuated)

### Ex 6 — closing, golden visa carousel
```
INVESTMENT IS NOT IMMIGRATION.
```
Why: 4 words. Total inversion of marketing pitch ("come invest, get residency"). Reads as warning to founders who confused two regulatory regimes.

### Ex 7 — frame, KITAS abuse dossier
```
THE SHAM INVESTOR KITAS IS NOT A LIFE HACK. IT IS A FELONY.
```
Why: contrast structure ("not a... it is a..."). "Felony" is plain English, no dancing around. Speaks to expat communities where "KITAS hacks" circulate as folklore.

## Register: ironico (dry calling-out)

### Ex 8 — body, B211A nomenclature obsolescence
```
B211A AND C312 ARE STILL ON YOUR AGENT'S BROCHURE.
THEY HAVEN'T EXISTED SINCE PERMENKUMHAM 22/2023.
ASK YOUR AGENT WHY.
```
Why: implicit reader-vs-agent tension. The agent wins the implicit comparison ("Bali Zero knows the new codes"). No name-calling.

### Ex 9 — body, "Are you thinking of moving to Bali?" anti-pattern
```
MOST PEOPLE MOVING TO BALI PICK THE WRONG VISA IN THE FIRST MONTH.
SIGN A LEASE THAT DOES NOT HOLD UP UNDER PP 18/2021.
FIND OUT ONLY AT TAX TIME.
WE SPEND OUR DAYS FIXING THAT.
```
Why: refuses the "are you thinking of moving to Bali?" engagement-bait. Goes straight to "you've already moved and you've already made the mistake". Self-positioning at the end is earned.

## Register: tecnico (bilingual lexicon-heavy)

### Ex 10 — body, hak pakai property dossier
```
HAK PAKAI IS NOT OWNERSHIP.
SHGB ONLY UNDER PT PMA.
NOMINEE STRUCTURES VIOLATE UU AGRARIA NO. 5/1960 ARTICLE 26.
THE NOMINEE IS YOUR LEASE LANDLORD ON PAPER. THEY CAN EVICT.
```
Why: every line is a load-bearing fact. Bilingual lexicon untranslated. The closing sentence is plain English emotional hit ("they can evict").

## Register: pedagogico (explainer, still uppercase)

### Ex 11 — body, Coretax 2025 explainer
```
CORETAX REPLACED DJP-ONLINE ON 1 JANUARY 2025.
LOGIN VIA ONELOGIN, NOT EFIN.
EFIN IS DEPRECATED FOR NEW REGISTRATIONS.
EXISTING WAJIB PAJAK NUMBERS MIGRATED AUTOMATICALLY.
IF YOUR ACCOUNTANT STILL SAYS EFIN, FIND A NEW ACCOUNTANT.
```
Why: numbered facts → punchline. The punchline ("find a new accountant") is the brand voice signature.

---

## Register: poetico (rare, pivot or closing)

### Ex 12 — pivot slide, "End of an Era" carousel about Airbnb crackdown
```
THE ROOMS ARE EMPTY.
THE LISTINGS ARE STILL UP.
THE AUDITS ARE COMING.
```
Why: triplet rhythm. Each line stands alone visually. Tense shift (past → present → future) carries the narrative arc.

---

## Anti-pattern: don't write like this

- ❌ "Hey Bali expats! Did you know about the new SPT extension? Let's dive into what it means for you!"
- ❌ "In today's ever-evolving regulatory landscape, navigating compliance can feel overwhelming."
- ❌ "Are you thinking of moving to Bali? Here are 5 visa tips you need to know!"
- ❌ "We're here to help make your Bali journey seamless."

(All four violate forbidden-phrases.md and miss the "concrete fact + sentence-bomb" structure.)
