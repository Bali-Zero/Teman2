# How to tag past carousels — simple guide

> Damar, this is for you. Read once. 5 minutes.

---

## What you do

You see one carousel at a time. You pick 5 things from dropdowns. You click "Save & Next".
That's it.

You'll see ~64 carousels. You can do them all at once (~30 minutes) or 5-10 at a time over
several days. Anything you tag is saved immediately.

---

## The 5 things you pick

### 1. Topic — what is it about?

Just type a few words. Lower-case. Use dashes between words.

| Carousel is about... | Type |
|---|---|
| KITAS rules for digital nomads | `digital-nomad-kitas` |
| Tax deadline May 31 | `spt-extension-may` |
| Marina Tuka shutdown | `marina-tuka` |
| New visa codes 2026 | `new-visa-codes-2026` |

If you really can't remember what it was about → **click SKIP** (no problem).

---

### 2. Domain — what topic family?

Pick ONE from the dropdown. The dropdown shows hints next to each option:

- **visa** — anything about KITAS, KITAP, B211B, work permits, who can stay how long
- **tax** — SPT, PPh, NPWP, Coretax, deadlines, tax forms
- **property** — SHGB, hak pakai, PBG, KKPR, villas, leases, who can buy what
- **hr** — BPJS, hiring, Permenaker, salary rules
- **regulatory** — government action, OSS, KBLI, mixed law topics
- **brand** — about Bali Zero itself (services, milestones)

If a carousel is about TWO things (like a tax+visa carousel), pick the BIGGER one.

---

### 3. Audience — who is the reader?

Pick ONE:

- **founder** — someone starting/running a business in Bali
- **investor** — someone buying property or assets
- **digital-nomad** — remote worker, KITAS for working from Bali
- **retiree** — older person, KITAP, retirement visa
- **mass-tourist** — short visit, visa-on-arrival
- **mixed** — when it's truly for everyone

Most carousels are **founder** or **investor**. Don't overthink it.

---

### 4. Voice register — how does it sound?

This is the hardest one. The agent has 7 ways of writing. Pick the closest:

- **rituale** — sounds solemn, like a ritual phrase. Example: *"EVERY QUARTER, THE PERIMETER TIGHTENS."*
- **analitico** — many facts and numbers. Like a news report.
- **ironico** — calls out something silly without being mean. Example: *"B211A AND C312 ARE STILL ON YOUR AGENT'S BROCHURE. THEY HAVEN'T EXISTED SINCE 2023."*
- **militante** — strong, short, punchy statements. Example: *"INVESTMENT IS NOT IMMIGRATION."*
- **pedagogico** — explainer mode, step-by-step.
- **poetico** — slow, reflective, emotional. Rare.
- **tecnico** — full of acronyms (KITAS, PT PMA, hak pakai). Reader must know jargon.

**If unsure** → pick **analitico** (this is the safe default). The agent learns even from
imperfect tags.

---

### 5. Main layout — what does most of the carousel look like?

Look at the cover slide on the left. Try to remember what slides 2-9 looked like. Pick the
ONE layout that appeared most:

- **cover-photo** — most slides are full-bleed photos with text on top
- **photo-headline-yellow-sub** — half photo on top, dark panel with text below
- **qa-dialogue** — two voices talking ("THE DEVELOPER SAID..." / "PARLIAMENT REPLIED...")
- **timeline-pinboard** — calendar-style dates and events
- **dark-status-list** — pure dark slides with item lists ("MARINA WORKS: STOPPED")
- **statement-bomb** — single big sentence centered (usually only one slide per carousel)

If you can't remember the inside of the carousel and only see the cover → just pick
**photo-headline-yellow-sub** (most common in Bali Zero). It's a guess and that's fine.

---

## What if you don't know?

**Click SKIP**. The agent will not learn from that one carousel. That's perfectly fine —
better than wrong tags.

You can also come back later. Skipped carousels are remembered as "skipped", they stay
skipped, you don't see them again.

---

## What happens with your tags?

The agent uses your tags 2 ways:
1. When designing a NEW carousel, it looks at past ones with the same domain/register and
   uses them as a style reference. Better tags = better future carousels.
2. The weekly Voyager check looks at coverage — "we did 5 visa carousels but 0 tax this
   month, let's plan a tax exploration variant".

If you skip 60% of carousels, the agent still works — just with less past data. Don't stress.

## How long it takes

- ~64 carousels
- ~20-30 seconds per carousel if you're confident
- Total: 20-30 minutes

You can do it in one session OR a few each day. The page remembers your progress.
