# How to pick the 5 anchor images — simple guide

> Damar, this is for you. Read once. 3 minutes.

---

## What is an anchor?

An anchor is **one cover photo that defines the look of all future carousels** in one topic
family. The agent will look at the anchor and copy its mood — same lighting, same colors,
same dark-and-cinematic feeling.

You pick 5 anchors, one per family:
- visa anchor
- tax anchor
- property anchor
- HR anchor
- regulatory anchor

The page is at `http://localhost:8765/anchors`.

---

## What you see

At the top: 5 empty slots, one per topic family.

Below: a grid of all the past carousel covers (~64 of them).

---

## How to pick

1. **Click an empty slot at the top** (e.g. "VISA"). It turns yellow — meaning "now I'm
   picking the visa anchor".
2. **Browse the grid below**. Use the filter dropdown to show only one domain at a time
   if it helps.
3. **Click any cover** that looks "the most Bali Zero" for that family.
4. Confirm in the popup. Done.
5. Repeat for the next 4 families.

Total time: ~10 minutes.

---

## What is a GOOD anchor?

The agent will copy the mood. So pick photos that have these:

✅ **Dark, moody background** — antracite or black, never bright
✅ **Yellow or red accent** — small touch of color, not overwhelming
✅ **Cinematic feeling** — looks like a movie still
✅ **No smiling people** — Bali Zero is investigative, not hospitality
✅ **No palm trees, beaches, sunsets** — we don't sell paradise, we sell facts
✅ **A specific subject** — a stamp on a document, a security gate, a dark desk with a lamp
✅ **35mm film grain feeling** — texture, not glossy

---

## What is a BAD anchor?

❌ Bright daylight, blue sky → too travel-magazine
❌ Smiling team photos → wrong tone
❌ Sunset over rice terraces → cliché
❌ AI-art with weird hands or melting faces → wrong, the agent will copy the weirdness
❌ Pure abstract / empty backgrounds → no subject for the agent to anchor to
❌ Logos or text-heavy slides → text comes from the agent, not from the anchor

## Examples (mental pictures)

### GOOD visa anchor
A passport open on a dark wooden desk, single overhead lamp casting a warm light, a hand in
the corner of the frame holding a stamp. Low-saturation, teal-amber feeling.

### GOOD tax anchor
A stack of paper documents, a calculator, a single fluorescent light from the side, motion
blur on a hand stamping. Like a Reuters news photo.

### GOOD property anchor
A construction site at dusk, scaffolding silhouetted against storm clouds, a distant security
light. Empty, ominous.

### BAD anchor
A smiling couple in front of a pool with a "Welcome to Bali!" sign. Too hospitality.

## What if NO past carousel looks right?

If after browsing you can't find a great anchor for a family:
1. Pick the **least bad one** for now.
2. Tell Antonello — he can replace it later with a better photo.

The agent works with what you give it. Imperfect anchor > no anchor. Anchors can be replaced
anytime; the previous one is auto-archived (not lost).

---

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. Just click the same slot again and pick a different cover. The previous anchor goes to
an archive folder.

---

## What does this NOT do?

- The anchor is **just a style reference**. Damar doesn't have to ever look at it again.
- It does NOT appear in any carousel — the agent generates fresh photos for each carousel,
  using the anchor only as a mood guide.
- It does NOT get published anywhere.
