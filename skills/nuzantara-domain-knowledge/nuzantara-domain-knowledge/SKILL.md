# Nuzantara Domain Knowledge

## Overview

This skill encapsulates critical business domain knowledge for the Nuzantara project. Its purpose is to provide the Gemini CLI agent with a deeper understanding of Bali Zero's operations, legal frameworks, and target audience, thereby enhancing autonomy and decision-making capabilities. The information contained herein is extracted directly from the project's codebase, documentation, and explicit user guidance.

## Domain Areas

The knowledge within this skill covers the following key areas of Nuzantara's legal support for foreigners (with future expansion to Indonesians):

- **Immigration & Visas:** Details on various visa types, application processes, and relevant Indonesian immigration laws.
- **Company Formation & Licensing:** Guidance on setting up companies in Indonesia, obtaining necessary licenses, and specific insights related to KBLI (Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia) studies.
- **Tax Reporting:** Information on personal and company tax obligations and reporting procedures in Indonesia.
- **Legal Properties:** Context around legal aspects of property ownership, leasing, and related regulations.
- **User Personas:** Descriptions of typical Nuzantara clients, their needs, challenges, and goals.
- **Business Overview:** General context about Bali Zero's mission, values, and strategic direction.

## How to Use This Skill

The domain knowledge is organized into dedicated Markdown files within the `references/` directory. When working on a task that requires understanding a specific domain area, refer to the relevant Markdown file(s) to gather necessary context. For example, to understand visa processes, access `references/immigration-and-visas.md`.

## Resources

This skill will primarily utilize `references/` to store structured domain knowledge.

### references/

Contains Markdown files (`.md`) detailing various aspects of Nuzantara's business domain, including legal frameworks, processes, user information, and business overviews. These files serve as a comprehensive knowledge base for the agent.
