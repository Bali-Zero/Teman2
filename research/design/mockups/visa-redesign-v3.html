02 /The assessment
III / VI 3 answers captured

What will you actually be doing while you’re here?

This is the single answer that most changes which visa is correct for you.

A
Working remotely for a company outside Indonesia
No Indonesian income, no local employer. This is what opens the E33G remote-worker route.
B
Running or building a company based in Indonesia
Local entity, local revenue. Moves you toward an investor or work-KITAS path, not a stay permit.
C
Staying long-term without working
Retirement, family, or self-funded living. Different route, different proof of funds.
D
Just visiting — tourism or a short look-around
Holiday, scouting, a conference. Likely a visa-on-arrival or short-stay, not a KITAS.
