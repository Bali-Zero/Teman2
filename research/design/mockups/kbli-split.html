Reading KBLI 56101 · Restaurants with you
Can a foreigner own a restaurant under 56101?
Yes — 56101 allows 100% foreign ownership as a PT PMA, with a minimum investment plan of IDR 10 billion at setup.
Source
BKPM Perka No. 4/2021 — Lampiran III (Positive Investment List). Current Jun 2026. Confidence: high.
Is the IDR 10B cash I must deposit?
No — it’s a planned-investment commitment declared at incorporation, not cash on day one. The company desk can show how it’s evidenced for your case.
Source
BKPM Perka No. 4/2021 — Pasal 6, minimum investment value for PMA. Current Jun 2026.
