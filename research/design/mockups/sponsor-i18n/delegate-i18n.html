Translation strategy — why the split, shown not asserted

Rejected

Literal: "Anda sedang menjawab untuk orang lain. Kasus mereka, fakta mereka — jawablah seperti wisatawan akan menjawab. Siapa yang melihat apa: {CUSTODY_WHO_SEES}."

Word-for-word render of the English chiasmus ("their case, their facts"). Reads as translated-not-written; "mereka" (they/them) under-specifies a single named relation the flow usually already knows; "seperti wisatawan akan menjawab" is a hypothetical construction where Indonesian prefers a direct instruction.

Recommended

Reformulated: "Anda sedang menjawab atas nama wisatawan. Jawablah sesuai fakta miliknya, bukan pendapat Anda sendiri — siapa yang dapat melihat jawaban ini: {CUSTODY_WHO_SEES}."

Keeps every regulatory-honesty commitment of the English (answer as the fact-holder, not a guesser; disclose who sees the answer) in the dictionary's own existing direct-instruction register — compare framing.body: "Jawab dengan jujur, termasuk 'Saya tidak tahu.'" (i18n.ts:831).

Contrast

The custody hint (Screen 4) took the OPPOSITE call — faithful/literal — because it is procedural/legal-adjacent copy where R4 §5's "no invented reassurance, no dropped nuance" constraint outweighs rhetorical smoothness. The split is by copy TYPE, not a single blanket policy (§3.3).

Sequencing — before vs. after family_relation resolves. who_answers and delegate_confirm both fire BEFORE family_relation (journey.html:199-200 vs. :211) — at that point the specific relation (spouse/parent/child) is not yet known, so both languages default to the neutral noun: EN "the traveller", ID "wisatawan". Once family_relation resolves (e.g. "spouse"), a downstream screen COULD narrow to the relation noun — EN "your spouse", ID "pasangan Anda" — but no such string exists in the current prototype (R5b's own downstream six-question sponsor sub-flow was not re-read for pronoun content this round). This is a recommendation to item 4's build lane (§5.3 of the dossier), not a measured requirement.

Branch note example — family_relation's own label (journey.html:211, template "@Your@ sponsor — 6 questions"): EN self "Your sponsor — 6 questions" / EN delegate "The traveller's sponsor — 6 questions" · ID self "Sponsor Anda — 6 pertanyaan" / ID delegate "Sponsor milik wisatawan — 6 pertanyaan". Sub-progress badge: EN "Sponsor {i} of {n}" / ID "Sponsor ke-{i} dari {n}" (Indonesian ordinal marker, not a literal "dari" repetition — §5.5).
