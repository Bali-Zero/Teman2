Translation strategy — why the split, shown not asserted

Rejected · draft 1

Literal: "Anda sedang menjawab untuk orang lain. Kasus mereka, fakta mereka — jawablah seperti wisatawan akan menjawab. Siapa yang melihat apa: {CUSTODY_WHO_SEES}."

Word-for-word render of the English chiasmus ("their case, their facts"). Reads as translated-not-written; "mereka" (they/them) is grammatically PLURAL and disagrees in number with the single person it refers to — corrected post-panel from an earlier "colder register" rationale (R16.iv, kimi F14ii: the real defect is number-disagreement, not tone); "seperti wisatawan akan menjawab" is a hypothetical construction where Indonesian prefers a direct instruction, and "wisatawan" is itself the wrong default noun (R16.ii).

Rejected · draft 2

Reformulated (this file's original recommendation, superseded): "Anda sedang menjawab atas nama wisatawan. Jawablah sesuai fakta miliknya, bukan pendapat Anda sendiri — siapa yang dapat melihat jawaban ini: {CUSTODY_WHO_SEES}."

Rejected on three independent grounds the panel raised separately: (i) "atas nama" denotes formal legal proxy / power of attorney in Indonesian administrative usage (agy F9) — the delegate has no such standing, so this phrase makes an unintended legal claim; (ii) "bukan pendapat Anda sendiri" is an ID-only added obligation with no EN source clause, breaking EN/ID parity, and reads as accusatory against the warm-formal register this dictionary otherwise holds (codex F10, kimi F14.iii); (iii) "fakta miliknya" is an unidiomatic possessive calque — formal Indonesian does not take "-nya" on "fakta" this way (codex F10, kimi F14).

Recommended

Corrected, post-panel synthesis: "Anda sedang membantu pengisian formulir untuk pemohon. Jawablah berdasarkan fakta pemohon — siapa yang dapat melihat jawaban ini: {CUSTODY_WHO_SEES}."

Replaces "atas nama" with agy's non-representative procedural phrasing (F9); drops the added-obligation clause entirely rather than softening it (EN/ID parity, not just tone); names the referent as an explicit noun ("fakta pemohon") instead of a possessive-suffix calque; uses "pemohon" throughout (R16.ii). Keeps every regulatory-honesty commitment of the English (answer as the fact-holder, not a guesser; disclose who sees the answer) in the dictionary's own existing direct-instruction register — compare framing.body: "Jawab dengan jujur, termasuk 'Saya tidak tahu.'" (i18n.ts:831). Still draft copy — R16.ix requires a native-speaker pass before this ships.

Contrast

The custody hint (Screen 4) took the OPPOSITE call — faithful/literal — because it is procedural/legal-adjacent copy where R4 §5's "no invented reassurance, no dropped nuance" constraint outweighs rhetorical smoothness. The split is by copy TYPE, not a single blanket policy (§3.3).

Sequencing — before vs. after family_relation resolves. who_answers and delegate_confirm both fire BEFORE family_relation (journey.html:199-200 vs. :211) — at that point the specific relation (spouse/parent/child) is not yet known, so both languages default to the neutral noun: EN "the traveller", ID "pemohon" (corrected post-panel, R16.ii — was "wisatawan"; "pemohon" is the funnel-wide default, "wisatawan" reserved for explicitly tourism-VOA- scoped copy only). Once family_relation resolves (e.g. "spouse"), a downstream screen COULD narrow to the relation noun — EN "your spouse", ID "pasangan Anda" — but no such string exists in the current prototype (R5b's own downstream six-question sponsor sub-flow was not re-read for pronoun content this round). This is a recommendation to item 4's build lane (§5.3 of the dossier), not a measured requirement.

Branch note example — family_relation's own label (journey.html:211, template "@Your@ sponsor — 6 questions"; "sponsor" here is CORRECT and untouched by R9 — this branch note names the family-route sponsor sub-flow itself, the one place the word is reserved for): EN self "Your sponsor — 6 questions" / EN delegate "The traveller's sponsor — 6 questions" · ID self "Sponsor Anda — 6 pertanyaan" / ID delegate "Sponsor milik pemohon — 6 pertanyaan" (pemohon swap, R16.ii).
