03 /Classification detail
56101
Restaurants

What it covers

Full-service restaurants where customers are seated and served. This is the classification you register a PT PMA under to open and operate a restaurant in Indonesia, including in Bali.

Foreign ownership

A restaurant under 56101 can be 100% foreign-owned as a PT PMA, with a minimum investment plan of IDR 10 billion required at setup.

What setting it up involves

PT PMA incorporation, NIB through OSS, the relevant operational licences, and tax registration. The capital figure is a planned-investment commitment, not cash you must deposit on day one.
