# GARUDA checkout — the design the funnel's highest-risk step never had

**What this file is.** NOT a loop round — the Design Study Loop closed at R7. This is the first
build-lane design deliverable of the R7 §4 backlog (item 1: "the funnel's highest-risk step has
NO mockup"), executed under the loop's doctrine: R4 is the law, claims carry their evidence
class (R7 §3.1), and the new declarations in §6 go through the round-grade process (report,
panel, dispositions, async veto) because they decide surface behavior, not just styling.
Study only: no product code, `GARUDA_PUBLIC_ENABLED` untouched.

## §1 Ground — what actually exists (class a, read from origin/main eeeadaead this round)

A live checkout page EXISTS, ship-dark: `apps/mouth/src/app/visa/voa/checkout/[resultId]/`
behind `GARUDA_PUBLIC_ENABLED` (fail-closed, noindex). It collects email + phone, shows
full name + passport read-only from the session handoff, and guards with "We need your
reviewed passport details first." — exactly the only state R1's capture could reach (R1:169).
On submit it creates the order and redirects to the PSP. Payments: Xendit **Invoices API**
(hosted page; sandbox-key enforced in this build) — the payment rail (QRIS / VA / card) is
chosen on Xendit's page, not ours; provider fee is absorbed into the one `price_idr`. The
return route is deliberately single (no success/failure split): "the browser return is an
OBSERVATION, never a truth" (xendit.py). Order shape (`models.py`): one `price_idr` figure,
`order_id` opaque; **no BZ-XXXX case-code generator exists anywhere** — the codes in R5/R5b
mockups are design placeholders (class a: repo-wide search found no generator).

Correction recorded while grounding (class a vs class e): `outbox_handlers.py`'s docstring
still claims nothing invokes the outbox drain in production — that text is STALE. Commit
63bfa19ec (#5035) shipped the drain dark, armed by one variable, and `main_api.py:127` runs
the scheduler. The R7 ladder's decay rule caught its second stale-doc claim in one day.

## §2 The two live-vs-law tensions this design resolves

**T1 — the live checkout shows NO price.** `CheckoutFlow.tsx:14-18` declares it by design
("the price only appears once, on the order tracker"). But R4's pricing law (Q6c) requires
the exact bundle price at quoted verdicts AND at payment, verbatim-identical; and R1-A7's own
evidence (Baymard 2025, cited at R1:132) counts 12% checkout abandonment because "the total
was not visible up front" — on the step R1 rated **5-quits** with "scam fear" as the dominant
emotion. A pay button with no number next to it, on a brand fighting scam-adjacency (R6 §4),
is the exact fear surface. The design puts the price ON the checkout page as Q6c's second
rendering (verdict → checkout → CTA), and §6.1 declares it.

**T2 — a rail selector we cannot honor.** M5's mockup renders QRIS/VA/card as a selectable
radiogroup, but the shipped provider is Xendit's hosted Invoice: the user picks the rail on
the NEXT page, not ours. A selector whose choice the product silently discards would be a
dishonest control (R3's autopsy class). The design demotes the rails to informational badges
inside "What happens after you pay" — the promise ("you choose how — there") is one the
backend already keeps. §6.2 declares it; M5's radiogroup stays correct for a future direct
rails integration and is not amended.

## §3 Design decisions (mockup: `mockups/checkout-garuda/checkout.html`, mobile-first 390px, light)

1. **Review-before-pay before anything else** (A7 cure; R4 question protocol): name and
   passport read-only with Edit→ links back to review; email/phone fields preceded by
   why-we-ask-and-who-sees-it copy — the backlog item 2 consent pattern applied at its first
   surface (class c: expert application of R1/R4).
2. **The price card** — Q6c rendering 2 of 3, verbatim-identical with the verdict and the CTA;
   composition line (R6 §8 item 2 verbatim scope: categories, never figures); receipt-itemizes
   promise (post-payment, outside the guardrail's surfaces); **the adopted PNBP line**
   (R7 §5.1 ruling, standing 27/8): "Government charges: IDR 5XX.XXX (PNBP, set by the state)
   — included in the total above, not added to it." One state-set figure, no fee split — the
   wording makes the guardrail's point FOR the reader: the number is inside, not on top.
   All figures masked; PricingTool remains the sole source (placeholders `{BUNDLE_EXACT}`).
3. **"What happens after you pay"** above the CTA (A7's carried demand: "total, inclusions,
   refund, time above the pay button" — R1:109): three steps with the rails as badges, the
   order identity (`{ORDER_ID}` — honest: no case-code exists, §5.1), the processing window,
   and the human-escalation promise.
4. **Team-control human module** — the FIRST surface designed under R6 §8 item 1's flipped
   production default: "our team answers within {SLA}", no named person, no photo. The named
   variant may only enter via the A/B under the verifiability contract; this page does not
   pre-empt it (class: doctrine application, not preference).
5. **Trust block, full §2.4 contract**: role-separation line, PT/NPWP/registry link, physical
   office address — and the talk-first WhatsApp card BELOW the trust block, offering contact
   BEFORE payment ("before you pay, not after"): kimi's R6 scam-adjacency dissent answered on
   the page where the fear peaks.
6. **Sticky CTA** — Q6c rendering 3 of 3 — with the payment-window honesty line ("this total
   holds for {PAYMENT_WINDOW}"): the R2/operator capture insight (Flado exposes a 30-day
   window vs the official 120 minutes) turned into a stated promise instead of an implicit one.
7. **Rem-native type scale** — the first surface applying R7 §2 amendment 3: every size in rem
   over a 16px root, identical default rendering, text-only zoom no longer inert (class b at
   build time: R6's runtime probe becomes this page's acceptance test).
8. **Guard state kept verbatim** ("We need your reviewed passport details first.") — the live
   copy already passes the walkthrough; only restyled under R4 tokens.

## §4 Behavioral acceptance (what a build of this page must prove)

- Price renders identically, to the rupiah, in the three Q6c places — a diff between any two
  is a failed build (would_fail_if: string inequality across verdict/checkout/CTA fixtures).
- The PNBP line renders ONLY when the state figure is resolved for the case type; no figure,
  no line (C1 discipline extended: no state number renders without a resolved source).
- Text-only zoom at 200% enlarges every line (would_fail_if: any computed font-size equal to
  its default-zoom value under text-only zoom — R6 probe 5's exact instrument, now green by
  construction).
- The rails block never renders as a selectable control while the provider is hosted-invoice.
- The guard state is reachable and the priced state is NOT, when the handoff is absent.

## §5 Findings handed to product lanes (not design's to fix)

1. **No case-code exists.** R7 backlog item 3 promises "the case code surviving onto the
   receipt and the first WhatsApp message" — there is nothing to survive: no generator, no
   column; the tracker/email use raw `order_id`. Either mint a human-grade code at order
   creation or amend item 3 to speak `order_id`. (class a)
2. **Price-at-checkout divergence** (T1): `CheckoutFlow.tsx` needs the price card once §6.1
   stands — it currently cannot render one by design. (class a)
3. **Stale outbox docstring** (§1 correction): `outbox_handlers.py` should stop claiming the
   drain is unwired — #5035 wired it dark. A doc that contradicts main misleads the next
   ground pass, and today it briefly did. (class a)
4. **Email/phone are asked at checkout only** — the why-we-ask copy in §3.1 papers over a
   structural question (should contact land earlier, with consent placement per backlog
   item 2?) that belongs to the consent lane, not this page. (class c)

## §6 Declarations (adopted under the standing pre-confirmation, async veto open)

1. **The checkout page shows the exact bundle price** as Q6c's second rendering — the
   tracker-only choice in the live code is superseded for the design. Alternative: keep the
   priced surface at verdict+PSP only (rejected: Baymard's 12% + A7's 5-quits both point at
   the unpriced pay step, and Q6c's "at payment" reads naturally as the page holding the pay
   button).
2. **Rails render as informational badges, not a selector, while the provider is a hosted
   invoice.** Alternative: keep M5's radiogroup on checkout (rejected: a control whose choice
   the product discards is a dishonest control; M5's component stays for a future direct
   integration).
3. **This page ships the team-control human module** per the flipped production default —
   no named person until the R6/R7-gated A/B decides. Alternative: named module (barred by
   doctrine until the human gate).
