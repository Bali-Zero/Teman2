# R4 — Identity: the Merah Putih token spec

**What this round is.** R0 measured six coexisting design systems; R1 found that «GARUDA has identity without honesty devices; the Oracle has honesty devices without identity»; R3 measured which red can carry text and inventoried 27 defects. R4 turns the twelve rulings into ONE identity — a named token set plus binding application rules — that R5's mockups implement. No product code (loop mandate); this file is the contract R5 builds against.

**The name.** «Merah Putih» — Zero's own words in ruling Q2 («…al posto del navy un bel rosso (sia in Bali Zero e sia il Merah Putih)»): red-and-white, the flag, and the literal composition of the identity — warm paper ground, Merah Putih red structure. It also closes one measured hex mismatch: the IG brand system (S5, 64 carousels) has carried `#C8102E` since May — the web perimeter now shares that red. Sharing the red is not sharing the system: S5 keeps its anthracite/gold/Montserrat-uppercase language and its own charter; one hex joins, two systems remain.

## UNKNOWNS — declared up front

1. No conversion or A/B data exists for any choice here (unchanged from R0-R3): every decision is design judgment constrained by measured contrast, Zero's rulings, and the R1 evidence shelf. §Meta separates what the numbers forced from what the rulings chose.
2. The Q5 axis («the honest human who knows the rules») rests on R1's weakest shelf — Evil 2003, Ert 2016 and Stanford/Fogg 2002, all single-run and held weak (R1 §9.2): the honest-human module is specified but provisional, and R5 must deliver BOTH variants (named people / no named people) so R6 can test them before R7 writes doctrine.
3. Dark mode is specified to FLOOR level only (ground, text, soft, panel, hairline, accent — all computed): a full dark token system is R5 work under the same contrast law. Nothing here was rendered on an OLED device; the chromostereopsis mitigation is structural (no red-ground long text), not measured.
4. The perimeter consequence for KBLI/tax/property funnels (§7) is declared debt, not design — those surfaces are NON-CONFORMING to this spec until their own lanes migrate them; no deadline exists (that is capacity, a Legge-5 matter).
5. Several component facts this spec depends on were never audited: the passport-upload retention/recipient list (R1), the public verifiability of the company identifier (R1), the logged-in my portal (R3 D-M2, hypothesis-capped). Every dependent rule below carries the flag instead of inventing the fact.

## §4 The contrast law and the restraint budget

1. **Text on carta/white**: ink, ink-soft, the logo-red family (5.11-6.24 across its cells), or a semantic state color inside its own state component (5.08-6.39). Never the bright reds (3.20-3.39 on the papers, 3.62-3.66 on white).
2. **White text**: only on merah/merah-action/merah-pressed (5.52-6.24) or state-error (6.26). Never on the WhatsApp green (1.98) — and the WhatsApp pattern is the card-with-icon component in §3, not a green button. (agy proposed WhatsApp dark-green `#128c7e` with white text citing 4.65; measured 4.14 — it fails normal-text AA and is rejected for text at normal sizes.)
3. **Dark mode floor**: text-dark (15.41) or soft-dark (8.58) on ground-dark; merah-dark-mode for accents (5.02; text duty only under D4); `#C8102E` structure-only (3.13). Full dark system: R5, same law.
4. **Restraint budget — testable form**: on funnel/content screens the red allowance is exactly: slim header ON CARTA (wordmark in merah, no red band), the progress fill, one primary CTA. The full-bleed merah band exists only on the home hero and footer — the single declared exception — and carries only wordmark, nav labels and one CTA, never long-form text. No other screen may paint a red field taller than its CTA. (This makes the budget checkable per screen and kills the band-everywhere reading the panel caught; it also serves the OLED rule: no large saturated red fields adjacent to dense ink text.) **The institutional-perception clause** (R3 red-risk 2; agy re-raised it against ruling Q2 — the ruling stands, the mitigation is the answer): in the bureaucratic SEA context a red-dominant page sits next to warning/sanction and scam-landing associations, so formal marks — the wordmark and the linked company identifier — are ALWAYS co-present wherever red structure appears, and the generous carta ground is what keeps the red reading institutional rather than promotional.
5. **No color-alone signals** (WCAG 1.4.1) and no hue-alone duty separation within the red family: position, shape, motion or icon always co-carry the meaning.
6. **Component identification** (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11): interactive boundaries use border-input (≥3:1); hairline is decoration.
