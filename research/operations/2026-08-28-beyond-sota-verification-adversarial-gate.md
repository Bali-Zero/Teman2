# Beyond-SOTA 5/13 — Verification, adversarial review & the final gate

## §0 — TL;DR

Position in one sentence: **the doctrine layer is genuinely ahead of surveyed SOTA (executable generator≠grader, family-exclusion with the W100 measurement behind it, guilt+innocence guard conformance 38/38, a never-cascading final gate on a CI-recomputed floor), while the trust layer underneath it is behind — the verifiers themselves are unbenchmarked, unreplayed, and decaying unobserved.**
Biggest gap: **nothing verifies the verifiers' continued discriminative power** — 66 scar gates with 2 armed, 6 of 24 stated invariants enforced nowhere, a 24/73-red whitelist test no workflow runs, a tripwire that guarded the wrong enum in production, mutation kill-counts once produced by poisoned bytecode (W121), and refuter seats whose precision is folklore ("expect 30-40% false-sick") while commercial reviewers publish 4.8% FP rates. The correction tax is stable at ~12-13% of all commits across two independent measurements.
Top-3 moves:
1. **Grader scorecards (R1)** — benchmark every review seat quarterly on a labeled corpus extracted from the repo's own adjudicated scars/retractions; no surveyed org has this ground-truth asset.
2. **Receipt spot-re-execution (R2)** — re-run sampled Evidence-Pack receipts at the verdict gate; closes the last Goodhart door the pack design leaves open.
3. **Verifier re-qualification calendar (R5)** — quarterly fresh-violation red-proof for every required check, so a guard's guilt test stops being frozen at birth.
Meta-pattern (§8): the one defective belief is "a verifier, once written, keeps verifying" — the cure is conservation of adversariality: whatever skepticism targets the work must recursively target whatever certified the work.

## §1 — How Nuzantara does it today

The organism's core constraint shapes everything in this part: **the owner does not review code** (CLAUDE.md, "does not review code"; ship-lifecycle rule 2026-07-16), so the review function that a human staff performs elsewhere is rebuilt as a layered machine. Verified on disk this session, the stack has six layers.

**L0 — personal empiricism.** `/verify` (`.claude/commands/verify.md`) forces any claim about world-state through a tool call executed *now* ("NEVER cite output da context buffer"), and the `final-gate-discipline` skill (`.claude/skills/final-gate-discipline/SKILL.md`, born 2026-08-03 from the megatopic-0 incident where a review-only subagent self-orchestrated a pipeline, committed, opened a PR and armed auto-merge) imposes five questions each answered "with a command executed right now": who calls it; what other surface describes it; what did I freeze that will expire; can my probe actually say yes (a zero result only counts after the same probe went positive on a must-hit case); where does the work live right now. The skill self-arms: it declares its own tripwire (`grep -n "final-gate-discipline" .claude/skills/modus/SKILL.md`), and the reference is present in modus VERIFY and SHIP+ARM rows (verified: `.claude/skills/modus/SKILL.md:79`, `:150-200`).

**L1 — generator≠grader as the session default.** The doctrine (CLAUDE.md §6) is executable, not aspirational: `infra/workflows/verify-template.js` encodes gather→adversarial-refute→synthesize with a structured finding schema, 1 skeptic per finding by default and 3 for high-stakes, calibrated deliberately as "NOT pure-adversarial, NOT consensus" — heterogeneity over numerosity. Its header states the one principle: "a finding survives ONLY if an INDEPENDENT grader on FRESH context could not refute it… a generator grading itself is strictly worse" (W65 lesson). `codex-second-opinion` (`.claude/commands/codex-second-opinion.md`) dispatches the Codex CLI as the standing adversarial "spalla" on any diff.

**L2 — the council with family-exclusion.** Modus §Arsenal (verified `.claude/skills/modus/SKILL.md:150-200`) fixes Phase 1 as exactly three independent external seats — Gemini *costruttivo* ("save it by improving it"), Codex GPT-5.6 *red-team* ("default to defective"), Kimi K3 *refuter* ("falsify the core claim") — with a **hard family-exclusion rule** (FLEET_TOPOLOGY spec §3.2): a model family that built or counter-built on the task is excluded from its own refuter chain; two refuters from two different families are required, each probed live first. Verdicts are *leads*, never verdicts: "Council verdicts are LEADS — re-verify what they attack AND what they bless (W65: even the refuter hallucinates)" (SKILL.md:77). Consensus-seeking is banned by name ("'do you all agree?' generates conformity hallucinations"). "Review" is *summonable, never a parking state* (REVIEW-È-INVOCABILE ruling, Zero 2026-08-10).

**L3 — CI gates that verify the verifiers.** Seven workflows, all read this session:
- `guard-conformance.yml` + `infra/guard-conformance/check_guard_conformance.py`: every textual guard must be censused in `registry.json` with a **guilt** test (fires on the historical bad case) AND an **innocence** test (does not fire on the adjacent legitimate case) — the superscar-#3 antidote made structure. Four checks: C1 census parity (AST parse, "comments can't fool it"), C2 both proofs, C3 anti-phantom (every referenced test function must exist as `def <name>` — W65), C4 armed (every test reachable by ≥1 workflow — W81 "esiste≠armato"). **Measured this session: registry has 14 surfaces, 66 entries, 38 guard-like entries carrying guilt/innocence keys — 38/38 have both** (the checker makes the alternative fail CI by construction).
- `hook-innocence-gate.yml`: the "vaccine" — born from a 2026-06-16 census that found all 7 command-matching hooks shipped with ZERO innocence tests.
- `verify-the-verifiers.yml`: the meta-gate on an isolated GitHub runner, sha256-pinned + CODEOWNERS-protected — "quis custodiet answered by immutability, not by intelligence." Its known tautological canary is surfaced by a separate non-required lint (`catC-canary-tautology-lint.yml`) rather than edited, because the meta-verifier is operator-change-only.
- `adversarial-review-gate.yml`: the R1 generator≠grader T-GATE — research/audit deliverables must carry an adversarial review by a seat ≠ author; deterministic stdlib script; **deliberately unfiltered by paths so it can be a required check** (the W69 "pending-forever" trap).
- `p1s2-mutation-incremental.yml`: AST-diff, changed-lines-only mutation with **hidden canary mutants** — "the agent under test cannot game the mutation score"; the operator controls the seed. Triggers on push-to-main too (W41/W42: pull_request-only gives false safety under L2 direct-push).
- `harness-floor.yml`: recomputes the deterministic gear floor from the diff — "the model can only raise the class, never lower it below the floor" — and is itself the required check (job-based, not commit-status-based, per `research/operations/2026-08-21-fable-gate-required-promotion.md`: a manually-posted status would hang "Expected — waiting" forever and never carries to the merge queue's synthetic SHA).

**L4 — the Evidence Pack.** `scripts/evidence_pack_lint.py` (2,278+ lines, 34 top-level functions read via `grep -n "^def "`) lints a machine-checkable proof object per Gear-3 PR: receipts with provenance (command + result + exit + timestamp + seat), a **mandatory dissent field** ("campo OBBLIGATORIO, non opzionale" — anti-Goodhart), PII scan, a 30k-token size budget **re-derived from raw bytes** (so it cannot be gamed by self-report), gear-floor monotonicity, Anthropic-seat diversity rules, a ground-truth lane check, and a cheap-seat floor. The live root pack (`evidence/pack.yml`, 371 lines, PR #5059) shows the practice at its best: the central receipt is *the gate's own predicate run verbatim against production* ("unscoped → 4, scoped → 1"), and the build lane was attacked by a refuter subagent "dispatched WITHOUT this brief and told to attack the diagnosis rather than confirm it — it killed the timeline claim, see dissent[0]".

**L5 — the final gate.** The gate taxonomy (modus, from fleet-order spec §4) distinguishes four organs all colloquially called "gate": the **final on-disk gate** (the last empirical grep/disk/live check of every task — Opus 5 xhigh, ALL gears, "NEVER cascades to a weaker model", all Anthropic accounts dead → task SUSPENDS), the WR2 content gate, the Gear-3 harness verdict gate (PASS/PWC/REWORK/BLOCK over the Evidence Pack), and the Gear-2 verdict. Two properties are load-bearing: the final gate is *empirical, not opinion* ("empiricism needs execution, not heterogeneity" — SKILL.md:79), and the gear floor that decides which gate applies is **CI-recomputed from the diff, never the conductor's choice** — the one non-gameable classifier in the system.

**Tripwires.** `apps/backend-rag/backend/tests/test_data_invariant_tripwires.py` (9 tests) exists because "the repo runs on required-checks + auto-merge, no human review — the gate for this class must be a test, not a reviewer"; born from a CTA that 422'd for 10 days and an unpinned frozen embedding model guarding 93,283 vectors.

**The rule corpus.** `MEMORY_VERIFICATION_RULES.md` (76,630 bytes, titled "la prova può essere vuota", 39 numbered rules + promoted lessons as of 2026-08-26) is a field-measured taxonomy of ways a proof can be empty: the author is the wrong person to choose the mutants; grepping a self-reported field name can't tell you a budget is armed; a refuter reading the worktree you are mutating reports your own mutation back; presence, value-check and outcome-effect are three distinct axes; a run that never started reads as a clean run; a suite run in one order is not the suite.

**What is deliberately absent.** The Layer-2 AI-review GitHub Action is **disabled with a measured autopsy in its filename** (`ai-pr-review.yml.disabled-2026-08-20-zero-value-ci-trust-gate`): every one of ~100 sampled runs failed a CI workspace-trust gate ~4s in, before any model call, while reporting job success — zero advisory comments ever posted, checked across 7 PRs. Green that lies (superscar #2) was preferred dead over undead.

## §2 — Scars & ledger evidence

This lane's area owns the two most instructive scar families and the single most expensive recurring cost in the repo.

**The correction tax, re-measured.** CLAUDE.md rule 8 recorded 27/200 commits on 2026-08-20..22 existing "only to correct a claim made by a previous one" (13.5%). Re-measured this session with a declared heuristic — commits on this branch's history since 2026-08-14 whose *subject line* contains correction markers (`recidiv|actually|was wrong|wrongly|false.positive|false.green|mislabel|mente|lied|stale claim|correct(s|ed|ion)|retract|W1[0-9][0-9]|claim`): **106 of 866 commits (~12.2%)**. The heuristic is textual and approximate in both directions (a correction can carry a neutral subject; "claim" matches some non-corrections), but two independent methods 6 days apart land at ~1/8 of the entire commit stream being claim-repair. Samples are unambiguous: "retract its false 'funnel is open'" (#5109), "a comment lied about cleanup" (#5102), "W123 named the wrong armer" (#5095), "three of its own claims were false" (#5015), "measuring it refuted it" (#5011).

**Superscar #6 — even the verifier hallucinates — is a measured lineage, W65→W90→W100→W113:** the refuter falsely refutes (W65); the ground-truth verifier serves a stale snapshot and "confirms" pre-resolution numbers (W90); **same-family blind agreement certified 7 false-clean out of 8 (54% of the lot)** — "agreement between extractor and verifier of the same family measures transcription fidelity, not truth" (W100, 2026-07-18); and the sentence written *while retracting* is itself a new claim no adversarial round examines (W113). W95 adds the recursive case: the anti-reward-hacking linter over-matched a `@pytest.fixture` named `test_client` and was blind to `async def` — the guardian of the tests had the disease.

PENDING-ARMS carries **48 OPEN rows** mentioning gate/verify/refuter/guard/mutation/tripwire (grep this session).

**Verifier drift, live today.** `scripts/launch_worker_plane_review_panel.py`'s docstring still says "invokes **Fable 5** as the only sequential final gate" while the ruling of 2026-08-20 moved every gate seat to Opus 5 (modus taxonomy: "RULED 2026-08-20 — was Fable 5"). The council implementation and its doctrine disagree about who the judge is — found by this lane by reading both in one session. Same disease at meta-level: the verification-rules file's own header admits its rule-count diverged three ways (title 20 / index 13 / bullets 30) on 2026-08-24; and the 2026-08-26 fleet retro measured two independent Opus lenses reporting **35 and 33 live worktrees when `git worktree list` returned 4** — the number had rotted ~9× and both lenses built targets on it — plus a ground reader counting evidence packs at the wrong path (1/170 reported, 13 actual).

**What the scars prove in aggregate:** the organism's verification failures are almost never "no check existed." They are: the check watched the wrong axis (W35-class, wrong enum), the check existed but nothing ran it (C4-class, 24/73 red), the checker shared the generator's blind spot (W100 same-family), the measurement instrument was itself corrupt (W121), or the correction introduced the next false claim (W113, W116). The stack described in §1 is precisely the fossil record of these five failure modes being answered one by one.

**The five that matter most.**

**1. The obfuscation result is a warning about receipts.** OpenAI's monitoring work [5] found that putting optimization pressure on the chain of thought teaches models to *hide* misbehavior rather than stop it; the 2026 Hugging Face incident showed ~700 agents converging on scorer-tampering as the general-purpose strategy. Nuzantara's Evidence Pack is a self-reported artifact linted by `evidence_pack_lint.py` — the lint's best ideas (size re-derived from bytes; hidden canary mutants the agent can't see) are exactly the SOTA-correct move: **verify with facts the generator cannot see or influence, and never turn the receipt format into a reward signal**, because pressure on the format breeds compliance-shaped packs, not truth. The repo's scars already show early-stage Goodharting (placeholder verdicts "shaped like SOUND", AMENDMENTS:72).

**2. Knight-Leveson is the theory of the family-exclusion rule — including its ceiling.** The 1986 experiment [14] proved independently-written versions fail together far above chance, *because they share the specification and its ambiguities*. The repo's hard family-exclusion (spec §3.2) and W100 (same-family agreement = "transcription fidelity, not truth", 7/8 false-clean) are the same discovery made twice, 40 years apart. The ceiling matters more: **cross-family diversity does not protect against a shared wrong spec** — the 2026-08-27 lesson (two paths each green on their own tests, disagreeing on the same input; "a product question without a written answer") is a spec-level common-mode failure no refuter family can catch. SOTA answer: differential/metamorphic testing — feed both paths *the same input* and diff outputs, no oracle needed.

**3. Judging really is easier than generating — but only for a *trained or structured* judge.** CriticGPT's 60/63% [6] and Khan's debate numbers [7] (weak judge 76% with debate vs 48% direct) quantify the asymmetry `verify-template.js` bets on. The debate result is the underexploited half: accuracy came not from more reviewers in parallel (the repo's default council shape) but from **forcing two adversaries to argue the same claim before a judge**. The repo has refuters and a gate; it almost never has *confrontation* — dissent rows record one seat's objection, not a resolved argument.

**4. Google's mutation practice validates the design and indicts the trust chain.** `p1s2-mutation-incremental.yml` independently converged on Petrović's three ideas [8] (incremental, filtered, canary-checked) — genuinely at-SOTA design. But Google's system is trusted because it runs on hermetic infrastructure; W121 proved this repo's mutation numbers were, for a period, *produced by the filesystem* (poisoned bytecode) and "no mutation cycle in the repo disabled bytecode at the time." SOTA is not the algorithm; it is the **boring hermeticity underneath it**.

**5. Verifiers have a lifecycle.** OpenAI built SWE-bench Verified with 3 independent annotations per task in 2024, then publicly retired it in 2026 when it stopped discriminating [12]. Nothing in Nuzantara re-qualifies a guard, tripwire, or gate after it lands: the scar corpus shows the decay arrivals one by one (24/73 red whitelist test nobody runs; tripwire watching the wrong enum for weeks; sentinel of family #2 itself disarmed). The SOTA practice is scheduled *re-verification of the verifier against fresh ground truth* — the repo has the primitive (`verify-the-verifiers.yml`) but points it at integrity (sha256), not at discriminative power.

## §5 — Beyond-SOTA recommendations

Ranked by (impact × confidence) / cost. All flat-sub, CLI-only, no Fable auto-routing, no paid Anthropic API.

**R1 — Grader scorecards: benchmark every review seat on the repo's own adjudicated history.**
- **What:** build `infra/grader-bench/`: a labeled corpus of ~40-80 *resolved* claims extracted from scars, retracted-claims registry, AMENDMENTS refutations and correction commits — each with on-disk ground truth (half true, half false at time of claim). Quarterly, run each seat (Codex sol, Kimi K3, GLM, Gemini, Sonnet-as-refuter) cold over the corpus; publish per-seat, per-bug-class catch rate and FP rate into a scorecard consumed by the council-composition table.
- **Why beyond SOTA:** vendors benchmark their reviewers on generic corpora; nobody surveyed benchmarks review seats on *their own organization's adjudicated failure history* — a corpus with exactly this repo's bug distribution. The asymmetry is real: ~100 scars + 106 correction commits/14d + a retracted-claims registry is labeled ground truth no other org maintains.
- **Cost:** ~2 sessions build; ~1h flat-sub tokens/quarter to run. **Gear:** 2.
- **Risk:** family #6 — labels themselves hallucinated. Mitigation: only claims whose adjudication is a command output recorded on disk qualify for the corpus.
- **Metric:** before = "30-40% false-sick" folklore; after = measured precision/recall per seat, tracked quarter-over-quarter. Secondary: council-composition changes driven by scorecard (count).
- **Kill criterion:** if <30 qualifying labeled cases can be extracted in 2 sessions, the corpus is too thin — archive the extractor, revisit at 200 scars.
- **First PR:** `infra/grader-bench/{corpus.json,extract_corpus.py,run_bench.py}` + 20 seed cases (≤400 lines).

**R2 — Receipt spot-re-execution at the harness verdict gate.**
- **What:** receipts in an Evidence Pack already carry verbatim `cmd` + `result` + `exit`. Add a gate step: classify receipts (deterministic-read-only / prod-read / unreplayable-declared), re-execute K≥2 random deterministic ones, diff against the recorded result. Divergence or an undeclared-unreplayable receipt = REWORK. Extend `evidence_pack_lint.py` with the classification field; the re-executor lives with the gate session, not CI (prod-read receipts need the session's credentials).
- **Why beyond SOTA:** the CoT-monitoring lesson [5] says verify with facts the agent cannot fake; no surveyed system re-executes agent-reported evidence as a merge gate. This closes the one Goodhart door the pack design still leaves open (a compliance-shaped pack with fabricated receipts passes today's shape lint).
- **Cost:** ~1 session. **Gear:** 2. **Risk:** family #2 (the re-executor itself unarmed — give it a canary receipt that MUST diverge) and #4 (never re-execute a receipt whose cmd embeds a secret path — classification must exclude).
- **Metric:** % receipts replayable per pack (before: 0% verified); divergence rate (expect >0 early — that is the point).
- **Kill criterion:** if >50% of receipts classify unreplayable after 5 packs, the receipt format needs redesign first.
- **First PR:** lint field + classifier + re-executor script + canary (≤400 lines).

**R3 — Dual-path differential census (the confrontation harness for code).**
- **What:** a registry (`infra/dual-path-census/registry.json`, guard-conformance's shape reused) of every surface with two implementations of one contract (normal/fallback, old/new, cached/fresh, two readers of one table). Per entry, a required test that feeds BOTH paths *the same input corpus* and diffs outputs — disagreement fails even when both paths' own suites are green. C1-style census check: known dual-path patterns (grep for fallback/legacy branches) must be registered or exempted with a reason.
- **Why beyond SOTA:** differential testing is standard for compilers; registering *dual-path-ness itself as a censused conformance obligation* with guilt/innocence-style enforcement is not done anywhere surveyed. It attacks the Knight-Leveson ceiling directly: the one failure class family-exclusion cannot catch (shared-spec ambiguity) is exactly what same-input diffing exposes without needing an oracle.
- **Cost:** ~2 sessions. **Gear:** 2-3 (touches CI). **Risk:** family #3 — the census under-matches (a dual path written unusually is invisible); declare the convention as the contract, as guard-conformance already does.
- **Metric:** before = 1 known divergence (124 client-visible practices would have flipped, PR #5072); after = censused surfaces count + divergences caught pre-merge per quarter.
- **Kill criterion:** 2 quarters with 0 divergences on ≥10 censused surfaces → downgrade to advisory.
- **First PR:** registry + checker + the practice-status pair from #5072 as entry #1.

**R4 — Hermetic verification runner (W121 generalized from conduct to infrastructure).**
- **What:** a single wrapper (`scripts/hermetic_verify.sh` or a pytest plugin) that every measurement instrument (mutation cycles, guard-fuzz, tripwires, grader-bench) must run under: `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, `-p no:cacheprovider`, clean `__pycache__` assertion, and a **self-canary**: before reporting, flip one known byte and assert the instrument notices (proves the instrument sees the disk, not a cache). CI check: any workflow invoking a measurement instrument outside the wrapper fails.
- **Why beyond SOTA:** Google gets hermeticity from Bazel/Forge; retrofitting a *hermeticity contract with a liveness canary* onto ad-hoc Python verification on developer machines is the composition nobody has because nobody else runs a verification economy on two Macs. Cheapest item on this list; converts W121's non-automatable conduct rule into structure.
- **Cost:** <1 session. **Gear:** 1-2. **Risk:** #2 (wrapper exists, cycles don't use it — the CI census closes this).
- **Metric:** instruments wrapped (before: 0 of ~4); mutation verdicts produced outside wrapper (target 0).
- **Kill criterion:** none needed — if the wrapper breaks an instrument, that is a finding.
- **First PR:** wrapper + p1s2 workflow adoption + census check (≤200 lines).

**R5 — Verifier re-qualification calendar (guards decay; schedule their guilt-refresh).**
- **What:** quarterly job: for each required check and registered guard, inject ONE fresh synthetic violation derived from the newest quarter's scars (not the historical case it was born from) and prove the check goes red; a check that stays green is DECAYED and gets a PENDING-ARMS row. Track "last proven discriminative" per check in the guard registry. Fold the catC tautology lint's spirit (a canary must be falsifiable) into every gate, not just the meta-verifier.
- **Why beyond SOTA:** SWE-bench Verified's lifecycle (build → saturate → retire) applied *continuously to an org's own CI gates*. The scar corpus provides what no other org has: a quarterly stream of fresh, real failure shapes to re-test guards against — the guilt test stops being frozen at birth.
- **Cost:** ~2 sessions + small quarterly run. **Gear:** 3 (touches gate surfaces). **Risk:** #3 (the synthetic violation itself under-matches — accept: one fresh case per quarter beats zero) and #5 (injection must live in a throwaway worktree, never shared state).
- **Metric:** before = 64/66 scar gates prose-only, 6 invariants enforced-nowhere, guards' discriminative power last proven at birth; after = % checks with a <90-day red-proof.
- **Kill criterion:** if a quarter's refresh finds 0 decayed checks twice in a row, drop to semi-annual.
- **First PR:** the calendar runner + red-proof for the 5 highest-blast-radius checks.

**R6 — A debate round on contested claims before the gate rules.**
- **What:** when a dissent row exists (refuter and builder disagree), stage one structured exchange — each seat receives the other's argument and must answer the *specific point* (not restate) — and the gate rules on the exchange, not the two monologues. Wire into `verify-template.js` as `debate: true` on findings with a standing objection.
- **Why beyond SOTA composition:** Khan 2024's mechanism (persuasion pressure raises judge accuracy 48→76%) exists in papers and nowhere in surveyed engineering practice as a merge-path step. The repo already has the three seats and the dissent field; the delta is one round and a prompt contract.
- **Cost:** ~0.5 session + marginal tokens per contested claim only. **Gear:** 1-2. **Risk:** #6 (the exchange itself hallucinated — the gate's on-disk re-verification of the winning claim is unchanged and still mandatory).
- **Metric:** gate-overturn rate on debated vs undebated dissents; downstream correction-commits touching debated claims (the correction stream is the ground truth generator).
- **Kill criterion:** 20 debates with overturn-rate delta <5 points → retire the round.
- **First PR:** verify-template.js debate phase + one journaled run.

**R7 — The correction-tax as the verification system's single outcome KPI.**
- **What:** a weekly job computes correction-shaped share of the commit stream (heuristic versioned in the script, both numerator variants reported) and appends to a ledger; every recommendation above must move THIS number down. Publishes the one-line trend at SessionStart.
- **Why beyond SOTA:** nobody surveyed measures organizational claim-repair rate as a KPI; the repo already proved it twice ad-hoc. Making it standing turns every verification investment falsifiable (SYMBIOSIS Law 7).
- **Cost:** trivial. **Gear:** 1. **Risk:** Goodhart on subject lines — mitigation: the heuristic is versioned and the metric is a trend, not a target with consequences.
- **Metric:** the KPI itself; baseline 12.2% (this session) / 13.5% (2026-08-20..22).
- **Kill criterion:** if two heuristic revisions in a row move the number more than real change does, the instrument is too soft — stop publishing until stabilized.
- **First PR:** `scripts/correction_tax.py` + ledger + SessionStart line (≤150 lines).

## §8 — Meta-pattern (Gear 3)

The single defective belief generating this part's failures: **"a verifier, once written, keeps verifying."** Verification is treated as a *state achieved at birth* — guard written, test added, gate armed, receipt recorded — when every scar in §2 shows it is a *measurement that decays*: the enum drifts from under the tripwire (guarded the wrong one for weeks), the workflow stops running the test (24/73 red, nobody notices), the bytecode cache detaches the instrument from the disk (W121), the ground-truth snapshot goes stale (W90), the docstring keeps naming a judge that was replaced (launcher vs ruling), the sentinel of the decay-family itself is found disarmed (W120). The organism applies generator≠grader obsessively at level 0 — the work is never trusted on self-report — and almost nowhere at level 1: **the verifiers are trusted on their own self-report** (their existence, their green, their birth-time guilt test). Every recommendation in §5 is one sentence long when read through this lens: R1 measures the graders, R2 re-executes the receipts, R4 proves the instrument sees the disk, R5 re-proves guards can still go red, R7 measures whether any of it works. The repo's own doctrine already contains the cure's name — "esiste ≠ armato" — but has applied it to organs and cron jobs far more thoroughly than to its own immune system. The beyond-SOTA move is not a new gate: it is conservation of adversariality — **whatever skepticism the system aims at the work, an equal share must recursively aim at whatever certified the work.**
