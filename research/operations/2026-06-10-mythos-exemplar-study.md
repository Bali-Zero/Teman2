# MYTHOS · Appendix E — Exemplar Study: Charismatic-Authority Patterns

**Lane:** `research-only` · **Method:** WebSearch + WebFetch by research subagent, 2026-06-10; every pattern cites a page actually fetched in-session. **Unreachable (honestly flagged):** economist.com direct (patterns cited via Designmodo/Fonts-In-Use coverage), sevenstonesindonesia.com. A `site:reddit.com` sweep returned zero results, so §C relies on expat-facing guides and scam-alert press rather than raw forum threads.

This is the raw material for the §7e charisma design language + the soul-defense rubric. Capped to usable patterns per charter.

## A. Usable patterns (14)

1. **Quantified-safety block with regulator language.** A dedicated "Safe at every step" section: three concrete numbers (customers, "65 licences worldwide", "7 million checks every day") — safety as arithmetic, not adjectives. — Wise (wise.com/us) → Bali Zero: a 3-stat strip under the hero: permits processed, years operating, license/registration number.
2. **Live counters as institutional proof.** "141,461 e-residents from 170+ countries / 41,843 companies founded" + "Backed by the Government of Estonia" — the state vouches, the counter proves motion. — e-Residency (e-resident.gov.ee) → CRM-fed counters: visas approved, PT PMAs registered, updated monthly.
3. **Named testimonial with origin + specificity.** Wise: "Samadrita T, United States — it took only 16 seconds"; e-Residency: five entrepreneurs with photo, name, company, country; Jobbatical: name + role + employer. Specific detail (time, visa type) is the credibility carrier. → client first name + nationality + service ("KITAS investor, approved in 9 days").
4. **Journey-stage architecture with honest timeframes.** e-Residency's "Become → Start → Run (3–8 weeks / 1–2 days / ongoing)"; Henley's five steps in first person plural: "We advise → We onboard → We assist → You are granted → We will continue." → per-service timeline with real durations; the "we…you" voice makes the agency the protagonist's guide, not a counter window.
5. **One-task-one-link (GOV.UK discipline).** Every service = one descriptive entry ("Find a job", "Check your record"), no nested menus, labels functional not persuasive. → service cards phrased as the user's task: "Extend my visa", "Open a PT PMA", each resolving to a single guide + single CTA.
6. **Authority via absence.** GOV.UK has no hero metaphor, no stock photos, no superlatives — plainness *is* the institutional signal. Cross-finding: "voted Bali's Number 1 Agency" is literally a documented scam tagline (The Bali Sun scam-alert coverage). → ban every self-superlative; **the scam-tagline test: if a fake agent could plausibly say it, cut it.**
7. **Byline + role under editorial content.** Monocle gives columnists illustrated headshots and located roles ("Istanbul correspondent"). → every guide/article signed: real name, photo, role ("Tax lead, Bali Zero") — expertise has a face before it has a claim.
8. **Dual-typeface editorial system + masthead block.** The Economist 2018 system: Milo Serif (display/body gravitas) + Econ Sans (UI workhorse); enlarged red masthead box, sticky header; "evolutionary not revolutionary" restraint. — via designmodo.com, fontsinuse.com → maps directly onto Cormorant Garamond + Inter; a persistent navy/red brand block as masthead, never logo soup.
9. **Publish proprietary instruments instead of claiming expertise.** Henley Passport Index; Fragomen's dated, geo-tagged immigration-alerts feed ("June 9, 2026 | Global"); Economist "Graphic detail". Owning a recurring, citable artifact = expertise made visible. → surface the existing regulatory-watcher as a public, dated "Indonesia Regulation Watch" feed.
10. **Segmented entry by persona, not service taxonomy.** Fragomen: "For Employers / For Individuals"; InCorp's "What are you looking for?" dropdown is the same idea executed coldly. → three doors: "I'm moving to Bali / I'm starting a business / I'm buying property", each with its own journey page.
11. **Transparent package pricing as differentiator.** Sleek exposes per-service pricing pages; the small Bali agents with 5-star reputations publish raw IDR (Gaya Bali Visa: 3.5M social visa, 750k/extension). **Every big Indonesia competitor hides pricing.** → publish PricingTool prices openly; the single largest open flank against Cekindo/Emerhub/LMI. *(Depends on D1/D2 resolution — PricingTool integration.)*
12. **Interactive value tool in the hero.** Wise puts a working currency calculator above the fold — utility before signup. → a 4-question "Which visa fits me?" selector in the hero; answer first, contact second. *(The Visa decision tree already exists — this is a placement insight.)*
13. **Compliance badge row with third-party verifiables.** Jobbatical: "4.8★ | 69 Reviews" (Google), success rate, ISO 27001; InCorp prints its NIB number in the footer. → license + NIB number, Google rating badge linked to the live listing, near the footer of every page.
14. **Soft primary CTA for high-stakes decisions.** e-Residency's primary CTA is "How it works" (exploratory); Emerhub's is "Schedule a free consultation" — not "Buy now". High-trust services convert through understanding, not urgency (Sleek's "7/100 slots claimed. Act fast" is the wince-inducing counterexample). → primary CTA pair: "See how it works" + WhatsApp; never countdown/scarcity mechanics.

## C. Audience-specific trust signals (foreigner choosing an Indonesian bureaucracy guardian)

1. **A physical, visitable, photographed office** — scam agents "work from home with no website, office, staff"; guides advise inspecting office photos. (legalindonesia.id; thebalisun.com) → show the Kerobokan office, real photos + map.
2. **WhatsApp as the primary, instant channel** — ~91M Indonesian WhatsApp users; 64% prefer messaging a business over calling. (hashmeta.com; letsmoveindonesia.com) → click-to-WhatsApp as universal secondary CTA, with stated reply-time.
3. **Google reviews: volume, recency, authenticity** — expats triangulate via Google rating; same-day review clusters are a recognized fake signal → link the live listing rather than quoting cherry-picked stars. (legalindonesia.id; thebalisun.com)
4. **Official-fee anchoring** — the documented scam pattern is 2.8M IDR charged for a 500k IDR VOA extension; publishing "official government fee vs our service fee" separately is a trust move no competitor makes. (thebalisun.com)
5. **Verifiable registration/license number** — guides advise checking agency registration with the Ministry; InCorp's footer NIB is the executable version. (legalindonesia.id; cekindo.com)
6. **Community word-of-mouth loci** — recommendations flow through the "Visa & Law Indonesia" Facebook group and friend referrals; being citable there (named staff, consistent pricing) matters more than ads. (via thebalisun.com)
7. **Free consultation as table stakes** — "employees should offer you free consultations before starting any work" — an expectation, not a perk. (legalindonesia.id)
8. **Anti-signal: unverifiable superlatives** — "Bali's Trusted Service", "voted Number 1" are documented scammer taglines; **restraint is regionally load-bearing, not just aesthetic.** (thebalisun.com)

## D. Refined 5-attribute Charisma Rubric (evidence-sharpened from the charter seed)

1. **Expertise published, not asserted** — dated regulatory alerts, official-fee anchors, named guides and instruments (Henley-Index-style); zero superlatives. *Test: could a fake agent paste this sentence onto their site? Then delete it.*
2. **Named humans at the point of decision** — byline + photo + role on every guide; a real face within one scroll of every CTA; founder findable. *Test: can a visitor name who will handle their case before contacting?* (Anti-pattern: all three competitors.)
3. **One next step per page, two channels max** — GOV.UK one-task-one-link; primary = soft/exploratory ("See how it works" / WhatsApp), secondary = booked consultation. *Test: exactly one primary action visible at any scroll depth.*
4. **Editorial restraint as the authority signal** — serif+sans dual system, one accent block, whitespace, dated content; no carousels, no scarcity timers, no brochure pop-ups. *Test: remove any element and ask if trust drops; if not, it was clutter.*
5. **Neither end of market winces — the backpacker-to-boardroom test** — transparent IDR/EUR pricing with official-fee split so the 500k-extension client doesn't feel hustled, plus institutional instruments (alerts feed, license numbers, counters) so the PT PMA investor doesn't smell amateur. *Test: both the Canggu nomad and the corporate GM find one thing built specifically for them on the homepage.*
