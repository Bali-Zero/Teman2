## 3. Inference (Architectural & Operational Deductions)

1. **Two Distinct Engineering Epochs Coexist Across `D04`–`D10`**:
   - **Hardened Subsystem Pattern (Contract-Ready)**: `visa_engine/evaluate_path.py` (`D04`), `accounting/reconcile_service.py` (`D05`), `garuda_orders/repository.py` (`D05`), `integrations/wa_outbox_worker.py` (`D06`), `kbli_notebook.py` (`D07`), `intake/writer.py` + `intake/crm_delivery.py` (`D08`), and `research_os/naga_persistence.py` (`D10`) already implement explicit state machines, `FOR UPDATE` / advisory locks, deterministic idempotency keys (`reserve_idempotency`, `garuda_payment_inbox`, `wa_reply_claims`, `compute_idempotency_key`, `research_os_objects.object_hash`), and fail-closed validation. These 7 subsystems are blocked in `consumer-matrix.csv` (`BLOCKED_DISCOVERY`) not because their code lacks transactional/idempotency discipline, but because their static/runtime operation contracts have not yet been extracted and bound into `B05` operation-instance records with `B03`/`B04` runtime principal & grant proofs.
   - **Legacy Multi-Statement Router/Service Pattern (Contract-Incomplete)**: `crm_practices.py`, `client_core.py` (non-phone update & buffered audit), `invoicing/invoice_service.py`, `war_room/repository.py` (provenance sidecar), `news.py` (`_enqueue_post_publish`), and `memory_service_postgres.py` execute multi-step database or cross-store mutations across separate connections/transactions and swallow secondary failures.
2. **Why `REAL-RUN-RECEIPT.json` Blocked `D04` and `D06`**:
   - For `D04` (`clients`), the restore drill failure (`VIOLATION: 1`) is a genuine data-plane hygiene violation in the production backup (`client_type_unknown` or `lead_metadata_not_object` in `scripts/ci/restore_drill_verify.py:221-229`) caused by historical writes that predated schema-level CHECK constraints.

Replacing or supplementing the `pg_temp` PL/pgSQL helper when running on read-only connections (or ensuring single-session execution with `TEMP` privileges on the restore target) is a direct dependency interface between `B04`, `B06`, and `B07`.
