    QUOTE: "No wave requires queue downtime."
    WHY WRONG: Exact-tree isolation requires draining the queue and excluding new admissions through the heal’s terminal state.     EVIDENCE: scripts/ci/setup_merge_queue_ruleset.sh:16-21 says ruleset updates replace the whole object; :34-49 exposes only status/enable/disable/apply; :94-108 configures `max_entries_to_build=5` and `max_entries_to_merge=4`. docs/runbooks/merge-queue-discipline.md:365-378 confirms those queue parameters. The proposed exclusion requirement is at research/operations/2026-08-10-merge-os-v2-submission-system.md:128-135.
    SCENARIO: After drain at main SHA S0, the heal regenerates and runs R1/R2. If admissions remain open, another PR advances main and the heal is stale. If admissions are blocked until terminal, every ordinary PR waits through heal R1 and R2—queue downtime. If neither is allowed, continuous traffic starves Wave 3 forever.

    WHY WRONG: GitHub consumes the old auto-merge request on ejection; the script then arms the PR again. A counter keyed to queue entry resets on every attempt. The identical-failure breaker also misses a storm whose infrastructure symptoms alternate.

    SCENARIO: One PR is successively ejected for Docker timeout, missing queue ref, runner shutdown, then repeats. Every new queue entry has retry count 1, and no identical cause reaches three within 30 minutes. The loop remains unbounded.

    SCENARIO: Capacity and savings calculations consume 25.7 minutes and an unexplained 30 PR/day while the cited source supplies 25.1 and 47.3 merges/day. The resulting baseline is presented as measured even though its inputs are mixed, absent, or arithmetically inconsistent.
