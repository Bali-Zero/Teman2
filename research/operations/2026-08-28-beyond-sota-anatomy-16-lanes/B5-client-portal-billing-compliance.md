## 2. Honest state vs. SOTA

- **Payment lane design: at or above SOTA; payment lane in production: zero.** The state machine, idempotency digest, provider-confirmed expiry and "late paid is real money, never neither" doctrine are exactly what Stripe and Xendit prescribe (§3). But the adapter cannot accept a live key, the routes are 503, and the cookie that gates the paid half of the product cannot be minted. Nobody has paid anything.
- **Billing portal: below SOTA.** Read-only PDFs, three states, no due dates, no pay link, no client-side reminder. Karbon, Ignition and Stripe's hosted invoice all close the loop in one click.
- **Email: near SOTA on the *sending* half, below on the *listening* half.** A ledger with retry/escalation and a health monitor every 30 minutes is better than most 10-person firms. Bounces, complaints and blocks are never heard; no DMARC, no one-click unsubscribe, no suppression list — the 2024 Google baseline is not met and cannot be proven met.
- **Compliance engine: SOTA architecture for a small firm, unmeasured operation.** Forecast → dedup → i18n → dispatch → feedback-autotune is Drata-shaped; but it runs once a day from a foreign CI cloud, two legacy notifiers still carry rosters as literals, and whether staff ever post outcomes (the autotune's fuel) was not measurable read-only.
- **LKPM: beyond generic SOTA.** One-receipt-per-KBLI modelling, ready packs formatted for OSS paste, PerBKPM 5/2025 deadlines — no practice-management SaaS ships Indonesian obligations as data.
- **Retention & S7 privacy posture: beyond SOTA in explicitness** (a code floor with a guilt/innocence proof; an LLM lane with no cloud branch by construction), **below in completion** (erasure not honourable; derogation not armed).
- **Portal product: half-built flows everywhere** — invite that does not send, prefs that are written and read only by an unarmed script, an archive that does not revoke login, an unread-count endpoint with no caller.

## 3. Deep research: the world's best

- **Stripe — webhooks** (docs.stripe.com/webhooks): return 2xx *before* business logic; verify HMAC-SHA256 over `t.payload` with constant-time compare and a 5-minute replay tolerance; **no ordering guarantee** ("be prepared… retrieve missing objects via the API"); duplicates expected — log processed event ids; automatic retries "for up to three days with exponential back off"; process asynchronously through a queue; roll signing secrets with a 24-hour overlap. Nuzantara's lane already matches all but the durable inbound-event log.
- **Stripe — Hosted Invoice Page** (docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page): a unique long-random URL where the customer views details/status, pays with any enabled method, downloads invoice and receipt PDFs; URLs expire 30 days after due date (never >120 days) and expired links redirect to a recovery page that re-sends the email; link appears in the email, the PDF footer and the API. This is the reference shape for "invoice as a live object".
- **Xendit — handling webhooks** (docs.xendit.co/docs/handling-webhooks): `x-callback-token` header per account; respond 2xx quickly; "retries up to six times with exponential backoff"; manual re-trigger from the dashboard; webhooks "may arrive in a jumbled sequence" — build a queue as a buffer; use `payment_id`/`capture_id` for uniqueness. Confirms the adapter's static-token model (`xendit.py:1-17`) is the provider's real mechanism, not a shortcut.
- **Google sender requirements** (support.google.com/a/answer/81126): all senders — SPF or DKIM, valid PTR, TLS, spam rate in Postmaster Tools below 0.3 %; senders ≥5,000/day — SPF **and** DKIM **and** DMARC (policy may be `none`), From aligned with SPF or DKIM domain, one-click unsubscribe for marketing/subscribed mail. **RFC 8058** (rfc-editor.org): `List-Unsubscribe` with an HTTPS URI plus `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, the POST honoured without confirmation, both headers covered by a valid DKIM signature, opaque per-recipient identifier. **BIMI** (bimigroup.org): SPF+DKIM+DMARC aligned, SVG Tiny PS logo, DNS BIMI record, VMC/CMC certificate (policy level and provider list not on the page fetched).
- **Brevo transactional webhooks** (developers.brevo.com): 15 events — `request, delivered, deferred, hard_bounce, soft_bounce, invalid_email, blocked, spam, unsubscribed, error, opened, unique_opened, click, proxy_open, unique_proxy_open` — with `message-id`, `reason`, `ts_epoch`, `tags`. **Postmark bounce webhook** (postmarkapp.com): hard bounce → suppress; soft bounce → watch `Inactive`/`CanActivate`; retries on any non-2xx so duplicates arrive — dedup on `MessageID+Email+BouncedAt`. Together they define the listening half Nuzantara lacks.
- **Knock preferences** (docs.knock.app): a PreferenceSet of `categories × channel_types × channels × workflows` resolving to booleans; unset = opted in; any applicable opt-out blocks; channel beats channel_type; tenant/recipient/environment merge hierarchy; `commercial_subscribed` flag; **workflow overrides bypass preferences for critical mail** (password reset). Nuzantara's `notification_prefs` is a 3-column subset of this.
- **Karbon client portal** (karbonhq.com): magic-link invitation, personalised task lists and timelines, "smart alerts and automated reminders that nudge clients when action is needed", secure upload saved "where it belongs", e-signature, multi-channel (email/push/in-app), invoice delivery, optional 2FA. **Ignition** (ignitionapp.com): proposal → engagement letter → payment details captured *in the proposal* → "once signed, billing and payment happen automatically"; a customer citing "$0 A/R after switching to automatic payments" (vendor claim). Clio for Clients and TaxDome pages returned 403 — their feature sets are **(unverified)** here.
- **GOV.UK task list** (design-system.service.gov.uk/components/task-list): for long services done across sittings; statuses in sentence case, `Completed` in plain black text and `Incomplete` as a blue tag "to draw attention to remaining work"; group lists under short headings; never show answers inside the list. **NN/g progress indicators**: users "were willing to wait on average 3 times longer" with a moving indicator; ≥10 s needs percent-done plus a time estimate ("About 3 minutes remaining"); indicators start slow and accelerate. The Domino's-tracker lesson transposed to a 60-day KITAS: show *estimated remaining days* from a rule, not just a stage name.
- **Drata continuous compliance** (drata.com/products/compliance): "all control statuses are kept updated daily", 300+ integrations for evidence, 30+ frameworks mapped once, "when drift occurs, you're notified immediately", evidence linked to controls. A third-party comparison (search snippet, **unverified**) puts Vanta at hourly tests. The transferable idea: an *obligation* is a **test with a last-checked timestamp**, not an email.
- **PerBKPM 5/2025** (smartlegal.id, 2025-10-14): Pasal 286(3),(5) moves the LKPM deadline from the 10th to the **15th**; quarterly 15 Apr / 15 Jul / 15 Oct / 15 Jan for medium-large; semester 15 Jul / 15 Jan for small enterprises. Matches `lkpm_service.py:39-44`. (A search snippet cited Pasal 285 — the article number is **unverified** against the primary text.)
- **Coretax e-invoicing** — search snippets only (**unverified**, page returned 403): from 2025 all PKP issue cleared XML e-Faktur with QR/NSFP; invoices uploaded after the 20th of the following month are not creditable. Relevant only if Bali Zero is PKP — a business fact this lane could not verify.

## 5. Recommendations — reach SOTA

## 7. §Meta-pattern

**The organism ships emitters and calls them features.** Every organ in this part builds the half that *sends* — a token minted and mailed, a preference upserted, an alert dispatched, an invite "sent", a pitch drafted, a 503 that will become a checkout — proves that half with green unit tests (189 files), and stops. The half that *consumes* — the page that exchanges the token, the sender that reads the preference, the plist that runs the watchdog, the email the CRM path never sends, the frontend that calls unread-count — is nobody's deliverable, and no probe crosses the boundary: superscar #2 (*esiste ≠ armato*) at the granularity of a product flow. The same belief explains the vocabulary drifts (a fourth `pending` label, an `overdue` fixture, `archived ≠ locked out`): each side evolves its own contract because nothing asserts the pair. The cure is structural, not another audit: a **consumer-boundary gate** (P0-2) plus the rule that a flow is *done only when the consuming end is exercised in prod* — which is also why P0-1 and P0-3, small in code, are the two most valuable items in this report.
