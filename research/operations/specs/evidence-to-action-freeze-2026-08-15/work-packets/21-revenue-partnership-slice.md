# Work Packet 21 — Revenue and Partnership Adoption Slice

**Architecture:** `research-os/v1.0.0`
**Wave:** 3 — measurement, learning, and business adoption
**Depends on:** Packets 12, 13, 14, and 18; authoritative `PricingTool` runtime access for every price
**Integrates with:** Packet 20 journey boundaries
**Unlocks:** one governed lane for renewals, qualified demand, partner signals, sales enablement, and internal newsletter drafts; reviewed legacy-retirement candidates for Packet 16
**Risk:** high commercial, privacy, pricing, consent, partner-compliance, and reputation risk; human approval is mandatory for outreach and partner effects

## Session prompt

You own the adoption slice that turns verified renewal, qualified-demand, market, and partner signals into reviewable commercial decisions in Packet 12's one Action Inbox. Reconcile the existing yield optimizer, lead qualification, CRM opportunity views, renewal signals, partner workflows, competitor/market research, sales-enablement artifacts, and newsletter draft paths. Do not create another CRM, outbound sequencer, partner ledger, or publication pipeline.

You are not alone in the codebase. Work in a dedicated worktree. Before editing, identify the machine and commit, inspect authoritative Pro state through read-only interfaces, declare every exact file, data object, route, event, component, job, and flag you intend to own, and check for active sibling work. Preserve concurrent edits. If a migration is required, request a serial number from the Conductor migration ledger; never allocate, guess, or renumber it yourself.

This packet authorizes no deployment, publication, newsletter send, email, WhatsApp, CRM mutation, partner invitation, commission creation, payout, quote issuance, or client-facing contact. It may produce validated internal proposals and drafts, separately approved shadow/no-op attempts, and evaluation artifacts. Every future outreach, quote, partner action, or source-of-truth mutation requires a current human `ApprovalReceipt` scoped to the exact effect.

## Mission

Build a privacy-preserving commercial lane that answers five questions with evidence:

1. Which existing client renewal or service-continuity decision deserves human review now?
2. Which inbound or observed demand is sufficiently qualified to merit an owned next action?
3. Which partner or channel signal warrants due diligence, enablement, or a bounded proposal?
4. Which verified insight should become an internal sales-enablement brief or newsletter draft?
5. What measurable outcome followed a human-approved action?

Each answer must become a traceable `DecisionPacket` with bounded downstream candidates. Where appropriate, an explicit Packet 18 operator–AI deliberation may revise and select one candidate into a narrow `RequestedActionSpec` inside an operator-confirmed `ConductorHandoff`; only then may Packet 12 materialize the canonical `ActionItem`/`ActionIntent` pair. Price-bearing artifacts must resolve live through `PricingTool`; no price may be guessed, copied from an old report, cached as truth, or generated by an LLM.

## Inputs and contract chain

Use `research-os/v1.0.0`, Packet 12 action semantics, and authoritative protected/local references:

1. `IntelEvent` captures authorized renewal, demand, public market, partner, content-response, or source-system observations.
2. `Evidence` and `Claim` support regulatory, numeric, competitor, market, eligibility, and consequential statements. CRM state uses protected source references and hashes rather than copied client prose.
3. `DecisionPacket` states why review is warranted now, evidence, uncertainty, risk, owner, alternatives, expected value basis, and allowed downstream candidates.
4. The commercial adapter stops at `DecisionPacket`. Packet 18 may turn one operator-selected or revised candidate—such as `review_renewal`, `qualify_demand`, `request_partner_due_diligence`, `prepare_local_outreach_draft`, `prepare_sales_brief`, or `prepare_newsletter_draft`—into a `RequestedActionSpec` inside an operator-confirmed handoff; only Packet 12 materializes it.
5. `ApprovalReceipt` binds a human decision to exact inputs, target, draft, price reference, channel, authority, expiry, and permitted effect.
6. `ExecutionAttempt` records only the immutable start of a specifically approved no-op/dry-run or later approved adapter invocation, including exact `ActionIntent` and `ApprovalReceipt` references, idempotency key, executor, and `started_at`. A separate typed `execution.result` `OperationalReceipt` records terminal outcome, effects, evidence/artifacts, error code, and reconciliation. A rejection or other non-`approve` decision creates no attempt. Attempted send is not delivered; delivered is not read; reply is not conversion.
7. `OutcomeEvent` records observed reply, meeting, qualification, accepted/declined proposal, partner review result, newsletter review, conversion, retention, correction, complaint, or unattributable outcome with explicit measurement window and attribution strength.

Packet 18 is the sole producer of the selected `RequestedActionSpec` and `ConductorHandoff`; it preserves hashed operator inputs, selected and rejected options, unresolved questions, and verification-receipt references without storing private reasoning or protected commercial context. Conversational text, a commercial recommendation, or an unconfirmed handoff cannot create an Action Inbox item.

Human approval is mandatory for all outreach, quotes, partner contact, newsletter sends, publication, source mutation, commission, and payment. Approval of a draft does not authorize a different channel, recipient, price, discount, or later revision.

## Privacy, consent, and channel boundary

- Client, lead, and partner PII remains in CRM or another approved Pro-local protected store. The general Research OS ledger holds opaque references and aggregates only.
- Client-specific signal processing and draft generation run locally. If the local model or protected resolver is unavailable, stop; do not fall back to a cloud model.
- Public market and competitor evidence may use approved cloud research, but never blend protected CRM context into the prompt.
- General-ledger CRM projections suppress or aggregate cohorts smaller than 10, and general analytics receive only privacy-reviewed aggregates. Missing, incomplete, or too-small samples emit `insufficient_evidence`; suppressed cohorts never become zero.
- Every outreach intent requires a valid consent/lawful-channel state from the protected source. Unknown consent fails closed.
- Sales-enablement and newsletter artifacts are internal drafts until an explicit later approval. Their filenames, states, and UI must not imply sending or publication.
- Partner signals can involve immigration, tax, contracting, anti-bribery, conflict, reputation, and payout risk. These remain human/specialist review gates, never model-only decisions.

## Commercial policy boundaries

### Renewals and compliance

If one event creates both a protection obligation and a commercial possibility, emit separate linked `DecisionPacket` objects. The compliance packet is never suppressed, delayed, downgraded, or reprioritized by expected revenue. The commercial packet may proceed only after the team confirms the client-benefit, consent, timing, and legal basis.

### Qualified demand

Qualification must distinguish observed intent, eligibility unknown, service fit, urgency, owner capacity, and consent. A fit score is a prioritization aid, not truth. Disqualifying or low-confidence results remain reviewable and cannot silently discard a lead.

### Prices

Every price, government fee, service fee, tax, discount, currency, and validity period shown in an internal draft must come from the current `PricingTool` response and carry its stable quote/result reference, service identifier, version or timestamp, and component semantics. If PricingTool is unavailable, unauthorized, ambiguous, stale, or lacks the product, the packet must say `PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` and route to review. Never fill the gap with a remembered value, market estimate, competitor price, or LLM output.

### Partners

A partner signal may propose due diligence or an internal conversation brief. It cannot approve a partner, create a contract, set a commission, decide withholding, onboard a foreign partner, or authorize a payout. Those effects require their existing specialist, legal/tax, and finance gates.

## Tests and metric definitions

Materialize every metric below as a canonical `MetricProfile` before evaluation. Pre-register its exact numerator, denominator, window, timezone, late-arrival policy, exclusions, minimum sample, estimator and confidence method, subgroups, guardrails, and decision rule. If a sample floor is unmet, the result is `insufficient_evidence`, and that metric cannot satisfy an exit gate.

Required tests:

- contract, stable identity, deduplication, replay, ordering, correction, and invalidation tests;
- eligibility, inclusion/exclusion, abstention, owner, SLA, consent, and compliance-separation tests;
- PricingTool identity, component, freshness, outage, ambiguity, stale approval, and no-fallback tests;
- approval scope, actor authority, recipient/channel/draft/price hash, expiry, and change-invalidation tests;
- local-only generation, prompt egress, log/trace/export, protected resolver, role, and aggregate re-identification tests;
- no-op execution, timeout-after-effect, reconciliation, and truthful delivery/response/conversion-state tests;
- Action Inbox parity, accessibility, and protected-detail authorization tests;
- partner-risk escalation and prohibited commission/payout tests.

Freeze metric definitions before canary:

- **Accepted-candidate precision** = shadow candidates independently judged worthy of operator review / all shadow candidates.
- **Qualified-demand recall** = independently labeled review-worthy demand represented by a canonical packet / all independently labeled review-worthy demand in covered sources.
- **Renewal coverage** = independently labeled in-window renewal decisions represented once / all independently labeled in-window renewal decisions.
- **Duplicate-opportunity rate** = extra active packets representing an already represented commercial decision / all active commercial packets.
- **Evidence/freshness coverage** = consequential packets with required current evidence, source time, and rule/model version / all consequential packets.
- **Owner/SLA coverage** = actionable packets with exactly one valid owner or explicit unassigned escalation and defined SLA / all actionable packets.
- **PricingTool provenance coverage** = price-bearing drafts whose every numeric price component resolves to a current PricingTool result reference / all price-bearing drafts. Required value: 100%.
- **Invented-price count** = price values not resolvable to the referenced PricingTool result. Required value: zero.
- **Consent coverage** = outreach intents with current, source-backed lawful-channel/consent state / all outreach intents. Required value before approval: 100%.
- **Unauthorized-effect count** = outreach, quote, source mutation, partner, commission, payment, send, or publication effects without an exact human ApprovalReceipt. Required value: zero.
- **PII leakage count** = protected or re-identifying data outside authorized local stores. Required value: zero.
- **Outcome attribution coverage** = observed commercial outcomes with subject reference, measurement window, attribution strength, and caveats / all outcomes reported.

## Shadow and canary

Shadow for at least two complete commercial operating cycles while all legacy queues and human processes remain authoritative. Compare candidates and exclusions, not only matches. Do not draft for suppressed, opted-out, unknown-consent, complained, or legally unresolved subjects.

The canary is an operator-selected internal cohort. It may expose read-only packets, PricingTool status, local draft hashes, triage/assign/snooze/request-evidence actions, and approval-denial receipts. Every outbound, CRM, partner, quote, newsletter, publication, finance, and payment adapter stays disabled. Reviewers inspect sensitive drafts only in the protected local view.

Expand signal families separately. Passing renewal does not authorize leads, partners, newsletters, or pricing changes. Passing an internal draft review does not authorize sending it.

## Exit criteria

Exit only when:

- accepted-candidate precision is at least 90% and qualified-demand recall is at least 95% on the frozen labeled set;
- renewal coverage is at least 99% and duplicate-opportunity rate is below 1%;
- evidence/freshness and owner/SLA coverage are 100% for high-risk canary packets;
- PricingTool provenance coverage and consent coverage are 100%;
- invented-price count, unauthorized-effect count, and PII leakage count are zero;
- protection and commercial packets remain separate and no compliance item is suppressed or delayed by commercial ranking;
- partner-risk cases abstain or route to the correct specialist and no commission/payout path can execute;
- delivery, reply, qualification, conversion, retention, and attribution states remain semantically distinct under replay and timeout;
- every mismatch is classified, the rollback drill passes, and legacy truth remains available;
- independent privacy/security, PricingTool, commercial-operations, and partner-risk reviewers return `PASS` or explicitly bounded `PASS_WITH_LIMITS`;
- the owner separately approves any deployment or channel activation proposal. This packet completion authorizes none.
