  # Beyond-SOTA — Lane 10: The Organizational Learning Loop

  ## 0. TL;DR

  Nuzantara's learning loop is **AT-to-AHEAD of world SOTA on capture and codification** (a 138-entry measured scar corpus, 10 CI-budgeted superscar families, ~68% executable antidotes, an adversarially-refuted self-refinement bench) but **BEHIND on the loop's terminal half**: antibodies recur for 3–4 generations before their family is structurally redesigned (W65→W90→W100→W113; W101→W101-recidiva→W107; family #3 at 9+ instances), activation is pull-based (grep) rather than push-based at the point of risk, and the loop's own artifacts have repeatedly exhibited the disease they catalog (the superscar bridge ballooned to 73,854 bytes while claiming ~2k tokens; 27 of 40 of its pointers were false). The biggest gap: **recidiva is recorded, never escalated** — nothing converts "this family bit us again" into a mandatory structural response. Top-3 moves: (1) a **recidiva escalator** — CI counts family members per 30 days and forces a redesign review past threshold; (2) **scar-family mutation operators** — use the incident taxonomy to generate mutants and prove the antibody corpus kills its own family's diseases; (3) **diff-scoped scar activation** — inject the 3–8-word family rows for scars whose Reference paths intersect the current diff, back-tested against the four known recidiva events.

  ## 5. Beyond-SOTA recommendations

  Ranked by (impact × confidence) / cost.

  ### R1 — The Recidiva Escalator (family-recurrence CI gate)
  **What:** A script (sibling of `lint_scar_number_collision.py`) that parses family membership counts per rolling 30-day window from the two body files, and when a family accrues ≥3 new members in 30 days (or any member whose heading contains `recidiva`), opens a mandatory **structural-redesign review** entry: the next modus session must answer "what redesign retires this family" before any new local antidote for that family may merge. **Why it beats SOTA:** Google SRE tracks action-item *closure*; nobody in the survey — not SRE, not ASRS, not the agent-memory literature — has a *recurrence-rate-triggered escalation to redesign* computed mechanically from the incident corpus. The organism's machine-parseable corpus (W-numbers + family tags) is the asymmetry that makes it cheap. **Cost:** ~150-line script + workflow; a few hours; zero tokens at runtime. **Gear:** 2. **Risk:** family #3 (the counter itself over/under-matching family tags) → count by the bridge's MEMBRI lines (the curated index), not by grep over prose. **Metric:** before = 4-generation chains (W101 line, W65 line, 9× family #3) detected only in prose; after = every recurrence chain ≥2 generations has a dated redesign-review entry within 7 days; target: no family reaches a 3rd generation in 2026-Q4. **Kill criterion:** escalations fire >2×/quarter and no redesign is ever accepted (signal is noise or the gate is ignorable — demote to report-only). **First PR:** below.

  ### R2 — Scar-family mutation operators (prove the antibodies kill their own disease)
  **What:** Define one **mutation operator per superscar family** (family #2: replace a check body with `exit 0`; family #3: widen a regex `\b`→substring; family #1: repoint a plist to a HOME copy; family #9: judge staleness by timestamp instead of content…) and run a quarterly sweep that applies each operator to the current guard/test surface and asserts the antibody corpus **kills the mutant**. **Why it beats SOTA:** mutation testing exists (and the organism uses it — W121), but generating the operator set *from the organization's own incident taxonomy* is not done by any surveyed system; it closes the exact hole W121 exposed (the instrument measuring "tests bite" was itself unverified) and converts the scar corpus from a narrative asset into a **generative test asset**. **Cost:** 2–3 days for the first four operators; runs on flat-sub CI minutes. **Gear:** 2 (design), 3 for the pilot review. **Risk:** family #2 recursing (a mutation sweep that silently no-ops) → inherit W121's antibody (`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, `-p no:cacheprovider`) and require per-mutant kill evidence in the report. **Metric:** before = each antidote is tested only on its own guilt/innocence cases (unknown family-kill rate); after = quarterly family-mutant kill rate ≥90%, published in the bench report. **Kill criterion:** kill rate plateaus <70% after two sweeps and the survivors are judged unfixable — the corpus is ceremonial, say so and redesign.

  ### R3 — Diff-scoped scar activation (CALLBACK, targeted)
  **What:** A SessionStart/diff hook: compute the session's touched-path set (or the PR diff), intersect with the `Reference:` paths of the scar corpus, and inject only the matching 3–8-word family rows (from the bridge) plus a one-line "this area carries W-scars" banner — instead of, or in addition to, the blanket 14 KB. **Why it beats SOTA:** ASRS pushes de-identified lessons "to persons in a position to correct them"; ReasoningBank retrieves per-task; nobody grounds retrieval in the **mechanical intersection of the working diff and the incident corpus** — possible here because References are paths and the fleet is CLI-driven. **Cost:** 1 day; back-test included. **Gear:** 2. **Risk:** family #3/#9 (path matching over/under-matching) → match on exact repo-relative paths from References, never substrings; and W92 (relative-path ambiguity) applies. **Metric:** back-test against the four known recidiva events (W80-recidiva, W101-recidiva, W84-tccutil-recidiva, W100 after W65): would the hook have injected the ancestor scar into the session that produced the recurrence?   ## 8. §Meta-pattern

  Everything above is generated by one defective belief: **"writing the antibody down is the same as the antibody being armed"** — superscar family #2 (Esiste ≠ Armato), operating on the learning loop itself. The corpus is the best-in-class at *recording*; its failures are all downstream of recording: the bridge that recorded "I am ~2k tokens" while being 73,854 bytes; the pointers that recorded where bodies live while 27/40 pointed nowhere; the antidote for W101 that recorded the fix while Fase 2 never ran (W101-recidiva); the mutation tool that recorded kill-ratios produced by the filesystem (W121); the alarm that recorded a true event while its cure was dead code (W116); the sentinel of the family itself disarmed (W120). Even recidiva — the signature finding of this lane — is the same belief one level up: the family is *recorded* as a family, and the recording is treated as the response, so the family keeps biting until the recording itself says "fourth generation". The beyond-SOTA move that follows is not better recording (the organism is past that frontier) but **mechanically closing the last meter between record and armament**: counters that escalate (R1), mutants that audit (R2), injections that arrive at the diff (R3), and ledgers that refuse to call a lesson learned until something executable exists (R4, R5). The organism already knows this law — it wrote it down as family #2. What it has not done is aim the family's own executable antidote at the loop that wrote it.
