# Beyond-SOTA 10/13 — The organizational learning loop

> How a trauma becomes a durable antibody in this organism, measured against the best incident-learning systems in the world, and where to go past them. Lane 2 owns how this material is *loaded* at session start; this lane owns how it is *produced, compressed, armed, and kept true*.

## 0. TL;DR

- **Position in one sentence:** capture and compression are AHEAD of everything surveyed — a push-injected, CI-budgeted, guilt/innocence-guarded scar corpus is a working industrial instance of the paradigm Karpathy only *named* ("system prompt learning"), and the structural inverse of NASA's LLIS graveyard — while **follow-through is BEHIND**, reproducing Google's measured disease ("the most common shortcoming is lack of follow-up") almost line for line.
- **Numbers (all measured this session):** 164 scar entries; monthly capture declining May 56 → Aug 18; **70%** carry an executable antidote (115/164); **49%** cite an earlier scar; superscar bridge saturated at 13,986/14,000 bytes; PENDING-ARMS **280 TECH-DEBT overdue of 441 open**; modus-bench dormant since 07-07 after converting 11/12 + 3 proposals into doctrine; AMENDMENTS silent during both of August's biggest bursts, by its own admission.
- **Biggest gap:** the belief that *a lesson captured is a lesson armed*. Prose antibodies are nearly free for an LLM fleet; armed ones cost review, rulings and CI — so the loop over-produces the former and lets the latter age (Google's data: open P1 action items age like P3s without closure mechanics). And the loop's own instruments keep catching the diseases they catalog (unarmed sentinel W120, formatter-corrupted records W112, scar-number collision W128).
- **Top-3 moves:** (1) **R1** — PENDING-ARMS burndown ratchet + monthly FixIt sweep + schema-self-testing digest sentinel; (2) **R2** — antidote-liveness lint (a cure a scar names must still exist AND still be wired into a workflow); (3) **R4** — replay drills: Google's Wheel-of-Misfortune converted into weekly executable fixtures per superscar family, mutation-verified, free at LLM economics.
- Measure the loop by **armed-antibodies-per-week**, never scars-per-week; prose is exhaust, gates are the product.

## 1. How Nuzantara does it today

The loop is a five-layer pipeline. Every layer exists on disk and was verified in this session.

**Layer 1 — Capture (trauma → structured record).**
- `/scar` (`.claude/commands/scar.md`, 61 lines): append-only structured entry — TRAUMA / ANTIBODY / GOTCHA / Reference — into `.claude/rules/cicatrix-scars.md`, severity-validated (P0…INFO), audited to `~/.claude/state/scar-audit.log`, **no auto-commit**. Live CLI twin at `~/.claude/scripts/scar` + `scripts/scar_query.py` for retrieval (`scar query`, `--list`, `--family N`).
- Memory bodies: 1,709 files in `$MEM` (measured `ls | wc -l`), typed by filename prefix: 546 discovery, 265 lesson/lessons, 161 decision, 83 project, 80 ops, 73 reference, 57 feedback, 39 fact. One fact per file, YAML frontmatter, `[[wikilinks]]`.
- `AMENDMENTS.md` (`.claude/skills/modus/`, 52,984 B): the **loop's own scar file** — one line per *procedural* misfire (`date | what misfired | evidence | proposed change`), 34 entries, explicitly "separate from cicatrix (which records organism traumas)". Consumed by `modus-bench` and the operator; "modus never merges changes to itself" (Legge 5).
- Retro documents with **adversarial review of the retro itself**: `2026-08-26-retro-fleet-sessions-25-26.md` (51 KB, three-seat verdict on "22 tricks", blind seats, owner-decisions section) and `2026-08-27-retro-corrections-after-dispatch.md` (`adversarial_review: kimi-k3` in frontmatter; its own §"Corrections after dispatch" retracts a dispatched claim that was working-tree noise, not a repo defect).

**Layer 2 — Consolidation (records → compressed, injected knowledge).**
- Scar corpus: `cicatrix-scars.md` (296,243 B, 72 entries) + `cicatrix-scars-archive.md` (396,609 B, 92 entries) = **164 total entries**; neither is context-loaded.
- The bridge: `cicatrix-superscar.md` (13,986 B) — 10 superscar *families* (HOME-fork, Esiste≠Armato, guard-over-match, secret-in-clear, sibling-race, phantom-citation, KeepAlive-misconfig, network-flap, state-schema drift, active-active) + orphans. Per family: MALATTIA / SEGNALE-PRECOCE / ANTIDOTO (with `→ ESEGUIBILE:` pointing at a script) / MEMBRI (3-8-word pointers). This file IS injected into every session and every subagent.
- Memory index: `$MEM/MEMORY.md` — 18,304 B against a **RULING Zero 14/8 target of ~17 KB**; "Ordine = priorità: il taglio cade in FONDO" (priority-ordered; the cut falls at the bottom); explicit exit criteria (a line leaves only when superseded — auto-loaded elsewhere, or closed with a live cure). Overflow bodies: `MEMORY_METHOD_LESSONS.md` (31.8 KB), `MEMORY_VERIFICATION_RULES.md` (76.6 KB, header: "39 regole + lezioni di prova, agg. 2026-08-26" — the lane brief said 43; the file says 39; the file wins), `MEMORY_SHELL_CLI_TRAPS.md` (21 traps, per the index), `MEMORY_ARCHIVE.md` (111.6 KB). `/mem-trim` exists because the harness silently truncates the index at ~24,440 B (issue #40614) — the trim command measures, proposes a move-plan, and edits only on operator `y`.

**Layer 3 — Enforcement (antibody → executable, CI-armed).**
- Named per-family antidotes, all verified on disk: `scripts/lint_home_fork.py` (#1), `scripts/pending_arms_report.py` (#2), `infra/guard-conformance/{registry.json,check_guard_conformance.py}` (#3: a guard censused without guilt+innocence tests fails CI), `scripts/secrets_permissions_audit.py` (#4), `scripts/lint_plist_keepalive.py` (#7), `scripts/branch_graveyard_cleanup.sh::content_on_main()` (#9), `scripts/lint_scar_number_collision.py` (W128), `scripts/lint_retracted_claims.py` + `infra/retracted-claims/registry.json` (W113 — marker-based claim retraction).
- Guards on the corpus itself: `scripts/tests/test_superscar_budget.py` (≤14,000 B byte-budget + every W-token must resolve to a real body heading — written after the bridge silently relapsed to 73,854 B while claiming "~2k token" in its own header); `.github/workflows/check-cicatrix-scar-pointers.yml` (pointer integrity: a 2026-08-09 parse found **27 of 40** `→ dettaglio:` claims false); `infra/launchagents/cicatrix_autoarchive.sh` (now DORMANT by decision 2026-06-16 — the magazzino is not context-loaded, so its 40 KB limit was raised to 10 MB); `infra/scar-gates/` (MANIFEST.json + 2 executable scar-replay tests: W82 under-match, HOME-fork live-vs-tracked).
- `.github/workflows/p7-lesson-harvester.yml`: the LEARN shadow harvester — derives rule *proposals* from the scar ledger, idempotent, kill-switched, gated G1-G4 (objective-anchor, no-enforcement, reversibility, recurrence-threshold). **Shadow-mode by design**: it proposes, never applies; promotion needs a human gate.

**Layer 4 — Loop self-refinement.**
`modus SKILL.md §SELF-REFINEMENT` (line 232): per-run misfire capture → `infra/workflows/modus-bench.js` — a two-lane sweep (INTERNAL: "which recent traumas would the current loop NOT have prevented?" × EXTERNAL: frontier watch), every proposal faces an adversarial refuter on fresh context, survivors become an operator-gated AMENDMENTS block. Honest terminology in the file: self-*healing/refinement*, "NOT recursive self-improvement of a model". Conversion is real but sparse: bench 2026-07-02 → **11/12 amendments applied** with Zero's GO, one atomic commit each (`git log`: #1952); bench 2026-07-07 → 3 more (#2099). No bench run since — the ~26 later SKILL.md commits are ruling- and arsenal-driven, not bench-driven.

**Layer 5 — Distillation to doctrine (and its failure mode).**
Corrections graduate: scar → superscar MEMBRI line → CLAUDE.md/skill text. The known failure mode is **doctrine drift**: `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` is *three per-machine copies with no comparing lint* ($MEM/discovery_the_global_claude_md_is_a_home_fork_three_copies_three_answers_2026_08_23.md). On 2026-08-23 the three copies gave three different Claude-seat counts (QUAD / TRE / reality: SIX); "a correction applied to one copy becomes a third version, not a cure". The seat block is now byte-identical by hand (sha256-prefix-verified) but the structural antidote is **still absent** — no organ compares the three copies.

**The economics that shape all five layers** (token-ceremony audit, `research/operations/2026-08-21-token-ceremony-ci-system-audit.md`): boot tax ≈ 42K tokens per session *and per subagent*; the superscar bridge alone was ~19K tokens (70,970 B) before the L7 "prefix diet" cut it to ≤14 KB with a CI budget. Compression is not cosmetic here — it is quota, context-room, and fidelity at once.

**Adjacent but stale:** `docs/EXPERIENCE_LIBRARY_OPS.md` / `docs/SKILL_REGISTRY_OPS.md` (trajectory + skill stores in the `cell_core.genome` SQLite, tiered promotion, propose-only jobs) date to 2026-04-15/16 and belong to the Mata Garuda layer whose learning loops were separately measured as "green but empty" (W74/F18: evoskill proposes 0 by construction; W81: WR3 reflexion stub `sys.exit(0)` green every Sunday). The *live* skill-learning path today is the curated `.claude/skills/` corner system + `skill-catalog` (on-demand MOS lookup to avoid the "orchestration-decay 8→0" context bloat), not the SQLite germline.

**The five that matter most.** (1) **Lunney et al. [3]** is the single most relevant document in this survey: Google's measured finding that *capture excellence does not produce closure* — open P1s age like open P3s, and the created-vs-closed curve widens into "postmortem technical debt" — is a photograph of PENDING-ARMS (280 overdue of 441). Their cures (closure SLO, burndown visibility in regular reporting, FixIt weeks, executive attention) are all mechanical and all absent here. (2) **NASA LLIS [6][7]** is the negative control: a lessons system whose *storage* worked and whose *consumption* was voluntary died of disuse in the world's most safety-critical engineering org. Every design choice in Nuzantara's loop that pushes lessons into the worker's context (bridge injection, SessionStart digests, corner skills) is the inverse of LLIS, and the 27/40 pointer-rot finding shows what happens the moment a piece of the chain becomes pull-based and unverified. (3) **ASRS [5]** explains by contrast why capture is this organism's strong suit: ASRS spends enormous institutional machinery (immunity, anonymization, neutral custodian) buying what LLM agents give for free — candid, immediate, shame-free incident reports. The scarce resource here was never candor; it is *arming*. (4) **Argote [8]** puts a number on decay: 3-6%/month even in going concerns. A 164-entry corpus without revalidation cadence should be *expected* to rot at that order — and the measured artifacts (stale pointers, three-way doctrine fork, "cicatrices age fast" in W71) confirm it. (5) **Karpathy [11] + Reflexion/ExpeL [9][13]** establish that the research frontier is converging on exactly this architecture — text-space learning with promotion gates — which means the organism's loop is not a curiosity: it is an early industrial implementation of a paradigm the field has named but not yet built at this completeness, and the parts it has built (CI-guarded budgets, retraction lints, guilt+innocence conformance) are publishable contributions, not just internal hygiene.

## 5. Beyond-SOTA recommendations (ranked by impact × confidence / cost)

**R1 — Antibody-debt burndown: make PENDING-ARMS closure a governed flow, and make its sentinel self-testing.**
*What:* (a) weekly SessionStart digest line naming the 10 oldest TECH-DEBT rows (the reporter already computes `counts.tech_debt_overdue`; W120's cure made the digest read the right key — extend it to name rows); (b) a monthly "FixIt sweep" mandate template (Google's FixIt week, agent-shaped): take the 10 oldest rows, each is *armed or formally retired with a written reason* — silence is not an outcome; (c) a CI trend ratchet on the ledger: overdue count may not exceed last week's snapshot without an explicit override line in the PR (same monotone-registry pattern as tg-gateway `grandfathered.json`, which already works); (d) schema self-test: the digest's parser asserts against `pending_arms_report.py --json` live output in CI, so a key rename can never silently zero the alarm again (W120's class, closed for good).
*Why beyond SOTA:* Google measured the disease [3] and cures it with human ceremony (VP reviews, FixIt weeks); no surveyed system makes the *debt curve itself* a CI-enforced monotone ratchet, and none self-tests the sentinel against the reporter's schema. This composes two pieces the organism already has (monotone-registry lint + ledger reporter) into a closure engine.
*Cost:* ~2 sessions, Gear 2. *Risk:* #3 over-match (ratchet blocking innocent PRs — the W109b two-reducers trap; cure: override line + compare against origin/main only); #2 (the ratchet itself must have guilt+innocence tests).
*Metric:* overdue TECH-DEBT 280 → <100 in 90 days; armed-antidote share of live scars 61% → 80%. Measured by `pending_arms_report.py --json | jq .counts` weekly, committed as snapshot.
*Kill criterion:* if two consecutive FixIt sweeps retire more rows than they arm (the ledger was aspirational, not debt), stop the ratchet and re-scope what enters the ledger instead.
*First PR:* `feat(ledger): pending-arms overdue ratchet + digest row-naming + reporter schema self-test` — `scripts/pending_arms_report.py`, `scripts/tests/test_pending_arms_ratchet.py`, one workflow step; ≤300 lines.

**R2 — Antidote-liveness lint: verify that every cure a scar names still exists and is still wired.**
*What:* a lint that walks live-scar ANTIBODY sections, extracts named artifacts (`scripts/…`, `test_…`, workflow names), and fails when (a) the file no longer exists, or (b) a named test/workflow is reachable from no `.github/workflows/*` trigger (armed-reference check, reusing the W108 lesson that a guard whose only executor is `continue-on-error` is theater). Superscar `→ ESEGUIBILE:` lines are checked the same way. Run weekly + on scar-file PRs (path-scoped), observing-first like `check-cicatrix-scar-pointers.yml`.
*Why beyond SOTA:* Argote says knowledge decays 3-6%/month [8]; industry freshness-checking stops at docs (link checkers). Nobody verifies that a lesson's *countermeasure* is still wired. This is W78's unlearning proposal made executable at the reference layer, and it generalizes the pointer-integrity gate from headings to cures.
*Cost:* 1-2 sessions, Gear 2. *Risk:* #3 under-match is guaranteed at first (antidotes named in prose forms the regex won't see — exactly the `lesson_a_prescribed_antidote_can_itself_be_the_under_match` trap); declare the blind spot in the lint header and measure coverage instead of claiming it.
*Metric:* % of live-scar antidote references verified-armed (baseline: unknown → measure in PR 1; target ≥95% sustained); pointer-rot recurrence 0.
*Kill criterion:* false-positive rate >20% after two weeks of observing mode → redesign the extractor before arming.
*First PR:* `feat(cicatrix): antidote-liveness lint (observing)` — `scripts/lint_scar_antidote_liveness.py` + tests + workflow; ≤350 lines.

## 8. §Meta-pattern

One defective belief generates nearly every finding in this lane: **"a lesson captured is a lesson armed."** The organism writes world-class antibody *prose* because writing is nearly free for an LLM fleet — and systematically under-produces *armed* antibodies because arming costs review, rulings, and CI plumbing. Every August wound reduces to this asymmetry: 280 overdue armings; the bench designed to sharpen the loop dormant 7 weeks; the misfire log twice silent during the loudest bursts; 27/40 pointers false; the bridge saturated to 14 bytes; three doctrine copies divergent for a month; the overdue-alarm itself never having fired. Google named the same disease from the human side — "the most common shortcoming is lack of follow-up" — and cured it with ceremony; here the cure must be mechanical, because the whole point of this organism is that no human reviews the flow.

The second-order pattern: **the loop's own instruments obey the scar families they catalog.** The sentinel of the ledger was unarmed (#2, W120); the formatter rewrote the scar records (#3, W112); two lanes collided on a scar number (#9-shaped, W128); a prescribed antidote was itself the under-match (#3, 2026-08-28). This is not an indictment — it is the strongest argument for the organism's own invention: guards-on-guards (budget test, pointer gate, collision lint, conformance registry). The beyond-SOTA program in §5 is one sentence: *finish applying the loop to itself* — give the ledger a ratchet, the antidotes a liveness check, the doctrine a probe, the drills a heartbeat, and the capture layer a silence alarm. Measure the loop by armed-antibodies-per-week, never by scars-per-week; prose is exhaust, gates are the product.
