# PT PMA foreign ownership for KBLI 2025 residual codes

> **Standing ruling (Zero, 2026-09-13, verbatim):** «si deve studiare dalle fonti governative
> per la certezza». Nothing below is a client-facing verdict. Facts carry a citation
> (instrument, article/annex row, URL, retrieval date); everything else is labelled INFERENCE.
> No annex row is quoted that was not read off a rendered page of `ps49-2021.pdf` on
> 2026-09-13. No client data is involved.

## 1. The legal chain that decides a residual code

### 1.1 The cascade, in the order the regulation imposes it

`Pasal 2(1)` → closed? / central-government-only? → `Pasal 2(1a)` (as inserted by Perpres
49/2021) → commercial in nature? → `Pasal 3(1)` → which of the four open classes → `Pasal 7`
(general foreign-investor gate) → `Pasal 13` (other sector law survives) → `Pasal 8/9`
(SEZ / portfolio carve-outs) → `Pasal 6(4)–(5)` (grandfathering, treaty rights, M&A continuity).

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 2(1), body text, `ps10-2021.pdf`, fetched 2026-09-13):**

> "Semua Bidang Usaha terbuka bagi kegiatan Penanaman Modal, kecuali Bidang Usaha: a. yang
> dinyatakan tertutup untuk Penanaman Modal; atau b. untuk kegiatan yang hanya dapat
> dilakukan oleh Pemerintah Pusat."

*All Business Fields are open to Investment, except Business Fields: a. declared closed to
Investment; or b. for activities that may only be carried out by the Central Government.*

**Fact (Perpres 49/2021 Pasal I angka 1, new Pasal 2(1a) and 2(2), `ps49-2021.pdf` pp. 1–7):**

> "(1a) Bidang Usaha terbuka sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) adalah Bidang Usaha yang
> bersifat komersial."
> "(2) Bidang Usaha yang dinyatakan tertutup untuk Penanaman Modal sebagaimana dimaksud pada
> ayat (1) huruf a adalah: a. Bidang Usaha yang tidak dapat diusahakan sebagaimana Bidang
> Usaha yang tercantum dalam Pasal 12 Undang-Undang Nomor 25 Tahun 2007 ... sebagaimana telah
> diubah dengan Undang-Undang Nomor 11 Tahun 2020 tentang Cipta Kerja; dan b. Industri Minuman
> Keras Mengandung Alkohol (KBLI 11010), Industri Minuman Mengandung Alkohol: Anggur (KBLI
> 11020), dan Industri Minuman Mengandung Malt (KBLI 11031)."

*(1a) The open Business Fields are Business Fields of a commercial nature. (2) The Business
Fields declared closed are: a. those that cannot be operated per Art. 12 of Law 25/2007 as
amended by Law 11/2020; and b. Hard Liquor Industry (KBLI 11010), Wine Industry (KBLI 11020),
Malt Beverage Industry (KBLI 11031).*

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 2(3)):**

> "Bidang Usaha untuk kegiatan yang hanya dapat dilakukan oleh Pemerintah Pusat ... adalah
> kegiatan yang bersifat pelayanan atau dalam rangka pertahanan dan keamanan yang bersifat
> strategis dan tidak dapat dilakukan atau dikerjasamakan dengan pihak lainnya."

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 3, unamended by Perpres 49/2021):**

> "(1) Bidang Usaha terbuka sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 2 ayat (1), terdiri atas:
> a. Bidang Usaha prioritas; b. Bidang Usaha yang dialokasikan atau kemitraan dengan Koperasi
> dan UMKM; c. Bidang Usaha dengan persyaratan tertentu; dan d. Bidang Usaha yang tidak
> termasuk dalam huruf a, huruf b, dan huruf c.
> (2) Bidang Usaha sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) huruf d dapat diusahakan oleh semua
> Penanam Modal."

*(1) Open Business Fields consist of: a. priority fields; b. fields allocated to or in
partnership with Cooperatives and MSMEs; c. fields with certain requirements; d. fields not
included in a, b or c. (2) The fields under (1)(d) may be operated by all Investors.*

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 7):**

> "(1) Penanam Modal asing hanya dapat melakukan kegiatan usaha pada Usaha Besar dengan nilai
> investasi lebih dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 ... di luar nilai tanah dan bangunan. (2) Penanaman
> Modal asing wajib dalam bentuk perseroan terbatas berdasarkan hukum Indonesia ..."

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 11, read verbatim from the primary text file
`scratchpad/pma/perpres10-2021.txt` lines 440–459, itself the watermarked peraturan.go.id
text):**

> "(2) Perizinan berusaha dan pelaksanaan kegiatan dalam rangka Penanaman Modal untuk Bidang
> Usaha keuangan dan Bidang Usaha perbankan dilaksanakan sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan
> perundang-undangan di bidangnya masing-masing."

*Licensing and conduct of Investment in financial and banking Business Fields follow their own
sectoral legislation.* — this is the article that carries the whole of divisions 64/65/66 out
of the Perpres annex logic and into OJK/insurance law.

**Fact (Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 13):** "semua ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang
mengatur mengenai Bidang Usaha Penanaman Modal tetap berlaku sepanjang tidak bertentangan
dengan ketentuan dalam Peraturan Presiden ini." — sector caps in other statutes survive.

### 1.2 The classes a KBLI 2025 code can fall in, and the foreign-ownership consequence

| # | Class | Legal locus | Foreign-ownership consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Closed** | Pasal 2(1)(a) + 2(2)(a) (UU 25/2007 Pasal 12 as amended) and 2(2)(b) (KBLI 11010, 11020, 11031 named verbatim) | 0%. No PMA, no partnership route. Grandfathered only per Perpres 49/2021 Pasal II angka 1 for licences issued before 25 May 2021. |
| 2 | **Central-government-only** | Pasal 2(1)(b) + 2(3) | 0% and also 0% domestic-private. ~~Not an investment field at all.~~ **Corrected §7.2:** still a *Bidang Usaha*, reserved to the Central Government; activity reservation, not an equity cap. |
| 3 | **Non-commercial** | Pasal 2(1a) (inserted 2021) | Outside the open universe: if the activity is not "bersifat komersial" (~~e.g. `…Pemerintah` school/film codes, most of division 84~~ — withdrawn, §7.3: status is per activity, never by title word or division), no PMA verdict is meaningful. INFERENCE that this is a distinct class — the regulation states the qualifier but does not enumerate the codes it excludes. |
| 4 | **Priority (Lampiran I)** | Pasal 3(1)(a) + Pasal 4(2) | **Not an ownership rule.** Lampiran I grants fiscal/non-fiscal incentives; its columns are `No.` / `BIDANG USAHA` / `KBLI` / `CAKUPAN PRODUK` / `PERSYARATAN` (read off p. 8 of `ps49-2021.pdf`). Ownership still comes from class 5/6/7/8. A `PERSYARATAN` cell can add a condition, so a priority row must still be read. |
| 5 | **UMKM-reserved (Lampiran II, `DIALOKASIKAN` column)** | Pasal 3(1)(b) + Pasal 5(1)(a), criteria in 5(2) | No direct PT PMA operator ("0%" is shorthand; the mechanism is allocation, not a cap). **Corrected §7.1:** Pasal 7(1) restricts a foreign investor to Usaha Besar; the explicit "PMA merupakan usaha besar" is Permen BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(1). An allocated field is reserved to Koperasi/UMKM. Scope is row-scoped, not code-scoped (Pasal 5(5)). |
| 6 | **Partnership-required (Lampiran II, `KEMITRAAN` column)** | Pasal 3(1)(b) + Pasal 5(1)(b) | PMA may enter only in partnership with a Koperasi/UMKM; the annex row and the sector rule set the form. No percentage stated in the body text. |
| 7 | **Open-with-requirements (Lampiran III)** | Pasal 3(1)(c) + Pasal 6(1)(a)–(d) as amended | Four sub-types: (a) domestic-investor-only → 0%; (b) explicit foreign-capital cap → the % printed in the row; (c) special licence; (d) alcohol-control regime, which Pasal 6(3a) enumerates as KBLI 46333, 47221, 47826. **The numeric caps live in the annex rows, never in the body text.** **Corrected §7.7:** Pasal 6(2) puts only (a)–(c) in Lampiran III; (d) is enumerated in the body text, Pasal 6(3a). |
| 8 | **Residual open** | Pasal 3(1)(d) + Pasal 3(2) | "dapat diusahakan oleh semua Penanam Modal" — no cap stated, **no annex of its own**. INFERENCE: up to 100% foreign, conditioned only by Pasal 7 (Rp 10 bn investment value, PT form) and by any surviving sector law under Pasal 13. The Perpres never writes "100%". |

**Structural fact that explains the whole 1,505 problem:** huruf a, b and c each have an annex
cross-referenced in Pasal 4(2), 5(4) and 6(2). Huruf d has none. The residual class is defined
**by exclusion** — it is the class the government deliberately did not enumerate. A code is
residual only if it is proven absent from Lampiran I, II and III *and* from the closed list.
Absence cannot be asserted from an annex that has not been read.

**Overlays that survive the class (all fact, all body text):** Pasal 8(1) — Lampiran III
requirements do not apply inside a KEK; Pasal 8(2) — tech start-ups in a KEK may invest at or
below Rp 10 bn; Pasal 9 — classes (b) and (c) do not apply to portfolio investment through the
domestic capital market; Pasal 6(4) — a cap does not bite on investment already approved, nor
on a treaty-privileged investor, unless the new rule is more favourable; Pasal 6(5) — in an
M&A the cap on the licence travels with the deal.

**Current investment/capital figures (INFERENCE-GRADE, secondary-corroborated only):** PP
28/2025 Pasal 212(2) restates >Rp 10 bn per 5-digit KBLI per location (per 4-digit for
construction, Pasal 212(3)(c)); Permen BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(9)–(10) lowers *paid-up* capital
to Rp 2.5 bn with a 12-month lock-up (Pasal 27). **Neither primary PDF was opened** — the
article numbers come from four independent Indonesian law-firm sources (smartlegal.id,
prolegal.id, bplawyers.co.id, et-consultant.com, all retrieved 2026-09-13). Do not publish
these article numbers as verified.

## 2. The method for per-code adjudication

Five steps, in this order. A tuple is "located" only when every step that applies has produced
an evidence record.

**Step 0 (blocking, one-off, not done).** Extract Lampiran I/II/III of Perpres 49/2021 into a
machine-readable table. `ps49-2021.pdf` pp. 8–102 are rastered scans with no text layer;
`pdftotext` returns page headers only; a 60-dpi Tesseract pass produced nothing usable and was
discarded rather than trusted. Every annex row quoted in this study was read off a rendered
page image. Until this table exists, **no code can be proven residual.**

**Step 1 — KBLI 2020 → 2025 concordance.**
*Source:* per-record `bps_2020_ancestors` in the dataset (`codes`, `sebagian`, and a real
`source_locator` = {lampiran, pdf_page, printed_page} into the BPS concordance annex), plus the
KBLI 2020 master (BPS Peraturan 2/2020) for the *scope text* of the ancestor code.
*Why it is first:* the annexes were signed on 25 May 2021 and name KBLI codes of that era; the
dataset is KBLI 2025. **Measured 2026-09-13: `status_mapping` is `MATCH_LANGSUNG` for 920 of
1,559 records, `CODICE_RINUMERATO` for 262, `MATCH_CON_AGGREGAZIONE` for 202, `BPS_ONLY` for
174, null for 1.** So a plain number-join between an annex row and a 2025 code is invalid for
638 codes (41%). **Also measured: `adjudication_status` is `mechanical-only` and
`inheritance_verdict` is `not-adjudicated` for 1,559 of 1,559 records — every code in the
catalogue, including the 54 already-located ones.**
*Evidence a tuple must carry:* ancestor code list, `sebagian` flags, BPS locator
(lampiran/pdf_page/printed_page), and a human/LLM verdict that the 2025 scope text is
co-extensive / narrower / broader than the ancestor's.

**Step 2 — Annex-row match (Lampiran I, II, III).**
*Source:* Perpres 49/2021 annexes, `https://peraturan.go.id/files/ps49-2021.pdf`, pp. 8–102,
retrieved 2026-09-13. Column shapes confirmed by direct read: Lampiran I `No.|BIDANG
USAHA|KBLI|CAKUPAN PRODUK|PERSYARATAN` (p. 8); Lampiran II `No.|BIDANG USAHA|KBLI|DIALOKASIKAN
UNTUK KOPERASI DAN UMKM|KEMITRAAN|SEKTOR` (pp. 77, 85); Lampiran III `No.|BIDANG
USAHA|KBLI|PERSYARATAN` (p. 102).
*Match rule:* match on the `BIDANG USAHA` text **and** the KBLI number, never the number alone
(Pasal 5(5) and Pasal 6(3): where a KBLI covers more than one business field, the restriction
binds only the field named in the annex's `BIDANG USAHA` column).
*Evidence:* annex number, page (pdf + printed), row number, the `BIDANG USAHA` cell verbatim,
the `PERSYARATAN`/`DIALOKASIKAN`/`KEMITRAAN` cell verbatim, retrieval date.

**Step 3 — Closed-list match.**
*Source:* Perpres 49/2021 Pasal 2(2)(b) (three alcohol codes, article text — already usable)
and UU 25/2007 Pasal 12 as amended by UU 11/2020 (**not fetched**; the list of six activities
commonly recited from memory is deliberately not reproduced here).
*Evidence:* article + the KBLI number as printed in the article, or the UU 25/2007 item.

**Step 4 — Sector-law overlay (Pasal 13 + Pasal 11(2)).**
*Source per sector:* press — UU 40/1999 Pasal 11 (verbatim: "Penambahan modal asing pada
perusahaan pers dibatasi agar tidak mencapai saham mayoritas...", retrieved 2026-09-13 from a
non-government mirror, cyrilla.org); construction — UU 2/2017 Pasal 32–33 as amended by UU
6/2023 (secondary-cited, primary not fetched); shipping/cabotage — UU 17/2008 Pasal 8
(secondary-cited); finance/banking/insurance — Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 11(2) plus the sector
statute (the dataset's own 6 insurance codes cite PP 14/2018 Pasal 5(1) jo. PP 3/2020 Pasal I
angka 1, 80% cap); education, health, retail — **not resolved, no source fetched**.
*Evidence:* statute, article, the operative sentence verbatim, URL, retrieval date, and whether
the source is the government text or a mirror.

**Step 5 — Minimum-investment / vehicle note.**
*Source:* Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 7(1)–(2) (verified primary); PP 28/2025 Pasal 212 and Permen
BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(9)–(10) (**secondary only**); Pasal 8(2) for the KEK start-up exception.
*Evidence:* the figure, the instrument, whether primary or corroborated-secondary, retrieval
date. Never merge the Rp 10 bn *investment value* with the Rp 2.5 bn *paid-up capital* — they
are different quantities under different instruments.

**Tuple schema for a located verdict** (superset of the dataset's current `pma_*` fields, which
carry no URL and no retrieval date for the regulation — measured gap):
`source_id` (instrument + LN/BN number) · `article_or_annex_row` (Pasal x ayat y / Lampiran N
row R page P) · `verbatim_id` (the Indonesian cell/sentence) · `url` · `retrieval_date` ·
`concordance_verdict` (from step 1) · `overlay` (step 4) · `investment_note` (step 5) ·
`grader_seat` (the second, cross-family reader) · `confidence`.

## 5. Effort to adjudicate all 1,505 — and the risk of not doing it

### 5.1 Shape of the work

**Phase 0 — annex extraction (blocking, ~1–2 days, one seat + one cross-family grader).**
Render `ps49-2021.pdf` pp. 8–102 at 300 dpi and read each page with a vision-capable seat into
a typed row table (`annex, page_pdf, page_printed, row_no, bidang_usaha, kbli, cells…`). Every
row double-read by a second seat of a different model family, because a fabricated annex row is
the worst failure mode available here and a single reader cannot detect its own fabrication.
Rows that disagree between readers go to a third read, not to a merge.
Also in phase 0: fetch the KBLI 2020 master (BPS Peraturan 2/2020) and UU 25/2007 Pasal 12 as
amended. Without these, phases 1–2 cannot terminate.

**Phase 1 — mechanical join (hours, scripted, 1,505 codes).** For each residual code: candidate
annex rows by (a) 2025 number, (b) every `bps_2020_ancestors` number, (c) fuzzy `BIDANG USAHA`
title match against `judul` + `uraian`. Output is a *candidate set*, never a verdict. Expected
outcome: the large majority of codes get an empty candidate set on all three channels, which is
the first real evidence of residual status.

**Phase 2 — judged adjudication (the actual cost).** A code needs human/LLM judgment when any of
these holds: non-direct concordance (638 codes measured), `sebagian=true` on any ancestor, a
non-empty candidate set from phase 1, or a sector-law division (42, 46/47 alcohol and pharma,
50–53, 58–61, 64–66, 85–87). Estimate **700–900 codes** needing a judged verdict, at ~3 minutes
each for the generator plus a cross-family grader pass ≈ **35–45 seat-hours**, parallelisable
over ~6 lanes → **1 week of background cron**, or 3–4 focused days. The remaining ~600–800
codes clear mechanically with a 10% audited sample.

**Phase 3 — write-back and guard.** Populate the full tuple (§2) per code; add a test that fails
if any code carries `pma_verification_status: "located"` without a URL + retrieval date +
annex/article locator, and a second test that fails if a published cap disagrees with the
extracted annex table. Without phase 3 the work decays on the next dataset refresh.

**Cross-family second seat is not optional.** Generator ≠ grader applies per the repo contract;
here it is also the only defence against a plausible-looking fabricated annex row, which is
exactly the failure class the current 54-code set cannot rule out for itself (those 54 rest on
the same unadjudicated concordance).

### 5.2 Risk of not doing it

- **Commercial.** 1,505 of 1,559 codes (96.5%) answer `NOT_VERIFIED` — including the single
  most-asked code class in the business. Verified 2026-09-13 via the in-house KBLI lookup for
  56101 (restoran): `{"status":"NOT_VERIFIED", "max_foreign_ownership_percent":null,
  "official_basis":null}`.
- **Latent publication risk.** The raw dataset still asserts a regime for every code:
  `pma_status` is TERBUKA for 1,447, TERTUTUP for 60, TERBATAS for 52, and `pma_max_asing` is
  100 for codes whose 2021 ancestors are closed or capped — e.g. **11030** (heir of the closed
  `11031`) holds TERBUKA/100; **90200** (heir of `90011`, an annex row reading "Modal dalam
  negeri 100%") holds TERBUKA/100; **58120, 58130, 60102, 60202, 46333** all hold TERBUKA/100.
  The English `intel_2026` prose on 58120 reads "Nationally, this activity is 100% open for
  foreign ownership (PMA)".
- **What currently prevents that from shipping** (verified on disk 2026-09-13, and it is
  genuinely holding): `apps/mouth/src/lib/kbli-pma-disclosure.ts` refuses to pass raw PMA fields
  unless provenance is `located`; `apps/mouth/src/lib/kbli-pma-editorial.ts` withholds the whole
  `intel_2026`/gold editorial block atomically on the same condition;
  `apps/mouth/src/lib/kbli-llms-corpus.ts` publishes the literal `NOT_VERIFIED`;
  `apps/backend-rag/backend/services/kbli_eye.py` and the two Qdrant sync scripts apply the same
  gate before embedding. So the honest answer is enforced in five places — **and the entire
  exposure is one boolean wide.** Any future "fill the gap mechanically" pass that flips
  `located` from a number-join would publish wrong verdicts for at least the 638 non-direct-match
  codes, with the press, broadcast, alcohol and UMKM-reserved classes among the first to break.
- **Regulatory.** A published foreign-ownership verdict that contradicts a Presidential
  Regulation annex is a consultancy-liability event, not a content bug. The two codes the
  President's own article text closes by number (11010, 11020) and the third whose heir is
  11030 are today shown to the client as "not verified" rather than "closed" — the conservative
  failure, but still a wrong answer that Bali Zero can fix tonight from article text alone.

### 7.1 Class 5: "Pasal 7(1) makes every foreign investor an Usaha Besar". **Objection HOLDS, citation corrected.**

The Pasal 7(1) text already quoted in `B-oss.md` restricts where a foreign investor may operate.
It does not define what a PMA is: *"Penanam Modal asing hanya dapat melakukan kegiatan usaha
pada Usaha Besar dengan nilai investasi lebih dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 ..."* (*a foreign
investor may only carry on business as a Large Enterprise, with investment value above Rp 10
bn*). The express classification sits in the implementing regulation, which this pass read in
its primary text:

**Fact (Permen Investasi dan Hilirisasi/BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(1), fetched 2026-09-14):**
> "Badan usaha sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 23 ayat (9) dan ayat (10) yang dikategorikan
> PMA merupakan usaha besar dan wajib mengikuti ketentuan minimum nilai investasi, kecuali
> ditentukan lain berdasarkan peraturan perundang-undangan."

*A business entity categorised as PMA is a large enterprise and must meet the minimum
investment-value rules, unless legislation provides otherwise.*

**Corrected chain:** Perpres 10/2021 Pasal 3(1)(b) + 5(1)(a) + 5(4) (allocation to Koperasi/UMKM)
→ Pasal 7(1) (a foreign investor may operate only as an Usaha Besar) → Permen BKPM 5/2025 Pasal
26(1) (a PMA *is* an usaha besar). **Wording corrected too:** "0%" is shorthand. The legal
mechanism is that the field is **allocated** to Koperasi/UMKM. It is not a foreign-equity cap.
A new PT PMA cannot be the licensed operator of the row's scope, and Pasal 5(5) limits that to
the row's `BIDANG USAHA` text.

**Side effect, upgrading §1.2 and step 5 from secondary to primary.** The same PDF gives these
figures in primary text. **Pasal 26(2):** more than Rp 10 bn excluding land and buildings, "per
bidang usaha KBLI 5 (lima) digit per lokasi proyek". **Pasal 26(3)(c):** "jasa konstruksi ...
per 4 (empat) digit awal KBLI". **Pasal 26(10):** "modal ditempatkan/disetor paling sedikit
Rp2.500.000.000,00 ... per perseroan terbatas". **Pasal 27(1):** paid-up capital "tidak dapat
dipindahkan dari rekening badan usaha untuk waktu paling singkat 12 (dua belas) bulan" (*may not
leave the company account for at least 12 months*). The 4-digit construction rule is therefore
**primary-located in Permen BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(3)(c)**. The PP 28/2025 Pasal 212 numbers
remain secondary-only; that PDF was still not opened.
