# Bali PT PMA — Low-Risk KBLI Block + Virtual Office Ban (verified + resolved 2026-06-09)

## TL;DR

As of **13 May 2026**, the OSS RBA system **blocks ALL Low-Risk AND Medium-Low-Risk KBLI** for any PT PMA
with a **Bali Province address** — island-wide, permanent, on Governor's letter **No. B.27.000/642/PM/DPMPTSP**.
**The block is class-wide, NOT limited to the 7/9 named codes** (resolved below). Separately, **virtual offices
are now BANNED** as PT PMA registered domicile in Bali. Only **Medium-High and High-risk** KBLI remain registrable
for PMA in Bali, and they require a **real, verifiable commercial physical address**.

This contradicts the national-level status (Perpres 10/2021), which stays open. National openness ≠ Bali registrability.

## RESOLUTION — it is the WHOLE CLASS (Reading B), confirmed by direct OSS testing

- **TraceWorthy** (own OSS testing across multiple codes): "OSS blocks ALL low-risk and medium-low risk KBLI for
  PT PMAs registering a Bali Province address... REGARDLESS of whether the code appears among the nine named.
  The ENTIRE low-risk and medium-low risk tier is subject to the block. No specific code is named. The restriction
  operates at the risk classification level."
- **Emerhub**: "The block applies to EVERY KBLI code in those two risk tiers... goes beyond these nine codes."
- OSS rejection text: "Pengajuan Tidak Dapat Dilanjutkan" / "kegiatan usaha dengan tingkat risiko Rendah dan
  Menengah Rendah tidak dapat dilanjutkan sesuai kebijakan yang berlaku."
- Earlier divergence (list-of-7 vs whole-class) is now closed in favor of whole-class.

## VIRTUAL OFFICE — banned for PT PMA in Bali

Same Governor letter (28 Jan 2026) + earlier (10 Dec 2025) requested a ban on virtual offices as PT PMA domicile
in Bali. Enforced in OSS since mid-May 2026. Real verifiable COMMERCIAL address required; co-working/serviced
office acceptable; DPMPTSP + immigration conduct site visits; substance-based field inspection from June 2026.
**Even a correctly commercial-zoned virtual office is at risk** — the ban targets virtual-office-as-domicile per se.

## CASE 2 — wholesale jewelry (KBLI 46494) + virtual office at Jl. Raya Anyar 2

- **KBLI 46494 — Perdagangan Besar Perhiasan dan Jam** = **RISIKO RENDAH** (official OSS table, NIB Otomatis),
  NOT "menengah rendah" as the client assumed. Either way it is INSIDE the blocked band.
- **TERBUKA 100% nationally** (Perpres 10/2021 — not on closed/conditional/SME/partnership schedules).
- **VERDICT: a wholesale-jewelry PMA at a Bali address is BLOCKED** (low-risk class) **AND** the virtual office at
  Jl. Raya Anyar 2 is independently **banned** for PMA domicile in Bali. DOUBLE block. The zoning being commercial
  (confirmed by client) is necessary but NOT sufficient — virtual-office-as-domicile is itself refused.
- Wholesale extra nuances (apply even outside Bali): "submit business activity reports to Minister of Trade";
  a **PT PMA Distributor is a PRINCIPAL SUPPLIER and must appoint a PMDN (local) company** as the actual
  distributor — STP Distributor/Agen sits on the appointed PMDN, not the PMA (MoT 24/2021 Art.4; HHR Lawyers;
  Lita Paromita Siregar). PMA wholesalers **cannot do direct retail (47xxx)**.
