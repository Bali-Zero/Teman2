## Canary configuration

### Publishing volume — intentionally conservative

**1 Instagram post per day.** No LinkedIn, no TikTok, no other channels
automated yet. All other publisher kill-switches remain `false` as shipped
in `09_wr2_weights.json::publisher_enabled_by_channel` (all `false`).

Rationale: the `09_wr2_weights.json::channel_priority_top3` says
`[instagram, linkedin, newsletter]`, but LinkedIn is a new channel (0
followers) and newsletter requires Brevo activation (dormant account
discovered Day 1). IG is our proven surface with 10,360 followers —
start there, observe, expand.

### Publisher mode — MANUAL only

`wr2_publisher_enabled` remains `false` for all channels during canary.
Publication happens via:

1. WR2 Council drafts the post as usual (reads `08_playbook.md` +
   persona weights from `09_wr2_weights.json`).
2. `canva_renderer` produces the design (PR #171 pipeline, already live).
3. Review Gate Telegram sends photo + Canva edit URL to Zero (PR #171).
4. Zero manually taps **"Approve"** AFTER double-checking in Canva.
5. Publisher executes WITH `force_manual=true` flag (not cron-triggered).

Why manual: 7 days of manual approval let Zero observe predictions vs
reality before trusting auto-publish. No surprise content.

### Content composition — per `09_wr2_weights.json`

**Persona targeting** (60% ID domestic + 40% expat):
- expat_techie_pma 25% → carosello/reel tecnici PMA/visa
- id_founder_pma 20% + id_umkm_digital 20% → ID-language carosello +
  shorts su KBLI/OSS/Coretax
- expat_italian_aire 13% → italiano + AIRE/quadro RW temi
- expat_boomer_retiree 12% → retirement KITAS + healthcare
- id_konsultan_kadin 10% → tecnico KADIN angle

**Objective mix** (leveraged heavily on Lead during canary):
- 4 of 7 posts: Lead objective (carousel 35% + reel_short 25% + long_post 15%)
- 2 of 7 posts: Authority objective (long_post 25% + carousel 20%)
- 1 of 7 posts: Audience objective (reel_short 45% + short_video 20%)

**Hook distribution** — DIRECT response to gap analysis:
- Gap 2 fix: ≥30% contrarian/negative-bias hooks ("Stop doing X",
  "Before you sign the nominee deed", "3 mistakes that kill your KITAS")
- Gap 3 fix: cap question hooks at ≤15%
- Gap 4 fix: target 25% stat hooks with non-rounded numbers
  ("37,881 villas", not "~38k")
- Reserve 15% story hooks (tone_resonance favors rituale for retiree)

**Posting time (WITA, per `cadence_by_channel.instagram`):**
`[07, 12, 18, 19, 21]` — schedule 5 of 7 days at 19:00 (peak), 2 days
at 07:00 (morning commute).

## Daily check-in flow

Every day at 09:00 WITA, Zero receives Telegram digest with:

1. **Yesterday's posted content**: screenshot + T+24h metrics (likes,
   saves, reach, ER)
2. **Predicted vs actual engagement**: per `09_wr2_weights.json`
   prediction range, delta in %
3. **Top comment** surfaced by Claude for review
4. **Next post draft in Canva** (already generated by canva_renderer,
   awaiting review)

Template for the digest body:

```
📊 Bali Zero SOTA — Day N canary

Yesterday's post (T+24h):
• ER actual: 2.8% (predicted range 2.0-3.5%, delta +0.3%)
• Saves: 67 (median target 60) ✅
• Reach: 8,400 (target 7k-10k) ✅
• Top comment: "..." (sentiment: positive)

Today's draft (Canva): https://canva.com/design/.../edit
Hook type: contrarian | Persona: id_founder_pma | Objective: lead
Review & approve → [Inline button]

Pillars status:
  LEAD:       on track (5/45 month progress)
  AUTHORITY:  0/800 LinkedIn (LinkedIn not yet active in canary)
  AUDIENCE:   +12 IG follow since day 1 (+2,500 90d target)
```

## Kill conditions (auto-toggle publisher off)

These trigger automatically once `scripts/m13_weekly_report.py` is wired
(deferred to implementation-plan Task 25). During canary week 1 they're
checked **manually** by Zero at Day 7 checkpoint.

- Any pillar metric drops >20% from baseline → immediate Telegram alert
  + auto-toggle `wr2_publisher_enabled_{channel}=false`
- M13 delta prediction error >50% for 2 consecutive posts → pause WR2,
  Telegram "Predictions diverging, review required"
- 2 consecutive Review Gate rejections from Zero → pause WR2, Telegram
  "Content off-brand, review persona/tone config"
- DeepSeek budget cumulative > $2 (fail-open to Gemini free for red-team)

## Day-7 checkpoint decision

On Canary Day 7, Zero reviews the week and replies in Telegram with
ONE of:

### GO

Enable `wr2_publisher_enabled_instagram=true`, scale to 2 posts/day.
Start manual drafting of LinkedIn posts in parallel (no automation
yet — adds to the canary).

### EXTEND CANARY

Another 7 days of manual-only. Usually because predictions diverged
but content quality was fine.

### PIVOT

Re-trigger Consiglio v1 with extended empirical: add the 7 canary
posts to `01_balizero_corpus.json` + re-run `sota_consiglio_playbook.py
--wave=final`. Keep canary manual for another week.

### KILL

Fall back to pre-SOTA content strategy. Investigate in a post-mortem.
Risk trigger: content received unusually negative response in IG
comments, or Bali Zero brand signals degraded in Ahrefs Brand Radar.

**No decision is auto-made at canary day 7 — Zero must reply GO /
EXTEND / PIVOT / KILL.** Silence = continue canary.

## Success criteria for canary week 1

This is NOT Gate 7 (Gate 7 = Zero's explicit approval). These are the
canary's internal success criteria:

1. **7 posts published** (no missed days)
2. **0 Zero rejections at Review Gate** (0-1 tolerated)
3. **Median ER stays within ±50% of prediction** (wider tolerance Week
   1; narrows to ±30% by Week 4)
4. **No pillar drops >20%** vs baseline
5. **Canva rendering pipeline stable** (no manual retries needed)

If all 5 true → recommend GO. If 3-4 true → recommend EXTEND. Otherwise
recommend PIVOT or KILL.

- ❌ Ad spend (paid amplification) — SOTA target is 90d ORGANIC uplift
- ❌ Competitor re-scraping (wait for Vino's first pass to complete)

These get scheduled into Day 30 / Day 60 / Day 90 checkpoints per the
spec Risk matrix.
