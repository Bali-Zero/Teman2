## C2 — PT PMA CAPITAL: THE TWO THRESHOLDS EVERYONE CONFUSES
domain: legal · tone: analitico+pedagogico · audience: business operator

- **Cover**: "YOUR PT PMA NEEDS TWO NUMBERS — NOT ONE" / sub: "IDR 2.5bn paid-up vs IDR 10bn invested"
- **S2 FACTS VS TAKE**: facts: BKPM 5/2025 Pasal 26(10) + PP 28/2025 Pasal 212(2). Take: post-Omnibus the old "10bn paid-up" figure is stale — mixing the two numbers is the #1 setup error.
- **S3 (rule, bullet=2)**: TWO DISTINCT THRESHOLDS. (1) Modal disetor (paid-up capital): >= IDR 2,500,000,000 per PT. (2) Nilai investasi (total investment): > IDR 10,000,000,000 per 5-digit KBLI per location.
- **S4 (citation)**: Pasal 27(1) BKPM 5/2025 — 12-month lock-up on paid-up capital. Cannot move from company account for >= 12 months (except asset purchase, construction, operations).
- **S5 (consequence)**: gap to reach > IDR 10bn = > 7.5bn, built progressively via capex/opex. Property/hospitality KBLI (68111, 55194): land + buildings COUNT toward the 10bn.
- **S6 (next step)**: LKPM (quarterly investment report) via OSS — missing it is the #1 cause of NIB suspension in 2026.
- **S7 statement-bomb**: "CAPITAL IS NOT A FEE. IT IS A COMMITMENT THE STATE TRACKS QUARTERLY."

## C3 — FOREIGN PROPERTY: THREE LEGAL STRUCTURES, ZERO MYTHS
domain: property · tone: tecnico+analitico · audience: property buyer/investor

- **Cover**: "YOU CANNOT OWN LAND IN BALI. YOU CAN CONTROL IT — THREE WAYS." / sub: "Hak Pakai · HGB · Leasehold"
- **S2 FACTS VS TAKE**: facts: UU 5/1960 (UUPA) — hak milik (freehold) is WNI-only, Pasal 21. Take: "owning" is a marketing lie; control is the real game, and each path has a different price.
- **S3 (bullet=3)**: THREE STRUCTURES. (A) hak pakai — WNA individual, 80 years (30+20+30), KITAS/KITAP, residential only. (B) HGB — via PT PMA, 80 years, commercial OK. (C) leasehold (hak sewa) — passport only, no BPN, contract is the only protection.
- **S4 (citation)**: hak pakai duration verbatim (Permen ATR/BPN 29/2016): 30y initial + 20y extension + 30y renewal = 80y max. Bali value thresholds IDR 2-5bn by regency (PP 28/2025).
- **S5 (consequence)**: inheritance trap — hak pakai heir must hold valid KITAS/KITAP or face forced divestment within 1 year (state auction). HGB via PT PMA: heirs inherit SHARES, no land-title problem.
- **S6 (due diligence)**: before buying — GISTARU zoning (RTRW/RDTR) + KKPR + BPN certificate check. Building in Zona Pertanian (sawah) = forced demolition (Bingin Beach villas demolished 21 Jul 2025).
- **S7 statement-bomb**: "THE 99-YEAR LEASE IS A SLOGAN, NOT A LAW. THE CONTRACT IS YOUR ONLY WALL."

## C4 — CORETAX: THE 2025 SWITCH THAT LOCKS OUT THE UNPREPARED
domain: tax · tone: analitico+pedagogico · audience: business operator

- **Cover**: "DJP ONLINE IS DEAD. CORETAX DECIDES IF YOU CAN FILE." / sub: "PMK 81/2024 — re-registration mandatory"
- **S2 FACTS VS TAKE**: facts: PMK 81/2024 — CoreTax (PSIAP) replaced DJP Online definitively. Take: old credentials no longer work; if you didn't re-register, you cannot file.
- **S3 (rule, bullet=3)**: ONE PLATFORM, THREE MODULES. e-Faktur (PPN/VAT) · e-Bupot (withholding) · SPT + e-Billing. All converge in CoreTax.
- **S4 (consequence — the immigration cross-check)**: CoreTax cross-analyzes immigration visa data in real time to set your SPDN/SPLN status. Wrong classification = automatic penalties.
- **S5 (citation/rule)**: residency test (UU 36/2008 Pasal 2) — WNA is SPDN (resident) if present > 183 days in 12 months OR habitual abode OR intent to settle (KITAS/KITAP). SPDN = taxed on worldwide income.
- **S6 (deadlines)**: PPh 25 monthly advance — 15th of next month. SPT Tahunan Badan (1771) — 30 April (calendar year).
- **S7 statement-bomb**: "CORETAX DOES NOT FORGET. IT READS YOUR VISA STAMPS BEFORE YOU FILE."

## C5 — WHAT REPATRIATING PROFITS ACTUALLY COSTS YOU
domain: tax · tone: analitico+tecnico · audience: investor/business operator

- **Cover**: "MAKING THE PROFIT IS EASY. TAKING IT HOME IS WHERE THEY TAX YOU." / sub: "PPh 26, branch profit tax, treaty rules"
- **S2 FACTS VS TAKE**: facts: UU 36/2008 Pasal 26 — 20% flat withholding on gross to non-residents. Take: the headline 22% corporate rate is not the whole bill — the exit toll is where founders get surprised.
- **S3 (rule, bullet=3)**: WITHHOLDING (PPh 23/26). 15% on domestic dividends/interest/royalties. 2% on technical/management/consulting services. 20% flat on payments to non-residents.
- **S4 (citation — the branch trap)**: BUT (permanent establishment) — Pasal 26(4): 20% ADDITIONAL branch profit tax on after-tax income, UNLESS reinvested in Indonesia. On top of 22% PPh Badan.
- **S5 (consequence)**: treaty relief (P3B) is the only way down from 20% — and PMK 112/2025 (in force 30 Dec 2025) now demands SKD (certificate of domicile) + Beneficial Ownership + LOB + Principal Purpose Test.
- **S6 (next step)**: PT PMA dividends vs branch repatriation — model both before you structure. Treaty benefit requires substance, not just paperwork.
- **S7 statement-bomb**: "THE TAX IS NOT ON EARNING. IT IS ON LEAVING."

## C6 — POSITIVE INVESTMENT LIST: WHAT A FOREIGNER CAN OWN 100%
domain: legal · tone: pedagogico+analitico · audience: business operator/investor

- **Cover**: "OPEN, CAPPED, OR CLOSED — KNOW BEFORE YOU INCORPORATE" / sub: "Perpres 10/2021 — the DNI is dead"
- **S2 FACTS VS TAKE**: facts: Perpres 49/2021 Pasal 2(1) — all business fields open EXCEPT closed or government-reserved. Take: the old Negative List (DNI) is gone; the default is now openness, with sharp exceptions.
- **S3 (rule, bullet=3)**: THREE CATEGORIES. TERBUKA (100% WNA allowed). TERBATAS (foreign cap 49%/67%/70% — needs WNI partner). TERTUTUP (closed to foreigners).
- **S4 (examples TERBUKA)**: KBLI 62010 software · 70201 business consulting · 55194 vila · 68111 real estate. 100% foreign control with repatriation rights.
- **S5 (the trap — TERBATAS)**: advertising (KBLI 73100) is widely mis-cited as TERBUKA. Treat as TERBATAS, local partnership obligation (per BPS 7/2025 + Perpres 14/2024). Verify on the current list, not 2021 text.
- **S6 (consequence)**: PT PMA is by law Usaha Besar (large enterprise) — it CANNOT operate in TERTUTUP fields. Using a nominee to get in = criminal in Bali (Perda 4/2026). No workaround.
- **S7 statement-bomb**: "THE LIST CHANGED. THE PENALTY FOR GUESSING DID NOT."
