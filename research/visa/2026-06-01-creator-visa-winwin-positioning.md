# Creator Visa — the win-win positioning (Indonesia-first)

> The soft-lobbyist spine of the "WHEN AM I ILLEGAL? — CONTENT CREATOR" content
> (carousel slide 10 "THE MISSING ROAD" + video beat 5). Argument register fixed by
> Antonello: **1=soft, 2=truth, 3=constatazione (not an attack on government), 4=dual
> audience (creators + regulatory ecosystem)**. The lobbying is legitimate ONLY because
> it is framed as alignment of interests — Indonesia gains first, the creator second.

## The legal fact it rests on (verified NB-2, 2026-06-01)

The operative variable in Indonesian law is **`source + beneficiary`, NOT `medium of payment`**
(NB-2 verbatim). Cash never enters the offence (UU 6/2011 Pasal 122 — payment is not an element).

| Source of the reward | Correct permit | Nature | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foreign** (brand abroad, AdSense, foreign sponsor) | **E33G** (1yr KITAS) or **C5A** (when live) | accessible, short/medium | Permenkumham 11/2024 Pasal 63(3): "kontrak kerja dengan perusahaan yang berbadan hukum di luar Wilayah Indonesia" |
| **Local** (Bali café/hotel/villa pays cash **OR** gives free stay/product for a post) | **E23 KITAS Kerja** — that business is the sponsor | long-term, tied | imbalan/sejenisnya da korporasi di Indonesia → hubungan kerja + RPTKA + DKP-TKA $100/mo |
| **Several local businesses** (freelance) | **NONE as freelance** → only **own PT PMA + E23** (invoice B2B) | long-term, capital (IDR 2.5bn) | One Sponsor Policy SE 3/836/PK.04/I/2026: 1 TKA = 1 sponsor; no foreign freelancer status |

**Three things the public rarely understands:**
1. **E33G does NOT cover local money** — it bans cash OR barter from any Indonesian entity. A free villa night in Bali is a local service → violates E33G too, not only a tourist visa.
2. **C5A does NOT cover local money either** — even when switched on. Larangan verbatim: *"Menerima imbalan, upah, atau sejenisnya dari perorangan atau korporasi di Indonesia"*. It lets you create + earn from abroad; it bans taking anything from a Bali business.
3. **For local money there is ONE route: E23** (sponsored) **or PT PMA + E23** (multi-client). Both long-term/structural. **No accessible + short-term route to collaborate-and-earn with the local market exists today.** ← the gap.

## THE WIN-WIN — why activating the category pays off (Indonesia-first)

| # | Benefit | For Indonesia 🇮🇩 | For the creator 🌍 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Revenue that is zero today** | Villa-for-Reel barter is 100% off-books: no tax, no fee, no DKP. An accessible category → the State collects fee + PNBP on an activity **that already happens anyway** | Pays little, fully legal |
| 2 | **Free, targeted tourism promotion** | Creators are Bali's marketing. Operasi Dharma Dewata today **deports** the people promoting Bali. A category → your best promoters move from "illegal" to "partner" | Promotes the place they love, no ban risk |
| 3 | **Less undeclared work** | 334 TAK in 5 months: enforcement is costly and catches people who **want** to be legal but have no walkable route. An accessible route → fewer cases, less detention, lower admin cost | Out of the grey line, sleeps at night |
| 4 | **Law catches up to reality** | C5A is **already classified** (May 2025): the State already recognised the category. No new law needed → just "switch on". Political cost ≈ zero | Uses the tool designed for them |
| 5 | **Traceability & safety** | Registered creators = known identities, traceable content. Fewer industrial-scale cases (Bonnie Blue), fewer scams. The State **sees** who operates | Clear status, no grey zone |

## The principle in one line (Indonesia-first)

> The category already exists in law. The activity already exists in reality. Today they
> don't meet — and the first to lose is the State (invisible revenue + costly enforcement),
> then the creator. Activating the bridge is win-win: no one is asking a favour, only to
> **switch on what Indonesia has already written.**
