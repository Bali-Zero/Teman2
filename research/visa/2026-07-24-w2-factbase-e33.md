# Visa Oracle v2 RulePack — FACT-BASE: E33 family (E33, E33A–E33G)

**Lane:** E33 remote-work / special-purpose family · **As-of:** 2026-07-24 (all `imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/{CODE}` pages re-fetched live today unless noted) · **Catalog anchor:** Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025 (signed 2025-05-02, effective 2025-06-01; dictum KEEMPAT revokes M.HH-02.GR.01.04/2023) — family confirmed in the repo's bonified 110-remap (`research/visa/2026-07-17-visa-catalog-bonifica-110-remap.md` rows E33–E33G, all KEEP; E33D/E33G confirmed via direct per-code fetch).

**Shared framework facts (apply to every E33 code below):**

- Visa class: **Visa Tinggal Terbatas (VITAS) → ITAS/KITAS** (limited stay), not visit visa; every page states the permit "dapat diperpanjang dan dapat dialihkan menjadi izin tinggal lainnya" (extendable + convertible to another stay permit).
- Visa validity after issuance: **90 days** to enter Indonesia, else re-apply (all pages, "Ketentuan lain").
- ITAS + MERP (Izin Masuk Kembali) issue automatically at the border on first entry → **MULTIPLE** entry policy for all codes.
- Universal prohibition text (verbatim, all pages): *"Anda dilarang tinggal di wilayah Indonesia melebihi jangka waktu izin tinggal, melakukan pekerjaan yang tidak sesuai dengan izin tinggal, melakukan penjualan barang atau jasa, terkecuali diperlukan dalam pekerjaannya"* — overstay ban; work-inconsistent-with-permit ban; sale-of-goods/services ban with a narrow "except as required in their [permitted] work" carve-out.
- Standard application document set (all codes): passport valid ≥6 months; 3-month bank statement ≥ **USD 2,000** (applicant or penjamin name); recent color photo; CV; travel itinerary. The USD 2,000 is an application-stage proof-of-funds bar, distinct from (and much smaller than) the qualifying thresholds — corpus flag: do not conflate.
- Cumulative stay caps (Permenkumham 22/2023 **Pasal 113**, primary-text verified in corpus `research/visa/2026-07-21-e31j-e33f-kitap-verification.md`): first grant <5y → **6-year** cumulative cap (ayat 1); first grant ≥5y → **10-year** cap (ayat 2). Pasal 185 defines which activities get 5-or-10-year first grants (investment, family reunification, repatriation, second home) — it sets first-grant duration, it is NOT an exemption list.
- Conversion to KITAP/ITAP (UU 6/2011 jo. UU 63/2024, Pasal 54(1) + Pasal 60, corpus `visa-catalog-sweep.jsonl` Q8/Q12): the elderly (lansia) are grouped with clergy/workers/investors — **3 consecutive years** on the qualifying KITAS + Integration Statement. The "5-year" figure on agency sites describes a different track, not the conversion clock.
