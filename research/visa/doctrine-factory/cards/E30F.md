## 3. Epistemic state

**Same class as E30E: NOT a doctrine gap.** Five claims exist, all VERIFIED or VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT:

1. **Category & purpose** (`CL-E30F-01`, VERIFIED) — index for foreign students in a bilateral
   student-exchange program (primary/secondary/tertiary including diploma/bachelor/master/doctorate),
   distinguished from standard E30 and E30A.
2. **Activities** (`CL-E30F-02`, VERIFIED) — permitted: study, domestic travel, personal purchases,
   family visits/sponsorship; part-time work only with separate authorization. Prohibited: full-time
   work, selling goods/services, unauthorized local income, overstaying.
3. **Entry/duration** (`CL-E30F-03`, VERIFIED) — multiple-entry, MERP auto-integrated (UU 63/2024);
   duration tied to the officially-approved exchange-program length, no fixed cap independent of the
   program (documented as "Varies" — the variability itself is the rule, not a missing figure).
   **Asymmetry flagged, not resolved**: unlike `CL-E30E-03`, this claim does not mention an initial
   single-entry VITAS phase — more likely an answer-omission than a genuine legal difference (both
   products go through the same general ITAS pipeline), but this batch never asked the follow-up to
   confirm either way. Carried forward as-is per the originating ledger's own explicit note.
4. **Extension/conversion.** (`CL-E30F-04`, VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT) — renewable in-country while the
   exchange program continues, no fixed maximum renewal count; after ≥3 consecutive years on KITAS,
   KITAP eligibility opens. Carries the SAME primary-law tension as E30E's CF-15 (a direct
   student→KITAP path is not supported by `UU 6/2011`/`PP 31/2013` without an intervening
   `Alih Status`), though this E30F answer surfaces the conflict less explicitly than E30E's did.
   Additionally rests partly on a cross-family citation (a Golden Visa guide) whose relevance to a
   student-exchange KITAP rule is unexplained anywhere in this batch — flagged as weak support, not
   equal-strength evidence.
5. **Sponsor.** (`CL-E30F-05`, VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT) — mandatory local sponsor: an accredited
   Indonesian educational institution participating in the exchange program. No primary-law article is
   actually cited for this specific point — rests on internal operational sources only (corrected in
   the originating ledger from an earlier draft that had wrongly attributed it to the Kepmen).
