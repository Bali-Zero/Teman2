## 3. Epistemic state

**Unlike E33A/B/C, this is NOT a doctrine gap — E30E has the fullest content coverage of any BLOCKED
product in this family.** Five claims exist, all VERIFIED or CONFLICTING (self-flagged), covering
category, activities, entry/duration, extension/conversion, and sponsor:

1. **Category & purpose** (`CL-E30E-01`, VERIFIED) — student index for formal education/training at
   an institution physically located and registered inside an Indonesian Special Economic Zone (KEK).
2. **Activities** (`CL-E30E-02`, VERIFIED) — permitted: KEK-based study, family sponsorship, tourism/
   leisure; part-time work only under a separate subsidiary authorization. Prohibited: overstaying,
   direct retail sales, unauthorized local work/compensation.
3. **Entry/duration** (`CL-E30E-03`, VERIFIED) — initial VITAS single-entry, 90-day validity to enter;
   upon onshore KITAS conversion, MERP auto-integrates (UU 63/2024). Stay duration tied to the
   certified academic/training program length.
4. **Extension/conversion — genuinely CONFLICTING, not a gap.** (`CL-E30E-04`, CONFLICTING) —
   renewable via Kemenimipas + school sponsor, but the primary national law (`UU 6/2011`,
   `PP 31/2013`) does NOT permit direct student→KITAP conversion; students must first `Alih Status` to
   another eligible category (e.g. E28A investor, E31A spouse-sponsored) before any KITAP path opens.
   Operational client-facing material advertises a "Path to KITAP" that the primary law does not
   support directly. Tracked as **CF-15** — flagged as a pattern that may recur across the whole E30
   student family (E30F's card carries a related, less-explicit instance), not resolved here.
5. **Sponsor** (`CL-E30E-05`, VERIFIED) — mandatory local sponsor (*Penjamin*): either the KEK
   educational institution itself or a WNI individual guarantor. Source: `Permenkumham 22/2023`
   Pasal 42.
