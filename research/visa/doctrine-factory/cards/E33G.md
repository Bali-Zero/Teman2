# Product Doctrine Card — E33G (Second Home — Remote Worker)

Refreshed 2026-10-13 against the active signed pack `rulepack-prod-029` (seq-29). The seq-7 text
of this card is in git history; the seq-7 rules `review.e33g.local-market`,
`review.e33g.local-company-ownership` and `el.e33g.income-60k-manual` are not in the pack (section 5).

## 1. Identity

- **Code**: E33G. **Name on the page**: Visa Rumah Kedua Pekerja Jarak Jauh.
- **Pack category**: `LIMITED_STAY`, `covered_purposes: [REMOTE_WORK, TOURISM, FAMILY]`; stay policy
  365 days.
- **Sponsor**: the page says you need no sponsor for this visa.
- **Price**: pricing key `E33G Remote Worker (Offshore)` (category `kitas_permits`), all-inclusive catalog price 13,000,000 IDR; the catalog does not split the fee.

## 2. Active rules (rulepack-prod-029)

E33G has 6 PRODUCTS-scoped rules.

| rule_id | stage | effect / reason_code | what the rule tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hf.e33g.indonesian-employer` | HARD_FILTER | EXCLUDE / `INDONESIAN_EMPLOYER_NOT_ALLOWED` | the employer is an Indonesian entity |

| `hf.e33g.annual-income-below-minimum` | HARD_FILTER | EXCLUDE / `E33G_INCOME_BELOW_THRESHOLD` | purpose REMOTE_WORK and annual foreign income below USD 60,000 (valid from 2026-10-11; unknown inputs: no effect) |

## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33G (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Income (CL-E33G-02)**: the page requires a bank account showing salary or income of at least
  US$60,000 per year. The figure is stated on the page, so the USD 60,000 claim now has a page
  citation. It is still not an article of the regulation.
- **Employment**: an employment agreement with a company established outside Indonesia.
- **Statement of funds**: a bank statement for the last 3 months, in the name of the foreigner or of
  the sponsor, of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent), as proof of living costs in Indonesia.
- **Stay**: 1 year; extension can be done online through evisa.imigrasi.go.id.
- **Activities**: living in Indonesia to carry out tasks for a company abroad; tourism and visiting
  friends or family.
- **Prohibitions on the page**: staying beyond the permit, work that does not fit the permit, and
  selling goods or services unless needed for the work.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.

## 4. Points to read together

- **Payer location (CL-E33G-01, CL-E33G-03, CL-E33G-04)**: the Indonesian-employer, Indonesian-client,
  Indonesian-source-compensation and PT PMA rules cite the Permenkumham 22/2023 jo. 11/2024 source
  record in the pack, not the page. The page does not mention Indonesian clients or company
  ownership. The pack's prohibited activities for E33G list work for an Indonesian entity, Indonesian
  source compensation including in kind, and Indonesian company ownership.
- **Penalty**: the E2b ledger records UU 6/2011 Art. 122(a) and (b) for a violation by the holder
  and by a facilitating sponsor.
- **E23 boundary**: where remote consulting for a foreign client turns into work for a local
  employer is only thinly sourced.

## 5. Retired from earlier packs

`review.e33g.local-market` and `review.e33g.local-company-ownership` (HUMAN_REVIEW rules in seq-7)
are now the two HARD_FILTER rules above. `el.e33g.income-60k-manual` (an ELIGIBILITY rule that never
tested income) is replaced by the USD 60,000 test inside `el.e33g.remote-work` and by
`hf.e33g.annual-income-below-minimum`.
