## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Core eligibility test — payer location, not place of work.** `CL-E33G-01`,
   **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** — permits residing and working physically in Indonesia provided the
   employment/service relationship is with an employer/client NOT domiciled in Indonesia; performing
   the work physically inside Indonesia does not by itself make compensation "Indonesian-sourced" —
   the decisive test is payer/beneficiary location. Caveat: corroborating sources are operational
   guides, not an independent primary-law article pinpoint for this specific test.
2. **Income threshold — claimed, but NOT encoded in the live pack. Real defect found by this
   session's adversarial pass, not a claim-ledger gap.** `CL-E33G-02`, **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** —
   minimum USD 60,000/year documented (foreign employment contract, payslips, PayPal/bank statements)
   per the doctrine claim. **But independently re-verified live this session: the string `60000`
   appears in NEITHER `rulepack-prod-007.source.json` NOR `rulepack-prod-008.source.json`.** The rule
   named for this check, `el.e33g.income-60k-manual`, has a `when` clause that is a byte-for-byte
   duplicate of `el.e33g.remote-work`'s condition (two identical nested `all()` branches, neither
   testing any income fact) — it fires for ANY clean remote worker, regardless of income, and the
   USD 60,000 figure lives ONLY in the rule's name/`reason_code` string, never in a tested predicate.
   An earlier draft of this card's §4 table stated this rule was "SUPPORTED (advisor-check for the
   USD 60k figure)" without disclosing that the figure is unenforced — corrected in §4 below.
3. **Work prohibition scope — the one claim with a real statutory citation.** `CL-E33G-03`,
   **VERIFIED** — any local-sourced compensation, monetary or in-kind, voids eligibility; payment
   channel/currency is irrelevant if payer/beneficiary is Indonesian. Violation is a criminal offense
   under `UU 6/2011 Art. 122(a)` (up to 5 years + Rp 500,000,000 fine); a facilitating Indonesian
   sponsor faces the SAME penalty under Art. 122(b). This is cross-cutting — the same compensation-
   source test governs C5A and visit/D-series visas too.
4. **Remote-work classification edges.** `CL-E33G-04`, **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** — digital-nomad-style
   short remote stints under visit visas remain prohibited if any local payer exists (same test as
   §3.3); E33G is compatible with owning/managing a foreign-registered company remotely, but not with
   drawing a local Indonesian salary; family riders are addressed under E33/E33E, not E33G. Caveat:
   the E23-boundary edge (when remote consulting for a foreign client tips into needing an
   E23/RPTKA because the "client" functions as a local employer) is thinner, PROSE_ONLY sourcing.
5. **Structural note — this is the only product in the family with TWO independent HARD_FILTER
   exclusion tests plus TWO human-review triggers**, per the live pack (§4) — the most heavily-gated
   product in the E30/E31/E33 family, consistent with §3.3's criminal-liability framing (the rule
   density mirrors the legal stakes).

## 6. Disposition

**REACHABLE_AND_SUPPORTED — coverage gate MET** per `coverage-matrix-after-batch3.json`
(`ALL_TOPICS_ANSWERED`). Every one of the 6 rules is claim-backed. The 3 VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT claims
share an honest, well-documented caveat class (operational-guide sourcing, not primary-law-article
pinpoint) rather than an unresolved dispute. **This card does NOT read the coverage gate as "the
income threshold is actually enforced"**: §3.2/§4 document a real engine defect found by this
session's adversarial review — `el.e33g.income-60k-manual` never tests any income fact, so the USD
60,000 figure is currently doctrine-only, not runtime-enforced. Recommend an E5 rule-authoring fix
(add an actual income-fact test to this rule) alongside the pre-existing doctrine gaps in §7.

## Adversarial review

Real cross-family review run this session via `kimi -m kimi-code/k3`, batched with E33E/E33F

   fact; the string `60000` appears in neither `prod-007` nor `prod-008`. Corrected §3.2, the §4

   convention, used across all cards in this family) is not itself supported by the pack JSON —
   `environment: PRODUCTION` is declared, "SHADOW" appears nowhere in the packs directory, and a
   sibling `rulepack-prod-008.source.json` (seq-8, PRODUCTION, `valid_from` one day after seq-7)
   exists. Mitigating: the 13 E33E/F/G rules are byte-identical between seq-7 and seq-8, so every
   threshold/predicate verification in this card holds regardless of which is truly "active." Not
   corrected here (this convention is inherited from the task brief and repeated across the whole
   batch — a single card cannot resolve it), but flagged for whoever finalizes pack-sequence status.
3. **[not contested]** All digest reproducibility, the E33-family claim states, and the rule-count/
   HARD_FILTER+HUMAN_REVIEW structural claims were independently re-verified and confirmed accurate.

Net: 1 high-severity real engine defect surfaced and disclosed (not fixed — out of this card's
scope), 1 low-severity framing caveat noted. Disposition unchanged (still REACHABLE_AND_SUPPORTED per
the coverage gate's own claim-backing criterion), but §6 now states plainly that "coverage gate MET"
