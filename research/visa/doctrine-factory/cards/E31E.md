## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Category & legal purpose.** Foreign minor child (under 18, unmarried) of a foreign parent
   holding ITAS/ITAP status — the child-dependent index in the E31 family. `CL-E31E-01`,
   **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** — pinpoint: `Permenkumham No. 22 Tahun 2023, Pasal 33 ayat (2) lettera h
   angka 5` (`"anak kandung yang belum berusia 18 (delapan belas) tahun dan belum kawin..."`).
2. **Age ≤18 and unmarried.** Both conditions required — the pack's two HARD_FILTER exclusions match
   at the category level, but `hf.e31e-married-excluded`'s actual predicate is `marital_status ≠
   SINGLE`, not literally "married" — it also excludes DIVORCED/WIDOWED applicants, a stricter test
   than "married excludes" implies. Corrected here and in §4's table (independently verified live
   against the pack).
3. **Caveat — CRITICAL, carried forward from batch-1.** `freshness-recheck-2026-08-16.md` record #10
   (`ecd22722-3e42-5808-be18-45fbb7d8e9c5`, the E31E OFFICIAL_PORTAL page) is flagged **CHANGED** and
   is the SOLE `source_ref` for both live HARD_FILTER rules (`hf.e31e-adult-excluded`,
   `hf.e31e-married-excluded`) — the freshness-recheck found the LIVE page contains **no text
   supporting either the under-18 or unmarried requirement it is cited for**. This card does NOT
   independently re-verify the live page in this session — it carries the flag forward per the
   task's freshness-recheck discipline (matching e2a's CF-6 pattern), rather than either asserting
   or dismissing it.
4. **Index-letter question.** Batch-1's CF-9 raised whether the age-≤18 rule belongs to E31B or
