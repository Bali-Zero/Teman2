Task: E3 bulk (LONG-STAY family). E31A is the mixed-marriage spousal index, distinct from E31B
(spouse of an ITAS/ITAP-holding foreigner) — the two are legally different products despite both

## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Category & legal purpose.** Granted to a foreign spouse of an Indonesian citizen (WNI),
   conditioned on a legally registered marriage (KUA/Catatan Sipil certificate) and the Indonesian
   spouse's sponsor status. — `CL-E31A-01`, **VERIFIED** (`Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025`, footnote
   [7]).
2. **Marriage registration.** `family.marriage_registered = true` is a required fact on every pack
   rule for this product — not merely descriptive, it gates SUPPORT directly.
3. **Sponsor nationality.** `family.sponsor_nationalities ∩ {ID}` — the sponsor must be Indonesian;
   this is the structural feature distinguishing E31A from E31B (sponsor's ITAS/ITAP status, not
   citizenship).
4. **Work rights.** E31A carries **broader local work-authorization language** than the
   foreigner-sponsored E31B/E31D line — `CL-E31A-02`, **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** (reported by the
   doctrine card's own comparison table, not independently cross-checked against a primary-law
   pinpoint in this batch — treat the differential as reported, not re-verified). Pack rule
   `el.e31a-spousal-work-article-61` (`SPOUSAL_WORK_ARTICLE_61_CONTEXT`) is the mechanical
   counterpart, but it does not evaluate any work-rights fact — its `when` clause is the same
   purposes/relation/nationality/marriage predicate as the other rules; it only attaches the
   reason_code as a SUPPORT effect. It shares its `source_refs` (`5a0d367b-…`) with
   `el.e31a-kitap-prerequisites` (point 6 below), not with a dedicated Article 61 text — so this is
   labeling, not independent verification of the article. Treat §3's original framing ("distinct
   source_id... consistent with a dedicated citation") as corrected here.
5. **Funds requirement.** `el.e31a-funds-2000`'s `when` clause contains **no funds-typed fact at
   all** — only purposes/relation/nationality/marriage, the same predicate as the category-match
   rule. The USD 2,000 figure exists only in the `rule_id` and `reason_code` strings; the rule fires
   `SUPPORT REQ_FUNDS_2000` unconditionally for any registered-marriage WNI-spouse applicant, without
   evaluating an amount. **GAP, more severe than "unpinpointed"** — no decision-relevant fact exists
   for this rule at all; flagged §7.
6. **KITAP prerequisites.** `el.e31a-kitap-prerequisites` fires `KITAP_TWO_YEAR_MARRIAGE_AND_
   INTEGRATION_NOT_VERIFIED` when `process.wants_onshore_conversion = true` — an advisory/gap flag,
   not an exclusion (`on_unknown: NO_EFFECT`, the only E31A rule with this softer unknown-handling).
