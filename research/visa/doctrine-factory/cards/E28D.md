### OD-4 disposition PROPOSAL (not a ruling — Zero decides at the E3 gate)

**Proposed: NOT `OUT_OF_COMMERCIAL_SCOPE` — but flag as HIGHER PRIORITY than E28B/C/F for a
pre-publication content-accuracy check, before any client-facing E28D card ships.** Rationale:

- The always-HUMAN_REVIEW rule design is plausibly the same intentional high-value-threshold safety
  gate as E28B/C — this card recommends retaining it, same as those cards.
- BUT: unlike E28B/C (where the caveats are internal-source ambiguities about figures within an
  otherwise-agreed product definition), E28D's conflict is about what the product FUNDAMENTALLY IS. If
  any live quoting or advisory surface reads E28D's category from the internal DB rather than the
  primary law, a client could be told they qualify for a bonds-investor product via E28D when the
  actual legal product for that purpose is a completely different index (arguably closer to E28C).
  This is a materially different risk class from a disputed KITAP-conversion-year figure.
- This card's recommendation: whoever authors E28D's operator-facing/client-facing content should be
  explicitly required to consult the PRIMARY-LAW reading (this card's §3.1/§3.3) and NOT
  `nb2_visa_types_final.txt`'s row, until CF-13's downstream-consumer remediation (named in §5) is
  complete — a content-authoring guardrail, not a rule-engine change (the rule engine, per §6, is
  already unaffected by which definition governs).
