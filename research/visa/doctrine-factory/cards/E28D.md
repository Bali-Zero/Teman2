### Evidentiary-depth and state-label discipline note (added per team-lead gate feedback, 2026-08-18)

Three coherence points a scoped adversarial pass on this card (and its E28F sibling) surfaced,
addressed here rather than scattered as silent fixes:

- **CL-E28D-03's pinpoint depth was under-displayed inline.** The underlying claim ledger entry for
  `CL-E28D-03` (cited by items 3, 6, 8, 11 above) carries the SAME Pasal/Ayat depth as `CL-E28D-01`
  (cited by items 1, 7, 12, 15): `Permenkumham 22/2023` Pasal 33/141; `Permenkumham 11/2024` Pasal 39
  Ayat 1&7, Pasal 40 Ayat 7, Pasal 191 Ayat (2)&(4). Earlier drafts of this card restated that depth
  inline for CL-E28D-01's items but not for CL-E28D-03's, creating an apparent (not real) evidentiary-
  depth gap between the two claims. Corrected here: the citation depth is genuinely comparable across
  both claims; what differed was only which items chose to restate it inline.
- **MERP/multiple-entry state-label inconsistency vs. E28F.** This card labels MERP auto-integration
  (item 6) `VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT` via `CL-E28D-03`; `E28F.md`'s item 8 labels the same underlying
  UU 63/2024 MERP mechanism `UNVERIFIED` via `CL-E28F-03`. Declared standard: the label attaches to the
  CLAIM AS A WHOLE, not to each fact-fragment bundled inside it — `CL-E28D-03` bundles MERP with
  onshore-conversion/KITAP/sponsor facts that ARE pinpoint-cited, earning the whole bundle
  VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT; `CL-E28F-03` bundles the SAME MERP fact with an E28F-specific duration FIGURE
  that was never found, earning the whole bundle UNVERIFIED. In neither card is MERP's own auto-
  integration actually in doubt — the differing labels reflect the worst-case component of each
  claim's bundle, not a disagreement about MERP itself. This is a real limitation of claim-bundling at
  this granularity, flagged here as input for E4/E5 rather than silently smoothed over.
- **Generic-family-basis claims do not get a uniform WITH-CAVEAT/plain-VERIFIED rule, and this card
  states the standard it actually applies.** `CL-CROSS-08` (family/minors general rules, item 14) is
  labeled plain `VERIFIED` despite resting on family-wide (not E28D-specific) authority; `E28F.md`'s
  `CL-E28F-04` (extension/sponsor) is labeled `VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT` despite also resting on a
  general/family-wide rule ("general investor-category rule, not E28F-specific pinpoint"). The
  distinguishing test this card applies (declared explicitly, not left implicit): a purely
  mechanical/administrative attachment or eligibility rule that does not vary by product within the
  family (CL-CROSS-08's dependent-attachment mechanism) stays plain VERIFIED, because there is no
  product-specific variant of that rule to be wrong about; a rule that could plausibly vary by product
  (an extension/KITAP-timing mechanism, a threshold figure) gets VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT even when sourced
  from general/family-wide authority, because the family-wide statement is a weaker proxy for THIS
  product's actual rule than a product-specific pinpoint would be. This standard, not an ad hoc
  per-claim judgment call, is why CL-CROSS-08 and CL-E28F-04 differ.
