## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33C (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Who**: a foreigner invited by the government because of their standing as a world figure, with
  the central government as sponsor.
- **Special requirement**: an invitation or statement from a central-government agency.
- **Documents**: valid passport (6 months), proof of sponsorship from a central-government agency, a
  personal bank statement of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent) for the last 3 months, a recent
  photo, CV, itinerary, other documents on the purpose of the visit.
- **Stay**: 5 or 10 years at the applicant's choice, counted from the entry stamp; extendable while
  the total stay stays within 10 years.
- **Activities**: investment, business or buying goods; work under the dual-activity (rangkap)
  rules; tourism and visiting family and friends.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.

## 4. Points to read together

- The pack rule tests the sponsor type and an invitation flag. The page does not say who counts as a
  world figure, and the card does not either.
- The USD 2,000 statement is the document the page lists; no rule tests it.
