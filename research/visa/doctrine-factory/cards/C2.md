## 3. Doctrinal facts (PDC-1 fields, each claim-referenced)

1. **Category & legal purpose.** C2 (*Visa Kunjungan Bisnis*) is a single-entry business visit visa
   authorizing commercial negotiations, contract signing, business meetings, seminar/conference
   attendance, field surveys, and office/factory/production-site inspections. It absolutely prohibits
   local commercial sales, local employment, and receiving any wage/reward/in-kind compensation from an
   Indonesian entity. — `CL-C2-01`, **VERIFIED** (source: `UU No. 6/2011`, `Permenkumham No. 22/2023`,
   `Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025`, all primary/official, structured `sources_used`). Matches the pack's
   own `covered_purposes` (`BUSINESS_MEETINGS`, `INVESTMENT`).
2. **Permitted activities.** Same as (1) — commercial negotiations, contract signing, meetings,
   seminars, field surveys, site inspections. — `CL-C2-01`, **VERIFIED**.
3. **Prohibited activities.** Absolute prohibition: local commercial sales, local employment, any
   wage/reward/in-kind compensation from an Indonesian entity. — `CL-C2-01`, **VERIFIED**. Reinforced
   by `CL-E23-02` (batch-1, **VERIFIED**), which names C2 explicitly: "B1/C2 visitors are absolutely
   prohibited from any hands-on machinery operation, repair, or installation" — narrower activity
   subclass, independently corroborating the general prohibition's severity for C2 specifically.
4. **Borderline/unresolved activities.** Per-diem/expense reimbursement for on-site business trips,
   foreign-HQ-paid, is explicitly PERMITTED — `CL-CROSS-02` (batch-1, **VERIFIED**, Products: ALL)
   names "C2/D2 (foreign-HQ-paid per-diem for on-site business trips)" directly in its six-row
   compensation taxonomy. This is the clearest borderline-activity resolution of any product in this
   slice — most of the others carry a **GAP** here.
5. **Single or multiple entry.** Single-entry; stay permit voids immediately on exit. — `CL-C2-02`,
   **VERIFIED** (batch-3) + `CL-XCUT-T5-01`, **VERIFIED** (batch-1, C2 named explicitly).
6. **Duration per entry & total validity.** Initial 60-day stay, extendable in-country up to a 180-day
   total ceiling; filing window opens 14 days before expiry. The two batch-3 answers backing this
   figure DISAGREE on the extension-increment size — `E2B3-C2-IDENTITY` states "30 or 60 days" per
   extension, `E2B3-C2-PROCEDURE` states "two extensions of 60 days each" for the same 180-day ceiling.
   **Not escalated to a numbered CF**: neither side carries a primary-law pinpoint for the increment
   size specifically, and both agree on the 60/180 anchor figures the pack actually consumes. —
   `CL-C2-02`, **VERIFIED** (with this disagreement carried explicitly, not smoothed). Matches the
   pack's own structured `stay_policy` (60/60 fixed days) and `extension_policy` (max 2 extensions,
   60 days per extension) — the pack's own encoding resolves the ambiguity toward "60 days each," but
   this card does not treat that as retroactively confirming the doctrinal ambiguity; it is a
   pack-authoring choice, not new doctrinal evidence.
7. **Extensions & conversions.** Extension mechanics per (6). Onshore conversion to ITAS: **⚠ CF-16 —
   OPEN CONFLICT**, see §5. Not a blocker for C2's own required topics (T8/onshore-conversion is not in
   C2's `required_claim_topics` list), but a genuine, unresolved product fact.
8. **Filing location (onshore/offshore) & onshore-status prerequisites.** Extensions filed onshore
   (implied by the 14-day-before-expiry filing window, same reasoning as C1.md §3.8) — no dedicated
   C2-specific claim states "onshore" explicitly; carried as directly implied by `CL-C2-02`, not
   independently claim-verified beyond that implication.
9. **Nationality restrictions incl. calling-visa applicability.** **GAP** — C2 carries no
   nationality-gating rule in seq-7 (unlike A1/B1); `CL-CROSS-06`'s T9 claim is topically present
   (Products: ALL) but has no C2-specific applicability since C2 has no nationality-list rule.
   Calling-visa (GLOBAL) out of scope, same as every card in this slice.
10. **Age limits.** Not applicable at the C2-specific rule level.
11. **Sponsor/guarantor requirements (post-UU 63/2024).** A sponsor (*penjamin*) is legally MANDATORY

    from a Sponsor, except for specific visits"; operationally satisfied by an inviting Indonesian
    company's formal business-invitation/sponsorship letter, with the sponsor assuming full
    legal/financial responsibility per `Permenimipas No. 5/2025`. — `CL-C2-03`, **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT**

does NOT block C2's disposition in §7, but it is a genuine, unresolved product-fact gap that matters
operationally the moment a client asks about C2-to-ITAS conversion. — **State: CONFLICTING.** Products:
C2. Provenance: `E2B3-C2-PROCEDURE`.

**Additional caveat found by the real Kimi K3 adversarial pass (see Adversarial review below), verified
directly against `rulepack-prod-007.source.json`:** the manual's "eligible" list item `B211A` is NOT
uniquely C2's legacy code — it is ALSO C1's and C6's shared legacy code (C1→`["B211A"]`,

reference to C2 (or to C1, or to C6, or to all three under the old undifferentiated single-entry visit
code) as an exclusion of it. This card does **not** resolve the ambiguity in either direction — it
records that CF-16's own "omits C2" framing rests on an unstated disambiguation the source material does
not actually make.
