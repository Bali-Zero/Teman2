# Product Doctrine Card — E33F (Second Home — Elderly, 1 year)

Refreshed 2026-10-13 against the active signed pack `rulepack-prod-029` (seq-29). The seq-7 text
of this card (titled "Retirement KITAS — standard pension") is in git history. The page calls this
code "Visa Rumah Kedua Lansia Untuk 1 Tahun" and does not mention a pension.

## 1. Identity

- **Code**: E33F. **Name on the page**: Visa Rumah Kedua Lansia Untuk 1 Tahun.
- **Pack category**: `LIMITED_STAY`, `covered_purposes: [RETIREMENT, BUSINESS_MEETINGS, TOURISM,
  FAMILY]`; stay policy 365 days.
- **Sponsor**: the page says you need a sponsor for this visa.

## 2. Active rules (rulepack-prod-029)

E33F has 3 PRODUCTS-scoped rules.

| rule_id | stage | effect / reason_code | what the rule tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hf.e33f.age-below-55` | HARD_FILTER | EXCLUDE / `AGE_BELOW_55` | purpose RETIREMENT and age below 55 |
| `el.e33f.retirement` | ELIGIBILITY | SUPPORT / `E33F_RETIREMENT_ELIGIBLE` | purpose RETIREMENT and passive monthly income of at least USD 3,000 |
| `hf.e33f.retirement-income-below-minimum` | HARD_FILTER | EXCLUDE / `RETIREMENT_INCOME_BELOW_THRESHOLD` | purpose RETIREMENT and passive monthly income below USD 3,000 (valid from 2026-09-23; unknown inputs: no effect) |

There is no deposit rule and no sponsor rule on E33F.

## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33F (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Statement of funds**: a bank statement for the last 3 months, in the name of the foreigner or of
  the sponsor, of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent), as proof of living costs in Indonesia.
- **Income**: proof of income or allowance of at least US$3,000 per month.
- **Sponsor**: the page says you need a sponsor, who files the visa through an evisa account and
  provides a letter of guarantee.
- **Stay**: 1 year; extension can be done online through evisa.imigrasi.go.id.
- **Activities**: living in Indonesia and doing business, meetings or shopping, including checking
  goods at an office, factory or production site; tourism and visiting friends or family.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.
- **Age**: the page states no age.

## 4. Points to read together

- **Age 55**: the age rule comes from the Permenkumham 22/2023 jo. 11/2024 source record cited by
  the pack, not from the page. The registry records the 55 versus 60 question under E33E.
- **Sponsor**: the page says a sponsor is needed; the pack has no rule that tests one. The card does
  not reconcile the two and does not add a rule.
- **USD 2,000 versus USD 3,000**: the USD 2,000 statement is a document the page lists; the USD 3,000
  per month is the proof of income. Only the USD 3,000 figure is tested by a pack rule. The superseded
  USD 1,500 figure is recorded in the registry.
- **Income wording**: the page says income or allowance; the pack fact is passive monthly income.
