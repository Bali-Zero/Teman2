## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Eligibility & work prohibition.** `CL-E33F-01`, **VERIFIED** — requires documented proof of a
   foreign-sourced pension (pension-fund statement or equivalent); prohibits local paid employment,
   same as the rest of the E33 family. Source: `kitas_e33f_pensionati_guida_2025.txt` (2025-03-27).
2. **Age minimum — this product's own version of the age tension is NARROWER than E33E's, and largely
   resolved by the same evidence.** `CL-E33F-02`, **CONFLICTING** at the time it was recorded — but
   this is the SAME underlying disagreement CF-7 tracks, not a distinct dispute. Critically, T1-037's
   own text (the source of CL-E33F-02 itself) reads Permenkumham 11/2024 as showing 55+ generically
   and its own operational reading assigns **60 to E33E specifically while leaving 55 to E33F alone**
   ("*lasciando la soglia dei 55 anni unicamente all'E33F pensionistico standard*"). CF-7's later
   RESOLVED disposition (55, per `Permenkumham No. 11 Tahun 2024` Pasal 33(2)(j)(4)/61(1)/62(1)/
   101(2)(f)(4)) therefore lands cleanly on E33F's side WITHOUT the Ngurah Rai counter-practice
   residual that complicates E33E specifically — no source in this batch attributes a live-practice
   60-year requirement to E33F the way it does to E33E. Recorded as CONFLICTING per its own original
   claim state (not silently upgraded), but the practical read is that E33F's age gate is the LESS
   contested of the two.
3. **Sponsor requirement.** `family.sponsor_confirmed = true` required (live pack) — a HARD_FILTER
   excludes if false. This is the substantive difference from E33E, which has no equivalent sponsor
   gate: **GAP** — no claim in this ledger discusses why E33F specifically requires a sponsor where
   E33E does not.
4. **Income floor.** `secondhome.passive_monthly_income_usd >= 3000` required — same figure as E33E's
   income test, but E33F has no equivalent deposit test (`secondhome.bank_deposit_usd`) — a structural
   difference from E33E worth noting explicitly since both are USD-3000-income-gated but only E33E
   also requires the USD-50,000 deposit.
