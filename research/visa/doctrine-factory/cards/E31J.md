## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Category & legal purpose.** `CL-E31J-01`, **VERIFIED** — `Permenkumham No. 11 Tahun 2024`
   Art. 33(2)(h)(9) (new provision, in force 3 May 2024) and Art. 50A(1) introduce a family-
   reunification index for a foreign minor (<18, unmarried) joining a sibling who holds ITAS/ITAP;
   `Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025` codifies this under index E31J.
2. **Permitted/prohibited activities.** `CL-E31J-02`, **VERIFIED** — permitted: tourism, shopping,
   visiting family/friends, entry/exit during MERP validity. Prohibited: overstaying, selling goods/
   services, receiving compensation/wages/commission from an Indonesian person or entity.
3. **Sponsor relation.** `family.relation_to_sponsor = "SIBLING"` — confirmed live against the pack;
   this is the first family index in this batch keyed on a SIBLING relation (contrast E31A SPOUSE,
   E31C/E31F/E31G/E31H PARENT/CHILD directions).
4. **Sponsor status — `known`-operator fail-open, independently re-verified live — affects ALL
   THREE rules, not two.** `el.e31j-sponsor-itas-itap` AND `el.e31j-dependency-age` AND
   `el.e31j-sibling-itas-support` all gate on `{"fact":"family.sponsor_status_code","op":"known"}` —
   the same value-blind operator documented as a fail-open finding in `E31B.md`/`E31H.md` (any
   non-null value satisfies it, sentinel included). An earlier draft of this card said "both" and
   named only two of the three rules — corrected here (§4's table now flags all three). Same
   mechanical gap, same recommended enum fix — this is the FOURTH product in the family sharing this
   root cause (E31B, E31E, E31H, now E31J).
5. **Dependency-age advisor check.** `el.e31j-dependency-age` fires an advisor-check
   (`E31J_DEPENDENCY_AGE_ADVISOR_CHECK`) when `derived.age_years >= 18` for a SIBLING relation — an
   adult sibling is not automatically excluded (no HARD_FILTER equivalent to E31E's age-18 exclusion),
   only routed to advisor review. **GAP** — no claim discusses what the advisor check actually
   verifies or what outcome it produces.
