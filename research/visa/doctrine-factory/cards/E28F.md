### Evidentiary-depth and state-label discipline note (added per team-lead gate feedback, 2026-08-18)

  UU 63/2024 MERP mechanism `VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT` via `CL-E28D-03`. Declared standard (same wording as
  `E28D.md`'s parallel note): the label attaches to the CLAIM AS A WHOLE, not to each fact-fragment
  bundled inside it. `CL-E28F-03` bundles the MERP fact with an E28F-specific duration figure that was
  never found, earning the whole bundle UNVERIFIED; `CL-E28D-03` bundles the SAME MERP fact with
  onshore-conversion/KITAP/sponsor facts that ARE pinpoint-cited, earning VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT. In
  neither card is MERP's own auto-integration under UU 63/2024 actually in doubt — the differing
  labels reflect the worst-case component of each claim's bundle, not a disagreement about MERP itself.
  Flagged as a real limitation of claim-bundling at this granularity, input for E4/E5, not silently
  smoothed over.
- **Generic-family-basis claims do not get a uniform WITH-CAVEAT/plain-VERIFIED rule, and this card
  states the standard it actually applies.** `CL-CROSS-08` (family/minors general rules, item 11) is
  labeled plain `VERIFIED` despite resting on family-wide (not E28F-specific) authority, while
  `CL-E28F-04` (item 4, this card) is labeled `VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT` despite also resting on a
  general/family-wide rule. The distinguishing test (declared explicitly, matching `E28D.md`'s parallel
  note): a purely mechanical/administrative attachment or eligibility rule that does not vary by
  product within the family (CL-CROSS-08's dependent-attachment mechanism) stays plain VERIFIED,
  because there is no product-specific variant of that rule to be wrong about; a rule that could
  plausibly vary by product (an extension/KITAP-timing mechanism) gets VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT even when
  sourced from general/family-wide authority, because the family-wide statement is a weaker proxy for
  THIS product's actual rule than a product-specific pinpoint would be.
