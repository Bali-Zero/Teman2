# Product Doctrine Card — E33 (Second Home Visa, base index)

Refreshed 2026-10-13 against the active signed pack `rulepack-prod-029` (seq-29). The seq-7 text of
this card is in git history. E33 is the base index of the Second Home family (E33, E33A, E33B, E33C,
E33E, E33F, E33G); each code has its own page and its own rules.

## 1. Identity

- **Code**: E33. **Name on the page**: Visa Rumah Kedua (Second Home Visa).
- **Pack category**: `LIMITED_STAY`; first grant 1,825 days (5 years) in the pack's stay policy.
- **Sponsor**: the page says you need no sponsor, individual or company, and that the immigration
  guarantee is used instead.
- **Price**: pricing key `E33 Second Home (5 Years)` (category `kitas_permits`), all-inclusive catalog price 35,000,000 IDR; the catalog does not split the fee.

## 2. Active rules (rulepack-prod-029)

E33 has 6 PRODUCTS-scoped rules.

| rule_id | stage | effect / reason_code | what the rule tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `el.e33.deposit-basis` | ELIGIBILITY | SUPPORT / `E33_DEPOSIT_BASIS_ELIGIBLE` | purpose SECOND_HOME, deposit of at least USD 130,000, at a state bank, in the applicant's own name |
| `el.e33.property-basis` | ELIGIBILITY | SUPPORT / `E33_PROPERTY_BASIS_ELIGIBLE` | purpose SECOND_HOME, qualifying property value of at least USD 1,000,000 |
| `el.e33.property-qualification` | ELIGIBILITY | SUPPORT / `E33_PROPERTY_QUALIFICATION_ADVISOR_CHECK` | property basis met (qualifying property value of at least USD 1,000,000), together with either basis (deposit of at least USD 130,000, at a state bank, in the applicant's own name) |
| `el.e33.guarantee-maintenance` | ELIGIBILITY | SUPPORT / `GUARANTEE_VALUE_MUST_BE_MAINTAINED` | either basis met (deposit of at least USD 130,000, or qualifying property value of at least USD 1,000,000); the same reason fires on the deposit basis and on the property basis |
| `review.e33.below-threshold-studio` | HUMAN_REVIEW | REQUIRE_REVIEW / `SECOND_HOME_BELOW_THRESHOLD_STUDIO` | purpose SECOND_HOME, deposit below USD 130,000 and property below USD 1,000,000 (valid from 2026-09-16; unknown inputs: no effect) |
| `hf.e33.employment-not-covered` | HARD_FILTER | EXCLUDE / `E33_EMPLOYMENT_NOT_COVERED` | purposes include both SECOND_HOME and EMPLOYMENT (valid from 2026-09-23) |

There is no age rule, no sponsor rule and no income rule on E33.

## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33 (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a page
source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Qualifying basis**: a commitment statement, filed with the application, to hold at least
  US$130,000 (or equivalent) in an account in the applicant's own name at a state-owned bank, or to
  buy a rumah susun or apartment in Indonesia worth at least US$1,000,000 (or equivalent).
- **90 days**: the commitment must be met and reported to the immigration office that issued the
  Izin Tinggal Terbatas within 90 days of entering Indonesia, with proof of the funds or the property.
- **Maintenance**: the guarantee value must be kept at least at the committed amount while the stay
  permit is valid.
- **Bank statement**: the page lists a personal bank statement of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent)
  for the last 3 months among the documents to upload.
- **Stay**: first permit up to 5 years, extendable while the total stay in Indonesia stays within
  10 years.
- **Activities**: tourism, buying goods, visiting family and friends; also work, study or other
  activities after reporting the dual activity (rangkap kegiatan) to the immigration office that
  issued the stay permit. Selling goods or services is prohibited unless needed for the work.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue; the page notes this differs from the length of stay.

The page confirms the USD 130,000 and USD 1,000,000 figures that the pack rules use. It is silent on
which title type qualifies for the property route (see `research/secondhome/e33-fact-registry.json`).

## 4. Points to read together

- **Work**: the page says work or study is possible after rangkap-kegiatan reporting. The pack rule
  `hf.e33.employment-not-covered` excludes E33 when the declared purposes include both SECOND_HOME
  and EMPLOYMENT. The card records both statements as they stand.
- **Age and family**: the age 55 versus 60 question belongs to E33E and E33F (see their cards). The
  page says family members can live in Indonesia if they meet the immigration rules.
- **KITAP after 3 years**: Permenkumham 22/2023 Pasal 179(1) with Pasal 173 huruf f (from the E2b
  batch-2b resolution, claim CF-8) gives 3 consecutive years; the registry keeps ITAP marketing
  forbidden until Ditjen Imigrasi answers in writing.
