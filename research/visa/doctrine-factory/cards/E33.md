   paid employment. — `CL-E33-01`, **VERIFIED** (`Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025 — Klasifikasi Visa`,

2. **Eligibility basis (this card's own product, not shared with E33E/F/G).** Per the active pack,
   E33's own SUPPORT rules key on `intent.purposes ∩ {SECOND_HOME}` plus one of two bases: (a) a
   ≥USD 130,000 blocked deposit at a state-owned bank, in the applicant's own name
   (`el.e33.deposit-basis`), or (b) ≥USD 1,000,000 qualifying property value
   (`el.e33.property-basis`). A third rule (`el.e33.property-qualification`) flags an

   life of the permit — `el.e33.guarantee-maintenance` fires the same `GUARANTEE_VALUE_MUST_BE_
   MAINTAINED` reason on either basis. Consistent with the family-wide prohibition on

## 4. CF-7 / CF-8 — RESOLVED (carried from the batch-2b EXTENSION, this card's authoritative citation)

- **CF-7 (age): RESOLVED at 55, not 60.** `Permenkumham No. 11 Tahun 2024` (in force 3 May 2024)
  Pasal 33(2)(j)(4), Pasal 61(1), Pasal 62(1), Pasal 101(2)(f)(4) — four independent articles, all
  giving 55 years — post-date and supersede `Permenkumham No. 22 Tahun 2023` (adopted 22 Aug 2023,
  pre-2024-reform), which is the source of the 60-year figure Bali Zero's own operational guide
  (`kitas_e33e_silver_hair_guida_2025.txt`, dated 27 March 2025) never updated after the reform. The
  60-year figure is not a fabrication — it correctly transcribed the pre-reform text — but it is
  stale relative to the currently governing article.
- **CF-8 (KITAP conversion): RESOLVED at 3 years, Pasal 179(1), not Pasal 76.** Pasal 76 of
  `Permenkumham 22/2023` governs only visa CANCELLATION, unrelated to KITAP timing. The governing
  article is Pasal 179(1) ("...telah berada di Wilayah Indonesia paling singkat 3 (tiga) tahun
  berturut-turut..."), scoped to the E33/E33E family via Pasal 173 huruf f. Neither `Permenkumham
  11/2024` nor `UU 63/2024` amends this 3-year figure. The 5-year figure in Bali Zero's internal
  guides traces to a general-purpose commercial guide describing `KITAP-INV`/`KITAP-RET` generically,
  not E33/E33E specifically — not called erroneous, just mis-scoped.

Both resolutions are `RESOLVED` per the batch-2b EXTENSION's own dedicated pinpoint-hunt queries
(`E2B2-CF7-A`, `E2B2-CF7-B`, `E2B2-CF8-A`), confirmed by the batch-3 closure ledger as genuinely
resolved (not merely deprioritized).
