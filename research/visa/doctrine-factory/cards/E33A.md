## 1. Identity

- **Code**: E33A. **Name on the page**: Visa Rumah Kedua Tenaga Ahli Undangan Pemerintah.
- **Pack category**: `LIMITED_STAY`, `covered_purposes: [EMPLOYMENT, TOURISM, FAMILY]`; stay policy
  1,825 to 3,650 days.
- **E33A is not the property route.** The page describes a foreigner invited by the government for
  their expertise, with the central government as sponsor. The property route is on the E33 page.

## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33A (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Who**: a foreigner invited by the government for their expertise, with the central government
  as sponsor. The page says you need a sponsor for this visa.
- **Special requirement**: an invitation or statement from the central government explaining why the
  foreigner is invited as a person with special expertise.
- **Documents**: valid passport (6 months), proof of sponsorship from the central government, a
  personal bank statement of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent) for the last 3 months, a recent
  photo, CV, travel history and itinerary, other documents on the purpose of the visit.
- **Stay**: 5 or 10 years at the applicant's choice, counted from the entry stamp; extendable while
  the total stay stays within 10 years.
- **Activities**: work in an employment relationship with the central government, tourism, buying
  goods, visiting family and friends, and other work under the dual-activity (rangkap) rules. The
  page says family may live in Indonesia if they meet the immigration rules.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.

## 4. Points to read together

- The pack rule tests the sponsor type and an invitation flag. It does not test the content of the
  invitation or which ministry issued it; the page does not name a ministry either.
- Public articles that call E33A "property investment" conflict with the page; that is a content
  matter outside this card.
