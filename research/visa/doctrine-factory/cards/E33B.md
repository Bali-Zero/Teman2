## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33B (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Who**: a foreigner invited by the government for their expertise, with no sponsor needed, using
  the immigration guarantee.
- **Guarantee**: a commitment statement to submit proof of cooperation with the government or a state
  institution; the proof is reported to the issuing immigration office within 90 days of entry,
  together with a certificate in a special field of expertise the state needs, or proof of graduation
  from one of the world's 100 best universities in the last 3 years with a GPA of at least 3.5 (or
  equivalent). The page lists the universities and the fields of expertise.
- **Documents**: valid passport (6 months), proof of the immigration guarantee, a personal bank
  statement of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent) for the last 3 months, a recent photo, CV, travel
  history and itinerary, other documents on the purpose of the visit.
- **Stay**: 5 or 10 years at the applicant's choice; extendable while the total stay stays within
  10 years.
- **Activities**: work in a collaboration with the government, tourism, buying goods, visiting family
  and friends, business and investment, and other activities under the dual-activity (rangkap) rules.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.

## 4. Points to read together

- The pack rules test the sponsor type and a collaboration flag. They do not test the expertise
  certificate or the university criterion that the page lists.
- E33B differs from E33A on the sponsor: E33A requires the central government as sponsor, E33B none.
