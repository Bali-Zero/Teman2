## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

   ("Silver Hair") member of the E33 retirement/second-home/passive-income family; no local paid
   employment.
2. **Age minimum — LEGALLY RESOLVED at 55.** `CF-7`, **RESOLVED** — `Permenkumham No. 11 Tahun 2024`
   (in force 3 May 2024) Pasal 33(2)(j)(4), Pasal 61(1), Pasal 62(1), and Pasal 101(2)(f)(4), FOUR
   independent articles, all give **55 years**; `Kepmen M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025`'s classification annex
   (in force 1 June 2025) independently confirms 55. The competing 60-year figure traces to the
   PRE-REFORM `Permenkumham No. 22 Tahun 2023` (adopted 22 Aug 2023), superseded on this specific
   point by the 2024 amendment — Bali Zero's own operational guide (dated 27 March 2025, i.e. AFTER
   the 2024 reform) was simply never updated to reflect it. Not a fabrication on either side, an
   honest supersession story.
3. **Age minimum — LIVE ENFORCEMENT PRACTICE UNRESOLVED — this is the residual the task brief asks
   this card to carry honestly, not smooth over.** The 55-year RESOLVED disposition (§3.2) does
   **NOT** settle whether the Kantor Imigrasi Ngurah Rai counter's actual real-world practice still
   requires 60. `CF-7`'s own resolution text says this explicitly: "*this does NOT resolve whether the
   Ngurah Rai counter's actual REAL-WORLD practice still applies 60 — a legal-text-vs-live-enforcement-
   practice question this batch's sources cannot settle*" — and the operational-practice source used to
   corroborate 60 (`E2B2-CF7-B`, dated 27 March 2025) is itself possibly stale by the time of this
   session. **Flagged for an operator field-check, not treated as resolved.** A client presenting at
   55-59 should be advised the legal minimum is 55 but that live-counter practice may diverge — the
   HARD_FILTER below encodes only the legal figure.
4. **KITAP conversion window — RESOLVED at 3 years — attribution note, this session.** `CF-8`,
   **RESOLVED** — `Permenkumham 22/2023` Pasal 179(1) ("...telah berada di Wilayah Indonesia paling
   singkat 3 (tiga) tahun berturut-turut..."), scoped to E33/E33E via Pasal 173 huruf f, NOT Pasal 76
   (which governs cancellation only). The 5-year figure in `nb2_golden_visa.txt`/
   `nb2_visa_types_final.txt` is a differently-scoped commercial-guide value (describes
   KITAP-INV/KITAP-RET generically), not specific to E33/E33E and not called erroneous — E33E's own
   dedicated internal guide already states 3 years correctly. **Flagged for the record**: this card's
   own §3.2 identifies `Permenkumham 22/2023` as the PRE-2024-reform decree superseded on the AGE
   figure — citing the same decree as the resolving authority here, for a different figure (KITAP
   timing), is not itself a contradiction (a decree can be current on one point and superseded on
   another), but this session's adversarial pass flagged the apparent tension as worth naming
   explicitly rather than leaving implicit.
5. **Deposit & income floor.** `CL-E33-04`, **VERIFIED** — minimum USD 50,000 blocked deposit in an
   Indonesian state-owned bank, in the applicant's own name, PLUS minimum monthly passive income of
   USD 3,000. Confirmed live against the pack's `el.e33e.retirement`/`.deposit-income-basis` rules
   (§4) — both test the exact three deposit sub-conditions and the income floor.
6. **Work prohibition.** `CL-E33-05`, **VERIFIED** — no local paid employment; passive equity/shares
   in Indonesian companies are permitted as a purely financial asset provided no executive/operational
   participation (per `E33.md`'s own §4 sourcing of the same claim family).
7. **55-59 disputed band — an explicit advisor-check exists for exactly this uncertainty.** The pack's
   `el.e33e.age-55-59-disputed-band` rule fires a dedicated advisor check
   (`E33E_AGE_55_59_ADVISOR_CHECK`) for applicants aged 55-59 meeting the deposit/income tests — this
   is the engine's own mechanical acknowledgment of the §3.3 residual: rather than silently applying
   the legal 55-year figure and risking a mismatch with live counter practice, the rule routes this
   exact age band to human review.
8. **`el.e33e.deposit-income-basis` is logically UNSATISFIABLE — real engine defect found by this
   session's adversarial review, not previously caught by the "re-verified live" claim in this card's
   first draft.** Independently re-derived this session: the rule's outer `all()` requires BOTH (a) an
   inner `any()` branch demanding exactly ONE of {deposit-met, income-met} (XOR shape: deposit-met-AND-
   NOT-income-met, OR NOT-deposit-met-AND-income-met) AND (b) a second branch demanding BOTH deposit-
   met AND income-met simultaneously. These two requirements are mutually exclusive by construction —
   no input can satisfy an XOR-of-two-conditions AND their conjunction at once. **This rule can never
   fire, for any applicant.** An earlier draft of §4 described it as "SUPPORTED (advisor-check for the
   deposit/income disjunction)" without disclosing this — corrected in §4 below.
