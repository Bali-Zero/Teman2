## 1. Identity

- **Code**: E33E. **Name on the page**: Visa Rumah Kedua Lansia Untuk 5 Tahun.
- **Pack category**: `LIMITED_STAY`; first grant 1,825 days (5 years).
- **Sponsor**: the page says you need no sponsor, individual or company, and that the immigration

## 3. What the official page says

Source: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33E (fetched 2026-10-13). This is a
page source, not an article of the regulation.

- **Age**: the page says 55 years or more. It does not mention 60.
- **Guarantee**: a commitment statement to hold at least US$50,000 (or equivalent) in an account in
  the applicant's own name at a state-owned bank, met and reported to the issuing immigration office
  within 90 days of entering Indonesia, with proof of the funds.
- **Income**: proof of income or allowance of at least US$3,000 per month.
- **Maintenance**: the guarantee value must be kept at least at the committed amount while the stay
  permit is valid.
- **Bank statement**: a personal bank statement of at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent) for the last
  3 months is among the documents to upload.
- **Stay**: first permit up to 5 years, extendable while the total stay stays within 10 years.
- **Activities**: tourism, buying goods, visiting family and friends; also work, study or other
  activities after reporting the dual activity (rangkap kegiatan) to the issuing immigration office.
  Selling goods or services is prohibited unless needed for the work. The page says family can live
  in Indonesia if they meet the immigration rules.
- **Visa validity**: 90 days from issue.

## 4. Points to read together

- **Age 55 and the 55-59 band**: the pack excludes under 55 and routes ages 55 to 59 through an
  advisor-check reason. The registry (`age_55_59_ambiguity_e33e`) records that Permenkumham 11/2024
  Pasal 33(2)(j)(4) says 55 while Pasal 33(10)(d) still reads 60, and that the live pages state 55.
  The E2b batch-2b resolution of CF-7 reads 55 from Pasal 33(2)(j)(4), 61(1), 62(1) and 101(2)(f)(4).
  The only source for 60 in the E2b chain is an internal operational guide dated 27 March 2025; the
  page text fetched on 2026-10-13 says 55 and does not mention 60.
- **KITAP**: Permenkumham 22/2023 Pasal 179(1) with Pasal 173 huruf f gives 3 consecutive years
  (E2b batch-2b resolution of CF-8); the registry keeps ITAP marketing forbidden until Ditjen
  Imigrasi answers in writing.
- **Work**: the page allows work or study after rangkap-kegiatan reporting; the pack has no
  employment rule on E33E.
