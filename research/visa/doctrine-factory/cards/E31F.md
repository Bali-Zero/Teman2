## 3. Doctrinal facts (claim-referenced)

1. **Category & legal purpose.** Foreign child reuniting with an Indonesian-citizen (WNI) parent
   (`Penyatuan Keluarga`) — `CL-E31F-01`, **VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEAT** (production-catalog cross-
   reference, `seed_visa_types_complete_2026.py:1228-1238`, same evidentiary class as
   `CL-E31C-01`/`CL-E31D-legal-01`).
2. **Index-letter confusion (NB-2-source only).** `CL-E31BCDEF-01`, **CONFLICTING** — same disposition
   as E31C: an internal-DB artifact, production catalog resolves the ambiguity, not re-litigated here.
3. **Sponsor relation and nationality — the key discriminator from E31C.** `family.relation_to_sponsor
   = "PARENT"` (sponsor is the parent, per pack-internal consistency verified against E31G/E31H's
   `CHILD` value on the reverse-direction products — see `E31C.md` §3.3), PLUS
   `family.sponsor_nationalities ∩ {ID}` — the sponsor must be Indonesian, which E31C's own support
   rule does NOT test. This nationality test is the genuine substantive difference between E31F
   (WNI-parent-specific) and E31C's broader PARENT-relation trigger.
4. **Age.** `el.e31f-adult-age-review` fires an advisor-check based on `derived.age_years` — E31F is
   not purely a minor-dependent index the way E31E is; adult children may require additional review.
   **GAP** — no claim discusses the age threshold or what triggers the review specifically.

   originally only in E31C.md — added the cross-reference note here too, since it's equally an E31F
   concern (an E31F applicant could be mis-routed through E31C's broader rule).

Net: both findings real and cured. Disposition unchanged.
