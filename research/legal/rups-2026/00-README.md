## The blindato fact chain

1. **RUPS approves the FY2025 annual report within 6 months of fiscal year-end** = **30 June 2026** for a 31 December year-end (base: **UU 40/2007**).
2. **Pasal 16 Permenkum 49/2025**: the RUPS approval must be in a **notarial deed** (*akta notaris*); the notary files it into **SABH/AHU Online within 30 days of the deed signing date** (*tanggal penandatanganan akta*) — NOT the RUPS date.
3. **Pasal 17**: sanctions = *teguran tertulis* (written warning) → *pemblokiran akses SABH* (access block → company legally frozen; cascades to KITAS/work-permit + OSS).
4. In force **17 Dec 2025**; FY2025 = first cycle. Applies to **all PT persekutuan modal incl. dormant** (obligation on the entity, not turnover). **PT Perorangan exempt** from the deed (direct e-filing).
5. The "**30 July**" Zero referenced = the practical outer edge (30 June RUPS + 30-day notary filing window), **not** a fixed statutory date.
