# WR2 Brief — Permenkumham 22/2023+11/2024 KITAP Impact for Existing Holders

## ⚠️ Critical Regulatory Flag

**"Permenkumham No. 22 Tahun 2024" does NOT exist** as a named regulation in NB-2's corpus (97 immigration sources).

The topic conflates two distinct instruments:
- **Permenkumham 22/2023** — base regulation (visa system + izin tinggal overhaul)
- **Permenkumham 11/2024** — amendment to 22/2023 (Golden Visa adjustments, sponsor definitions)
- **UU No. 63/2024** — national law amendment (MERP auto-integration into KITAS/KITAP)
- **Permenimipas 5/2025** — new sponsor/penjamin rules (supersedes Permenkumham 36/2021)

**Storyboarder instruction**: do NOT write "Permenkumham 22/2024". Frame the carousel as "the 2023–2024 immigration reform package".

## Key Facts (NB-2 sourced)

1. **No mandatory conversion**: All permits issued before new regulations remain fully valid to natural expiry. Zero action required pre-expiry. — NB-2 [IMM-356]
2. **MERP abolished as separate document**: UU 63/2024 integrates re-entry authorization automatically into KITAP from issuance date. No separate MERP application, no separate MERP fee. — NB-2 [IMM-362]
3. **MERP transitional rule**: Old MERP documents stay valid until their own expiry. At next KITAP renewal, new document auto-includes MERP. — NB-2 source 27394849
4. **Unlimited duration KITAP**: After first 5-year issuance, KITAP can be renewed for *jangka waktu tidak terbatas* (unlimited duration). — NB-2 Pasal 128 Ayat 1
5. **5-year Lapor obligation**: Unlimited-duration KITAP holders must check in (Lapor) at local Kantor Imigrasi every 5 years. Fee: IDR 0 (explicitly exempt from charges). — NB-2 [IMM-195], Permenkumham-29-2021 Pasal 144 Ayat 1
6. **Renewal window**: 3 months before expiry (earliest) to expiry date (latest). — NB-2 Pasal 128 Ayat 3
7. **Overstay protection**: If KITAP renewal is filed AND PNBP paid before expiry, holder is NOT considered overstay even if processing extends past expiry. Renewal runs from original expiry date. — NB-2 Pasal 128 Ayat 4-5
8. **Sponsor independence for KITAP**: Day-to-day residency requires no active sponsor — unlike KITAS E33F (requires licensed agency). — NB-2 [IMM-100]
9. **But: penjamin still files renewal**: A penjamin (guarantor) must formally present the KITAP renewal request. — NB-2 [IMM-169], confirmed in Permenimipas 5/2025 regime
10. **Passport renewal = Imigrasi visit**: If holder renews passport, KITAP must be updated with new passport number at Kantor Imigrasi. This is a SEPARATE procedure from MERP — still required. — NB-2 source 27394849
11. **New ministry in 2024**: Immigration authority moved from Kemenkumham to Kemenimipas (Kementerian Imigrasi dan Pemasyarakatan). — NB-2 [IMM-365]
12. **New sponsor rules 2025**: Permenimipas 5/2025 supersedes Permenkumham 36/2021 for sponsor/penjamin obligations. — NB-2 [IMM-366]
