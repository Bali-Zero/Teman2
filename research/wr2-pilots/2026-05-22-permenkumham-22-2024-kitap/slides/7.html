KITAP UPDATE IS MANDATORY — SEPARATE FROM MERP
NEW PASSPORT: ONE MORE VISIT TO IMIGRASI
If you renew your passport, you must update your KITAP with the new passport number at your local Kantor Imigrasi (local immigration office). This requirement is independent of MERP, unaffected by the 2023–2024 reform, and fully in force. One visit. Bring both documents.
