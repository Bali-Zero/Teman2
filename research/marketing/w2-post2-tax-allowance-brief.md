# W2 Post 2 — Tax Allowance vs Tax Holiday

## Theme

Many foreign investors confuse “tax allowance” and “tax holiday” in Indonesia.

## Objective

Educate investors using simple comparison framing.

## Key Message

Tax allowance and tax holiday are different incentive systems with different eligibility, scale, and benefits.

## Core Comparison

### Tax Allowance

- Partial incentive
- Usually deduction/reduction based
- More accessible
- Sector dependent

### Tax Holiday

- Larger exemption potential
- Usually strategic/high-value investment focused
- Higher qualification threshold

## Audience

- Foreign investors
- PT PMA founders
- Manufacturing sector
- Medium-large investment projects

## Suggested Carousel

1. Hook:
   “Tax Allowance ≠ Tax Holiday”

2. Main Difference
3. Who Usually Qualifies
4. Common Misunderstanding

## Compliance Notes

- Do not promise tax savings.
- Avoid definitive eligibility claims without BKPM/tax review.
- Final incentive analysis depends on KBLI and investment structure.
