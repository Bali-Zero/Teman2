# W2 Post 1 — PPN 12% Indonesia 2026

## Theme

“12% headline vs 11% reality — siapa sebenarnya yang bayar 12%?”

## Objective

Clarify confusion around Indonesia VAT (PPN) increase.
Position Bali Zero as trusted explainer, not fear-based publisher.

## Key Message

Most goods and services in Indonesia are NOT effectively taxed at full 12%.

For most transactions, effective VAT burden remains around 11% because of:

- DPP Nilai Lain mechanism
- 11/12 calculation basis
- PMK 131/2024 implementation

The 12% full rate mainly affects luxury goods categories.

## Angle

Do NOT frame:
“Semua barang sekarang kena 12%”

Correct framing:
“Headline 12%, tetapi mayoritas transaksi efektif tetap sekitar 11%.”
