### SLIDE 2 — THE RULE

**Headline:**
What the law actually says

**Body:**
Under UU PPh No. 36/2008:

→ 183+ days in Indonesia within any 12-month window
= domestic tax resident

Doesn't matter what visa you hold.
Doesn't matter where you're "officially based."

**Visual note:** Clean text slide. Source: "UU PPh No. 36/2008" visible as a caption — adds credibility.

---

### SLIDE 3 — THE MISTAKE

**Headline:**
The mistake almost everyone makes

**Body:**
Most expats count January–December.

The law counts _any_ 12-month window.

You could cross 183 days without realising it — especially if you arrived mid-year.

**Visual note:** Timeline graphic showing a rolling 12-month window crossing year boundary.

### SLIDE 4 — WHAT CHANGES

**Headline:**
What changes once you're a tax resident

**Body:**
Indonesia can tax your _worldwide_ income.

Not just what you earn here.

→ Foreign salary
→ Overseas dividends
→ Freelance income paid abroad
→ Rental income from property outside Indonesia

All of it.

**Visual note:** Simple list, each item revealed per swipe or stacked clearly.

### SLIDE 5 — THE RELIEF

**Headline:**
There is one saving grace

**Body:**
First-time Indonesian tax residents get a
**4-year exemption** on foreign-sourced income.

After those 4 years?
Everything comes in — no exceptions.

**Visual note:** Countdown/timeline visual. "Year 1–4: exempt" vs "Year 5+: taxable".

### SLIDE 6 — WHO THIS HITS

**Headline:**
Who needs to pay attention right now

**Body:**
→ PT PMA directors spending long stints in Bali
→ Digital nomads on E33G or tourist visas
→ Remote workers "just working from Bali for a few months"
→ Foreign investors with overseas rental or dividend income

If any of these is you — you need to check your day count.

**Visual note:** Icon-based list. Each persona gets a small icon.

---

### SLIDE 7 — WHAT TO DO

**Headline:**
3 things to check today

**Body:**

1. Count your in-country days — use _any_ 12-month window, not the calendar year

2. Know your visa type and what it means for tax status (E33G ≠ exempt by default)

3. If you've crossed 183 days and haven't registered NPWP — voluntary disclosure is always safer than an audit

**Visual note:** Numbered checklist, clear and actionable.
