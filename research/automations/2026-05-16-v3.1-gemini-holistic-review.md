### 3. New Concerns Introduced by v3.1
*   **LaunchAgent Self-Teardown Suicide (F0.6 & F3.1)**: Both the TTL sentinel (F0.6) and the pg-proxy 24h recheck (F3.1) attempt to self-teardown by executing `launchctl bootout` on their own label, immediately followed by `rm -f <plist>`. Executing `bootout` instructs `launchd` to unload the job and immediately send a `SIGTERM` to the executing script. The process will commit suicide instantly, meaning the `rm -f` command will **never execute**. This will leave orphaned `.plist` files scattered in `~/Library/LaunchAgents/`. The `rm` must be executed *before* `bootout` (or the teardown delegated).
