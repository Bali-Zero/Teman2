# Bali Villa Operator Architecture Under the 2026 PMA Restrictions

## Purpose and decision status

This dossier captures the full legal, licensing, tax, operating, and business-design analysis developed for a Bali villa rental case involving a foreign investor or PT PMA, a genuine Indonesian principal, and a possible Perseroan Perorangan operating or management layer.

It is a product-development and pre-clearance dossier, not a legal opinion or a declaration that the structure has been approved. The strongest commercial opportunity is not a paper workaround for the Bali restrictions. It is a controlled owner–operator architecture in which every contract, bank flow, OTA account, tax invoice, staff instruction, and economic risk points to the same lawful principal.

**Decision as of 31 August 2026:**

- Proceed with a single brownfield pilot only after written pre-clearance.
- Prefer the owner-led model as the baseline.
- Treat a disclosed management model as conditional.
- Do not launch a Perseroan Perorangan as mere lessee/sublessee under KBLI 55203 until the meaning of `pemilik` is confirmed in writing for the exact property right and entity.
- Reject any arrangement whose real purpose is to give the foreign party hidden operational control or sweep the Indonesian operator's residual profit.

The governing design principle is simple:

> The receipt follows the legal cause and operational substance; it does not create them.

## Executive verdict

The superficially attractive chain — foreign individual or PT PMA leases the villa, an Indonesian Perseroan Perorangan collects guest revenue, and most of the margin is returned to the foreign party through receipts — is a high-risk structure when used as a disguised ownership or control mechanism. It can fail simultaneously under investment, land, licensing, tax, immigration, and beneficial-ownership analysis.

The robust version separates three roles:

1. a lawful Indonesian owner or right-holder who is the real accommodation principal;
2. a substantive operator or manager who performs a real, licensed function and bears the corresponding risks;
3. a foreign party or PT PMA that is paid only for genuine, documented, arm's-length services, financing, technology, brand, or other lawful consideration.

No entity may be inserted merely to issue a receipt. Guest contracting, OTA merchant identity, bank settlement, PBJT registration, pricing authority, employment authority, complaint handling, refunds, and residual profit must agree with the chosen model.

## What the Bali restriction is — and is not

No verified primary source establishes a blanket legal moratorium on every villa or every PT PMA activity in Bali.

The verified landscape is narrower and more complex:

- Bali Regional Regulation 4/2026 has been in force since 24 February 2026. It addresses productive-land conversion and arrangements that facilitate nominee control of land by foreign nationals.
- A Governor's letter dated 28 January 2026 requested restrictions. The letter is not itself the final national licensing instrument.
- A targeted OSS closure for 18 old KBLI codes applicable to new PMA applications was reported as operational across Bali from the third week of May 2026.
- The public text of the decisive Ministry/BKPM instrument and the official OSS configuration implementing that closure were not located in the research record.
- The published list uses KBLI 2020 codes. A complete authoritative concordance showing how the closure applies to KBLI 2025 successors has not been obtained.
- Every asset remains subject to parcel-specific controls, including spatial plan, KKPR, productive-land status, PBG function, SLF, environmental requirements, tourism standards, access, fire safety, and wastewater controls.

Therefore, “moratorium solution” is the wrong product promise. The correct promise is an asset-specific licensing truth assessment and written pre-clearance process.

## KBLI analysis

### KBLI 55203 — villa activity

The recorded description covers short-term accommodation in private houses rented specifically to tourists with facilities and `dikelola sendiri oleh pemiliknya` — managed directly by the owner.

This sentence is the central legal gate. No primary source located in the research defines a long-lessee or sublessee as `pemilik` for this classification. A WNI founder and the founder's Perseroan Perorangan are distinct legal subjects. If the individual holds the land or building right and the company merely leases it, the company remains a lessee unless an authority accepts that particular arrangement as owner-management for KBLI 55203.

The code cannot therefore be used as an automatic path for a paper operator. The exact right, entity, property, and operational facts must be put to DPMPTSP/OSS and tourism authorities in writing.

### KBLI 55901 — accommodation management services

The recorded description covers a third party responsible for the performance and operation of various accommodation businesses, including daily operations and managerial decisions. It acts for the property owner and receives a management fee. It does not own and directly operate the accommodation as principal.

This makes 55901 conceptually suitable for a disclosed principal–agent model. It does not automatically solve the villa issue:

- the detailed checklist refers to hotel management;
- the related codes shown by the Navigator are hotel codes 55101–55106, not villa code 55203;
- the old 55900 is among the codes connected to the public description of the Bali restriction;
- an authoritative legal concordance from old 55900 to new 55901 has not been obtained;
- full daily management could conflict with the 55203 requirement that the villa be managed directly by its owner.

The model survives only if the owner remains the lawful principal and the authorities confirm that the proposed management scope is compatible with 55203.

### KBLI 55400 — accommodation intermediation

This classification covers digital or non-digital intermediation that connects accommodation providers and clients for a commission. It is a booking or marketplace layer, not a licence to operate the property, employ its staff, bear guest liability, or take the residual accommodation profit.

It can be a useful PT PMA or vendor layer only when the platform function is real and the accommodation principal remains visible in the guest contract, OTA records, payment flow, and tax records.

### KBLI 68112 — residential property leasing

The recorded description covers leasing and operation of residential real estate owned or leased by the operator. It does not mention tourist accommodation or short stays. It should not be used to relabel daily or weekly villa accommodation as ordinary residential leasing.

A truly long-term residential product can be structured in this lane, with a different tax and licensing analysis. The facts must show residential use, not transient hospitality dressed as a tenancy.

### KBLI 68111 — residential property development

Under the KBLI 2025 record, 68111 concerns residential development, sale, and projects for more permanent or annual use. It is not a short-stay villa-operation code. The fact that the Navigator lists PT PMA among related entity forms does not prove that a particular PMA application is open in Bali.

### Adjacent codes surfaced by the Navigator

The semantic search also surfaced three adjacent codes. They create legitimate product lanes only when the physical asset and operating facts genuinely match them.

- **55209 — other short-stay accommodation:** the record covers formats not already included in 55201–55204, such as bungalow, cottage without daily housekeeping, hostel, bed-and-breakfast unit, mountain lodge, capsule hotel, tree house, cabin, and glamping. It is not a generic substitute for a villa that belongs in 55203. The record is regulated at medium-low risk, but its licensing data is inherited from old 55199 and its PMA status is not verified.
- **55201 — homestay:** the record requires an individual to use a residential building occupied by its owner, rent only part of it daily or weekly, and give tourists an opportunity to interact with the owner's daily life. It is not a scalable replacement for a detached villa operation or a paper-company model. Its licensing data is inherited from old 80100 and must be treated cautiously.
- **68292 — fee-based residential property management:** the record covers management, maintenance, repairs, residential charges, budgets, and house rules under a fee or contract. It is regulated at medium-high risk and shows P4-related requirements. It can support a genuine long-stay residential management product, but it does not authorise tourist accommodation or solve 55203. Its licensing data is inherited from old 68200 and PMA status is not verified.

## Perseroan Perorangan constraints

A Perseroan Perorangan is a separate legal person, but it is not a device for transferring foreign control to an Indonesian name. Its founder must be an Indonesian citizen, acts as sole shareholder and director, and must meet the applicable MSME framework. It must convert when its shareholder structure or business scale no longer meets the statutory conditions.

The operator must have real substance:

- Indonesian decision-maker and authorised bank signatory;
- sufficient working capital;
- genuine exposure to operating profit and loss;
- employees or contracted resources appropriate to its function;
- its own bookkeeping, tax registration, contracts, and compliance calendar;
- control of the activities for which it is licensed;
- transparent beneficial-ownership and source-of-funds records.

It must not be indemnified against all losses, receive a fixed nominee fee, sign blank transfer documents, grant an irrevocable operational proxy, or hand the foreign party passwords and veto rights.

## Architecture A — owner-led villa

This is the preferred baseline for a small villa.

### Legal and operational design

- A genuine Indonesian owner or accepted owner-right holder is the accommodation principal.
- The principal holds or obtains the appropriate 55203 licensing chain.
- The guest contract, OTA merchant identity, bank account, receipt, refund obligation, guest liability, and PBJT registration all name the principal.
- The principal controls pricing, staffing, vendors, maintenance, refunds, and complaints.
- The principal bears operating costs and retains a meaningful residual profit or loss.
- A Perseroan Perorangan may perform a limited, independently licensed function only if that function is real.
- A PT PMA may supply genuine software, brand, booking, marketing, financing, or administrative support consistent with its KBLI and tax treatment.

### Why this model is stronger

It aligns legal title or accepted owner-right, commercial control, customer-facing identity, cash flow, and taxes. It also gives the foreign investor lawful economic participation through explicit contracts without pretending that those contracts transfer ownership of the villa business.

### Failure conditions

- the Indonesian principal is risk-free or receives only a nominee fee;
- the foreign party controls bank devices, OTA credentials, pricing, staff, or exit;
- related-party charges strip the entire residual profit;
- the entity using 55203 is not accepted as `pemilik`;
- property-level permits do not support the use.

## Architecture B — disclosed accommodation management

This is commercially promising but requires the 55203/55901 compatibility gate.

### Legal and operational design

- A separately lawful owner/operator remains the principal and merchant of record.
- The guest contract, OTA account, bank settlement, PBJT, gross revenue, and guest liability remain with that principal.
- A domestic manager or otherwise eligible manager performs the defined management function.
- The manager records only its management fee, not the principal's guest revenue.
- The principal withholds and reports the applicable tax on the management payment.
- The contract includes a precise RACI matrix, budget authority, service levels, reporting rights, and termination rights.

### Why it can work

The 55901 description expressly contemplates third-party management for an owner and payment by management fee. This creates a defensible principal–agent logic when the operational records follow it.

### Why it remains conditional

The same description contemplates daily operational control by the manager, while 55203 requires owner-management. The internal licensing checklist also points to hotel-management standards rather than villa 55203. Written confirmation is required before treating this as a saleable villa solution.

## Architecture C — pure accommodation intermediation

This is a narrower 55400 product:

- the intermediary connects guest and operator;
- the operator remains clearly disclosed as accommodation supplier;
- the intermediary earns a commission;
- guest funds are either settled directly to the operator or held and reconciled under a clearly documented agency arrangement;
- the intermediary does not control property operations or record the accommodation gross as its own revenue.

This model may support a technology or booking business, but it cannot be sold as property management under another label.

## Architecture D — genuine long-term residential rental

Where the commercial product is truly a residential tenancy rather than hospitality, 68112 may be relevant. The evidence must show longer-term residential occupation, tenancy rights and obligations, and no hotel-like daily services or transient guest turnover.

The model fails if stays, marketing, pricing, cleaning cadence, check-in procedures, and guest services reveal a short-term tourist accommodation business.

## Prohibited architecture — paper operator and residual sweep

The following pattern is a no-go:

- a WNA or PT PMA supplies all funds and controls the asset;
- an Indonesian founder creates a Perseroan Perorangan only to hold the NIB and receive guest money;
- the foreign party controls OTA, bank, prices, staff, vendors, and refunds;
- the Indonesian is protected from losses and receives a fixed fee;
- rent, interest, royalty, service, or management invoices transfer nearly all residual profit to the foreign party;
- side letters, proxies, blank transfers, or guaranteed returns preserve hidden foreign control.

Even if every payment has a receipt, the combined contracts can be recharacterised according to their substance. The arrangement also creates land nominee, investment nominee, beneficial ownership, transfer-pricing, immigration, and tax risks.

## Tax architecture

### Guest revenue and PBJT

Villa accommodation is generally within the local PBJT framework. The exact liable person, rate, tax base, and treatment of service charges, refunds, and OTA deductions must be confirmed with the relevant regency or city Bapenda.

The guest bill, receipt, OTA supplier record, bank settlement, and PBJT filing should identify the same accommodation principal.

### PP 20/2026 final tax

The 0.5% final-tax regime is a tax treatment, not permission to conduct an otherwise unlicensed business.

For a qualifying Perseroan Perorangan:

- the personal Rp500 million allowance does not apply to the company;
- the 0.5% calculation begins from the first rupiah of qualifying gross turnover;
- turnover of the founder, spouse, and connected Perseroan Perorangan is aggregated for the Rp4.8 billion threshold;
- OTA commissions, management fees, rent, interest, and royalties do not automatically reduce gross turnover;
- artificial fragmentation among multiple companies is not defensible;
- the duration and exit rules of the final regime must be monitored.

Whether PBJT collected for Bapenda is excluded from the PP 20/2026 turnover base should be confirmed in writing with DJP for the actual invoicing method.

### Principal–agent revenue recognition

In a true management model:

- the principal records guest accommodation revenue and PBJT;
- the manager records only the management fee;
- OTA and bank records must support that separation;
- if the manager is named as supplier, receives the gross as beneficial owner, or bears guest obligations, the tax authority may treat it as principal regardless of contract wording.

### Land and building rent

Genuine land or building rent to a domestic lessor is generally subject to 10% final income tax on the gross rental amount under PP 34/2017. Accommodation service is not automatically the same as land/building rent.

Rent paid by the operator does not reduce the operator's gross-turnover base under the 0.5% final regime. It is a cash expense, not a turnover deduction.

### Domestic PT PMA service payments

A PT PMA incorporated in Indonesia is a domestic taxpayer despite foreign ownership. A qualifying service payment is therefore analysed under domestic withholding rules, including PPh 23 where applicable, rather than PPh 26 merely because shareholders are foreign. VAT and PKP status require separate analysis.

### Non-resident payments

Payments to a non-resident WNA or foreign company may trigger PPh 26, treaty, beneficial-owner, Form DGT, permanent-establishment, and transfer-pricing questions. These must be analysed payment by payment.

### Related-party rent, debt, intellectual property, and services

Each lane must have independent economic substance:

- **service:** actual personnel, deliverables, benefit, invoice, and compatible KBLI;
- **booking commission:** demonstrable intermediation and a market commission;
- **loan:** actual disbursement, commercial purpose, repayment capacity, arm's-length interest, debt/equity review, and lawful security;
- **royalty:** owned and used intellectual property, valuation, territory, and enforceable licence;
- **rent:** legal right to lease or sublease and compatibility with the accommodation-principal model.

Do not stack rent, interest, royalty, management fee, and service fee merely to eliminate the Indonesian principal's residual profit.

## Investor protection without hidden foreign control

Lawful investor protection should protect capital and information without transferring the operator's regulated activity to the investor.

Potential controls include:

- read-only bank and OTA reporting;
- monthly third-party reconciliation and audit rights;
- insurance and minimum reserve requirements;
- approved annual budget with objective variance thresholds;
- fixed or benchmarked commercial returns for genuine contracts;
- asset-preservation and default covenants;
- legally valid security over eligible assets or receivables;
- transparent beneficial-ownership and source-of-funds records;
- termination rights for fraud, permit failure, tax default, or misuse of funds.

Prohibited or presumptively dangerous controls include:

- shared OTP or administrator credentials;
- foreign signatory or approval rights over operating bank payments;
- veto over prices, staff, refunds, or vendors;
- irrevocable powers of attorney;
- blank share or asset transfer documents;
- guaranteed return that leaves the Indonesian principal risk-free;
- WNI indemnification against all losses;
- automatic residual-profit sweep;
- cross-default terms that effectively transfer the business on demand.

## Commercial products worth launching

### 1. Villa License Truth Scan

An asset-level diagnostic covering current KBLI, entity, location, OSS history, zoning, KKPR, PBG, SLF, tourism standards, PMA restriction exposure, and old-to-new KBLI uncertainty.

### 2. Owner–Operator Substance Audit

A red-team review of beneficial ownership, funding, loss exposure, bank and OTA access, employment authority, pricing, vendor control, side agreements, and immigration conduct.

### 3. Villa Principal–Agent Structuring Pack

A coordinated package containing the principal–agent contract, RACI, merchant-of-record map, payment control design, fee benchmark, tax matrix, and evidence checklist for either limited intermediation or disclosed management.

### 4. Villa Tax Stack and Turnover Monitor

A recurring service tracking PBJT, PP 20/2026 gross turnover, founder/spouse/company aggregation, PPh 23/26, VAT, rent tax, related-party payments, OTA gross-to-net reconciliation, and conversion triggers.

### 5. Thirty-Day Villa Go/No-Go Dossier

A pre-clearance sprint that converts the legal uncertainty into written authority questions, asset evidence, tested cash flow, a risk score, and a documented launch decision.

### 6. Verified Indonesian Operator Network

A vetted network of genuinely independent owner-operators and managers with verified licences, staff, accounting, banking, and compliance history. This product must never be marketed as access to nominee operators.

### 7. Brownfield PMA Rescue

A review for existing PMA businesses assessing grandfathering, new locations, permit mutations, expansion, shareholder changes, KBLI migration, operational controls, and a lawful restructuring or exit path.

### 8. Long-Stay Residential Operations Pack

A separate 68112/68292 product for genuinely residential leases and fee-based residential management. It should include tenancy classification, P4 requirements, owner–manager scope, rental withholding, maintenance budgets, resident charges, and controls preventing drift into unlicensed short-stay hospitality.

## Hard stops

Stop the project if any of the following remains true:

- no written answer exists on `pemilik` for a lessee-based 55203 model;
- 55900 is confirmed as blocking 55901 or 55901 is confirmed not to cover villas;
- third-party management is incompatible with owner-management under 55203;
- the foreign party controls the operating bank, OTA, prices, staff, vendors, refunds, or exit;
- the Indonesian principal is indemnified against risk or paid only a fixed nominee fee;
- the asset fails zoning, productive-land, KKPR, PBG, SLF, or tourism requirements;
- Perseroan Perorangan exceeds its legal or tax limits without conversion and transition;
- related-party rent, interest, royalty, or service payment lacks substance and benchmarking;
- a foreign person performs operational work without proper authorisation;
- an undocumented side agreement changes ownership, control, or economics.

## Final commercial recommendation

Launch the advisory products now, but do not launch the client structure as a pre-approved workaround.

The first market-ready offer should be the **Thirty-Day Villa Go/No-Go Dossier**, with the **Villa License Truth Scan** and **Owner–Operator Substance Audit** as its intake modules. The deliverable should lead to one of three outputs:

1. **Green:** lawful principal, compatible KBLI, complete property permits, coherent cash/tax flow, and no hidden control;
2. **Conditional:** model is commercially promising but requires a named written authority answer before operation;
3. **No-go:** paper operator, wrong principal, wrong land/building use, incompatible KBLI, residual sweep, or foreign operational control.

For the first pilot, use a genuine brownfield owner-led asset with clean title/right, existing property permits, complete historical accounting, and a cooperative operator. Test the disclosed management model in parallel on paper, but do not activate it until 55203/55901 compatibility and the old-to-new KBLI treatment are confirmed in writing.
