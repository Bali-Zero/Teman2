### Extension procedure 2025-2026
- File via eVisa (online), max 14 days before expiry, latest before expiry
- 1-5 days later: physical summons at Kanim of declared domicile (Denpasar/Ngurah Rai)
- Biometric + photo + interview MANDATORY for every extension
- DO NOT travel during process (you must stay in declared jurisdiction)
- Processing: 3 working days from PNBP payment (Permen 11/2024 Pasal 99(2))
- e-ITAS issued via email (Pasal 161) — physical card optional

### Bali C1 + PMA shareholder (Circolare Kanim 31 Mar 2026)

## D2 — KITAS → KITAP Conversion (Alih Status)

### Eligibility (Permen 11/2024 Pasal 173)
**Eligible**: workers (E23), religious (rohaniwan), investors (E28), family reunion (E31), repatriation (E32), Second Home/Special Skills/World Figure/Lanjut Usia 60+ (E33).
**NOT eligible**: E29 research, E30 student, E33G remote worker (max 2y, can't reach 3y), E35 working holiday.

### Standard rule
3 consecutive years on KITAS. Apply max 30 days before expiry (Pasal 174(2)).

### Exceptions
- **E28B/E28G Golden Visa**: direct KITAP-INV after 5y (no language test, maintain investment)
- **E31A spouse of WNI**: 3y KITAS + marriage min 2y registered (Pasal 179(3))
- **E32 ex-WNI direct KITAP**: ONLY if losing WNI citizenship while physically in Indonesia (Pasal 120(2)+(4))

### E28A KITAP threshold INCREASED
KITAS E28A: Rp 10mld shares. **KITAP E28A: Rp 15mld** (Permen 11/2024 Pasal 176(2)(c)).

### Documents (Permen 11/2024 Pasal 176)
- KITAS valid copy
- Passport: NO 6-month minimum residual required (special exemption)
- Pernyataan Integrasi (mandatory)
- Sponsor declaration (if applicable)
- Marriage cert (E31A)
- NO biaya hidup proof required (special exemption)
- SKK/SKLD/SKTT: NOT in central law list — local Bali practice only

### Timeline (~14 working days)
- Kanim verification: 3d
- Kanwil (regional) review: 3d
- Dirjen Imigrasi approval: 5d
- Kanim issuance: 3d

### PNBP
- KITAP 5y: Rp 7.000.000
- KITAP 10y: Rp 12.000.000
- KITAP unlimited: Rp 15.000.000

### Output
e-KITAP virtual (Pasal 161) via email. Optional physical card.

### Language test
B1 Bahasa Indonesia + Pancasila/UUD 1945 knowledge.
**Exempt**: Golden Visa investors (E28B/E28G).
NB-2 has NO documented exemption for age 60+/65+, NO mention of BIPA/UKBI specific exam names.

### Maintenance
- 1st KITAP: 5y
- Then "tidak terbatas" (unlimited) renewal
- Lapor Diri every 5 years (FREE, Permen 29/2021 Pasal 144(1))

### Revocation (Pasal 141)
- Crime against state
- Activities threatening security
- Violation of integration declaration
- Hiring illegal foreign workers
- False data in application
- Divorce from WNI spouse — UNLESS marriage already >10 years (Pasal 141(1)(g))
- Leaving Indonesia >12 months continuously (auto-expiry)

### Citizenship path
After 5y consecutive KITAP (or 10y total legal residence). Test bahasa/Pancasila. **MUST renounce original citizenship** (Indonesia no dual citizenship for adults).

## D3 — Visa-to-Visa Conversion (Alih Status)

### NOT convertible to KITAS (Pasal 163(2))
- **B1 VoA** (and B2/B3/B4)
- **A1/A4 Bebas Visa**
- **Crew permits (A36/A37)**

These ALWAYS require exit + new offshore VITAS.

### Convertible Visit→KITAS (Pasal 163(1))
- C1 Tourism → E28A investor / E33G remote / others (most common path)
- C2 Business → E23 work (if RPTKA approved) / E28A
- C12 Pre-investment → E28A natural path (after PT PMA setup)
- C18 Work Trial → E23 (NOT automatic, needs RPTKA approval first)
- C22 Internship → E30 student / E23 work
- D1/D2 Multi-entry → KITAS if requirements met

### Bridging Visa = Izin Tinggal Peralihan (NEW Permen 11/2024 Pasal 86A + 94A)
- Duration: 60 days, NON-extendable
- Apply max 3 days before current visa expires
- Available for: VoA holder, KITAS holder, KITAP holder
- No overstay if pending approval (Pasal 94A(5))

### KITAS → KITAS (change category/sponsor)
- Pernyataan pelepasan from previous sponsor (Pasal 167(3))
- Approved by Dirjen in 5 working days
- e-ITAS issued in 3 days
- **WARNING**: SE Kemnaker 3/836/2026 enforces "One Sponsor Policy" for E23 work — sponsor on ITK MUST equal sponsor on RPTKA, else alih status DENIED → forced offshore scheme

### Onshore vs Offshore
**Onshore (alih status)** advantages (Pasal 168(2)):
- No 6-month passport residual required
- No biaya hidup proof
- No overstay if paid before expiry

**Offshore wait times**:
- A: RPTKA almost done → 3-7 working days
- B: RPTKA in progress → 10-20 calendar days
- C: Just started → 3-6 weeks

### KITAS PNBP comparison
- Offshore: VITAS Telex USD 150 + KITAS Rp 2.5-3jt
- Onshore: Verifikasi Visa Cat I Rp 1jt OR Cat II Rp 2jt + KITAS fee
