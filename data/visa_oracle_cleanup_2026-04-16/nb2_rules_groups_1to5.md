## Group 4 — KITAS Investor / Research / Student / Skills / Work-Travel (E28/E29/E30/E33/E35)

## Group 5 — KITAS Family (E31) and Ex-WNI (E32)

**Pasal 105(8) of Permen 11/2024** governs E31 family duration. KITAP conversion after 3 consecutive years.
