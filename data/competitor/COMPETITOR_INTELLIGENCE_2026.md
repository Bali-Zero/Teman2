## 1. Executive Summary

Bali Zero operates in a fragmented Indonesian consulting market dominated by five established players: **Emerhub** (the multi-country incumbent with $3M revenue but declining workforce), **InCorp Indonesia** (PE-backed regional giant with scale but terrible employer reputation), **LetsMoveIndonesia** (the transparent-pricing pioneer now shrinking fast), **Seven Stones Indonesia** (the content-and-property hybrid punching above its weight in web traffic), and **Acclime** (the pan-Asian enterprise play). The market is simultaneously being pressured from above by global EOR platforms (Deel, Horizons, Multiplier) that threaten to commoditize entity setup, and from below by a dozen small Bali-based visa agencies competing on price. Bali Zero's position is unique and defensible: we are the **only competitor with a production AI stack** (96 MCP tools, 56K-node knowledge graph, 66K indexed legal documents, KBLI 2025 Navigator covering all 1,563 codes), the only one offering a unified client portal with real-time visa tracking, and the only one with an omnichannel AI assistant (Zantara) operating 24/7 across WhatsApp, Telegram, Web, and Instagram. Every competitor either lacks technology entirely (LMI uses Canva; Seven Stones uses QuickBooks) or uses legacy CRM stacks (Emerhub on Salesforce; InCorp on HubSpot) without any AI layer. The critical strategic window is **now**: all four Tier 1 competitors are shrinking or stagnating in headcount, signaling market stress, while Bali Zero's AI-first model enables scale without proportional headcount growth. The primary risks are InCorp's PE-funded expansion capacity and Deel's potential downstream move into full entity setup services.

## 3. Tier 1: Direct Competitors (Detailed Profiles)

### 3.1 EMERHUB

**Company Overview**

Emerhub is the most established multi-country consulting firm in Southeast Asia's mid-market segment. Founded in 2011 with headquarters in Jakarta, the company has expanded to six countries: Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, and (historically) Thailand. Their positioning is "lowering entry barriers in emerging markets" — targeting foreign entrepreneurs and SMEs who want to set up in Southeast Asia without navigating local bureaucracy.

With 65 employees and an estimated $3M annual revenue, Emerhub is the largest dedicated competitor by brand recognition in the Indonesia market. However, their workforce has contracted by 8.5% year-over-year, suggesting revenue pressure or strategic downsizing.

### 3.2 INCORP INDONESIA (formerly Cekindo)

### 3.3 LETSMOVEINDONESIA (LMI Consultancy)

**Company Overview**

LetsMoveIndonesia (LMI) was the **disruptor** of the Indonesian consulting market when it launched in 2015-2016. Their breakthrough was simple but revolutionary at the time: **they published their prices**. In a market where every competitor said "request a quote," LMI put prices on their website and claimed to be "the first agency to openly advertise prices." This built enormous trust, particularly among the digital nomad and expat community.

Headquartered at Bellagio Mall in Jakarta, LMI operates an impressive 4-office network: Jakarta, plus three Bali locations (Seminyak, Canggu, Sanur). This physical presence across Bali's main expat hubs gives them walk-in accessibility that most competitors lack.

However, LMI is in **serious decline**. Headcount has dropped **23.5% year-over-year** to just 26 employees — the steepest decline among all competitors. Monthly web traffic is a modest 5,977, and their tech stack is essentially non-existent (Canva, TikTok, Adobe — tools for social media, not business operations).

_Source: letsmoveindonesia.com — Verified March 15, 2026_

- **"5-50 million IDR cheaper"** than competitors (their website claim)
- This translates to approximately **40-60% cheaper** than Emerhub's published rates
- Currently running: **10% off all visa services until March 31, 2026** — suggesting demand pressure

## 5. Tier 3: Global Players

### 5.1 DEEL

| Attribute             | Detail                                                   |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |

Deel is the 800-pound gorilla of global employment services. Their EOR (Employer of Record) product allows companies to hire in Indonesia without setting up a local entity — directly threatening the PT PMA setup revenue stream of every competitor in this report, including Bali Zero.

**Current threat level:** **Medium-High and increasing.** Deel's current Indonesia offering is primarily EOR (hiring without entity setup), but they are steadily expanding into entity management, compliance, and immigration services. If Deel launches a full-service PT PMA setup product, it would instantly become the most well-funded competitor in the market.
