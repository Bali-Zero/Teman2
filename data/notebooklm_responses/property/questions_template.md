## Hak Pakai (Foreign Usage Rights) (10 domande)

### 001_hak_pakai_definition

```
Cos'è Hak Pakai per stranieri in Indonesia?

Rispondi in dettaglio:
- Hak Pakai definition (Right to Use)
- Solo foreigners possono avere Hak Pakai
- Duration: 30 years initial
- Renewal: 20 years + 30 years (total 80 years max)
- Property types allowed:
  * House (residential)
  * Villa
  * Apartment/condo
- Restrictions:
  * Location (some provinces restrict foreigners)
  * Size limitations (varies by province)
  * Must be for residence (not commercial)
- Selling rights:
  * Can sell to other foreigners
  * Can sell to Indonesians (converts to Hak Milik)
- Inheritance rules

Cita PP 18/2021 articoli rilevanti.
Lunghezza: 400-500 parole.
```

### 002_hak_pakai_requirements

```
Requirements per ottenere Hak Pakai come straniero?

Requisiti completi:
- KITAS or KITAP holder (any type: work, investor, retirement)
- Property value minimum (varies by province):
  * Bali: Rp 1-2 billion
  * Jakarta: Rp 3-5 billion
  * Other areas: varies
- Physical residence in Indonesia
- Good standing visa status
- Property must be for personal use

Documents needed:
- Passport + KITAS/KITAP
- NPWP (Tax ID)
- Bank statements (proof of funds)
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)

Process:
- Purchase agreement
- Notary (PPAT) processes title
- Registration at BPN (Land Office)
- Issuance Hak Pakai certificate

Timeline: 3-6 months.
Lunghezza: 350-450 parole.
```

### 003_hak_pakai_renewal

```
Hak Pakai renewal process prima della scadenza 30 anni?

Processo step-by-step:
- When to start: 2 years before expiration
- Requirements renewal:
  * Still hold valid KITAS/KITAP
  * Property used for residence
  * No violations
  * NPWP active
- Application via BPN (Land Office)
- Notary assistance
- Fee structure:
  * Government fees
  * Notary fees
  * BPHTB (transfer tax) may apply
- Timeline: 6-12 months
- First renewal: +20 years
- Second renewal: +30 years

What if not renewed? (property reverts to state)
Lunghezza: 300-400 parole.
```

## Hak Milik (Freehold - Citizens Only) (5 domande)

### 011_hak_milik_definition

```
Cos'è Hak Milik e perché stranieri NON possono averlo?

Spiegazione:
- Hak Milik definition (Freehold ownership)
- ONLY Indonesian citizens allowed
- Duration: perpetual (no renewal needed)
- Property rights:
  * Full ownership
  * Can sell, rent, mortgage freely
  * Inheritance to heirs
- Why foreigners excluded:
  * Agrarian Law (UUPA) protection
  * National land sovereignty
  * Prevent foreign land accumulation

Conversion scenarios:
- Foreigner marries Indonesian:
  * Can property be Hak Milik? (NO, unless prenup)
  * Spouse can own Hak Milik separately
- Indonesian sells to foreigner:
  * Must convert Hak Milik → Hak Pakai

Cita UUPA (Basic Agrarian Law) articles.
Lunghezza: 350-450 parole.
```

### 012_nominee_arrangement_risks

```
Nominee arrangements (prestanome) per property: legali? Rischi?

ATTENZIONE: Questa è una zona grigia legale.

Cos'è nominee arrangement:
- Foreigner funds purchase
- Indonesian "nominee" holds Hak Milik title
- Private agreement gives control to foreigner

Legality:
- Technically ILLEGAL per Agrarian Law
- Considered "circumvention" of law
- Widespread in practice (especially Bali)
- Government crackdowns periodically

Risks:
- Nominee can sell without permission
- Nominee can mortgage property
- Family disputes if nominee dies
- Legal recourse very limited
- Property can be confiscated by government

Alternatives safer:
- Proper Hak Pakai (legal, secure)
- Long-term rental (sewa)
- PT PMA ownership (for business property)

Lunghezza: 400-500 parole.
```

## HGB (Hak Guna Bangunan - Building Rights) (4 domande)

### 016_hgb_definition

```
Cos'è HGB (Hak Guna Bangunan) e quando si usa?

Definizione:
- HGB = Right to Build
- For commercial/business buildings
- Duration: 30 years + extensions
- Who can hold:
  * Indonesian citizens
  * Indonesian companies
  * PT PMA (for business purposes)
- Typical uses:
  * Apartments/condos (developer holds HGB)
  * Hotels
  * Commercial buildings
  * Factories

Difference from Hak Pakai:
- HGB: business/commercial
- Hak Pakai: residential

Foreigners + HGB:
- Cannot hold HGB personally
- Can hold via PT PMA for business
- Apartment buyers get Strata Title (SHM Satuan Rumah Susun)

Lunghezza: 300-400 parole.
```

## Rental & Lease (Hak Sewa) (6 domande)

### 020_long_term_rental

```
Long-term rental agreements per villas/houses in Indonesia?

Contratto sewa (rental):
- Duration: any period (common 1-25 years)
- Advantages:
  * No KITAS requirement
  * No minimum property value
  * Simpler process than Hak Pakai
  * Lower upfront cost
- Typical structure:
  * Upfront payment (e.g., 5 years prepaid)
  * Renewable
  * Notarized agreement

Legal protection:
- Contract registered at notary
- Can include clauses:
  * Renovation rights
  * Sublease rights
  * Extension options
- Less secure than Hak Pakai (owner can sell)

Common terms:
- 3-year, 5-year, 10-year, 25-year leases
- Annual vs lump-sum payment
- Utilities responsibilities
- Maintenance obligations

Lunghezza: 350-450 parole.
```

### 021_villa_rental_business

```
Villa rental business (short-term rentals) come straniero?

Options:
1. Personal Hak Pakai + rent out:
   - RISKY: Hak Pakai for "personal residence"
   - Short-term rental = commercial use
   - Can violate Hak Pakai terms

2. PT PMA + HGB/Hak Pakai:
   - Proper business structure
   - KBLI 68100 (Real Estate Leasing)
   - Or KBLI 55xxx (Accommodation)
   - Requires business licenses

Licensing requirements:
- TDUP (Tourism Business Registration)
- Health permits
- IMB (Building Permit)
- Environmental clearance
- Tax registration (NPWP)

Tax implications:
- Income tax on rental income
- PPN 11% (if PKP registered)
- Annual reporting

Lunghezza: 400-500 parole.
```

## Property Taxes (5 domande)

### 026_pbb_land_building_tax

```
PBB (Pajak Bumi dan Bangunan) - Land & Building Tax?

Dettagli:
- PBB definition (annual property tax)
- Calculation basis:
  * NJOP (government assessed value)
  * Not market value
  * Updated periodically by province
- Tax rate:
  * 0.1% - 0.3% of NJOP
  * Varies by province and property type
- Who pays:
  * Property title holder (Hak Pakai, Hak Milik, etc.)
  * Or tenant if specified in rental agreement
- Payment:
  * Annual (usually Sep-Oct deadline)
  * Via bank transfer or online
- Penalties late payment

Exemptions:
- Low-value properties (below NJOP threshold)

Lunghezza: 300-400 parole.
```

### 027_bphtb_transfer_tax

```
BPHTB (Transfer Tax) quando si compra property?

BPHTB details:
- BPHTB = Bea Perolehan Hak atas Tanah dan Bangunan
- One-time tax on property transfer
- Rate: 5% of transaction value
- Paid by: buyer (usually)
- Calculated on:
  * Sale price, OR
  * NJOP (whichever higher)
- Exemptions:
  * First-time buyers (up to Rp 60-80 million)
  * Varies by province
- Payment:
  * Before title transfer
  * At notary or online
  * Receipt required for BPN registration

Additional costs:
- Notary fees (1-2%)
- BPN registration fees
- Total: 6-8% of property value

Lunghezza: 300-400 parole.
```

## Property Purchase Process (5 domande)

### 031_property_purchase_steps

```
Step-by-step process per comprare property come straniero (Hak Pakai)?

Full process:

1. Property search & selection
2. Price negotiation
3. Due diligence:
   - Title verification (certificate check at BPN)
   - Liens/encumbrances check
   - Building permits (IMB) verification
   - Zoning compliance
4. Purchase agreement (PPJB - Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli):
   - 10-30% deposit
   - Conditions precedent
   - Notarized
5. Full payment
6. Title transfer at notary (PPAT):
   - AJB (Akta Jual Beli) signing
   - BPHTB payment
7. Registration at BPN (Land Office):
   - Title conversion (if needed)
   - Hak Pakai issuance
8. Certificate delivery

Timeline: 3-6 months total.
Costs breakdown.

Lunghezza: 400-500 parole.
```

## Provincial Variations (5 domande)

### 036_bali_property_rules

```
Regole specifiche Bali per property ownership stranieri?

Bali specifics:
- Minimum property value:
  * Residential: Rp 1-2 billion (varies by regency)
  * Commercial: higher thresholds
- Location restrictions:
  * Tourism zones: more permissive
  * Agricultural land: restricted
  * Coastal areas: special rules
  * Rice field (sawah) conversion limits
- Hak Pakai availability:
  * Widely available in Denpasar, Badung, Gianyar
  * More restrictive in Tabanan, Buleleng
- Governor regulations (Perda):
  * Updated periodically
  * Check latest Perda Bali

Additional requirements:
- TDUP if short-term rental
- Environmental impact (AMDAL) for large projects
- Community approval (banjar) sometimes needed

Lunghezza: 350-450 parole.
```

## CRITICAL NOTES

### For ALL Property Questions:

1. **Title Types Hierarchy:**
   - Hak Milik (Freehold) = Indonesians ONLY
   - Hak Pakai (Usage) = Foreigners OK
   - HGB (Building) = Business/commercial
   - Hak Sewa (Rental) = No ownership

2. **Nominee Warnings:**
   - Always warn about nominee risks
   - Technically illegal
   - Not recommended
   - Explain legal alternatives (Hak Pakai, rental)

3. **KITAS Requirement:**
   - Hak Pakai requires valid KITAS/KITAP
   - Any type OK (work, investor, retirement)
   - Must maintain visa status for renewals

4. **Provincial Differences:**
   - Bali: Minimum Rp 1-2B
   - Jakarta: Minimum Rp 3-5B
   - Always mention variations exist
