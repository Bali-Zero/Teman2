## PPN (VAT) (8 domande)

```
Aliquota PPN (VAT) in Indonesia 2026?

IMPORTANTE: Conferma aliquota corretta 11% (NOT 12%)

Spiega:
- Aliquota standard: 11% dal 2022 (UU 7/2021)
- Timeline storico:
  * Pre-2022: 10%
  * 2022-presente: 11%
  * Pianificato 12%: POSTPONED (not yet implemented)
- Quando si applica PPN:
  * Goods sold
  * Services rendered
  * Imports
- Exemptions (healthcare, education, basic foods)
- Zero-rated (exports)

Cita UU 7/2021 Article 7.
Lunghezza: 300-400 parole.
```

### 018_pph_21_expat_183_days

```
PPh 21 per expat: regola 183 giorni tax residency?

Spiega:
- 183-day rule per tax residency
- If < 183 days/year: non-resident
  * Taxed on Indonesia-source income only
  * Flat 20% rate (PPh 26)
  * Tax treaty may apply
- If >= 183 days/year: resident
  * Taxed on worldwide income
  * Progressive PPh 21 rates
  * Tax treaty benefits
- How to count days (physical presence)
- KITAS/KITAP implications
- Reporting requirements

Cita tax treaty provisions se applicabile.
```

## Tax Treaties (DTA) (6 domande)

### 037_tax_treaty_indonesia_italia

```
Tax Treaty Indonesia-Italia (DTA): vantaggi per investitori italiani?

Dettagli treaty:
- Reduced withholding rates:
  * Dividends: 10-15% (vs 20% domestic)
  * Interest: 10% (vs 20% domestic)
  * Royalties: 10-15% (vs 20% domestic)
- Business profits:
  * Permanent Establishment (PE) definition
  * When Italian company not taxed in Indonesia
- Capital gains treatment
- Dispute resolution mechanism
- Certificate of Residence (SKD):
  * Issued by Agenzia delle Entrate (Italy)
  * Submit to Indonesian company for reduced rates

Lunghezza: 400-500 parole.
```

### 038_tax_treaty_application

```
Come applicare tax treaty benefits in Indonesia?

Step-by-step:
1. Obtain Certificate of Residence (SKD/DGT form)
   - From home country tax authority
   - Proves tax residency
2. Submit SKD to Indonesian payer:
   - Before payment made
   - Or claim refund later
3. Indonesian payer applies reduced rate:
   - Withhold at treaty rate (not 20%)
   - Report to DJP with SKD copy
4. If already withheld at 20%:
   - File refund claim with DJP
   - Timeline: 12 months
5. Annual reporting requirements

Common pitfalls to avoid.
Lunghezza: 350-450 parole.
```

   - NOT 12% (pianificato ma postponed)
   - Sempre citare UU 7/2021 Article 7

2. **PPh Badan Rate:**
   - Standard: 22% (UU 7/2021)
   - Small company reduction: possible
   - Always cite current law

3. **Tax Treaty Benefits:**
   - Certificate of Residence (SKD) obbligatorio
   - Mention common treaties: Italy, Singapore, USA, Australia
   - Reduced withholding rates specifici

4. **Citations Required:**
