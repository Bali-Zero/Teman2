   - Bank statements
6. Enter Indonesia on E-Visa

   - Immigration office
   - Investment verification
   - KITAS issuance (up to 2 years)

Timeline total: 4-6 mesi
Costi stimati: dettaglio

Cita:
- PP 28/2025 (KBLI + PMA)
- PP 31/2013 (Immigration)
- Permenkumham 28/2024 (E-Visa)

Lunghezza: 600-800 parole.

```
Se KITAS scade, cosa succede a Hak Pakai property?

Scenario critico:
- Straniero con Hak Pakai (30 anni)
- KITAS expires (non rinnovato)
- Property status?

Legal implications:

Immediate:
- Hak Pakai certificate still valid (30 years)
- NO automatic cancellation
- Property ownership continues

BUT renewal problems:
- Hak Pakai renewal (dopo 30 anni) REQUIRES valid KITAS
- If no KITAS at renewal:
  * Cannot renew Hak Pakai
  * Property may revert to state
  * Or forced sale

Solutions:
1. Maintain KITAS status (any type):
   - KITAS Investor
   - KITAS Retirement (E33E)
   - KITAS Work
2. Convert to long-term rental agreement:
   - Sell Hak Pakai
   - Rent back from new owner
3. Sell property before KITAS expires

Risk management critical.
Lunghezza: 400-600 parole.
```

### 101_hak_pakai_property_tax_obligations

```
Hak Pakai property: quali tasse devo pagare ogni anno?

Tax obligations proprietario straniero:

1. PBB (Land & Building Tax):
   - Annual property tax
   - 0.1-0.3% of NJOP (assessed value)
   - Due: Sep-Oct annually
   - Payment: online or bank
   - Penalty late payment: 2% per month

2. BPHTB (Transfer Tax):
   - ONE-TIME at purchase
   - 5% of transaction value
   - Paid by buyer

3. Income Tax (if rental income):
   - If rent out property (even long-term)
   - PPh on rental income:
     * Individual: progressive rates
     * Or final tax 10% (gross rental)
   - Annual reporting

4. Capital Gains Tax (if sell):
   - 2.5% of gross sale price (final tax)
   - Or 25% of net gain (if opt-in)

NPWP requirement:
- Need NPWP for property purchase
- Annual tax filing obligation

Exemptions:
- PBB exemption if low-value property
- Personal residence (not rented): no income tax

Lunghezza: 400-600 parole.
```

1. **Always show BOTH domain perspectives:**
   - Company setup → Visa implications
   - Visa status → Property rights
   - Property ownership → Tax obligations

2. **Timeline coordination:**
   - Which comes first?
   - Sequential vs parallel processes
   - Dependencies explicit

3. **Legal entity separation:**
   - PT PMA (company) ≠ Individual (visa/property holder)
   - Different tax treatments
   - Different ownership rights

4. **Risk scenarios:**
   - What if Company fails → Visa status?
   - What if KITAS expires → Property ownership?
