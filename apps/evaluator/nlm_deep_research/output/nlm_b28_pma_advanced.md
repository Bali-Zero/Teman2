## Nominee Arrangements & Consequences

- [~ provisional | 0.71] In Indonesia, **nominee arrangements**—where a business is formally held by an Indonesian citizen (WNI) on behalf of a foreigner—are considered **illegal and unenforceable**.
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Fines and Imprisonment:\*\* Engaging in illegal activities that bypass state regulations can result in significant administrative fines or, in extreme cases related to environmental or safety violations, prison sentences [3].
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Direct Ownership:** A PT PMA allows the foreigner to hold shares and property rights (such as **HGB\*\*) directly and legally, removing the risks associated with nominees.
- [~ provisional | 0.71] 1. **Establishing a PT PMA:** This replaces the risky nominee structure with a 100% legal entity.
