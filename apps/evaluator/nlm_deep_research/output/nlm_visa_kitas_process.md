## Renewal & Extension

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] The process for a KITAS renewal in Indonesia generally takes **3 to 4 weeks** to complete. It is critical to start the application at least **30 days before your current permit expires** to ensure there is enough time for processing and to avoid complications [7].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Application Window:\*\* You must apply for an extension at least 30 days before your KITAS expires [7].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Please note that for certain types of KITAS, such as the Spouse KITAS (E26), specific documents like a valid marriage certificate and an up-to-date domicile registration (SKTT) are required for the renewal [7].
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Extension Duration:\*\* A Limited Stay Permit (KITAS/ITAS) can be granted for a maximum of 2 years per extension [3].
- [~ provisional | 0.71] KITAS Sponsorship:\*\* Companies must show proof of active BPJS for local employees to sponsor or renew KITAS (Work Permits) for foreign staff. [8]
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Registration is mandatory for all PT PMA companies with employees and all KITAS holders. Proof of BPJS payment is required for the extension of a KITAS [8].
- [~ provisional | 0.70] Extensions:** The 1-year Retirement KITAS can be extended. The price for a Retirement Extension through Bali Zero is **10,000,000 IDR\*\* [Tool].

## Conversion to KITAP

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Share Ownership:\*\* For those seeking to change status from a Limited Stay Permit (KITAS) to a Permanent Stay Permit (KITAP), share ownership must be at least IDR 15,000,000,000 (15 billion) [3].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Permanent Residency (KITAP):** After 5 years of holding a Retirement KITAS, you are eligible to apply for a **Retirement KITAP**, which offers permanent residency [8]. The price for a Retirement KITAP with a Multiple Exit Re-entry Permit (MERP) is **45,000,000 IDR\*\* [Tool].
