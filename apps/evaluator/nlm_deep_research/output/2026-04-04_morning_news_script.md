## STORY 1: IMMIGRATION — Bali Immigration Slams Door on C1 Extensions for Company Owners

As of March 31, 2026, Denpasar and Ngurah Rai Immigration offices automatically reject C1 Visit Visa extensions for any foreigners registered as shareholders or directors in an Indonesian PT PMA. The system cross-references company records in real-time. The One Sponsor Policy (SE Kemnaker No. 3/836) requires exact sponsor matching for KITAS conversion.

**Legal basis:** Pengumuman Kanim Denpasar & Ngurah Rai (March 31, 2026): C1 extensions rejected for PT PMA shareholders. UU No. 63/2024: MERP integrated into KITAS/KITAP.

This is the most impactful immigration change in 2026 for our clients. If you own a PT PMA and are still on a C1 tourist visa, your extension will be rejected on the spot.

**Action required:** Contact Bali Zero immediately to transition to the correct visa type. Do not attempt to extend your C1 if you are a company shareholder.

## STORY 2: COMPANY SETUP — PT PMA Entry Barrier Slashed 75% But Read the Fine Print

BKPM Regulation No. 5 of 2025 reduces minimum paid-up capital from IDR 10B to IDR 2.5B. Total investment plan still must exceed IDR 10B per KBLI. 12-month capital lock-up applies. Investor KITAS E28A still requires IDR 10B personal shareholding.

**Legal basis:** BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025, Article 26-27. PP 28/2025 on OSS continuous compliance.

Easier entry does not mean easier residency. The gap between company formation capital and KITAS threshold creates a two-tier system.

**Action required:** Verify your KBLI codes are updated to 2025 standard by June 18, 2026.

## STORY 3: TAX & FISCAL — Individual Tax Returns: Penalty Waived Until April 30

The government waived the IDR 100,000 late penalty for 2025 individual SPT Tahunan submitted by April 30, 2026. Corporate SPT must be filed via Coretax by April 30. LKPM Q1 due April 7.

File now, not later. The Coretax system is new and unpredictable.

**Action required:** File SPT by April 30. LKPM Q1 due April 7 — do not miss it.

## STORY 4: PROPERTY — Nominee Deals Now a Crime: Up to 5 Years Prison

Perda Bali No. 4/2026 signed February 24 by Governor Koster criminalizes nominee land ownership. Both parties face up to 5 years prison and IDR 1B fine. Mandatory building demolition. Villa NIB deadline passed March 31.

This is not a warning. It is law. Restructure nominee arrangements immediately.

**Action required:** If you have a nominee arrangement, seek legal restructuring to Hak Pakai or PT PMA with HGB.

## STORY 5: COMPLIANCE — One Missed Meeting Freezes Your Entire Company

Permenkumham 49/2025 makes Annual GMS a mandatory filing. Must hold within 6 months of fiscal year-end, convert to notarial deed, upload to AHU Online within 30 days. Failure triggers automatic AHU system block.

A blocked company cannot process KITAS, update licenses, or change directors. This is operational paralysis.

**Action required:** Schedule 2025 Annual GMS before June 30, 2026. Coordinate with notary early.
