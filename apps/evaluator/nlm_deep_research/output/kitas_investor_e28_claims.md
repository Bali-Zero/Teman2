## 1. Capital & Investment Thresholds

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Differentiation:** The context provides requirements for the **E28C Investor Visa**, which is a separate category for those **not\*\* establishing a company. That visa requires a commitment to purchase Indonesian Government bonds or public company shares worth between USD 350,000 and USD 700,000 [2].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] E28B (Company Establishment):\*\* For those entering Indonesia to set up a company. This requires a commitment to invest at least USD 2,500,000 (for a 5-year stay) or USD 5,000,000 (for a 10-year stay) within 90 days of entry [2].
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Minimum Share Ownership:** For an Investor KITAS to be eligible for conversion, the foreigner must hold share ownership of at least **IDR 15,000,000,000 (15 Billion)\*\* [1].
- [~ provisional | 0.71] Minimum Capital:** For an Investor KITAS (such as the E28A), the company must have a minimum paid-up capital of **IDR 2.5 billion\*\* [7].

## 2. Eligibility, Categories & Distinctions

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Residence Permit:** Foreign directors typically apply for the **E28A Investor Residence Permit\*\*, which is valid for two years and allows for multiple entries [8].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] E28B Investor Visa:\*\* Designed for those establishing a company in Indonesia. It has a 90-day initial validity and can be processed within 5–7 working days [1].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Investment Commitment:** For the E28B Investor Visa (establishment phase), the applicant or sponsor must commit to a minimum investment value of **US$2,500,000\*\* for a 5-year stay [1].
