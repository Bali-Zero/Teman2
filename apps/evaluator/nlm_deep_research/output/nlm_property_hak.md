## HGB (Right to Build)

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Title Conversion for Companies:\*\* If you are managing property through a company (PT PMA), you may need to convert the land title to HGB (Right to Build). This process typically involves negotiated fees plus additional costs of approximately 7-10% [8].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Property/Land Documents:\*\* A valid land certificate (HGB) or a formal lease agreement.
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] If the HGB period ends and no application for extension or renewal is filed within the required timeframe, the land rights are considered to have expired and the land reverts to the state [8].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Renewal (Pembaruan):** If the initial term or extension has already ended, the holder can apply for a renewal. This application must be submitted **no later than 2 years after the expiry date\*\* of the HGB or its extension [1, 6].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Extension (Perpanjangan):** An application for an extension can be submitted once the land has been utilized according to the purpose of the grant, or **at the latest before the current HGB period expires\*\* [6].

## Hak Milik Restrictions

- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] One-Year Rule**: If a foreigner acquires Hak Milik through inheritance (without a will) or through marriage (mixing of assets), they must release or transfer that right within **one year\*\* [3, 4].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Deed of Establishment (Akta):\*\* Prepared by a notary and approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights.
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] PT Deed (Akta) & Ministry Approval:\*\* Official PDF of the company establishment and Kemenkumham validation [1].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] E28B (Company Establishment):\*\* For those entering Indonesia to set up a company. This requires a commitment to invest at least USD 2,500,000 (for a 5-year stay) or USD 5,000,000 (for a 10-year stay) within 90 days of entry [2].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Oil and Gas:** According to **Regional Regulation No. 20 of 2008**, any business entity or permanent establishment wishing to open a representative office must submit an application to the **Regent (Bupati)\*\* through the relevant local Department (Dinas) [1].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Investment Commitment:** For the E28B Investor Visa (establishment phase), the applicant or sponsor must commit to a minimum investment value of **US$2,500,000\*\* for a 5-year stay [1].
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] Prohibition:** Foreigners are strictly **prohibited\*\* from holding a Freehold Title (SHM) under the Indonesian Constitution and the Basic Agrarian Law (UUPA 1960).
- [✓ VERIFIED | 0.77] For a property transfer, a Sale and Purchase Deed (AJB) must be processed. This document is typically handled by a Notary or a Land Deed Official (PPAT), who then submits the SHM application to the BPN [4].
