# Jakarta Intercultural School Campus

**Source:** JIS Jakarta (T2)

We have four divisions — early years, elementary, middle and high school — spread across three sprawling campuses, all located in South Jakarta.
Our three campuses are regarded by many as the most beautiful school grounds in the city. JIS Middle School and High School are located on one campus (CIL) along Jl. Terogong Raya in Cilandak, which connects to Pondok Indah Elementary (PIE) via a walking bridge.
With roots dating back more than 70 years, our historic Pattimura Elementary (PEL) campus can be found in Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta.

Our Facilities
Our expansive garden-like campuses offer lush greenery while hosting an immersive array of leading-edge facilities, including swimming pools, sports fields, STEAM labs, theaters, and libraries. These inviting spaces provide countless opportunities for learning, growth, and collaboration, bringing our community together and strengthening our shared spirit of being Best for the World.

- 280+ classrooms, including state-of-the-art science, robotics, and design tech labs, and a spacious lecture hall.
- More than six main buildings in each division (Elementary School, Middle School, and High School).
- Four libraries with 100,000+ titles
- Dedicated creative and performing arts facilities, including classrooms, dance, music, and visual arts studios, as well as four professional-standard theaters equipped with high-quality sound and lighting systems to support a wide range of performances.
- Dedicated play areas for Early Years and Elementary students.
- Four multi-purpose gymnasiums that support a range of sports.
- Three covered (semi-outdoor) tennis courts.
- A 400-meter, eight-lane running track surfaced with a high-performance material certified by World Athletics.
- One outdoor soccer field (FIFA certified) and rugby field (World Rugby certified) with artificial turf, and one multi-purpose field with fresh grass.
- A dedicated golf driving range.
- Two 25-meter swimming pools and one 17-meter practice pool.
- International standard baseball and softball fields.
- A health and wellness fitness center with cardio and strength training equipment.
- Athletic training facilities with an on-campus physiotherapy center.
- Seven food service venues, including cafeterias, grab and go counters, and juice and coffee stations.
- Spacious courtyards for hosting learning activities and celebrations.
- An open-air amphitheater and a covered semi-outdoor stage.
- PEL theater with a 469-seat capacity and a LCD screen.
- A new JIS Learning Center Secondary Program building that supports the development of life skills.
  Our facilities are an important part of the learning experience at JIS. We renovate and update our facilities on a regular basis to enhance our teaching practices and the student experience.

  Impact on the Environment
  The Cilandak campus (CIL) and Pondok Indah Elementary (PIE) contribute to the ecological service of the surrounding area, their trees removing more than 60 pounds of air pollutants and 25 pounds of carbon monoxide every year.
  Meanwhile, solar panels on the High School Gym are expected to reduce 2.15 tons of carbon dioxide, which is equivalent to planting three trees, and share clean energy with other learning spaces across CIL.
