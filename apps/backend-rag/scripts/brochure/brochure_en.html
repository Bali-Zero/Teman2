Immigration

Stay permits

A KITAS is a limited stay permit — six months to five years, renewable. A KITAP is the permanent one. Either will normally make you an Indonesian tax resident, which is decided by how long you stay and why, not by the permit alone.

Offshore or onshore

Offshore means the permit is applied for while you are outside Indonesia — the usual route, and the cheaper one. Onshore (Altus) is applied for while you are already here: faster, no flight, slightly more.

Tax & accounting

Staying compliant

A PT PMA files withholding tax every month, VAT every month once it is VAT-registered (PKP), and a corporate return every year. If you hold a KITAS you will normally be a tax resident yourself, with your own annual return. We act as your local representative for both.

The numbers that decide your structure

Personal income tax runs 5% to 35% progressive; corporate income tax is 22%, with reduced rates for smaller companies; VAT is 12%. An NPWP is mandatory once you hold a KITAS, the annual return is due 31 March, and a tax resident is taxed on worldwide income — which is exactly why the structure is worth getting right before the first filing, not after.

Property — before you sign anything

A foreign national cannot hold Hak Milik, Indonesian freehold. What you can hold is Hak Pakai (right of use) or Hak Sewa (leasehold), and a PT PMA can hold Hak Guna Bangunan. Any arrangement that promises you freehold through a nominee is void under Indonesian law, and the land is not yours. Ask us first.
