What is KBLI?

KBLI is Indonesia's official business classification system. Think of it as a unique number that defines what your company does. Every business registered in Indonesia must have one. The 2025 edition contains 1,562 five-digit codes maintained by BPS (Statistics Indonesia) under Regulation No. 7/2025.

Foreign Investment Rules

Each business code has a foreign investment status that tells you if foreigners can invest:
Open — Foreign investors can own up to 100% of the company
Restricted — Foreign investment is allowed but with conditions (e.g., max ownership percentage or local partner required)
Closed — Foreign investment is not permitted in this sector
