# KITAS Indonesia: Every Type Explained (2026 Guide)

Most people arrive at a notary or immigration consultant's office and say "I need a KITAS." What happens next reveals a common and expensive misunderstanding: KITAS is not a single document. It is an umbrella category — _Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas_, or Limited Stay Permit — covering at least six distinct permit types, each governed by different regulations, different sponsorship chains, and different eligibility criteria.

Asking for "a KITAS" without knowing which type you need is like walking into a pharmacy and asking for "some medicine." The pharmacist will ask questions. So will Indonesian Immigration. The difference is that choosing the wrong KITAS can result in overstay penalties, deportation records, or a permit that simply cannot be extended when your circumstances change.

All permit codes and references in this guide use 2026 nomenclature as updated by Permenkumham 22/2023 and UU 63/2024. Legacy codes referenced in older guides or pre-2024 immigration paperwork have been superseded and are no longer accepted by Ditjen Imigrasi.

## E23 — Working KITAS {#e23-working-kitas}

The E23 is the standard work permit for foreign nationals employed by — and sponsored by — a legally established Indonesian entity. To qualify, your employer must first obtain a RPTKA (_Rencana Penggunaan Tenaga Kerja Asing_), the government-approved foreign manpower utilization plan, followed by a DKP-TKA levy payment. Only after both clearances are in place can the E23 application proceed. The permit is typically issued for one year and is renewable for up to two years per cycle, provided the sponsoring company remains in good standing. Roles in certain protected sectors — education, law, media — carry additional restrictions regardless of sponsor status.

## E28A — Investor KITAS {#e28a-investor-kitas}

The E28A is designed for foreign shareholders of a PT PMA (_Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing_) — Indonesia's foreign direct investment vehicle. The trigger threshold is a minimum paid-in capital of Rp 10 billion, and the applicant must hold an active shareholding position in the company's deed of establishment. Unlike the E23, the E28A does not require an employment relationship: you are here as an owner, not an employee. This distinction matters for tax treatment, social security obligations, and what business activities you are legally permitted to conduct. The permit is generally valid for two years with renewable extensions, and it can run concurrently with board director roles.

## E28D — Director KITAS (Representative Office) {#e28d-director-kitas}

The E28D applies to directors of representative offices — specifically KPPA (_Kantor Perwakilan Perusahaan Asing_) and KP3A (_Kantor Perwakilan Perusahaan Perdagangan Asing_) — which are foreign company liaison offices permitted to operate in Indonesia for coordination and market research purposes only. A representative office cannot generate local revenue, which makes the E28D a fundamentally different permit from the E28A: it is tied to a non-trading entity. The director role must appear in the official BKPM registration documents, and the permit duration mirrors the validity of the representative office license itself, typically two years.

## Spouse KITAS {#spouse-kitas}

Foreigners legally married to Indonesian citizens can obtain a Spouse KITAS, which grants limited residency rights without requiring a local employer or company structure. The cornerstone document is a marriage certificate that has been registered with the Indonesian Civil Registry (_Dinas Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil_) — overseas marriages must be legalized and transcribed first, a process that adds two to four weeks to the overall timeline. The Spouse KITAS is typically issued for one year and does not automatically grant the right to work; a separate work authorization is needed if the holder intends to take up employment. It does, however, allow property leasing, banking, and driving license conversion.

## Retirement KITAS {#retirement-kitas}

Indonesia's Retirement KITAS is available to foreign nationals aged 55 and above who can demonstrate consistent passive income from outside Indonesia — pension, investment returns, or a combination. The current income threshold sits at a minimum of USD 1,500 per month, evidenced by bank statements or pension authority letters covering the past three to six months. The applicant must not work for compensation in Indonesia; the permit explicitly prohibits local employment. It is one of the few KITAS categories that does not require a corporate sponsor, making it popular among retirees based in Bali and Lombok. Validity is one year, renewable indefinitely provided income evidence holds.

## Student KITAS {#student-kitas}

The Student KITAS covers full-time enrollment at Indonesian higher education institutions accredited by the Ministry of Education (_Kemendikbud_) or the Ministry of Religious Affairs (_Kemenag_) for Islamic universities. The institution acts as the sponsor and submits the application on the student's behalf — the student cannot self-apply. Required documents include an official acceptance letter, proof of active enrollment, and a financial guarantee confirming the student can support themselves without working locally. The permit is issued for one year and is tied directly to enrollment status: if you drop out or transfer institutions, the permit must be revoked and reapplied under the new sponsor.
