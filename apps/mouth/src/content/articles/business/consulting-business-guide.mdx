## PT PMA for Consulting

### KBLI Codes for Consulting

| Code  | Activity (KBLI 2025)                                 | Risk Level      | PMA Status   |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------ |
| 70209 | Konsultasi Manajemen dan Bisnis Lainnya              | Rendah          | TERBUKA 100% |
| 70202 | Konsultansi Manajemen dan Bisnis Industri            | Menengah Tinggi | TERBUKA 100% |
| 70203 | Konsultansi Manajemen dan Bisnis Perdagangan         | Rendah          | TERBUKA 100% |
| 70201 | Konsultansi Manajemen dan Bisnis **Pariwisata**      | Rendah          | TERBUKA 100% |
| 62209 | Konsultansi Komputer dan Manajemen Fasilitas Lainnya | -               | TERBUKA 100% |
| 62201 | Konsultansi dan Manajemen Keamanan Siber             | -               | TERBUKA 100% |

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="KBLI 2025: Old Codes Removed">
The following KBLI 2020 codes **no longer exist** in KBLI 2025:
- ~~62020~~ (IT consulting) → Now split into 62201, 62204, 62209
- ~~69209~~ (other professional services) → Restructured
- ~~74909~~ (other business support) → Restructured

If your existing PT PMA uses old codes, you will need to migrate to KBLI 2025 before the June 2026 deadline (BPS Reg. 7/2025).

</InfoCard>

<InfoCard variant="note" title="100% Foreign Ownership Confirmed">
  All consulting KBLI codes listed above have PMA status **TERBUKA** with
  `pma_max_asing: 100%`. No Indonesian partner required. Source: Perpres
  10/2021, 49/2021, 14/2024.
</InfoCard>

### Investment Requirements

Consulting PT PMA:

- **Minimum total investment**: IDR 10 billion (~USD 625,000)
- **Minimum paid-up capital**: IDR 2.5 billion (~USD 156,000)
- These are statutory minimums per PP 5/2021

<InfoCard variant="tip" title="Bali Zero Consulting Setup Costs">
- **New Company (PT PMA):** IDR 20.000.000
- **Virtual Office** (company domicile): IDR 5.000.000
- **Working KITAS (E25B Director):** IDR 34.500.000 (Offshore), IDR 36.000.000 (Onshore), IDR 31.500.000 (Extend)

These are Bali Zero service fees, separate from the statutory capital requirements above.

</InfoCard>

## Billing & Payments

### Invoicing Indonesian Clients

With PT PMA, you can:

- Invoice in IDR
- Issue tax invoices (Faktur Pajak)
- Collect VAT (11%)
- Accept local bank transfers

### Invoicing Foreign Clients

From PT PMA:

- Invoice in USD or other currencies
- Receive to Indonesian bank account
- Convert to IDR
- Foreign exchange considerations

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="VAT on Services">
  Consulting services are subject to **11% VAT (PPN)**. You must charge this to
  Indonesian clients and remit to tax office monthly.
</InfoCard>

---

## Work Permit Considerations

### As Director of Your PT PMA

You need a **Director KITAS (E25B - Visa Kerja Direktur Perusahaan)**:

- RPTKA (required even for own company)
- DKP-TKA fee (USD 100/month)
- E25B Director KITAS

<InfoCard variant="tip" title="Bali Zero Working KITAS (E25B)">
  - **Offshore** (apply from abroad): IDR 34.500.000 - **Onshore** (apply from
  Indonesia): IDR 36.000.000 - **Extend** (renewal): IDR 31.500.000
</InfoCard>

### Hiring Other Foreign Consultants

Additional foreign staff requires:

- Individual RPTKA
- Position justification
- Indonesian counterpart training
- Full permit costs per person

## Tax Obligations

### Company Taxes

| Tax                     | Rate            | Frequency       |
| ----------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| VAT (PPN)               | 11% on services | Monthly         |
| Corporate Income Tax    | 22% on profit   | Annual          |
| Withholding on payments | Varies          | Per transaction |

### Personal Taxes

As director/owner:

- Salary subject to PPh 21
- Dividends taxable
- Annual SPT required

## Common Consulting Types

### Allowed for Foreigners

- Management consulting
- Strategy consulting
- IT consulting
- Financial advisory (not regulated)
- Marketing consulting
- HR consulting (non-placement)
- Technical consulting

### Restricted Areas

- Legal advice (requires Indonesian lawyer)
- Tax advice (technically restricted)
- Audit services (licensed accountants)
- Certain financial services

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Partnerships">
  For restricted services, consider partnering with licensed Indonesian

## Remote Consulting

### Working from Indonesia Without PT PMA

If clients are purely foreign:

- E33G Digital Nomad Visa options
- Invoice from foreign company
- No Indonesian employment
- Cannot serve Indonesian clients

### Limitations

- Cannot build Indonesian client base
- Bank account difficulties
- Limited long-term stability
- Visa renewals required
