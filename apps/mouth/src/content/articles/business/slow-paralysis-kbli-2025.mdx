Let's skip the drama. No raids. No deportations. No government agent knocking at your door. That's not how it happens anymore.

The real threat in 2025 is silence.

---

On **December 17, 2025**, Indonesia restructured 1,563 business classification codes under **KBLI 2025**. Some were split. Some were merged. Some — like the infamous **63122** — simply ceased to exist.

But here's the part nobody's talking about: **the OSS system hasn't switched yet.**

It's still running on **KBLI 2020**. Your NIB looks fine. Your dashboard shows green. Everything feels normal.

It's not.

---

## What Is Slow Paralysis?

The moment the system migrates, every PT PMA in the country will need to sync. Notarial deed. AHU filing. OSS update. License check. LKPM reporting. Each step has a queue. Each queue has a bottleneck.

And that's where Slow Paralysis begins.

| Month       | What happens                                                |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Month 1** | You renew a license. _"Pending."_                           |
| **Month 2** | Your accountant submits LKPM. _Rejected — code mismatch._   |
| **Month 3** | KITAS renewal. _Stuck._                                     |
| **Month 4** | Your lawyer says the amendment queue is 8 weeks.            |
| **Month 5** | Your business is technically alive but functionally frozen. |

No crash. No warning. Just a slow, silent withdrawal of your ability to operate.

---

## Why the Gap Between KBLI 2025 and OSS 2020 Is the Real Risk

KBLI 2025 is the new **official national standard** — recognized by BPS, BKPM, and the Ministry of Law. But OSS, the system where you actually register and renew, hasn't migrated yet.

This creates a split reality:

- Your **company documents** (notarial deed, AHU) may already reference the new KBLI 2025 codes
- Your **OSS registration** still shows the old KBLI 2020 code
- When the migration happens, **the system won't reconcile this for you**

The businesses with mapping evidence ready can remediate in days. The rest may wait months — watching operations freeze one system at a time.
