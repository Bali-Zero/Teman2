## The Facts

## The Facts

Companies with a quarterly investment reporting obligation can submit their Q3 2026 LKPM report during 1–15 October. DPMPTSP Bantul's notice, published on 21 September, confirms that window and says the transition to KBLI 2025 does not prevent reporting through the existing mechanism. [Official Q3 notice](https://dpmptsp.bantulkab.go.id/web/berita/detail/1145-penyampaian-lkpm-triwulan-iii-tahun-2026-dibuka-1-15-oktober).

LKPM is the investment activity report. DPMPTSP Padang's guidance distinguishes quarterly reporting for medium and large businesses from half-yearly reporting for small businesses, with micro businesses exempt. Determine the obligation using the company's actual classification and current OSS requirements. [Reporting categories](https://dpmptsp.padang.go.id/berita/detail/gaspol-lkpm-gerakan-aktif-sisir-pelaku-usaha-optimalkan-laporan-lkpm).

## Bali Zero Take

For an owner, the useful question today is who will prepare, review and submit the applicable report. Treat the reporting window as time to complete a documented process. An NIB alone is not enough information to assume that every business files the same report.

## In Practice

Our recommendation is to reconcile figures against supporting records before entering them in OSS. Gather investment expenditure records, relevant workforce information and an explanation of project progress or obstacles. Match the figures to the correct project and period.

A business still preparing to operate should check its obligations rather than assume that having no commercial revenue removes them. Ask how to correct an earlier error instead of repeating an unsupported figure.

## Next Steps

Confirm OSS access and assign responsibility for the submission now. Leave time for questions before 15 October.

The Bantul notice links an online Q3 LKPM clinic during 1–15 October, from 09.00 to 12.00 WIB, equivalent to 10.00–13.00 WITA. Check its participation details and limits before joining.

Keep a copy of the submitted report and acknowledgement alongside its supporting records. This is an editorial preparation checklist; the business's actual reporting requirements should be confirmed through the official service.
