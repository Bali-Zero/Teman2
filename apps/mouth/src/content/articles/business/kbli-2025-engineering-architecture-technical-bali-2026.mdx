The engineering and architecture sector in Bali is entering a period of simultaneous constraint and opportunity. The Sarbagita moratorium limits greenfield development in South Bali's most active zone, while the North Bali Airport (Perpres 12/2025) and the Gilimanuk-Mengwi toll road are creating a new infrastructure corridor worth billions of USD. Overlaid on this is the critical IMB→PBG transition creating a massive retrofit and inspection market, and the OSS-RDTR GPS geospatial validation system that now gates every construction project.

## Regulatory Framework 2026

### KBLI 2025 Structural Changes for Group 71

KBLI 2025 (BPS Reg 7/2025, based on ISIC Rev.5) introduced the most significant restructuring of subgolongan 7120 in the KBLI's history:

| Old KBLI 2020                           | New KBLI 2025                     | Change                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 71200 (Testing & Analysis — aggregated) | 71201 (Certification)             | **Fragmented into 5 specific codes**                                    |
| 71200                                   | 71202 (Inspection)                |                                                                         |
| 71200                                   | 71203 (Verification & Validation) |                                                                         |
| 71200                                   | 71204 (Technical Testing)         |                                                                         |
| 71200                                   | 71209 (Other Testing)             |                                                                         |
| 71101 (Architecture)                    | 71101 (Architecture)              | Retained, description expanded (IBC reference added)                    |
| 71109 (Engineering)                     | 71109 (Engineering)               | Retained, expanded to include project management and safety engineering |

**Action required**: All companies with KBLI 71200 must re-map their activities to the specific new codes and update their NIB in OSS by **18 June 2026**.

### PMA Structure: UU Jasa Konstruksi (UU 2/2017 + PP 14/2021)

For construction-related PT PMA (BUJK PMA):

| Requirement                       | Detail                                              |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| SBU required                      | Via LPJK-accredited body                            |
| Maximum foreign ownership         | **67%** (minimum 33% local partner)                 |
| Net worth minimum (consultancy)   | IDR 250 billion (perencana dan pengawas konstruksi) |
| SKK (Work Competency Certificate) | Mandatory for every technical staff member          |
| Knowledge transfer                | Mandatory for all foreign TKA employed              |

### OSS-RDTR GPS: The New Construction Gatekeeper

PP 28/2025 elevated RDTR (Rencana Detail Tata Ruang) from a planning instrument to the **primary legal determinant for OSS licensing**. The verification is automatic:

1. Entrepreneur enters project GPS coordinates in OSS
2. System cross-references coordinates against digital RDTR zoning maps (800+ RDTR integrated nationally by end 2025)
3. If KBLI activity is incompatible with the zone → **NIB automatically blocked**. No administrative discretion.

For engineering and architecture projects, this means:

- RDTR compliance check must occur **before** any lease, land purchase, or OSS registration
- Same location may be authorized for one activity type and prohibited for another
- Non-compliance can invalidate all downstream permits (PBG, environmental permits, operating licenses)
- Interactive RDTR tool: https://oss.go.id/rdtr-interaktif

### IMB → PBG + SLF: The Critical 2026 Legacy Transition

The old IMB (Izin Mendirikan Bangunan) system has been fully replaced by:

- **PBG** (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung): Building design approval before construction begins — verifies compliance with RTRW, structural safety, and environmental impact
- **SLF** (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi): Functional fitness certificate issued after construction completion, verified by a licensed pengkaji teknis with IPTB
- Managed online via SIMBG (simbg.pu.go.id)

**All existing structures with IMB must migrate to PBG+SLF.** This creates a massive market across all 71xxx codes: architectural audits, technical inspections, material testing, and engineering assessments for thousands of Bali's hotels, villas, and commercial structures.

## KBLI 71101 — Architectural Services

**The Bali Aesthetic Paradox**

The highest-value differentiation in Bali's architecture market is the ability to resolve a fundamental design conflict: clients demand vernacular Balinese architecture — bamboo, rammed earth, alang-alang roofs, rice terrace integration, and the philosophical framework of **Tri Hita Karana** — while requiring international clinical and hospitality performance: concealed HVAC, sanitary standards, seismic compliance for Ring of Fire conditions.

The **15-meter height limit** ("no taller than a coconut tree") — established by SK Gubernur 1971 and confirmed in Perda 16/2009 Art. 95 — forces creative solutions: cliff-edge design building downward, semi-underground spaces, and cascading terraces. The rare exceptions (hospitals, airports, transmission towers) do not apply to hotels or resorts.

**Operational requirements**: SBU certification via LPJK under AR001/AR002 sub-classifications. Minimum 33% local partner required. SKK mandatory for each architect. Foreign architects must complete knowledge transfer (alih teknologi) program.

**2026 opportunity zones**:

| Zone                   | Status           | Rationale                                            |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Uluwatu                | Growing fast     | International airport incoming; still early-stage    |
| North Bali (Buleleng)  | Best opportunity | Outside Sarbagita moratorium; airport coming         |
| Sanur                  | Underserved      | Medical tourism KEK expansion                        |
| Tabanan/West Bali      | Emerging         | Gilimanuk-Mengwi toll road corridor                  |
| South Bali (Sarbagita) | Constrained      | Moratorium limits greenfield; retrofit market strong |

## KBLI 71109 — Engineering Consulting (General)

**Most versatile and highest-demand code in the 71xxx group**

KBLI 2025 expands the scope of 71109 to explicitly include project management and safety engineering — making it the optimal core license for a PT PMA engineering firm. Covers civil, hydraulic, traffic, MEP, geotechnical, acoustic engineering, and more.

### 2025–2030 Infrastructure Pipeline

**North Bali Airport** (Perpres 12/2025):

- Location: Kubutambahan, Buleleng Regency (north coast)
- Scale: 2,800 hectares, significant marine reclamation component
- Target capacity: up to 50 million passengers
- Construction start: estimated ~2027 (currently in preparatory/financial phase)
- Engineering demand: geotechnical, coastal, marine, structural, traffic

**Gilimanuk-Mengwi Toll Road**:

- Length: 97 km across three sections
- Cost: IDR 5 trillion (construction) + IDR 4 trillion (land acquisition), PPP scheme
- Target completion: 2027–2029
- Impact: reduces Gilimanuk-Mengwi journey from 3–4 hours to 1–1.5 hours

**Post-flood demand** (September 2025 Bali floods): explosive growth in hydrological studies, drainage engineering, and risk mitigation — particularly under Pergub 24/2020 requirements for lake/spring/river/sea protection.

## KBLI 71201 — Technical Certification

**New granularity in KBLI 2025** — covers product, process, service, and system certification: ISO, HACCP, ISPO, SMK3, halal certification, wood legality (SVLK), and SBU construction certification.

**Primary Bali demand driver**: ecolabel and sustainable tourism certification. KBLI 2025 explicitly includes _sertifikasi kegiatan pariwisata_ within this code's scope. Green building certification (EDGE, Greenship) growing on eco-resorts.

**Entry barrier**: KAN (National Accreditation Committee) accreditation required. Market dominated by SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV. Better positioned as complementary service to a PMA already operating in 71101/71109 than as a standalone business.

---

## KBLI 71202 — Technical Inspection

**Immediate 2026–2028 market opportunity**

Separated from old 71200 in KBLI 2025. Covers pipeline and structural inspection not connected to repair/installation. The IMB→PBG transition is generating a **massive backlog of inspection requirements**:

- Thousands of Bali hotels, villas, and commercial structures require technical inspection for PBG/SLF migration
- Post-September 2025 floods: structural inspections on damaged buildings in Sarbagita and Buleleng
- Periodic water crisis drives pipe network inspection demand

IPTB (Building Technical Practitioner License) required for inspectors. High legal liability — inspection errors create litigation risk. **Excellent as add-on to 71101 or 71109** for a complete design+inspection package.

---

## KBLI 71203 — Technical Verification & Validation

**Entirely new in KBLI 2025** (not in KBLI 2020). Covers verification and validation of technical processes, systems, and documents without issuing final certification.

Particularly relevant for pre-OSS-RDTR validation consulting — helping clients verify GPS coordinate compliance before official submission. In complex eco-resort projects, simultaneous compliance with seismic standards, environmental regulations, the 15m height limit, and international standards (WELL, LEED, FGI 2026) creates a specialized validation niche.

Best positioned as a **complementary code** within a multi-KBLI offering.

## KBLI 71204 — Technical Testing

**High potential, high barrier**

Physical, chemical, and analytical testing of materials, products, and environmental conditions. Bali-specific demand:

| Testing Type                 | Bali Application                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Water quality testing        | Contamination from overtourism; KEK Sanur medical requirements      |
| Local construction materials | Structural bamboo, Paras Bali stone, rammed earth per SNI standards |
| Seismic testing              | Existing structures post-earthquake compliance                      |
| Acoustic testing             | Open-air resort design vs. privacy requirements paradox             |
| Indoor environment testing   | FGI 2026 Evidence-Based Design for wellness centers and clinics     |

**Market gap**: Most Bali samples are currently sent to Surabaya or Jakarta for testing. A KAN-accredited laboratory in Bali would address this gap. Partnership with Udayana University can reduce CAPEX significantly.

---

## KBLI 71209 — Other Technical Testing

Catch-all residual code. Covers forensic testing, physical models (airports, dams), and product quality analysis. Relevant Bali applications: forensic analysis of post-flood structural failures, marine impact modeling for North Bali Airport reclamation, quality testing of artisanal Balinese products for export certification.

**Recommended only as additional code** alongside a firm already covering 71204. Functions as a regulatory insurance policy for activities at the margins of other codes.

### Recommended PMA KBLI Configuration

**Core**: 71101 (Architecture) + 71109 (Engineering General)
**Value-add**: 71202 (Inspection) + 71204 (Testing) — complete full-service package
**Compliance hedge**: 71203 (Verification) + 71209 (Other Testing) — regulatory coverage
**Optional**: 71102 (Industrial) + 71201 (Certification) — add if pipeline justifies

With the reduction of paid-up capital to IDR 2.5B per KBLI (BKPM 5/2025), including multiple codes is more accessible. Each additional KBLI still requires >IDR 10B total investment and appropriate SKK certifications.
