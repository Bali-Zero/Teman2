## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

A return percentage is a starting point for questions. Our recommendation is to ask what the number measures: gross booking revenue, net operating income or cash remaining after financing and other costs. Different definitions can make two apparently similar offers difficult to compare.

This article does not establish a safe or achievable Bali-wide ROI benchmark. It also does not identify any particular developer as fraudulent. The useful response to a strong marketing claim is to request evidence specific to the property.

---

## In Practice

Ask for the calculation behind the projection, including the assumed nightly rate, occupied nights and the period covered. For an operating property, request records that can support those assumptions. For an unbuilt project, distinguish a forecast from an operating history.

Build a separate cost schedule for management, marketing, utilities, maintenance, taxes, financing and a reserve for repairs. Check which expenses the seller's model includes. Test what happens if bookings are lower or the opening date slips; describe those alternatives as your scenarios rather than predictions.

An income projection and an exit plan answer different questions. Ask how a proposed resale price was estimated, what comparable completed transactions support it, and what costs or contractual restrictions could affect a sale.
