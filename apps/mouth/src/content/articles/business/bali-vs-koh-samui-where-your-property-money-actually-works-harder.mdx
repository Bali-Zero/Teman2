## The Two Markets at a Glance

Bali and [Koh Samui](/business/thailand-vs-bali-the-hidden-cost-of-picking-the-wrong-jurisdiction) are the two most popular destinations in Southeast Asia for foreign property buyers seeking both lifestyle and income. Both markets share a structural constraint: foreigners cannot own [freehold land](/business/leasehold-vs-freehold-what-foreign-investors-must-know-in-southeast-asia) outright. Everything else diverges significantly.

**Bali (Indonesia)**
Foreigners cannot hold SHM (Sertifikat Hak Milik — freehold title) directly. Legal investment routes include:

- **Leasehold (Hak Sewa / [Hak Pakai](/business/foreign-property-ownership-in-indonesia-what-the-law-actually-allows)):** Typically structured as 25 to 30 years with extension options.
