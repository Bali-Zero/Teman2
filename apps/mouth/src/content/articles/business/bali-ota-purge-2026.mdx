## The Final Countdown

The "grey zone" of Bali property rentals is about to go dark. Under the mandate of **Government Regulation (PP) 28/2025**, the Indonesian government has moved from observation to digital enforcement.

If you own or manage a villa in Bali, there is one date you cannot ignore: **March 31, 2026.**

### The NIB Requirement: No License, No Listing

By the end of March 2026, all Online Travel Agencies (OTAs)—including Airbnb, Booking.com, and Expedia—will be API-linked to the Indonesian **OSS (Online Single Submission)** system.

The rule is binary: any listing that lacks a **verified NIB (Business Identification Number)** cross-referenced with a valid tax ID (NPWP) will be **automatically delisted**. There will be no grace periods. Properties without the correct KBLI codes for hospitality will simply vanish from search results overnight.

### The Enforcement is Real: The Bingin Precedent

For those who doubt the government’s resolve, the July 2025 demolition of 48 illegal structures at Bingin Beach serves as a stark reminder. These structures, built in protected zones or without proper **PBG (Building Permits)** and **SLF (Functionality Certificates)**, were leveled to restore legal order.

The message is clear: the government is willing to destroy multi-million dollar assets to enforce zoning and environmental laws.

### The Compliance Checklist for 2026

To protect your investment, your property must satisfy the following "Era of Order" requirements:

1. **KKPR (Zoning Confirmation):** Digital verification that your land is compatible with tourism (Pink Zone/ITR).
2. **PBG & SLF:** The old IMB is obsolete. You need the modern building permit and the certificate of agibility.
3. **KBLI 55203:** The specific business code for "Tourism Villas" must be active in your PT PMA.
4. **Tax Compliance:** Integration with the **Coretax** system to ensure correct PB1 (10% hospitality tax) payments.

### The Opportunity in Transparency

While these regulations increase the barrier to entry, they are "gold" for professional operators. By purging the illegal competition, the government is stabilizing nightly rates and rewarding those who invest correctly.

**Action Item:** Do not wait for April. Audit your PT PMA structure and verify your NIB status today.
