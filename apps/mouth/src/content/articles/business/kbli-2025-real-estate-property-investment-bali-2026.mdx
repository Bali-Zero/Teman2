# KBLI 2025 Real Estate Investment Guide: Property Development, Management & Brokerage in Bali

Real estate under KBLI 2025 represents one of the most significant expansions in the entire classification — from 5 codes to 14 codes, with **all 14 codes fully open to 100% foreign ownership (PMA)**. For investors navigating Bali's property market in 2026, understanding which KBLI code governs which activity — and what operational constraints apply — is not optional. It is the difference between a compliant investment and a costly compliance failure.

This guide covers all 14 KBLI 68xxx codes with 2026-specific intelligence: land title structures, the Sarbagita construction moratorium, OSS-RDTR GPS-linked zoning, transaction taxes, broker licensing, and the ongoing digitalization of Indonesian land records.

---

## The KBLI 68xxx Architecture: 14 Codes Across 3 Pillars

KBLI 2025 organizes real estate activities into three distinct pillars:

**Pillar 1 — Property by the Company's Own Account (68111–68129)**
The company develops, owns, leases, or manages real estate using its own assets.

**Pillar 2 — Real Estate Services / Intermediation (68210)**
The company acts as a broker or agent facilitating transactions on behalf of others.

**Pillar 3 — Fee-Based Real Estate Services (68291–68299)**
The company provides appraisal, management, or advisory services for a fee.

Understanding this structure is critical for PT PMA registration: each pillar carries different capital requirements, licensing obligations, and operational scope.

## KBLI 68111: Property Development — Residential (Hunian)

**Code:** 68111
**Title:** AKTIVITAS PENGEMBANGAN BANGUNAN DAN LAHAN HUNIAN MILIK SENDIRI
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Development and sale of residential property (villas, housing complexes, apartments) by the company using its own land and capital.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Bali residential development is under intense pressure from the Sarbagita moratorium and OSS-RDTR GPS enforcement. The market is bifurcating: luxury villa compounds in pre-approved zones command premium prices, while speculative development in moratorium-affected areas faces permit stalls.

**Bali Nuance:** The four Sarbagita regencies (Denpasar, Badung, Gianyar, Tabanan) now enforce GPS-coordinate validation through OSS. A development permit is only issued if the project coordinates fall within a green zone in the RDTR digital map. Sites with legacy approvals obtained before OSS-RDTR integration face re-validation requirements.

**Operational Risks:**

- Sarbagita moratorium enforcement suspended all new construction permits in select zones — verify your specific coordinates before acquisition
- OSS-RDTR GPS validation: the system cross-checks NIB application coordinates against the Rencana Detail Tata Ruang (RDTR) digital layer; mismatches trigger automatic rejection
- BKPM Reg. 5/2025: PT PMA requires IDR 2.5B paid-up capital + IDR 10B total investment, with a 12-month lock-up period before repatriation
- Girik (adat land documents) expired on 2 February 2026 — any acquisition using Girik-based title is now legally invalid; demand Sertifikat (BPN-issued)
- Sertifikat Elektronik rollout: BPN is digitizing land certificates; verify status at ATR/BPN office before closing

**Investment Outlook:** Strong long-term demand for structured villa compounds in Canggu, Seminyak corridor. Near-term entry requires meticulous RDTR coordinate verification and budget for 12–18 month permit processing.

**Legacy Bridge (KBLI 2020 → 2025):** 68111 in KBLI 2025 is largely equivalent to 68111 in KBLI 2020 for residential development. No code migration required — verify OSS registration reflects the 2025 classification.

## KBLI 68112: Property Leasing — Residential

**Code:** 68112
**Title:** AKTIVITAS PENYEWAAN BANGUNAN DAN LAHAN HUNIAN MILIK SENDIRI
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Long-term leasing of residential buildings and land owned by the company.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** This is the structural code for PT PMA companies that own and lease villa portfolios on long-term contracts (12+ months). Demand from digital nomads and expat families is robust, with occupancy averaging 85%+ in the Canggu/Seminyak corridor.

**Bali Nuance:** 68112 is distinct from 55203 (short-term villa rental/hospitality). If your company leases villas on annual contracts to individuals — without providing hotel-like services — 68112 is the correct code. Mixing 68112 with short-term OTA listings (Airbnb, Booking.com) without also registering 55203 creates a compliance gap.

**Operational Risks:**

- IMB/PBG permit must specify "hunian sewa" (residential rental) use — misclassified permits create audit exposure
- Long-term leases to foreigners must comply with PP 18/2021 restrictions; use HGB (max 30+20+30 = 80 years), not Hak Sewa agreements that circumvent title law
- CoreTax CTAS NITKU: each rental property location now requires a separate NITKU identifier cross-referencing KBLI codes for tax audits

**Investment Outlook:** Stable, cash-generating model with limited capital appreciation speculation. Best combined with 68111 in a vertically integrated property holding structure.

**Legacy Bridge:** New granularity in KBLI 2025 — previously grouped under broader 68110. Update OSS registration if your current NIB shows the legacy 2020 code.

## KBLI 68121: Tourism Zone Management

**Code:** 68121
**Title:** PENGELOLAAN KAWASAN PARIWISATA
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Development and management of integrated tourism zones (kawasan pariwisata) — resorts, eco-parks, tourism estates.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Kawasan pariwisata development is a high-barrier, high-reward play. Bali's established tourism zones (Nusa Dua, Tanah Lot corridor, Ubud highlands) are constrained by existing concessions; new zones require RTRWP alignment and KLHK environmental clearance.

**Bali Nuance:** ITDC (Indonesia Tourism Development Corporation) controls access to Nusa Dua zone. Private tourism zone development requires a Kawasan Pariwisata license from the Ministry of Tourism + AMDAL (full environmental impact assessment) for projects above 5 hectares.

**Operational Risks:**

- Sarbagita overlap: tourism zones within the four regencies require Bali Governor approval in addition to national OSS licensing
- Eco-zone requirements: green belt obligations and Balinese cultural buffer zones (setback from pura temples = minimum 5km in some zoning designations)
- KLHK AMDAL timeline: 6–18 months for large projects; budget for consultant fees of IDR 500M–IDR 2B

**Investment Outlook:** Strategic code for large-scale integrated resort developers. Not suitable for single-villa or small-cluster operations — use 55203 or 68112 for those structures.

**Legacy Bridge:** Refined from the KBLI 2020 category; ensure your OSS KBLI reference is updated from any 2020-era tourism zone codes.

## KBLI 68210: Real Estate Brokerage & Intermediation

**Code:** 68210
**Title:** AKTIVITAS JASA INTERMEDIASI REAL ESTAT
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Real estate brokerage — facilitating property sales, leases, and transfers for a commission.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** The real estate broker market in Bali is professionalizing rapidly under new licensing requirements. Companies without formal SIU-P4 certification are increasingly shut out of institutional and PMA-to-PMA transactions.

**Bali Nuance:** SIU-P4 (Surat Izin Usaha Perusahaan Perantara Perdagangan Properti) is mandatory for brokerage companies since PERMEN PUPR 11/2021. Requirements: minimum 2 certified brokers with AREBI (Asosiasi Real Estat Broker Indonesia) credentials + NIK-registered office address.

**Operational Risks:**

- SIU-P4 is a non-OSS license issued by the Ministry of PUPR — it cannot be self-declared; requires physical inspection and certificate issuance
- Brokers who facilitated PPJB agreements without SIU-P4 face retroactive liability under PERMEN PUPR 11/2021
- PPJB vs AJB distinction is critical: PPJB (Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli) is a preliminary sale agreement with no title transfer — commonly used for off-plan; AJB (Akta Jual Beli) is the final deed executed before PPAT, triggering BPHTB + PPh
- Foreign buyer restrictions: foreigners cannot hold Hak Milik (freehold); they can only acquire Hak Pakai (through marriage to WNI) or use PT PMA with HGB

**Investment Outlook:** Consolidation opportunity — SIU-P4 requirement is filtering out informal brokers. Compliant PT PMA brokerages can capture market share from the informal channel, especially for international buyer segment.

**Legacy Bridge:** 68210 in KBLI 2025 is equivalent to 68200 in KBLI 2020. Update OSS NIB if still showing 2020 code.

**Code 68292:** PENGELOLAAN REAL ESTAT HUNIAN ATAS DASAR BALAS JASA — Fee-based management of residential properties owned by third parties

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**68291 — Appraisal:**

- Valuation reports are required by Indonesian banks for mortgage financing and by BKPM for capital verification
- MAPPI (Masyarakat Profesi Penilai Indonesia) certification required for individual appraisers; company must employ minimum 1 certified appraiser for 68291 registration
- CoreTax CTAS has flagged real estate appraisal companies for enhanced audit given role in capital verification for PMA transactions

**68292 — Residential Property Management:**

- This is the code for companies that manage villa or apartment portfolios owned by others (property management companies)
- Villa management companies operating OTA platforms on behalf of foreign villa owners must use this code in combination with 55203 (short-term accommodation)
- Management fee structures (typically 20–30% of revenue) must be documented in formal management agreements compliant with Indonesian contract law

## Critical Regulatory Framework: What Every Bali Real Estate PT PMA Must Know

### HGB vs Hak Milik: The Foreign Ownership Reality

PT PMA can only hold **HGB (Hak Guna Bangunan)** — the right to build on and use land for a term of years. PT PMA **cannot** hold Hak Milik (freehold).

| Feature      | Hak Milik       | HGB                                                            |
| ------------ | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who can hold | WNI only        | PT PMA allowed                                                 |
| Duration     | Perpetual       | 30 years + 20 year extension + 30 year renewal = 80 years max  |
| On expiry    | N/A             | Must be renewed; reverting to state land is the legal default  |
| Transfer     | Full alienation | Can be pledged, mortgaged, and transferred within HGB duration |
| Construction | Unlimited       | Permitted within HGB term                                      |

**Critical:** HGB extensions require active application to ATR/BPN before expiry. Automatic renewal does not exist — failure to apply results in the land reverting to the state or original Hak Milik holder.

### PPJB vs AJB: The Two-Stage Transaction Process

Most off-plan and high-value property transactions use a two-stage process:

1. **PPJB (Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli)** — Preliminary sale agreement executed before a Notaris (notary). No title transfer occurs. Commonly used when: building not yet complete, buyer arranging financing, or parties need a locked transaction while permits are processed.

2. **AJB (Akta Jual Beli)** — Final sale deed executed before PPAT (Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah — land deed official). Title transfers at this moment. Triggers:
   - **BPHTB (Bea Perolehan Hak atas Tanah dan Bangunan):** 5% of transaction value, paid by buyer
   - **PPh Final 2.5%:** Paid by seller on the gross transaction value (PP 34/2016)
   - **PNBP (state fee):** Certificate issuance fee payable to ATR/BPN

### Sarbagita Moratorium: Construction Freeze Zone

The Sarbagita Greater Area (Denpasar, Badung, Gianyar, Tabanan) is subject to construction density controls under Bali Governor Regulation. Key impacts:

- No new IMB/PBG issued in saturated zones pending RDTR revision
- Existing permits with valid IMB pre-dating the moratorium are grandfathered
- Satellite villages (outside the 4 regencies — Karangasem, Buleleng, Jembrana) are unaffected and attracting developer migration

### OSS-RDTR GPS Integration: Digital Zoning Enforcement

As of 2025, OSS validates all license applications against the national RDTR (Rencana Detail Tata Ruang) digital layer:

- GPS coordinates submitted in NIB application are automatically cross-referenced against the RDTR polygon map
- Applications falling in red/yellow zones are automatically flagged or rejected
- No manual override exists at OSS level — rejected applications require physical zone reclassification at BKPM/DPRD level before resubmission

**Practical implication:** Any land acquisition for PT PMA real estate must be preceded by RDTR coordinate verification at the local BKPM office or via consultant tools before signing any PPJB.

### Girik and the Digital Land Certificate Transition

As of **2 February 2026**, Girik documents (informal customary land documents that pre-date formal BPN certification) have expired as evidence of land ownership. Any land acquisition where the seller presents only Girik — without a BPN Sertifikat — is legally unsafe for PT PMA buyers.

The Sertifikat Elektronik (digital certificate) program is actively replacing paper certificates in Bali. Verify the digital status of any target parcel at the ATR/BPN Bali office before proceeding with PPJB.

## Strategic Recommendations for PT PMA Real Estate Entry in 2026

**For villa developers / resort builders (68111):**

- Complete RDTR coordinate verification before land purchase
- Budget 12–18 months for permit processing in Sarbagita zone; 6–9 months outside
- Use two-phase structure: PPJB to lock acquisition, AJB after PBG confirmed

**For villa portfolio operators (68112 + 55203):**

- Register both KBLI codes in your NIB — 68112 for ownership/leasing, 55203 for short-term accommodation activity
- Single KBLI registration creates a gap: OTA operators targeting your property for homestay/villa bookings will flag the absence of 55203

**For brokerages (68210):**

- SIU-P4 is mandatory — obtain it before commencing operations
- Budget IDR 50–100M for AREBI broker certification (2 minimum, exam + annual fees)
- Never facilitate an AJB without a PPAT co-signature — personal liability risk

**For property management companies (68292 + 68299):**

- Management agreement must specify fee structure, maintenance obligations, and dispute resolution
- BPJS Ketenagakerjaan enrollment required for any maintenance or operations staff
- CoreTax CTAS registration with NITKU per managed property is the 2026 compliance priority
