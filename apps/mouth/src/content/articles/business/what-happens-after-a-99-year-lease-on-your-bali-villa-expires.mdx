## The Facts

The 99-year leasehold structure is the primary legal vehicle through which foreign nationals hold long-term rights over Bali property, given that Indonesian law (Agrarian Law No. 5/1960) prohibits foreigners from owning freehold (Hak Milik) land. Upon expiration of a 99-year lease — typically structured as Hak Sewa (right to lease) or Hak Pakai (right to use) — all rights to the property revert to the Indonesian landowner unless a renewal or extension agreement was negotiated in advance.
