## The Agriculture Opportunity in Indonesia

Indonesia's agriculture sector represents **13.3% of GDP** and employs **29% of the workforce**. The KBLI 2025 classification system introduces 141 specialized agriculture codes under Category A (01xxx), providing foreign investors with unprecedented clarity on PMA ownership rules, risk classifications, and operational boundaries.

For Bali specifically, agriculture intersects with tourism to create the "agritourism" model — coffee plantations that host visitors, organic farms offering workshops, and livestock operations integrated with eco-resorts. This article dissects the KBLI codes that enable these business models.

---

## Understanding Category A: Agriculture Structure

KBLI 2025 divides agriculture into **7 major groups**, each with dozens of five-digit sub-classifications:

| Group    | KBLI Range  | Coverage                                           | Typical PMA | Risk Level |
| -------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| **01.1** | 01111-01199 | Food crops (rice, corn, vegetables)                | 100%        | Low        |
| **01.2** | 01211-01299 | Plantations (coffee, tea, cacao, palm oil)         | 100%        | Low        |
| **01.3** | 01301-01399 | Plant breeding, nurseries, horticulture            | 49%-100%    | Low        |
| **01.4** | 01410-01490 | Livestock (cattle, buffalo, goat, poultry)         | 100%        | Low        |
| **01.5** | 01500       | Mixed farming (crops + livestock)                  | 100%        | Low        |
| **01.6** | 01611-01699 | Agricultural support services (machinery, storage) | 100%        | Low        |
| **01.7** | 01701-01799 | Post-harvest activities (hunting, animal breeding) | 100%        | Low        |

**Key insight:** Unlike real estate (where 5 codes expanded to 14), agriculture codes remain stable with 141 classifications. The government is not restricting access — it's formalizing existing activities.

**Critical code: 01284** — This code covers coffee, tea, and cacao. It's the legal basis for Bali's famous "coffee plantations" that double as tourist attractions. 100% PMA, Low risk, and no minimum investment for non-priority sectors.

### Coffee Plantation + Agritourism Strategy

A typical Bali coffee plantation operates under a **multi-KBLI structure**:

1. **01284** — Primary code for coffee cultivation and processing
2. **79110** — Travel agency services for organizing tours
3. **56101** — Restaurant services for on-site cafe
4. **47911** — E-commerce retail for online coffee sales

This structure allows you to:

- Grow and process coffee beans (01284)
- Host paid tours with tasting sessions (79110)
- Operate a cafe serving meals (56101)
- Sell packaged coffee online (47911)

**License requirement:** The "plantation + tour" model only requires a Low risk NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha) for 01284 and 79110. High risk codes like food processing (10xxx) are NOT required if you're only roasting beans for immediate consumption on-site.

## Plant Breeding and Nurseries (01.3): The PMA Exception

This group includes nurseries, seedling production, and plant propagation. It contains one of the **few PMA-restricted agriculture codes**.

### Sample Codes: Plant Breeding

| KBLI  | Title (Bahasa)            | Title (English)          | PMA     | Risk |
| ----- | ------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------- | ---- |
| 01301 | Tanaman Hias              | Ornamental plant growing | 100%    | Low  |
| 01302 | Pengembangbiakan Tanaman  | Plant breeding           | **49%** | Low  |
| 01303 | Pembibitan Tanaman Pangan | Food crop seedling       | 100%    | Low  |
| 01726 | Penangkaran Anggrek       | Orchid breeding          | 100%    | Low  |

**Critical restriction: 01302** — Plant breeding for genetic improvement is limited to **49% foreign ownership**. This protects Indonesia's seed sovereignty under Law No. 22/2019 (Plant Genetic Resources Protection).

**Workaround:** If you want to run a premium orchid export business (popular in Bali), use **01726** (Orchid breeding), which allows 100% PMA. The "breeding" in 01726 refers to propagation for commercial sale, not genetic modification.

## Agritourism: The Multi-KBLI Strategy

Agritourism businesses combine agriculture operations with visitor experiences. The OSS system (Online Single Submission) allows you to register **up to 5 KBLI codes** on a single NIB, creating a legal framework for diversified revenue.

### Agritourism Model 1: Coffee Plantation + Tours

**Business:** Coffee farm with guided tours, tastings, and on-site cafe.

**KBLI structure:**

1. **01284** (Primary) — Coffee plantation
2. **79110** — Travel agency/tour services
3. **56101** — Restaurant/cafe
4. **10839** — Tea and coffee processing (if selling packaged products)

**PMA:** 100% on all codes
**Risk:** Low (01284, 79110) + Medium (56101, 10839)
**License:** NIB covers all 4 codes; Medium risk codes trigger food safety certification (BPOM)

### Agritourism Model 2: Organic Farm + Workshops

**Business:** Organic vegetable farm with farm-to-table cooking classes.

**KBLI structure:**

1. **01133** (Primary) — Fruit vegetable horticulture
2. **85496** — Educational courses (non-formal)
3. **56101** — Restaurant (for cooking class meals)
4. **47911** — E-commerce retail (for produce sales)

**PMA:** 100% on all codes
**Risk:** Low (01133, 47911) + Medium (85496, 56101)
**License:** NIB + teaching permit (for 85496) + food safety cert (for 56101)

### Agritourism Model 3: Livestock Sanctuary + Eco-Resort

**Business:** Animal rescue center with on-site accommodation and educational programs.

**KBLI structure:**

1. **01440** (Primary) — Goat and sheep farming
2. **55101** — Hotel/accommodation
3. **93219** — Recreation and amusement parks
4. **85496** — Educational courses (animal care workshops)

**PMA:** 100% on all codes
**Risk:** Low (01440) + Medium (55101, 93219, 85496)
**License:** NIB + hospitality permits (for 55101) + education permit (for 85496)

**Key principle:** Your **primary KBLI** determines your company's tax classification. If 01440 (livestock) is primary, you're taxed as an agriculture business, which may qualify for tax incentives under Law No. 6/1983 (General Tax Provisions).

## Land Ownership: The Hak Pakai Requirement

**Critical legal constraint:** Foreigners and foreign-owned PT PMA companies **cannot own land** (Hak Milik) in Indonesia. Agriculture businesses must use one of three legal structures:

### Option 1: Hak Pakai (Right to Use)

**Structure:** PT PMA holds **Hak Pakai** title, valid for 30 years, renewable for 20 more.
**Use case:** Best for plantation businesses (coffee, cacao) where you control the land long-term.
**Process:** Apply via BPN (National Land Agency) with approved NIB showing agriculture KBLI.

### Option 2: Lease (Sewa)

**Structure:** Indonesian citizen or local entity owns land (Hak Milik), PT PMA leases for up to 25 years.
**Use case:** Suitable for agritourism where you don't need permanent ownership (farm tours, workshops).
**Process:** Standard lease agreement, notarized, registered with local land office.

### Option 3: Contract Farming

**Structure:** PT PMA contracts with local farmers who own land; PT PMA provides seeds/tech/market access.
**Use case:** Low-capital model for vegetable/fruit export businesses (01133, 01134).
**Process:** Farming agreements under Ministry of Agriculture guidance (Permentan 03/2017).

**Recommendation for foreigners:** Start with **Option 2 (Lease)** for your first 3-5 years. This minimizes upfront capital and de-risks land disputes. Once cash flow is proven, convert to Hak Pakai (Option 1) for long-term security.

## Capital Requirements and Investment Incentives

### Minimum Capital (PP 5/2021)

| KBLI Range  | Business Type         | Min. Paid-Up Capital | Min. Total Investment |
| ----------- | --------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------- |
| 01111-01799 | Agriculture (general) | IDR 10 billion       | None                  |
| 01284       | Plantation (priority) | IDR 10 billion       | IDR 100 billion\*     |

\*Priority sectors (coffee, cacao, rubber) under Presidential Regulation 10/2021 (Tax Holiday) require IDR 100 billion total investment to qualify for CIT Holiday (0% for 5-10 years).

**Non-priority agriculture** (vegetables, livestock support) requires **IDR 10 billion paid-up capital** but has **no minimum total investment**. This is the lowest barrier to entry in Indonesian PMA.

### Tax Incentives (Law No. 2/2020 - Job Creation Law)

| Incentive           | Eligibility                                 | Benefit                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Tax Holiday**     | Priority plantation (IDR 100B investment)   | 0% CIT for 5-10 years            |
| **Tax Allowance**   | Agriculture processing (e.g., coffee mills) | 30% asset depreciation deduction |
| **Import Duty Cut** | Capital goods for agriculture               | 0% import duty on machinery      |
| **Super Deduction** | R&D (plant breeding, organic methods)       | 300% deduction of R&D expenses   |

**How to qualify:** Submit application to BKPM (Investment Coordinating Board) within 1 year of NIB issuance. Include investment realization proof (land lease, machinery invoices) and business plan showing job creation targets.

## Risk Management: Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Mixing Production and Processing Codes

**Problem:** Registering 01284 (Coffee plantation) and 10839 (Coffee processing) as equal primary codes.
**Result:** OSS system flags you for **industrial processing permits** (SIUP Industri), which require environmental impact assessments.

**Solution:** Make **01284 primary** (60%+ of revenue from cultivation). Register 10839 as secondary for "on-site roasting" only. This keeps you in Low risk category.

### Pitfall 2: Tour Operations Without 79110

**Problem:** Running paid tours under agriculture code only (01284).
**Result:** Tax office classifies tour revenue as "illegal services" — subject to fines and back taxes.

**Solution:** Always add **79110** (Travel agency) as secondary code if you charge entrance fees or tour packages. This is non-negotiable for agritourism models.

### Pitfall 3: E-Commerce Sales Without Retail Code

**Problem:** Selling produce online without 47911 (E-commerce retail) registered.
**Result:** BPOM (food safety agency) may reject product certification because your NIB doesn't cover "retail trade."

**Solution:** Register **47911** as tertiary code. This adds zero additional cost but makes your NIB compliant for online sales platforms (Tokopedia, Shopee, own website).

- [ ] Deposit minimum paid-up capital (IDR 10 billion) to company bank account
- [ ] Obtain NIB (includes business license, tax ID, importer ID if needed)

### Step 4: Operational Permits (Week 13-16)

- [ ] Apply for sectoral permits if Medium/High risk codes (e.g., BPOM for food processing)
- [ ] Register with Ministry of Agriculture for plantation codes (01284, 01285)
- [ ] Obtain local business permit (Izin Usaha) from Kabupaten/Kota government

### Step 5: Tax Incentive Application (Optional, Week 17-20)

- [ ] Prepare investment realization proof (land docs, machinery invoices)
- [ ] Submit Tax Holiday or Tax Allowance application to BKPM
- [ ] Coordinate with tax office (KPP) for incentive activation

**Total timeline:** 4-5 months from business plan to operational status.

## The Bottom Line

KBLI 2025's 141 agriculture codes provide foreign investors with a **clear roadmap** to Indonesia's agriculture and agritourism sectors. The key strategic insights:

1. **100% PMA dominance** — Only 1 code (01302 plant breeding) restricts foreign ownership to 49%. All other codes allow full foreign control.

2. **Low risk = fast licensing** — Most agriculture codes carry Low risk classification, meaning 2-4 week NIB processing with minimal sectoral permits.

3. **Multi-KBLI advantage** — Agritourism thrives on code combinations. Register 3-4 codes (plantation + tour + cafe + retail) to capture diversified revenue.

4. **Land workaround** — You can't own land, but Hak Pakai (30 years) or long-term lease (25 years) provides sufficient security for ROI.

5. **Tax incentives available** — Priority plantations (coffee, cacao, rubber) qualify for 0% CIT Holiday if you invest IDR 100 billion+.

For Bali specifically, the intersection of agriculture and tourism creates a **"premium commodity" play** — you're not just selling coffee beans, you're selling the experience of walking through a plantation at sunrise with Mount Batur in the background. KBLI 2025 finally gives that business model a legal structure.

The mandatory migration deadline is **June 18, 2026**. If you're operating an existing agriculture business under KBLI 2020, audit your codes now and prepare the transition. If you're a new investor, this is the optimal time to enter — the regulatory framework is settled, and the agritourism market in Bali is only beginning to mature.
