## The Facts

In its [26 February 2026 announcement](https://www.bkpm.go.id/index.php/id/info/siaran-pers/pemerintah-sesuaikan-sistem-oss-dengan-pp-28-tahun-2025-perkuat-kepastian-dan-kemudahan-berusaha), the Ministry of Investment and Downstream Industry/BKPM described adjustments to OSS under Government Regulation 28/2025. It said implementation was gradual and included changes to licensing procedures and specified basic requirements.

The announcement supports the existence of an evolving system. It does not promise that every applicant, activity or location receives all approvals immediately.

OSS also publishes an [official guide to converting older KBLI classifications to KBLI 2025](https://s3.oss.go.id/oss/cms/Panduan-Proses-Konversi-KBLI-2020-dan-Di-Bawah-2020-menjadi-KBLI-2025-%281%29-03e7993969c07e8cdedff108ee3a5046.pdf). That guide directs applicants to the relevant licensing process for their risk category. An old activity code should therefore be checked against the current system before reusing it.

### What is the NIB?

Nomor Induk Berusaha is the business identification number issued through OSS. For an application, check both the issued number and the status of the licences or requirements associated with each activity.

### What should a PT PMA owner ask before filing?

Ask which entity, activity classification, project location and risk category the application covers. Have the adviser distinguish incorporation, NIB issuance and any remaining permissions in the written scope of work.

### Does this article promise a processing time?

No. The sources reviewed do not establish a guaranteed completion time for an individual application.

## Bali Zero Take

### The Hidden Insight

A faster form is useful only if the data entered describe the business that will actually operate.

### Our Analysis

Our practical recommendation is to prepare an application brief before entering the portal. Describe what the business sells, how it delivers the service, where it operates and which entity signs contracts. Ask the adviser to reconcile that brief with the selected classification and application data.

For an existing business, preserve the current documents before making changes and ask whether an activity conversion or data update affects anything already issued. For a new business, request a clear list of what will remain outstanding after the NIB stage.

### Our Advice

Evaluate a registration service by its documented scope and completion evidence. Ask for the output at each stage and an explanation of any unresolved requirement before treating the project as ready to operate.
