## The Facts

This is an evergreen legal explainer, not an announcement of a new September law. Event date: n/d !; no new event is asserted.

[Government Regulation 18/2021](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Download/154522/PP%20Nomor%2018%20Tahun%202021.pdf) distinguishes the holder and the underlying land. Article 34 makes Hak Guna Bangunan (HGB) available to Indonesian citizens and legal entities established under Indonesian law and domiciled in Indonesia. A qualifying Indonesian PT PMA is distinct from its foreign shareholders; company-held rights are not the shareholder's personal land title.

Article 49 includes foreign individuals among eligible holders of time-limited Hak Pakai. Articles 69–72 regulate foreign residential ownership, including immigration documentation and restrictions on price, area, quantity and residential use. This is a conditional route, not unrestricted freehold access.

For HGB on state land or Hak Pengelolaan land, Article 37 provides up to 30 years, an extension of up to 20 years and renewal of up to 30 years. Extension and renewal are subject to conditions; they are not an automatic 80-year grant. Different underlying land can produce different arrangements.

A contractual lease is not the same instrument as HGB or Hak Pakai. Have an independent Indonesian notary/PPAT or qualified legal adviser identify the proposed instrument, the lawful holder, registration requirements and enforceability. Do not treat a nominee's name on a certificate as personal foreign freehold ownership.

Official verification for an actual purchase requires the competent ATR/BPN land office's certificate and registration records, the operative regulations and the buyer's eligibility documents. This article does not certify any transaction. Cover: AI-generated editorial illustration.

## Bali Zero Take

### The Hidden Insight

The decisive question is who holds which right, not whether a sales brochure calls a property “ownership.”

### Our Analysis

A company structure, an individual residential right and a lease allocate control differently. Each needs its own due diligence; none alone proves permission for short-term accommodation.

### Our Advice

Resolve the title, holder, permitted use and expiry conditions before paying a non-refundable deposit.
