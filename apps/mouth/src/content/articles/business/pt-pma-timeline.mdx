Setting up a PT PMA typically takes **4-8 weeks**. Here's what happens in each phase and how to plan your timeline.

## Overview

| Phase                  | Duration  | Cumulative |
| ---------------------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Planning & Documents   | 1-2 weeks | Week 1-2   |
| Notary & Legal Entity  | 1-2 weeks | Week 2-4   |
| Tax & OSS Registration | 1 week    | Week 3-5   |
| Bank Account           | 1-2 weeks | Week 4-7   |
| Operational Permits    | 1-3 weeks | Week 5-8   |
