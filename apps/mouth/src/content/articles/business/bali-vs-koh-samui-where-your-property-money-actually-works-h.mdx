## The Two Markets at a Glance

Bali and Koh Samui are the two most popular destinations in Southeast Asia for foreign property buyers seeking both lifestyle and income. Both markets share a structural constraint: foreigners cannot own freehold land outright. Everything else diverges significantly.

**Bali (Indonesia)**
Foreigners cannot hold SHM (Sertifikat Hak Milik — freehold title) directly. Legal investment routes include:
