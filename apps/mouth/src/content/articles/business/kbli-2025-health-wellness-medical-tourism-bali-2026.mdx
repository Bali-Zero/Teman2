# KBLI 2025 Health & Wellness Investment Guide: Medical Tourism, Private Clinics & Wellness Retreats in Bali

Bali's health and wellness sector is undergoing its most significant transformation in a generation. Two forces are converging: **UU Kesehatan 17/2023** (the comprehensive Health Law that revokes 11 prior laws and restructures the entire regulatory framework) and **KEK Sanur** (the 41-hectare medical tourism special economic zone). Together, they are opening Indonesia's healthcare market to foreign investment at a level previously unavailable.

For PT PMA investors, the timing is critical. The KBLI 2025 deadline is June 18, 2026. And the **October 2026 Halal certification mandate** will hit every cosmetics, traditional medicine, and OTC product used in Bali's 3,000+ wellness businesses. This guide covers all relevant KBLI 86xxx codes plus supporting codes 74910 (IP brokerage) and 96120 (beauty/salon services) with actionable 2026 investment intelligence.

## The Bali Wellness Market: 2026 Landscape

**Medical Tourism:** Growing 15–20% YoY. Key feeder markets: Singapore, Australia, Japan, Europe. Main drivers: competitive pricing (50–70% below Singapore/Australia for equivalent quality), improving international accreditation (JCI, KARS), and Bali's lifestyle appeal for post-treatment recovery.

**Wellness Tourism:** Growing 20–25% YoY. Traditional Balinese healing (melukat, traditional massage, jamu), yoga retreats, and integrated wellness programs are Bali's strongest experiential differentiator globally.

**Regulatory window:** UU 17/2023 removed the IDI (Indonesian Medical Association) endorsement requirement that previously blocked foreign specialist applications. For the first time, PT PMA health companies can recruit foreign medical professionals via a streamlined KKI (Konsil Kedokteran Indonesia) process.

## KEK Sanur: The Medical Tourism Special Zone

**Location:** Sanur, Denpasar Selatan
**Area:** 41 hectares
**Focus:** Medical tourism, international hospital development, pharmaceutical manufacturing, medical devices, health R&D

**Key KEZ advantages under PP 24/2022:**

- Tax holiday: 5–20 years depending on investment scale and sector
- 100% foreign ownership (PT PMA) fully permitted
- Simplified licensing: one-stop via KEK management body (BUPP)
- Relaxed TKA: foreign medical professionals may practice for up to 2 years under supervised arrangement without full KKI equivalency process
- Import duty exemptions on medical equipment and supplies

**Entry requirements for KEK Sanur:**

- Investment commitment letter to KPPIP
- Business plan approved by KEK management
- Environmental compliance pre-assessment
- IDR 20B minimum investment for hospital development (varies by activity type)

**Practical note:** Non-KEK health investments outside Sanur follow standard BKPM Reg. 5/2025 requirements: IDR 2.5B paid-up + IDR 10B total investment per KBLI code.

## KBLI 86103: Private Hospital (Rumah Sakit Swasta)

**Code:** 86103
**Title:** AKTIVITAS RUMAH SAKIT SWASTA
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed (especially within KEK Sanur)
**Activity:** Development and operation of private hospitals providing inpatient and outpatient medical services.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Private hospital development is booming. International benchmarks BIMC Nusa Dua and Kasih Ibu are expanding. The KEK Sanur anchor hospital (rumored to involve a Singapore Group) will set the new quality standard for the island.

**Bali Nuance:** KEK Sanur is the preferred entry point for premium international hospital PT PMA. Outside KEK, Badung and Seminyak/Kuta hospital zoning is available but faces competition from established players. Ubud highlands (wellness-oriented hospital concept) is an underserved premium niche.

**Operational Risks:**

- UU Kesehatan 17/2023 Section 4: all hospitals must register with Sistem Informasi Rumah Sakit (SIRS) national database within 90 days of operational license
- BPJS Kesehatan accreditation (KARS Level Paripurna minimum) required to receive BPJS patient flow — without it, hospital is limited to self-pay patients
- Foreign doctors: STR (Surat Tanda Registrasi) + SIP (Surat Izin Praktik) from KKI are mandatory even under KEK simplified rules — allow 3–6 months for KKI processing
- Halal certification mandatory from October 2026 for all pharmaceutical products, food/beverage, and OTC products used in hospital premises (BPJPH PP 39/2021)
- Medical waste: Permen LHK 56/2015 requires licensed medical waste transporter + TPS B3 temporary storage facility with Dinas Lingkungan Hidup approval

**Investment Outlook:** Strong 5–10 year ROI for international standard hospital with JCI accreditation + medical tourism focus. Target: 200+ beds, specialist-led, English-speaking clinical staff.

**Code Note:** Use **86103** (Rumah Sakit Swasta) for PT PMA hospital investment. **86101** (Rumah Sakit Pemerintah) is government-owned and not available for PT PMA.

## KBLI 86105: Private Clinic (Klinik Swasta)

**Code:** 86105
**Title:** AKTIVITAS KLINIK SWASTA
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Operation of private outpatient clinics providing medical consultation, treatment, and minor procedures.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Private clinics are the most accessible PT PMA entry into Bali's health sector. Setup cost IDR 500M–IDR 2B; payback 18–24 months in premium tourist zones. Aesthetic medicine (botox, dermal fillers, laser) is the highest-margin subspecialty.

**Bali Nuance:** Canggu, Seminyak, and Ubud have active clinic clusters with 85%+ occupancy serving expats, digital nomads, and wellness tourists. Key winning differentiators: 24/7 emergency service, English-speaking staff, international insurance acceptance, telemedicine follow-up.

**Licensing Path:**

1. NIB from OSS (KBLI 86105)
2. Surat Izin Operasional Klinik from **Dinas Kesehatan Kabupaten/Kota** (not issued through OSS — requires physical inspection)
3. Minimum staff: 1 licensed doctor on premises during operating hours (dokter penanggung jawab)
4. Medical waste disposal contract with licensed transporter
5. Annual renewal from Dinas Kesehatan

**Operational Risks:**

- Aesthetic procedures using imported medical devices require BPOM Alkes (alat kesehatan) registration — each device type requires separate registration, process 3–6 months
- Foreign doctors: KKI STR process for non-ASEAN doctors is 3–6 months minimum; "visiting specialist" arrangement available under UU 17/2023 for up to 6 months initially
- BPJS participation: optional but recommended for local patient market — significantly expands patient base
- Permenkes 9/2014 updated under UU 17/2023 sets minimum facility standards (examination room dimensions, sterilization equipment, emergency equipment list)

**Investment Outlook:** Aesthetic medicine clinics and sports medicine practices in Canggu/Seminyak are generating USD 50K–USD 200K monthly revenue. Dental specialty clinics with panoramic X-ray and implant capabilities show 24-month breakeven in high-tourist zones.

## KBLI 86201 & 86202: Specialist Medical Practice

**Code 86201:** AKTIVITAS PRAKTIK DOKTER — General medical practice
**Code 86202:** AKTIVITAS PRAKTIK DOKTER SPESIALIS — Specialist medical practice
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed (via PT PMA with Indonesian doctor on record)

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**86201 — General Practice:**
Bali's 300,000+ long-term foreign residents + 15M+ annual tourists generate consistent demand for English-speaking GPs. Expat-focused general practice clinics (Canggu Health Center model) serve as primary care anchors for the foreign community.

**Key compliance note:** The "dokter penanggung jawab" (responsible doctor on the SIP) must hold an Indonesian KKI license. For PT PMA structures: the Indonesian director/local partner holds the SIP; foreign GPs practice under supervised arrangement pending their own KKI registration.

**86202 — Specialist Practice:**
The UU 17/2023 change that removed IDI endorsement for foreign specialists is the 2026 game-changer. Previously, a foreign cardiologist, orthopedic surgeon, or dermatologist needed IDI organizational approval (which was discretionary and politicized). Now, KKI directly evaluates the credential equivalency — the process is procedural, not political.

**KEK Sanur advantage for specialists:** Under PP 24/2022, specialist doctors in KEK Sanur can practice for 2 years under simplified arrangement while their full KKI STR application is processed. This enables immediate deployment of international specialists.

**High-demand specialties in Bali (2026):**

1. Orthopedics/Sports Medicine (surf/dive injury volume)
2. Dermatology/Aesthetic Medicine (cosmetic demand + skin cancer screening for sun-exposed tourists)
3. Cardiology (medical tourism from Southeast Asia)
4. Oncology (KEK Sanur medical tourism target)
5. Ophthalmology (LASIK, cataract — significant cross-border patient flow)

## KBLI 86992: Traditional Health Services (Jamu & Balinese Wellness)

**Code:** 86992
**Title:** AKTIVITAS PELAYANAN KESEHATAN TRADISIONAL
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Traditional medicine, herbal health services, Balinese healing practices, jamu production and service.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Traditional wellness is Bali's most differentiated global offering. Melukat (Balinese purification ritual), traditional boreh (herbal body wrap), and jamu therapy align with global "authentic wellness" trends driving premium pricing.

**Bali Nuance:** The distinction matters — 86992 covers traditional and herbal health services; 86995 covers massage/spa; 96120 covers beauty salons. Integrated wellness retreats typically need a combination of 55203 (villa accommodation) + 86992 (traditional wellness) + 86995 (massage) in their NIB.

**October 2026 Halal Mandate — Critical for Wellness:**
BPJPH PP 39/2021 mandates Halal certification by October 2026 for:

- All jamu and traditional herbal products
- Cosmetics and personal care products used commercially
- OTC medicines (obat bebas/obat bebas terbatas)

**Practical impact:** 3,000+ Bali wellness businesses must certify their product supply chain before the deadline. Non-certified products cannot legally be used in commercial services. Certification path: BPJPH registration + MUI (Majelis Ulama Indonesia) Halal audit + certificate issuance (typically 3–6 months; priority track available for small businesses).

**Practitioner Requirements:**

- STPT (Surat Tanda Pendaftaran Tenaga Penyehat Tradisional) from Dinas Kesehatan for traditional health practitioners
- BPOM registration required for any manufactured jamu products (not required for immediate-service preparations)

**Investment Outlook:** Premium integrated Balinese wellness retreat (86992 + 55203) generating USD 200–USD 800/night is among the highest-EBITDA models in Bali hospitality. Target market: 3–7 day "wellness immersion" programs for European and Australian clients.

## KBLI 86995: Spa & Massage Establishments (Rumah Pijat)

**Code:** 86995
**Title:** AKTIVITAS RUMAH PIJAT
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Dedicated spa and massage establishments offering therapeutic and relaxation massage, body treatments.

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Bali's spa industry is consolidating from 5,000+ fragmented operators toward branded concepts. Premium international spa brands command 10–15x pricing over street-level operators. The market is bifurcating: ultra-premium retreat spa vs. high-frequency "express" spa for tourists.

**Bali Nuance:** Standalone day spa/massage centers use 86995. Hotel spa operations integrated into a villa or resort register under the hotel's primary code (55203, 55101). Mixing these is a common error that creates licensing gaps.

**Operational Risks:**

- Therapist certification: BNSP (Badan Nasional Sertifikasi Profesi) competency certificate required for massage therapists per Permenaker 2/2016
- Hiburan tax exposure: Badung and Gianyar classify massage services as "hiburan" (entertainment) in some zones → **10–15% gross revenue hiburan tax** in addition to standard PPN/PPh
- Halal certification October 2026: all aromatherapy oils, scrubs, body wraps, and cosmetic products used in services must be Halal-certified
- BPOM import license required for commercially-used non-ASEAN cosmetic/aromatic products

**Facility Requirements:** Permenkes on spa facilities (updated under UU 17/2023 implementing regulations) specifies: minimum room dimensions, ventilation, sterilization equipment, water quality standards.

**Investment Outlook:**

- Budget spa chain (IDR 200M setup): 12–18 month payback in tourist zones
- Premium concept (IDR 1B–IDR 2B setup): 24–36 month payback; significantly higher LTV per client
- Franchise model (under Indonesian brands or international concepts): lower risk, faster ramp

## KBLI 96120: Beauty Salons & Nail Services

**Code:** 96120
**Title:** AKTIVITAS SALON KECANTIKAN
**PMA Status:** 100% Foreign Ownership Allowed
**Activity:** Hair salons, nail salons, beauty treatment centers (non-medical aesthetic).

### 2026 Investment Intelligence

**Market Sentiment:** Beauty salon market is high-frequency, high-repeat — one of Bali's most stable small business segments. Premium international concepts (blow-dry bars, nail art studios, Korean beauty salons) are showing 20%+ market growth.

**Bali Nuance:** 96120 is the correct code for non-medical beauty services. Medical aesthetic procedures (botox, chemical peels, laser) require 86105 (klinik swasta) + licensed doctor. Mixing medical aesthetics and beauty services under 96120 creates regulatory exposure.

**October 2026 Halal Mandate — Specific Impact:**
All commercial cosmetics used in salon services must be Halal-certified by October 2026:

- Hair dyes (cat rambut)
- Nail polish and gel systems
- Skin care products used in facials
- Waxing and hair removal products

This will require all salons to audit their product suppliers for BPJPH certification status.

**Operational Risks:**

- BNSP competency certificates: hairdressers and nail technicians require BNSP certification
- Badung/Gianyar hiburan tax: some beauty services in resort zones classified as entertainment
- BPOM: import of non-ASEAN professional cosmetics for commercial use requires Alkes/Cosmed registration

**Investment Outlook:**

- Budget salon (IDR 100M–IDR 200M): 6–12 month payback in tourist density areas
- Premium concept salon (IDR 300M–IDR 800M): 12–18 month payback
- Salon franchise: lowest-risk entry, national brand support

### October 2026 Halal Deadline — Action Plan

All Bali health and wellness businesses must complete by **October 17, 2026**:

1. **Inventory audit:** List all products used commercially (cosmetics, traditional medicines, OTC drugs, food/beverages)
2. **Supplier check:** Verify which suppliers already have BPJPH Halal certification
3. **Product substitution:** Replace non-certifiable products with Halal-certified alternatives
4. **BPJPH registration:** Submit products that can be certified
5. **Documentation:** Maintain Halal compliance records for future audits

**Non-compliance:** Post-deadline, using non-certified products in commercial services exposes businesses to Dinas Kesehatan closure orders and BPOM administrative penalties.
