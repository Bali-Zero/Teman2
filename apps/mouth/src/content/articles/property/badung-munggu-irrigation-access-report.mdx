## The Facts

[The Bali Times reported on 15 September 2026](https://thebalitimes.com/the-island/badung-public-order-agency-to-remove-villa-access-blocking-irrigation-channel-in-bali/) that Badung's public order agency, Satpol PP, had agreed in principle to remove a concrete structure associated with villa access in Subak Munggu, Mengwi.

According to the report, officials first need to reconcile boundaries involving Bali provincial government land and a Jalan Usaha Tani, or agricultural service road. A joint inspection is expected before enforcement. These are attributed statements from the newspaper; Bali Zero has not independently obtained an inspection finding or removal order.

The source's publication date is established. The calendar date of the official's statement, described only as Monday, remains unverified. No completed demolition, exact removal timetable or liability finding is established by this article.

### What should a buyer verify?

Ask for a plan that identifies the property, the full access route and any irrigation crossings. Then ask the relevant adviser and authorities to reconcile that plan with the documents being relied upon. A seller's description should be checked against evidence specific to the transaction.

### Does this report prove that a particular villa is unlawful?

## Bali Zero Take

### The Hidden Insight

A property can be easy to reach on a site visit while the documentary basis for that access remains unclear. That gap belongs in the purchase checklist.

### Our Analysis

Our due-diligence recommendation is to separate three questions: which land is being acquired, what supports the route across adjoining land, and whether the physical route affects a water channel. Request evidence for each instead of treating a single document as the answer to all three.

This is a recommendation for reviewing a proposed transaction, not a legal finding about the properties mentioned in the report.

### Our Advice

Before committing funds, ask an independent qualified property adviser to identify any unresolved access or boundary issue in writing. Where irrigation is involved, include the competent local authority and subak representatives in the enquiries.
