## The Facts

Tax planning for life and investment in Bali begins with separating the individual from the business. An expat's residence, a shareholder payment and a company's sales are different questions. Treating them as one “foreigner tax” can produce the wrong answer before a calculation even starts.

This guide reviews established rules and practical records to check. It does not announce a new October 2026 tax package or an open tax-amnesty programme.

## Residency is more than a day count

The rules published by DJP identify residence in Indonesia, presence exceeding 183 days within twelve months, or presence during a tax year with an intention to reside as routes to domestic tax-subject status. Being below the day threshold does not settle every case.

The same framework provides a limited Indonesian-source treatment for qualifying foreign nationals with specified expertise during four tax years, subject to its conditions. It is not a general exemption for every expat. Work performed in Indonesia can remain relevant even if payment arrives abroad. [DJP: residency and foreign-national provisions](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pelaksanaan-undang-undang-nomor-11-tahun-2020-tentang-cipta-kerja-di-bidang-pajak)

For a practical review, gather travel records, housing arrangements, work contracts and the countries in which income arises. Ask the adviser to explain the status conclusion and any treaty or special-rule treatment being relied on.

## There is no general 20–40% foreign-worker bracket

DJP lists domestic individual income-tax bands of 5%, 15%, 25%, 30% and 35%, applied progressively to annual taxable income. The thresholds are Rp60 million, Rp250 million, Rp500 million and Rp5 billion. The top percentage is not automatically applied to every rupiah of salary.

Taxable income and gross receipts are different measures. Applicable deductions, allowances, final-tax income and tax credits need separate consideration. [DJP: individual income-tax calculation](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/mekanisme-penghitungan-pajak-penghasilan-orang-pribadi)

That is why a headline claiming a special 20–40% rate for foreign workers should not be used as a payroll assumption.

## Keep company payments separate from the owner's tax position

Article 26 withholding is another assessment. DJP's guidance requires checking whether the recipient is a foreign tax subject and whether treaty treatment is available. Certain covered payments carry a domestic rate of 20% of gross income, while treaty provisions can alter the result when their conditions are met. [DJP: Article 26 withholding](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/pemotongan-pajak-penghasilan-pasal-26)

A PT PMA owner should therefore identify the nature of each payment rather than group everything under “money taken from the company.” Keep the agreement, transaction purpose, recipient information and supporting tax documents together.

This article is not a substitute for assessing an individual payment. The useful discipline is to make the legal and accounting description agree with what actually occurred.

## A premium price does not determine the VAT treatment

Hotel accommodation and related activities need their own classification. PMK 70/2022 specifies qualifying hotel services and other activities outside VAT, alongside distinctions and exclusions. A high price or the word “luxury” does not by itself turn every Bali accommodation transaction into a VAT-taxable supply. [PMK 70/2022](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/70-pmk-03-2022)

Before changing an invoice template, identify the service, operating arrangement and applicable national or local tax treatment. Keep accommodation, property rental and other services distinct in the review rather than assuming one label covers them all.

## Match the records before filing

Coretax DJP began implementation on 1 January 2025, with that start date set by Finance Minister Decision 456/2024. The tax-administration framework is PMK 81/2024, as amended; a review should use the provisions applicable to the relevant period rather than rely on the launch decision alone. JDIH lists PMK 1/2026 as its fourth amendment. [Implementation decision](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/implementasi-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan) · [PMK 81/2024 and amendment history](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024/overview)

Coretax is part of the current administrative setting, not evidence of a new tax rate by itself.

As a preparation checklist, reconcile recorded income, withholding evidence, payments and filed returns for the relevant period. Review company and personal records separately, then check the transactions connecting them.

The strongest starting point for an expat or investor is an accurate fact pattern: residence, income type, payer, recipient, activity and period. Those details make it possible to apply the correct rule without relying on an outdated summary.
