## The Facts

Indonesia's cost landscape for short-term visitors spans an unusually wide range: a budget backpacker can survive on $25–$40 per day while a luxury traveller routinely spends $300 or more. For the middle segment — the most relevant cohort for business travellers and prospective expats — the realistic daily spend lands between $70 and $130, putting a seven-day trip at roughly $1,000 to $1,200 per person, excluding international flights.

The Indonesian rupiah currently trades at approximately 15,500–17,000 to the US dollar. International airfare adds $150–$200 for regional Southeast Asian routes and up to $1,200 from North America or Europe, making the total outlay for a mid-range traveller from the West anywhere between $1,200 and $2,400 for a week.

The most consequential cost decision, the analysis argues, is not daily discipline but island selection. Bali is consistently the most expensive island in the archipelago for visitors — Java and Lombok both offer comparable or superior experiences in specific categories at 30–50% lower daily cost. The Bali premium is concentrated in accommodation and dining in tourist-heavy corridors such as Seminyak, Canggu, and Ubud.

Seasonal pricing plays a significant role. January, February, October, and November are identified as the cheapest months for travel, while July, August, and late December carry peak surcharges across accommodation, domestic transport, and tourist-adjacent services. These peaks align with European summer holidays and the global festive season.

At the budget tier, the typical profile involves hostel dormitories or basic homestays, warung street food, scooter rental, and free public beaches — a combination that keeps costs at or below $420 for seven days. The luxury tier, priced at approximately $3,200 for seven days, reflects private villa accommodation, private drivers, fine dining, and curated experiences. The article notes that both traveller profiles can share the same island, eat the same dish, and pay vastly different prices for the privilege.
