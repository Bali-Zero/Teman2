## What Is the Global Minimum Tax?

The Global Minimum Tax (GMT) is the popular name for the OECD's Pillar Two framework, formally known as the Global Anti-Base Erosion (GloBE) rules. Its central mechanism is simple: any multinational enterprise (MNE) group with annual consolidated revenue of **€750 million or more** must pay a minimum **15% effective tax rate (ETR)** on profits in every jurisdiction where it operates.
