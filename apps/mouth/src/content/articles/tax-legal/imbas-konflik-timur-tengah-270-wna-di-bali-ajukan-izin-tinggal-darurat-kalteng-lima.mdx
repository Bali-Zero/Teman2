## The Facts

As a consequence of the ongoing Middle East conflict, 270 foreign nationals (WNA) residing in Bali have applied for emergency stay permits (Izin Tinggal Darurat). This development reflects the broader humanitarian and administrative impact of geopolitical instability on Indonesia's expatriate and tourist population in Bali. The Indonesian Immigration Office (Imigrasi) is processing these applications under emergency provisions that allow foreign nationals unable to return to their home countries
