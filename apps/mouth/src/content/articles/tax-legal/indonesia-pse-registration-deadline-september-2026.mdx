## The Facts

A [Komdigi press release published by InfoPublik on 17 September 2026](https://infopublik.id/kategori/siaran-pers/989544/index.html) states that notices were sent on 16 September to 25 private electronic system operators: 23 domestic and two foreign.

The release sets 22 September 2026 as the registration deadline and cites Ministerial Regulation 5/2020. It describes the obligation as applying to operators that meet the relevant criteria. This article does not claim that all 25 have already been blocked or that every operator remains unregistered as of publication.

The [official private-sector PSE registration portal](https://pse.komdigi.go.id/home) explicitly directs applicants to OSS. Use that official starting point and the instructions appropriate to the operator's status.

### Is an OSS business account the same as completed PSE registration?

Do not use account access alone as proof of completion. Check the relevant registration record and the actual status of the electronic system concerned.

### Does having Indonesian users settle every registration question?

It does not replace a review of the applicable criteria and the operator's actual service. The notified group includes foreign operators, but this report does not establish that every foreign applicant must incorporate an Indonesian company.

### Has the deadline passed?

As of this editorial check on 21 September 2026, 22 September is still upcoming. Any later enforcement outcome requires a fresh official update.

## Bali Zero Take

### The Hidden Insight

The useful evidence is the registration status of the service people use. An old email, an account login or a colleague's assurance may leave that question unanswered.

### Our Analysis

For a Bali business operating a booking platform, marketplace or other digital service, we recommend identifying the responsible entity and the exact system before requesting a compliance assessment. Record the domain, service description, domestic or foreign operator status, and any notice received.

Businesses using another company's platform have a different practical concern: continuity. Ask the provider for a current status statement and maintain an alternative way for customers to reach the business.

### Our Advice

If a notice has arrived, assign one person to coordinate the response, preserve the notice and submission evidence, and use the regulator's official assistance channels for technical issues. Do not assume that starting an application proves it is complete.
