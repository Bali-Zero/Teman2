# STM/ERP/MERP 2026: Exit and Re-Entry Permits for KITAS Holders in Indonesia

If you hold a KITAS (Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas) or KITAP (Kartu Izin Tinggal Tetap) in Indonesia, understanding exit and re-entry permits is crucial before you book any international travel. **Leaving Indonesia without the proper permit will automatically cancel your KITAS**, forcing you to start the entire application process from scratch—a costly and time-consuming mistake.

This guide covers three types of exit permits: EPO (Exit Permit Only), ERP (Exit Re-entry Permit), and MERP (Multiple Exit Re-entry Permit). You'll learn when to use each type, how much they cost, and how to apply.

## Understanding Indonesian Exit Permits: The Basics

Indonesia's immigration system requires all temporary and permanent residents to obtain explicit permission before leaving the country. This isn't bureaucratic red tape—it's how the government tracks legal residency status and ensures permit holders intend to return.

### Why Exit Permits Exist

When you receive a KITAS or KITAP, you're granted the right to stay in Indonesia for a specific period. The government needs to know:

- Whether you plan to return to Indonesia
- How many times you'll exit and enter during your permit validity
- Whether you're leaving permanently

Without an exit permit system, Indonesia couldn't effectively manage its foreign resident population or enforce visa regulations.

### The Three Types of Exit Permits

Indonesian immigration law recognizes three distinct exit permits, each serving different travel needs:

**1. EPO (Exit Permit Only)** - Also called STM (Surat Tidak Masuk, literally "No Return Letter")

- Purpose: Permanent departure from Indonesia
- Effect: Your KITAS/KITAP is cancelled upon exit
- Cost: IDR 700,000 (standard) or IDR 1,000,000 (urgent)

**2. ERP (Exit Re-entry Permit)** - Single-use re-entry

- Purpose: One-time international trip with return to Indonesia
- Effect: Allows one exit and one return within KITAS validity period
- Cost: IDR 800,000 (standard) or IDR 1,300,000 (urgent)

**3. MERP (Multiple Exit Re-entry Permit)** - Multi-use re-entry

- Purpose: Unlimited international travel during validity period
- Effect: Multiple exits and entries allowed
- Cost: IDR 4,000,000 (1 year) or IDR 5,000,000 (2 years)
- Validity: 1 or 2 years, cannot exceed your KITAS expiry date

## EPO vs ERP vs MERP: Complete Comparison

Choosing the right permit type depends on your travel plans and how long you intend to stay in Indonesia. Here's a detailed comparison:

| Feature                    | EPO (Exit Only)               | ERP (Single Re-entry)          | MERP (Multiple Re-entry)                    |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Full Name**              | Exit Permit Only              | Exit Re-entry Permit           | Multiple Exit Re-entry Permit               |

| **Number of Exits**        | 1 (no return)                 | 1                              | Unlimited                                   |
| **KITAS Status After Use** | Cancelled                     | Remains valid                  | Remains valid                               |

| **When to Use**            | Leaving Indonesia permanently | One international trip planned | Frequent international travel               |

### Cost Analysis: Which Permit Makes Financial Sense?

**Scenario 1: Two trips in one year**

- 2x ERP: IDR 1,600,000
- 1x MERP (1 year): IDR 4,000,000
- **Verdict:** ERP is cheaper for infrequent travel

**Scenario 2: Five trips in one year**

- 5x ERP: IDR 4,000,000
- 1x MERP (1 year): IDR 4,000,000
- **Verdict:** MERP offers better value and convenience

**Scenario 3: More than five trips in one year**

- 6x ERP: IDR 4,800,000
- 1x MERP (1 year): IDR 4,000,000
- **Verdict:** MERP is significantly cheaper

**Break-even point:** If you plan to leave Indonesia 5 or more times per year, MERP pays for itself.

## When to Choose Each Permit Type

### Choose EPO (Exit Permit Only) When:

1. **You're relocating permanently** - Your employment contract ended, you're moving to another country, or you no longer need Indonesian residency
2. **Your company is closing** - The sponsor company is dissolving, and you won't find a new sponsor
3. **You're switching to a different visa type** - Some visa changes require cancelling your current KITAS first
4. **KITAS expires soon and you won't renew** - If your KITAS has less than 30 days remaining and you don't plan to extend

**Important:** Once you use EPO, your KITAS is permanently cancelled. You cannot change your mind at the airport.

### Choose ERP (Single Re-entry) When:

1. **You have one international trip planned** - Attending a conference, family wedding, short vacation
2. **You travel internationally 1-4 times per year** - Occasional business trips or holidays
3. **Your KITAS is close to expiring** - Less than 6 months remaining, so MERP wouldn't be cost-effective
4. **You need flexibility** - You can get ERP at the airport if you forgot to arrange it beforehand (though this costs more)

**Pro tip:** If you have a sudden emergency requiring international travel, you can get ERP at the airport with urgent processing.

### Choose MERP (Multiple Re-entry) When:

1. **You travel internationally frequently** - 5+ trips per year for business or family
2. **You split time between countries** - Digital nomads, executives with regional responsibilities
3. **You have long-term Indonesian residency** - Your KITAS has 1-2 years remaining
4. **You value convenience** - No need to apply for exit permits before each trip
5. **You have unpredictable travel** - You might need to leave Indonesia on short notice

**Strategic advantage:** MERP holders can leave Indonesia immediately for emergencies without visiting immigration offices.

## How to Apply for Exit Permits: Step-by-Step Process

### Application Process for EPO and ERP

Both EPO and ERP follow similar application procedures, with options for advance application or airport application.

#### Method 1: Apply at Local Immigration Office (Recommended)

**Step 1: Prepare Required Documents**

- Original passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Original KITAS card
- Photocopy of passport bio page
- Photocopy of Indonesian visa page
- Photocopy of KITAS card (both sides)
- Sponsor letter from company (for work KITAS) or guarantor
- Payment receipt for government fees

**Step 2: Visit Immigration Office**

- Go to the immigration office that issued your KITAS
- Submit documents at the STM counter
- Pay government fees at the cashier (IDR 700,000 for EPO, IDR 800,000 for ERP)
- Receive application receipt

**Step 3: Processing**

- Standard processing: 3-5 working days
- Urgent processing: Same day or next day (+IDR 300,000 for EPO, +IDR 500,000 for ERP)
- You may need to return to collect the permit, or some offices stamp it directly in your passport

**Step 4: Collection**

- Return to immigration office with receipt
- Your passport will be stamped with exit permit
- Verify the stamp includes correct dates and permit type

**Timeline:** Plan to apply at least 7 working days before travel to account for potential delays.

#### Method 2: Apply at Airport (More Expensive, Same-Day)

If you forgot to arrange your exit permit in advance, you can apply directly at the airport—but expect higher fees and longer processing times.

**Available at:** Soekarno-Hatta (Jakarta), Ngurah Rai (Bali), Juanda (Surabaya), and other major international airports

**Process:**

1. Arrive at airport at least 4 hours before departure
2. Go to the immigration office (not the departure counters)
3. Submit passport, KITAS, and sponsor letter
4. Pay fees (urgent processing rates apply automatically)
5. Wait for processing (30 minutes to 2 hours depending on volume)
6. Receive passport with exit permit stamp
7. Proceed to check-in and departure as normal

**Costs at airport:**

- EPO: IDR 1,000,000 (urgent rate)
- ERP: IDR 1,300,000 (urgent rate)

**Drawbacks:**

- Significantly more expensive
- Risk of missing your flight due to processing delays
- Limited to major airports (smaller airports may not offer this service)
- High stress if problems arise with documents

### Application Process for MERP (Multiple Re-entry Permit)

MERP requires more advance planning and cannot be obtained at the airport.

**Step 1: Gather Complete Documentation**

- Original passport (minimum 1 year validity)
- Original KITAS card
- Photocopy of passport bio page and Indonesian visa page
- Photocopy of KITAS (both sides)
- Recent passport photo (4x6 cm, white background)
- Sponsor letter from company or guarantor stating:
  - Your need for multiple international trips
  - Your employment/residency status
  - Confirmation that you'll return to Indonesia
- IMTA (work permit) copy if applicable
- Proof of address in Indonesia
- Payment receipt for government fees

**Step 2: Visit Immigration Office**

- Go to the immigration office where your KITAS was issued
- Submit application at MERP counter
- Pay government fees:
  - IDR 4,000,000 for 1-year MERP
  - IDR 5,000,000 for 2-year MERP
- Provide biometric data if required

**Step 3: Processing Period**

- Processing time: 3-5 working days (urgent processing not available for MERP)
- Immigration office may verify information with your sponsor
- You may be contacted for additional documents or clarification

**Step 4: Collection and Activation**

- Return to immigration office with receipt
- Receive MERP sticker/stamp in passport
- Verify details: validity dates, permit type, number of entries
- Your MERP is now active and valid for unlimited exits

**Important:** MERP validity cannot exceed your KITAS expiry date. If your KITAS has 8 months remaining, you can only get MERP valid for 8 months, even if you pay for 1 year.

### Required Sponsor Letter Format

All exit permit applications require a sponsor letter. Here's what it should include:

```

Date: [Application Date]

[For MERP:] We confirm that [Name] requires frequent international travel as part of their employment responsibilities with our company. They will make multiple trips during the validity period of their KITAS and will maintain their residence in Indonesia.

```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Leaving Without Any Exit Permit

**The mistake:** Assuming your KITAS allows you to travel freely, or forgetting to check exit permit requirements before booking flights.

**The consequence:** Your KITAS is automatically cancelled the moment you exit Indonesia through immigration. When you return, you'll be treated as a tourist (or denied entry entirely if you don't qualify for visa-free access). To resume residency, you'll need to apply for a completely new KITAS from scratch—a process costing IDR 15,000,000+ and taking 2-3 months.

**How to avoid:** Check your passport for valid exit permits before booking any international travel. Set calendar reminders for exit permit applications.

### 2. Applying Too Close to Travel Date

**The mistake:** Visiting immigration 1-2 days before your flight, expecting same-day processing.

**The consequence:** Standard processing takes 3-5 working days. If you apply too late, you either:

- Pay urgent processing fees (50-60% premium)
- Pay even higher fees at the airport
- Miss your flight entirely
- Have to cancel/reschedule travel plans

**How to avoid:** Apply for exit permits at least 7 working days before departure. For MERP, apply 10 working days in advance. Consider getting MERP if you have any international travel planned within the next year.

### 3. Choosing MERP When KITAS Expires Soon

**The mistake:** Paying IDR 4,000,000 for 1-year MERP when your KITAS expires in 3 months.

**The consequence:** Your MERP can only be valid until your KITAS expiry date, so you pay for 12 months but get only 3 months of validity. When you extend your KITAS, you'll need to apply for a new MERP.

**How to avoid:** Calculate your KITAS remaining validity before choosing MERP. If you have less than 6 months remaining, stick with ERP until after you extend your KITAS, then apply for MERP.

### 4. Using EPO When You Meant to Return

**The mistake:** Choosing EPO because it's slightly cheaper than ERP, not understanding it cancels your KITAS.

**The consequence:** Permanent loss of KITAS status. You cannot reverse this decision at the airport or after departure. To return to Indonesia, you need a completely new visa process.

**How to avoid:** Read all application forms carefully. Confirm with immigration officers that you understand the difference between EPO (no return) and ERP (with return). If there's any possibility you might want to return, always choose ERP or MERP.

### 5. Forgetting to Bring Original Documents

**The mistake:** Arriving at immigration with photocopies only, or leaving your KITAS at home.

**The consequence:** Application rejected, you waste a trip to immigration, and you risk running out of time before travel.

**How to avoid:** Create a checklist of required documents. The night before your immigration visit, verify you have:

- Original passport
- Original KITAS card
- All photocopies
- Sponsor letter
- Recent photos (for MERP)
- Payment method for fees

### 6. Overstaying Your Exit Permit Validity

**The mistake:** Booking a return flight after your ERP expires, or staying abroad longer than planned without MERP.

**The consequence:** Your ERP becomes invalid if you return after your KITAS expiry date. Immigration may refuse entry, or you'll need to apply for a new visa at the Indonesian embassy abroad.

**How to avoid:** When booking flights, verify your return date is before your KITAS expiry date. If your travel plans change while abroad and you'll return late, contact your sponsor immediately to explore options (KITAS extension may be possible, but complex).

### 7. Not Updating MERP After KITAS Extension

**The mistake:** Extending your KITAS but forgetting to extend your MERP, which remains tied to your old KITAS expiry date.

**The consequence:** Your MERP becomes invalid when your old KITAS would have expired, even though you now have a new KITAS valid for another 1-2 years.

**How to avoid:** When you extend your KITAS, immediately check your MERP validity. If your MERP will expire before your new KITAS period ends, apply for a new MERP. Some immigration offices allow MERP extension alongside KITAS extension in a single process.

## Urgent Processing: When and How

Both EPO and ERP offer urgent processing options for emergency situations—but MERP does not.

### When Urgent Processing Makes Sense

**Legitimate urgent situations:**

- Family emergency requiring immediate international travel
- Unexpected business meeting or critical work requirement
- Medical emergency abroad
- You genuinely forgot to apply in advance (not ideal, but happens)

**Not recommended for:**

- Poor planning or procrastination (the fees are punitive for a reason)
- Regular business travel (get MERP instead)
- Cost-saving attempts (urgent fees are 43-63% more expensive)

### Urgent Processing Fees and Timelines

**At Immigration Office:**

- EPO urgent: IDR 1,000,000 (standard IDR 700,000 + IDR 300,000 urgent fee)
  - Processing time: Same day or next working day
- ERP urgent: IDR 1,300,000 (standard IDR 800,000 + IDR 500,000 urgent fee)
  - Processing time: Same day or next working day

**At Airport:**

- EPO: IDR 1,000,000 (urgent rate applies automatically)
  - Processing time: 30 minutes to 2 hours
- ERP: IDR 1,300,000 (urgent rate applies automatically)
  - Processing time: 30 minutes to 2 hours

**Note:** Airport processing is inherently risky. If there's any problem with your documents, or if multiple passengers need urgent permits simultaneously, you may miss your flight.

### How to Request Urgent Processing

When applying at the immigration office:

1. **Arrive early** - Get to immigration when they open (usually 8:00 AM)
2. **Explain your situation** - Provide evidence of urgency (flight booking, emergency documentation)
3. **Request urgent processing explicitly** - Say "Saya perlu proses urgent" (I need urgent processing)
4. **Pay urgent fees** - Fees must be paid upfront
5. **Ask for estimated completion time** - Some offices give you a specific pickup time
6. **Provide contact information** - In case immigration needs to reach you
7. **Stay flexible** - Even "same day" might mean late afternoon

### Airport Exit Permit Process in Detail

If you absolutely must get your exit permit at the airport, here's what to expect:

**Location:** Don't go to the departure gates. Find the immigration office (Kantor Imigrasi) inside the terminal before check-in. At major airports:

- Jakarta (CGK): Terminal 3, Level 1, near check-in counters
- Bali (DPS): International terminal, after duty-free, near gates
- Surabaya (SUB): Terminal 2, near immigration counters

**Timeline:**

- Arrive 4 hours before departure (minimum 3 hours)
- Allow 1-2 hours for exit permit processing
- Still need time for check-in, security, and boarding

**Peak times to avoid:** Sunday evenings, early Monday mornings, and Indonesian public holidays see the highest volume of last-minute exit permit applications.

## KITAS Extension and Exit Permits: What You Need to Know

Your exit permits and KITAS extensions interact in important ways that can affect your travel plans.

### Can You Travel While KITAS Extension is Processing?

**Short answer:** No, not recommended.

**Explanation:** During KITAS extension processing, your passport is usually held by immigration or your sponsor's visa agent. Without your passport, you cannot travel internationally. Additionally, leaving Indonesia during KITAS processing can complicate or invalidate your extension application.

**What to do:** Plan international travel either:

- Before starting your KITAS extension (get MERP if your extension is approaching)
- After your KITAS extension is complete and you have your passport back

### MERP Validity After KITAS Extension

When you extend your KITAS, your MERP does not automatically extend with it.

**Example scenario:**

- Original KITAS valid until December 31, 2026
- You get MERP valid until December 31, 2026
- In November 2026, you extend KITAS until December 31, 2027
- Your MERP still expires December 31, 2026

**Solution:** Apply for a new MERP after your KITAS extension is approved. Some immigration offices allow you to apply for both simultaneously to save time.

### Strategic Timing for MERP Applications

**Best time to apply for MERP:** Right after receiving a new or extended KITAS, when you have the maximum remaining validity period.

**Avoid applying for MERP:** Within 6 months of your KITAS expiry date, unless you're certain you'll extend your KITAS immediately and can apply for a new MERP right away.

## Exit Permits for Different KITAS Types

Not all KITAS types have identical exit permit requirements or processes.

### Work KITAS (KITAS Bekerja)

- **Sponsor:** Your employer must provide the sponsor letter
- **MERP considerations:** Most employers prefer workers to have MERP if job requires international business travel
- **EPO process:** Requires employment termination letter from company
- **Special notes:** If you're switching employers, your new sponsor may require you to cancel your old KITAS (EPO) before issuing a new one

### Investor KITAS (KITAS Investor)

- **Sponsor:** Your PT PMA (foreign investment company) is the sponsor
- **MERP considerations:** Highly recommended for investors who split time between Indonesia and home country
- **EPO process:** Requires company resolution or director statement
- **Special notes:** If your company is closing, you must cancel KITAS (EPO) as part of the company dissolution process

### Spouse/Dependent KITAS (KITAS Keluarga)

- **Sponsor:** The primary KITAS holder (your spouse/parent) is your sponsor
- **MERP considerations:** Families often get MERP for all members to travel together freely
- **EPO process:** Requires statement from primary KITAS holder
- **Special notes:** Your exit permit validity cannot exceed the primary KITAS holder's permit validity

### Retirement KITAS (KITAS Pensiunan)

- **Sponsor:** Your guarantor (Indonesian citizen or institution) provides the letter
- **MERP considerations:** Very common for retirees who visit family abroad regularly
- **EPO process:** Requires statement that you're leaving Indonesia permanently
- **Special notes:** Many retirees get 2-year MERP to match their KITAS validity

### Student KITAS (KITAS Pelajar)

- **Sponsor:** Your educational institution provides the letter
- **MERP considerations:** Useful for students who return home during breaks
- **EPO process:** Requires confirmation that you've completed or left your studies
- **Special notes:** Some schools include exit permit assistance in their international student services

## KITAP (Permanent Residence) Exit Permits

KITAP (Kartu Izin Tinggal Tetap) holders have the same exit permit requirements as KITAS holders, but with some differences:

### KITAP Permit Options

- **EPO:** IDR 700,000 - Cancels your permanent residence (rarely used)
- **ERP:** IDR 800,000 - Single exit and return (occasional use)
- **MERP:** IDR 5,000,000 for 2 years - Most common choice for KITAP holders

**Key difference:** KITAP holders almost always choose MERP, since they have indefinite residency status and likely travel frequently. The 2-year MERP aligns well with the long-term nature of KITAP.

### KITAP MERP Renewal

Unlike KITAS holders who must deal with permit validity tied to KITAS expiry, KITAP holders simply renew MERP every 2 years as a routine administrative task.

**Process:** Same as initial MERP application, with your KITAP as supporting document instead of KITAS.

**Solution:**

- Request sponsor letter from employer confirming employment termination
- Apply for EPO (IDR 700,000) at immigration office
- KITAS is cancelled upon departure
- No need to pay for ERP/MERP since not returning

**Common mistake to avoid:** Don't let your KITAS expire before leaving. Overstaying results in fines (IDR 1,000,000 per day) and potential immigration blacklist. Get EPO and depart before KITAS expiry date.

**Profile:** Maria, Spanish, holds Spouse KITAS in Bali, receives news of family emergency in Spain

**Situation:** Flight departs in 36 hours, Maria doesn't have any exit permit

**Solution:**

- Option 1: Visit immigration office immediately, request urgent ERP processing (IDR 1,300,000), return same day or next day for stamp
- Option 2: If immigration office can't guarantee same-day processing, get ERP at airport (IDR 1,300,000, processed in 1-2 hours)
- Request urgent sponsor letter from husband (primary KITAS holder)

**Lesson:** Consider maintaining MERP even if you don't travel frequently, specifically for emergency situations like this.

### Scenario 4: Frequent Regional Business Traveler

**Profile:** Michael, American, Regional Sales Director based in Jakarta, travels to Singapore, Bangkok, and KL weekly

**Travel pattern:** 3-4 international trips per month (40+ trips per year)

**Solution:**

- Get 2-year MERP (IDR 5,000,000) immediately

- Total cost: IDR 2,500,000/year vs. IDR 32,000,000 for individual ERPs

**ROI:** MERP pays for itself after just 7 trips. With 40+ trips annually, Michael saves IDR 27,500,000 per year.

### Scenario 5: Student Going Home for Summer Break

**Profile:** Lisa, Japanese, studying at university in Yogyakarta, holds Student KITAS

**Situation:** Summer break lasts 3 months, Lisa wants to visit family in Japan

**Solution:**

- Apply for ERP (IDR 800,000) at least 7 days before departure
- Sponsor letter from university stating she'll return to continue studies
- Single ERP covers 3-month vacation period
- Return to Indonesia before KITAS expires

**Why not MERP:** Students typically travel only during breaks (2-3 times per year), making ERP more cost-effective than IDR 4,000,000 MERP.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Extended)

### Can I apply for MERP online?

No. As of 2026, MERP applications must be submitted in person at your local immigration office. ERP and EPO can also only be applied for in person (either at immigration office or airport).

Indonesia has no online e-permit system for exit permits, unlike visa applications which have moved partially online.

### What if I lose my passport while abroad with MERP?

1. Report passport loss to local police and get police report
2. Visit Indonesian embassy/consulate to get emergency travel document
3. Your MERP was in the lost passport, so it's also lost
4. Return to Indonesia with emergency travel document
5. After getting new passport, visit immigration office with:
   - New passport
   - Police report of lost passport
   - Copy of old passport (if available)
   - KITAS card
6. Apply for new MERP (you'll need to pay the full fee again)

**Prevention:** Photograph every page of your passport including MERP stamp, and store photos securely in the cloud.

### Can I extend MERP before it expires?

No. Indonesian immigration doesn't offer MERP extensions. You must apply for a completely new MERP, paying the full fee.

**Best practice:** Apply for new MERP 2-3 weeks before current MERP expires to avoid any gap in coverage.

### Do I need exit permit for domestic flights within Indonesia?

No. Exit permits (EPO, ERP, MERP) are only required for **international travel** departing Indonesia.

You can travel freely to any Indonesian city (Jakarta, Bali, Surabaya, Medan, Makassar, etc.) using your KITAS without any additional permits.

### Can I switch from EPO to ERP after application but before departure?

Possibly, but very difficult. If you've already applied for EPO but haven't yet departed Indonesia:

1. Return immediately to the immigration office where you applied
2. Explain you made a mistake and need ERP instead
3. Request to cancel EPO application and submit new ERP application
4. You may need to pay fees for both applications

**Much better:** Double-check your choice before applying. EPO permanently cancels your KITAS—this isn't something to decide lightly.

### What happens if immigration officers question my MERP usage?

MERP is legal and routine—there's no issue with using it frequently. However:

- Immigration officers may ask about your residence in Indonesia if you're outside the country more than you're inside
- If you spend less than 183 days per year in Indonesia, you may be questioned about whether you're truly a resident
- Maintain proof of Indonesian residence: apartment contract, utility bills, employment letter

MERP doesn't guarantee unlimited absence—it guarantees unlimited exits and entries, but you must still maintain substantial residence in Indonesia to keep your KITAS valid.

### Can I get MERP if my KITAS expires in 8 months?

Yes, but your MERP will only be valid for 8 months, not the full 1 year you pay for.

**Recommendation:** Wait until you extend your KITAS, then apply for MERP with full 1-2 year validity. In the meantime, use ERP for any international trips.

### Does MERP guarantee entry back to Indonesia?

MERP guarantees your **right to re-enter**, but immigration officers can still deny entry if:

- Your KITAS has expired
- Your passport has less than 6 months validity
- You have outstanding violations or fines
- You're on an immigration blacklist

MERP + valid KITAS + valid passport = you can re-enter Indonesia freely, barring extraordinary circumstances.
