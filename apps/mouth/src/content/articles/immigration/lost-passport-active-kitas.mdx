## Do Not Panic: Your KITAS Is Safe

Losing your passport is stressful, especially when it contains your KITAS stamp. But here is the important fact: **your KITAS exists in the immigration electronic database, not just in your passport.** The passport stamp is simply a physical endorsement of a digital record. Losing the passport does not cancel your KITAS.

That said, you need to act quickly and follow the correct procedure. A lost passport without prompt action can create complications with law enforcement, your employer, and immigration authorities.

## Immediate Actions (First 24 Hours)

### Action 1: Secure Your Location

Before anything else:

- Note the exact location, time, and circumstances of the loss/theft
- If stolen, move to a safe location
- Check thoroughly (hotel safe, bag pockets, restaurant lost-and-found) before assuming it is gone
- If at a hotel, inform the front desk immediately

### Action 2: File a Police Report

This is the most critical first step. Go to the nearest **police station (Kantor Polisi)** and file a **Surat Tanda Lapor Kehilangan (STLK)** - a report of loss.

**Where to go:**

- Any police station (Polsek, Polres, or Polda)
- In Bali: Polres Badung, Polresta Denpasar, or the nearest Polsek
- Tourist police offices (available in major tourist areas)

**What to bring:**

- Copy of passport (if you have one - always keep a separate copy)
- KITAS card
- Any identification you still have
- Your address details

**What happens at the police station:**

| Step | Action                                                        | Time      |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1    | Report at the SPKT (Sentra Pelayanan Kepolisian Terpadu) desk | 15 min    |
| 2    | Provide details of the loss/theft                             | 15-30 min |
| 3    | Statement is typed and you review/sign it                     | 15-30 min |
| 4    | STLK is issued                                                | Immediate |

**Cost:** Free (police reports for lost documents are not charged)
**Language:** Bring someone who speaks Indonesian if possible. Tourist police areas may have English-speaking officers.

**The STLK document is essential.** You need it for everything that follows: embassy, immigration, insurance claims.

### Action 3: Contact Your Embassy or Consulate

Call your embassy or consulate the same day. Most embassies have emergency hotlines for outside business hours.

**Key embassies in Indonesia (Bali consular offices):**

| Country        | Location                          | Emergency Hotline     |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Australia      | Denpasar (Renon)                  | Check embassy website |
| United States  | Denpasar                          | Check embassy website |
| United Kingdom | Jakarta (honorary consul in Bali) | Check embassy website |

**Note:** Most European countries have their main embassy in Jakarta with honorary consuls or agents in Bali. For full passport replacement, you may need to visit Jakarta. Emergency travel documents can sometimes be issued by honorary consuls.

### Action 4: Notify Your KITAS Sponsor Company

Inform your sponsoring company (employer) immediately. They need to know because:

- They may need to provide a sponsor letter for the immigration process
- Your RPTKA and company records reference your passport number
- The company may have copies of your passport that you need

## Embassy Passport Replacement Process

### Option A: Emergency Travel Document (ETD)

If you need to travel urgently or your embassy cannot issue a full passport quickly:

| Feature         | Details                                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| What it is      | A single-use travel document for return to home country |
| Issued by       | Your embassy/consulate                                  |
| Validity        | Usually 1-7 days, one-way travel only                   |
| Cost            | Varies by country (USD 50-200 typical)                  |
| Processing time | 1-3 days                                                |
| Limitation      | Cannot receive KITAS stamp; for departure only          |

**Important:** If you use an ETD to leave Indonesia, your KITAS must be formally addressed at the immigration office before departure. Immigration will issue an exit endorsement on the ETD.

### Option B: Full Passport Replacement

For continuing your stay in Indonesia with your active KITAS:

| Step | Action                                                | Timeline        |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 1    | Visit embassy with police report, photos, application | Day 1-3         |
| 2    | Embassy verifies identity and processes application   | 1-2 weeks       |
| 3    | New passport printed and issued                       | 2-6 weeks       |
| 4    | Collect new passport from embassy                     | Day of issuance |

**Documents typically needed at the embassy:**

| Document                  | Notes                                      |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Police report (STLK)      | Original from Indonesian police            |
| Passport photos           | Per your country's specifications          |
| Passport application form | Embassy-specific                           |
| Identity verification     | Driver's license, national ID, or other ID |
| Copy of lost passport     | If available (always keep a separate copy) |
| Payment                   | Embassy processing fee                     |

**Processing times by country (approximate):**

| Country        | Standard Processing | Rush Available?      |
| -------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- |
| United States  | 2-4 weeks           | Yes (additional fee) |
| Australia      | 2-3 weeks           | Yes                  |
| United Kingdom | 3-6 weeks           | Yes (additional fee) |
| Germany        | 4-8 weeks           | Limited              |
| Canada         | 2-4 weeks           | Yes                  |
| New Zealand    | 2-3 weeks           | Yes                  |

## Immigration KITAS Transfer to New Passport

Once you have your new passport, the next step is transferring your KITAS records from the old (lost) passport to the new one.

### Documents for Immigration

| Document             | Notes                                                          |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New passport         | Original                                                       |
| KITAS card           | Original                                                       |
| Police report (STLK) | Original                                                       |
| Embassy letter       | Confirming new passport issuance and old passport cancellation |
| Sponsor letter       | From your company confirming your status                       |
| Passport photos      | 4x6 cm, red/blue background                                    |
| Copy of old passport | If available                                                   |
| SKTT                 | Your current address registration                              |

### Process at Immigration Office

| Step | Action                                               | Timeline |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| 1    | Submit documents at the KITAS service counter        | Day 1    |
| 2    | Immigration verifies your KITAS record in the system | 1-3 days |
| 3    | New passport receives KITAS endorsement stamp        | 1-5 days |
| 4    | Immigration system updated with new passport number  | Same day |
| 5    | Collect passport with new stamp                      | Day 3-7  |

**Cost:**
| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| KITAS re-endorsement fee | IDR 500,000-2,000,000 |
| Agent service fee | IDR 2-5 million (if using an agent) |
| Photocopy and admin | IDR 50,000-100,000 |

### Important: STM/MERP Re-issuance

If you had a multiple exit re-entry permit (MERP/STM) in your old passport, you need a new one:

- The old MERP was in the lost passport and is no longer valid
- Apply for a new MERP at the same time as the KITAS re-stamping
- Cost: IDR 1,000,000 for new MERP

## Prevention: Protecting Your Passport

### Digital Backup

- Photograph every page of your passport (photo, visa stamps, KITAS stamp)
- Store copies in a secure cloud service (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox)
- Email copies to yourself and a trusted person
- Keep a physical photocopy separate from the original

### Physical Security

- Use hotel safes when not carrying the passport
- Never leave passports unattended at beaches, restaurants, or co-working spaces
- Carry a photocopy for daily use; keep the original secured
- In Bali, bag-snatching from motorbikes is a known risk - be cautious with bags containing documents

### Travel Insurance

- Ensure your travel/health insurance covers lost document expenses
- Some policies cover emergency passport issuance costs
- Keep the insurance policy number and emergency number separate from the passport

## What NOT to Do

1. **Do not ignore it** - Hoping someone will return the passport is not a strategy
2. **Do not delay the police report** - The longer you wait, the more complicated it becomes
3. **Do not try to leave Indonesia without informing immigration** - This will create serious problems
4. **Do not pay "fixers" who promise quick solutions** - Use official channels only
5. **Do not accept a found passport from strangers without verifying** - Scams exist targeting tourists and expats
