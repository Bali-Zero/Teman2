This action elevates beyond a temporary recall — a common protest measure — to a formal and indefinite downgrade of diplomatic representation. The move reflects Spain's deepening criticism of Israeli military operations, likely in Gaza, and aligns with broader European and Latin American diplomatic pressure on Israel.
