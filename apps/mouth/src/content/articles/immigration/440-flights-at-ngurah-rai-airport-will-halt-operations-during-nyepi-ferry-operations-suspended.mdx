## The Facts

During Nyepi (Balinese New Year/Day of Silence), Ngurah Rai International Airport in Bali will suspend all 440 scheduled flights, and ferry services will also be halted. Nyepi is a Hindu religious observance requiring complete silence, fasting, and cessation of activity across Bali. This annual shutdown affects all air and sea travel to and from the island for a 24-hour period, typically from 6 AM on Nyepi day to 6 AM the following morning.
