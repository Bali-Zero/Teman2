## When Marriage Ends, So Does E33F KITAS

Divorce is difficult under any circumstances. When it happens in Indonesia and one spouse holds an E33F dependent KITAS, the immigration consequences add another layer of complexity. Here is what you need to know.

**The core rule:** E33F KITAS is granted on the basis of marriage to a primary KITAS holder. When the marriage legally ends, the basis for the E33F ceases to exist, and the KITAS must be cancelled.

This applies whether:

- The divorce is initiated by the E33F holder or the primary KITAS holder
- The divorce is processed in Indonesia or abroad
- The separation is amicable or contested

## The Timeline: What Happens Step by Step

### Phase 1: Divorce Proceedings

Divorce in Indonesia follows specific legal procedures depending on the couple's religion and nationality:

| Situation                               | Divorce Authority                            | Timeline    |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Both foreigners, married abroad         | Home country court (recognized in Indonesia) | Varies      |
| Mixed marriage (Indonesian + foreigner) | Indonesian Religious or District Court       | 3-12 months |
| Both foreigners, married in Indonesia   | Indonesian District Court                    | 3-12 months |

**Key document:** The final divorce decree (Akta Cerai) or foreign divorce decree legalized for use in Indonesia.

### Phase 2: KITAS Cancellation

Once the divorce is final:

1. The primary KITAS holder's company (sponsor) should report the change in marital status to immigration
2. Immigration initiates cancellation of the E33F KITAS
3. The E33F holder receives notification of cancellation

**Important note:** Some primary KITAS holders delay reporting the divorce to immigration. This does not help the E33F holder because:

- The E33F will eventually be caught during renewal
- Immigration databases can cross-reference civil records
- Delayed reporting creates complications for both parties

### Phase 3: Grace Period

After formal cancellation, the E33F holder typically receives a **grace period** of 30-60 days. During this time:

- You remain legally in Indonesia
- You must arrange alternative immigration status or prepare to depart
- You should not work (your E33F never authorized work)
- You can travel domestically but international departure may complicate re-entry

### Phase 4: Resolution

You must resolve your immigration status before the grace period expires:

| Option                      | Action Required                                        | Timeline                 |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Leave Indonesia             | Book departure, exit through immigration               | Before grace period ends |
| Convert to work KITAS       | Establish company or find employer, apply for E25B/E23 | 4-8 weeks                |
| Convert to retirement KITAS | Meet E311A requirements (age 55+, financial proof)     | 3-5 weeks                |
| Convert to investor KITAS   | Meet E28A requirements (IDR 10B investment)            | 4-6 weeks                |
| Apply for B211 visit visa   | Bridge visa while arranging long-term status           | 1-2 weeks                |

## Your Options After Divorce

### Option 1: Convert to E25B Director KITAS

If you want to stay in Indonesia and have the means to operate a business, converting to E25B Director KITAS is the most common path.

**What you need:**

- A PT PMA company (existing or newly established)
- Appointment as Director in the company deed
- RPTKA approval from Ministry of Manpower
- DKP-TKA payment (USD 1,200 for 12 months)

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks
**Cost:** IDR 30-60 million (including company setup if new)

**Practical tip:** If divorce is anticipated, start the company establishment process before the divorce is finalized. This gives you a head start on the KITAS conversion.

### Option 2: Convert to E23 Employee KITAS

If you have a job offer from an Indonesian company, you can convert to an E23 Employee KITAS.

**What you need:**

- Employment offer from a qualifying company
- Company submits RPTKA on your behalf
- DKP-TKA paid by employer

**Timeline:** 3-6 weeks
**Cost:** Borne by the employer

### Option 3: Convert to E311A Retirement KITAS

If you are 55 or older and have retirement income:

**Requirements:**

- Age 55+
- Proof of pension/savings (USD 2,500/month)
- Health insurance
- Accommodation proof

**Timeline:** 3-5 weeks

### Option 4: Apply for B211 Visit Visa (Bridge)

If you need more time to arrange a long-term solution:

**B211 Social/Cultural Visit Visa:**

- Valid for 60 days, extendable up to 180 days total
- Provides breathing room to arrange a work KITAS or other solution
- Does not authorize work
- Requires a local sponsor

**This is a bridge strategy** - use the B211 period to establish a company, find employment, or make other arrangements.

### Option 5: Depart Indonesia

If none of the above options are viable, you must leave Indonesia before the grace period expires. You can always return later on an appropriate visa.

## Children's KITAS After Divorce

Children's immigration status is often the most pressing concern. Here is how it works:

### If Children Stay with the Primary KITAS Holder

- Children's E33E KITAS remains unchanged
- No immigration action needed
- KITAS continues under the primary holder's sponsorship

### If Children Go with the E33F (Divorced) Parent

This creates an immigration gap because:

- The children's E33E was dependent on the primary KITAS holder
- The divorced parent needs their own KITAS first
- Children's KITAS then needs to be transferred to the new parent's KITAS

**Process:**

| Step | Action                                                   | Timeline  |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1    | Divorced parent obtains own KITAS (E25B/E23/etc.)        | 4-8 weeks |
| 2    | Apply for children's E33E transfer to new parent's KITAS | 2-3 weeks |
| 3    | Children's KITAS re-issued under new sponsor             | 1-2 weeks |

### Joint Custody Considerations

Indonesian immigration does not have a formal "joint custody" visa arrangement. Practically:

- Children can only have one KITAS sponsor at a time
- The parent with primary physical custody should sponsor the children's KITAS
- The other parent can still visit/spend time with children normally
- Custody agreements (from the divorce decree) should be kept available for immigration if questions arise

### International Child Custody Complications

If one parent wants to leave Indonesia with the children and the other parent objects:

- Indonesian exit immigration can flag children for departure prevention
- Court orders may be needed to authorize international travel
- Both parents' consent may be required for minors' departure
- This is a legal matter requiring family law attorney involvement

## Special Cases

### Mixed Marriage (Indonesian + Foreigner) Divorce

If you were married to an Indonesian citizen and held E33F:

- The divorce process goes through Indonesian courts
- Property division follows Indonesian law
- Children may have dual citizenship considerations (if under 18)
- Your E33F KITAS is still cancelled upon divorce
- You may have additional time to arrange immigration status due to court proceedings

### Divorce During KITAS Renewal Period

If your E33F is due for renewal and you are in the middle of divorce:

- Immigration may or may not renew the E33F (depends on when the divorce is finalized)
- If renewed before divorce is final, the KITAS remains valid until the next renewal
- Once divorce is final, cancellation will be initiated regardless of remaining validity

### Domestic Violence Considerations

If the divorce involves domestic violence:

- Seek help from your embassy immediately
- Indonesian law provides protection for DV victims regardless of immigration status
- Immigration authorities generally provide extended grace periods in DV cases
- Legal aid organizations in Indonesia can assist with both legal and immigration matters

## Practical Checklist for Divorce with KITAS Implications

### Before Divorce Is Finalized

- [ ] Consult an immigration agent about your options
- [ ] Begin establishing a PT PMA if you plan to convert to E25B
- [ ] Gather all immigration documents (KITAS, passport, company letters)
- [ ] Secure your passport (keep it in your possession)
- [ ] Open a personal bank account (separate from joint accounts)
- [ ] Arrange accommodation in your own name
- [ ] Consult a family law attorney for custody matters

### After Divorce Is Finalized

- [ ] Obtain certified divorce decree
- [ ] Report divorce to immigration (or confirm sponsor has done so)
- [ ] Apply for conversion or bridge visa within 30 days
- [ ] Transfer children's KITAS if they are staying with you
- [ ] Update bank account and other registrations
- [ ] Inform your embassy of change in status

### Ongoing

- [ ] Keep all immigration documents current
- [ ] Monitor KITAS validity dates
- [ ] Maintain communication with immigration agent
- [ ] Keep custody agreements accessible
