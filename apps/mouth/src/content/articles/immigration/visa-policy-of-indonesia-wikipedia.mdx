## The Facts

Indonesia maintains a tiered visa policy governing entry for foreign nationals, with provisions ranging from visa-free access to visa-on-arrival and various long-stay permits. The policy is anchored in Immigration Law No. 6/2011 and its implementing regulations. Indonesia offers Visa-on-Arrival (VOA) to citizens of eligible countries, granting 30-day stays extendable once for an additional 30 days.
