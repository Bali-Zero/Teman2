## The Facts

Indonesia's education system presents expatriate families with a layered set of choices that vary significantly by institution type, location, and immigration status. The most accessible option for foreign nationals is the international school, which operates under curricula from the United Kingdom, United States, International Baccalaureate, or other foreign frameworks. These schools are authorized to enroll foreign students without restriction and typically conduct instruction in English or another international language.

National-plus schools — sometimes called sekolah nasional plus — occupy a middle tier. They follow Indonesia's national curriculum while incorporating additional foreign language instruction and, in some cases, dual accreditation. Enrollment eligibility for foreign children in these institutions depends on the school's specific licensing and the student's residency status in Indonesia.

Fully Indonesian-curriculum state schools are generally not accessible to foreign nationals under standard immigration pathways. Exceptions may apply in specific circumstances, but for the vast majority of expatriate families, enrollment in a state school is not a practical option.

Costs vary dramatically across the spectrum. International schools in Bali, particularly those aligned with British or IB curricula, command annual tuition fees ranging from tens of millions to over two hundred million Indonesian rupiah per year, with additional charges for registration, uniforms, extracurricular activities, and transportation. National-plus schools occupy a lower cost bracket while still offering bilingual instruction.

Geographically, Bali's international school infrastructure is concentrated in the Seminyak-Canggu corridor, Sanur, and the Ubud region. Families settling outside these areas should factor school commute time into their housing decisions. Enrollment capacity at the most sought-after institutions can be limited, and waiting lists are common, particularly for entry-level and secondary year groups. Families are advised to initiate enrollment inquiries well in advance of a planned relocation.
