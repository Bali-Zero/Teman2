# Investor KITAS Guide: Living in Indonesia as an Investor 2026

The Investor KITAS is the E28A visa: a limited-stay visa (ITAS) for a foreigner who holds shares in a PT PMA. This page is the short overview. The [E28A Investor KITAS guide](/immigration/e28a-investor-kitas-guide) carries the full document list and the journey.

## At a Glance

| | E28A Investor KITAS |
| --- | --- |
| Permit | Limited-stay visa (ITAS), extendable and convertible into another limited-stay permit; Bali Zero sells it for 2 years |
| Sponsor | Required: the PT PMA you invested in |
| Threshold | Own shareholding of at least IDR 10 billion in the sponsoring PT PMA |
| Visa stage | Five working days after the visa fee is paid; Bali Zero typically takes 7-10 working days (indicative) |
| Entry | The visa is valid 90 days from issuance; the ITAS and the re-entry permit are issued automatically when you are admitted at the checkpoint |
| Price | All-inclusive: IDR 17,000,000 offshore, IDR 19,000,000 onshore, IDR 18,000,000 extension |

## Who Qualifies

- Own shareholding of at least IDR 10 billion (or the equivalent) in the sponsoring company, registered with the Ministry of Investment/BKPM.
- E28A admits passive shareholders with sufficient own shareholding.
- If your shares are worth less than IDR 10 billion and you also hold a post in the company (director or commissioner), you must apply for a work visa matching that post instead.
- The PT PMA's own paid-up capital of IDR 2.5 billion (BKPM Regulation 5/2025) is the company's threshold, not the test of your holding.

## Rights and Prohibitions

You may invest, carry out business, investment and company-establishment activities, act as a member of the board of directors or the board of commissioners of the company you invested in, supervise its production, bring your family, and leave and re-enter while the re-entry permit is valid.

Prohibited: staying beyond the stay permit, work that does not match the stay permit, and selling goods or services unless the work requires it.

## Price

Investor KITAS prices are all-inclusive: government fees and Bali Zero service are included.
