# Investor KITAS Guide: Living in Indonesia as an Investor 2026

If you're investing in an Indonesian company, you may qualify for an Investor KITAS (visa code E28A) - allowing you to live in Indonesia based on your shareholding in a PT PMA, not employment. This guide covers the current requirements per Keputusan Menteri Imigrasi M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025 and Bali Zero's official service pricing.

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="2026 Update (Source: imigrasi.go.id)">
**Hybrid KITAS System (since May 2025):**

Indonesian Immigration now uses a **hybrid system** for KITAS applications:

- **Online Phase**: Initial application, document upload, payment via imigrasi.go.id portal
- **In-Person Phase**: Biometrics (fingerprints, photo), original document verification, physical KITAS collection

**Important**: Physical presence in Indonesia is still required for biometrics. Processing times may vary by local immigration office. Per E28A, ITAS and re-entry permit are issued automatically upon entry.

</InfoCard>

## What is Investor KITAS?

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Investor KITAS (E28A)">
A Limited Stay Permit (ITAS) for foreign investors who:
- Own shares worth minimum IDR 10 billion in a PT PMA registered with BKPM
- The company has been approved by Kemenkumham
- May or may not work in the company

**Key benefit**: Pure investors don't need RPTKA (work authorization) or DKP-TKA fees. If your shareholding is below IDR 10B and you hold a director/commissioner position, you must apply for a Working KITAS (E25B) instead.

</InfoCard>

## Can You Work on Investor KITAS?

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Working as an Investor">
**Pure Investor (E28A only)**: No RPTKA needed, no DKP-TKA fee.

**Investor + Director**: If your shareholding is below IDR 10B and you hold a director position, you need a Working KITAS (E25B - Visa Kerja Direktur Perusahaan) with RPTKA and DKP-TKA fee.

Many foreign investors choose to be both shareholder AND director, which requires Working KITAS (E25B) instead of Investor KITAS (E28A).

</InfoCard>

### What "Not Working" Means

As a pure investor, you can:

- Attend board meetings
- Review company performance
- Receive dividends
- Make investment decisions

You cannot:

- Manage daily operations
- Sign contracts as director
- Have employees report to you
- Represent company to clients

## Benefits of Investor KITAS

1. **No DKP-TKA** - Save USD 100/month (if not working)
2. **Longer validity** - 2 years standard
3. **Path to KITAP** - Permanent residency after 3 consecutive years
4. **Simpler process** - No RPTKA if pure investor
5. **Family sponsorship** - Can sponsor spouse and children

## Related Investor Visa Options

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Other Investor Visa Categories (from Keputusan Menteri M.IP-08.GR.01.01/2025)">
The E28 family includes several investor visa types beyond the standard E28A:

- **E28B** - Investor (Company Establishment): 5-year visa requires USD 2.5M investment commitment, 10-year requires USD 5M. Must establish company within 90 days of entry.
- **E28C** - Investor (Without Company): 5-year visa requires USD 350K in government bonds or public company shares, 10-year requires USD 700K.
- **E28D** - Investor (Branch/Subsidiary): 5-year requires USD 25M, 10-year requires USD 50M. For large multinational expansions.
- **E28E** - Investor (Special Economic Zone): For investments in designated SEZs.
- **E33G** - Remote Worker (Pekerja Jarak Jauh): For digital nomads working remotely for foreign employers. Does NOT require PT PMA establishment. Bali Zero service: IDR 10M (Extend), IDR 13M (Offshore), IDR 14M (Onshore).

For the latest requirements, always check imigrasi.go.id or contact Bali Zero.

</InfoCard>
