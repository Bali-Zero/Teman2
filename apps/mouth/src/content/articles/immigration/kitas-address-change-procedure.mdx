## The 24-Hour Rule: Why Address Reporting Matters

When you move to a new address in Indonesia, you are legally required to report the change within **24 hours** under Government Regulation No. 31/2013 on Implementation of the Immigration Law. This applies to all foreign nationals holding KITAS, KITAP, or any other stay permit.

The 24-hour rule is part of Indonesia's broader foreign resident tracking system. Every foreigner must be registered at a specific address, and the local government (from the neighborhood level up to the immigration office) must know where you live.

Is it strictly enforced to the hour? In practice, no. Most foreigners complete the reporting within a week or two without issue. However, failing to report at all creates problems during KITAS renewals, immigration checks, and any interaction with government offices.

## Understanding the Registration Layers

Indonesia's address registration system has multiple layers, and you need to update each one:

| Level                | Office                    | What Gets Updated                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Neighborhood         | RT (Rukun Tetangga)       | Resident registry at the smallest community level |
| Sub-neighborhood     | RW (Rukun Warga)          | Community group registry                          |
| Village/Sub-district | Kelurahan/Desa            | Local government records                          |
| District             | Kecamatan                 | District government records                       |
| Civil Registry       | Dukcapil (Kabupaten/Kota) | SKTT (Surat Keterangan Tempat Tinggal)            |
| Immigration          | Kantor Imigrasi           | Wajib Lapor (mandatory reporting) record          |

**The good news:** You do not need to visit all six offices separately. The process flows upward from RT to Dukcapil, and immigration is handled separately.

## Step-by-Step Address Change Process

### Step 1: Notify Your New RT/RW (Day 1)

The **RT (Rukun Tetangga)** is the smallest administrative unit in Indonesia, covering approximately 30-50 households. Every neighborhood has an RT head (Ketua RT).

**What to do:**

1. Introduce yourself to the RT head of your new neighborhood
2. Provide a copy of your KITAS card and passport
3. Request a Surat Pengantar (introduction letter) from the RT head
4. The RT head registers you in the neighborhood records

**What you need:**

- Passport (original + copy)
- KITAS card (original + copy)
- Rental agreement or property ownership document
- Passport photo (some RT offices request this)

**Cost:** Usually free or a small voluntary contribution (IDR 50,000-100,000)
**Time:** 1-3 days (depends on RT head availability)

### Step 2: Deregister from Old RT/RW (Day 1-7)

If your old address is in the same city, visit your former RT head to inform them of your departure. They will issue a Surat Pindah (moving letter).

**If moving between cities/provinces:** You may need a formal moving letter from the old Kelurahan/Kecamatan level as well.

### Step 3: Kelurahan/Kecamatan Registration (Day 1-14)

Bring the RT's Surat Pengantar to the **Kelurahan** (village office) or **Kecamatan** (sub-district office) to register at the next administrative level.

**Documents needed:**

- RT Surat Pengantar
- Passport + KITAS card copies
- Rental agreement
- Previous SKTT (if you have one)

**Processing time:** 1-5 working days
**Cost:** Free

### Step 4: SKTT at Dukcapil (Within 14 Days)

The **SKTT (Surat Keterangan Tempat Tinggal)** is the official temporary residence certificate for foreigners. This is issued by the local **Dukcapil (Dinas Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil)** office.

**Documents for SKTT:**
| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Passport (original + copy) | Photo page and visa pages |
| KITAS card (original + copy) | Both sides |
| Surat Pengantar from RT | Original |
| Kelurahan/Kecamatan registration | Original |
| Rental agreement or property proof | Must match the new address |
| Previous SKTT | For cancellation if from the same jurisdiction |
| Passport photos | 3x4 cm and 4x6 cm (2 each) |
| Application form | Provided at Dukcapil office |

**Processing time:** 1-7 working days
**Cost:** Free (by government regulation, SKTT issuance should be free)

**SKTT validity:** Matches your KITAS validity period. When KITAS is renewed, SKTT must also be renewed.

### Step 5: Immigration Office Update (Within 14 Days)

This is the critical step for your immigration record. You must update your **Wajib Lapor (mandatory reporting)** at the immigration office.

**Same jurisdiction move** (e.g., within Denpasar immigration area):

1. Visit the immigration office
2. Report the address change at the Wajib Lapor counter
3. Bring SKTT from new address
4. Immigration updates their records

**Cross-jurisdiction move** (e.g., Denpasar to Jakarta):

1. **Deregister** at the old immigration office (Denpasar)
2. **Re-register** at the new immigration office (Jakarta)
3. This is more complex and takes longer

**Documents for immigration:**

| Document                | Notes                                     |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Passport                | Original                                  |
| KITAS card              | Original                                  |
| New SKTT                | From Dukcapil                             |
| Old SKTT                | For cancellation                          |
| Rental agreement        | New address proof                         |
| Company sponsor letter  | Confirming you still work for the sponsor |
| Wajib Lapor certificate | Previous reporting certificate            |

### Step 6: Notify Your KITAS Sponsor Company

Your sponsoring company should be informed of the address change because:

- They may need to update their records with the Ministry of Manpower
- Future immigration correspondence goes to the registered company address
- The company may need to issue a new sponsor letter reflecting the address

## Moving Between Provinces: Additional Considerations

Moving across provincial boundaries adds complexity:

| Scenario                       | Complexity  | Additional Steps                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Within same kelurahan          | Low         | RT/RW + Dukcapil update only                                    |
| Within same kecamatan          | Low         | RT/RW + Dukcapil update                                         |
| Within same city/kabupaten     | Medium      | Full SKTT re-issuance                                           |
| Between cities (same province) | Medium-High | Deregister + re-register at both Dukcapil                       |
| Between provinces              | High        | Full deregister/re-register at all levels including immigration |

### Cross-Province Move Checklist

1. [ ] Notify old RT/RW of departure
2. [ ] Obtain Surat Pindah from old Kelurahan
3. [ ] Cancel old SKTT at old Dukcapil
4. [ ] Deregister Wajib Lapor at old immigration office
5. [ ] Register with new RT/RW
6. [ ] Register at new Kelurahan/Kecamatan
7. [ ] Obtain new SKTT at new Dukcapil
8. [ ] Register Wajib Lapor at new immigration office
9. [ ] Notify sponsor company

## Penalties for Not Reporting Address Changes

### Administrative Penalties

| Violation                                        | Potential Consequence                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Late reporting (weeks)                           | Written warning during KITAS renewal              |
| No reporting (months)                            | KITAS renewal complications                       |
| Deliberate non-reporting                         | Administrative sanctions                          |
| Wrong address on record during immigration check | Questioning, potential detention for verification |

### Practical Consequences

Beyond formal penalties, not updating your address creates practical problems:

1. **KITAS renewal delays** - Immigration compares your registered address with current records. Mismatches trigger additional verification.
2. **Bank account issues** - Some banks verify your SKTT address against their records during account reviews.
3. **Police reporting** - If you need to file a police report (theft, accident), your address must match official records.
4. **Delivery of official documents** - Immigration correspondence goes to your registered address.
5. **Emergency services** - In medical or safety emergencies, authorities reference your registered location.

## Common Questions About Address Changes

### What if I move frequently?

If you change addresses multiple times per year (common for renters in Bali), you technically need to re-register each time. In practice:

- Keep copies of each SKTT and registration
- Ensure your immigration Wajib Lapor is always updated
- Consider longer-term rentals to reduce administrative burden

### What if my landlord will not provide a rental agreement?

This is a common issue with informal rentals. Without a formal rental agreement:

- Ask the landlord for a Surat Keterangan (statement letter) confirming you reside there
- The RT head can issue a letter based on their knowledge of your residency
- For immigration purposes, having the RT letter is usually sufficient

### What about hotel/guesthouse stays?

If you are staying in a hotel or guesthouse:

- The accommodation provider is responsible for reporting your stay
- You do not need to register at RT/RW for hotel stays
- Long-term stays (1+ months) at hotels should be reported to immigration separately

### Does address change affect my KITAS validity?

No. Changing your address does not change your KITAS validity, type, or sponsorship. The KITAS itself remains the same. Only the registered address in the system changes.

## Pro Tips for Smooth Address Changes

1. **Build rapport with your RT head** - A friendly relationship makes future administrative tasks easier
2. **Keep multiple copies** - Photocopy every document before submitting originals
3. **Use an agent for cross-province moves** - The complexity justifies professional help
4. **Update all registrations simultaneously** - Do not leave gaps between RT and immigration updates
5. **Keep your old SKTT** - Even after cancellation, keep a copy for your records
