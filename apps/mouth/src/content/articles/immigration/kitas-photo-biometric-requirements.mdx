## Why KITAS Photo Specifications Matter

Immigration photo requirements in Indonesia are not just bureaucratic formalities. They serve two critical purposes:

1. **Biometric matching** - Your photo is compared against facial recognition databases at immigration checkpoints, airports, and during random checks
2. **Document integrity** - Your KITAS card, immigration records, and sponsor files all use the same photo for identity verification

Submitting photos that do not meet specifications is one of the most common reasons for KITAS application delays. Getting it right the first time saves days or weeks of processing time.

## Physical Photo Specifications

### Required Sizes

Indonesian immigration requires photos in multiple sizes for different purposes:

| Size   | Purpose                              | Quantity Typically Needed |
| ------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| 4x6 cm | KITAS card, immigration applications | 8-12 prints               |
| 3x4 cm | Supporting documents, Wajib Lapor    | 4-6 prints                |
| 2x3 cm | Additional forms (less common)       | 2-4 prints                |

**Always prepare more than you think you need.** Immigration offices, Dukcapil, police, and banks all request photos at various times. Having a supply of 20+ photos in each size prevents return trips to the photo studio.

### Background Color Requirements

| Background        | Used For                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Red (merah)**   | KITAS card, ITAS application, Wajib Lapor, most immigration forms              |
| **Blue (biru)**   | Certain immigration forms, passport-style applications, some embassy documents |
| **White (putih)** | Some embassy passport applications (country-specific)                          |

**Recommendation:** Get photos taken with both red and blue backgrounds. Most photo studios in Indonesia can switch backgrounds digitally, so ask for both versions.

### Face and Composition Requirements

| Specification       | Requirement                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Face coverage       | 70-80% of frame height            |
| Head position       | Straight, facing camera directly  |
| Expression          | Neutral (no smiling, no frowning) |
| Eyes                | Open, looking directly at camera  |
| Mouth               | Closed                            |
| Ears                | Visible (both ears if possible)   |
| Hair                | Should not cover face or eyes     |
| Chin to top of head | Fully visible in frame            |

### What to Wear

| Item                                            | Allowed?   | Notes                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Formal/smart casual clothing                    | Yes        | Collared shirt preferred                      |
| T-shirt                                         | Acceptable | Not ideal but not rejected                    |
| Religious head covering (hijab, turban, kippah) | Yes        | Face must remain fully visible                |
| Hat or cap                                      | No         | Unless religious requirement                  |
| Glasses (prescription or sun)                   | No         | Must be removed for the photo                 |
| Heavy jewelry                                   | Avoid      | Can cause glare or obstruct face              |
| Uniform                                         | Acceptable | Some prefer to avoid military/police uniforms |

### Technical Quality Standards

| Parameter     | Requirement                               |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Focus         | Sharp, clear focus on face                |
| Lighting      | Even, no harsh shadows on face            |
| Exposure      | Properly exposed (not too bright or dark) |
| Color         | Natural skin tones, no color cast         |
| Print quality | High-resolution print on photo paper      |
| Paper type    | Glossy or semi-glossy photo paper         |
| Edges         | Clean cut, no creases or bending          |

## Digital Photo Upload Specifications

For online applications through the immigration system (M-Paspor app, online KITAS applications), digital photos must meet specific technical requirements:

### Digital File Specifications

| Parameter        | Requirement                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| File format      | JPEG (.jpg)                          |
| File size        | 100 KB - 500 KB (varies by platform) |
| Resolution       | Minimum 300 DPI                      |
| Pixel dimensions | 400 x 600 pixels (minimum)           |
| Color space      | sRGB                                 |
| Background       | Solid red or blue (no gradient)      |

### Common Digital Upload Errors

| Error                    | Cause                             | Fix                                           |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| "File too large"         | Photo exceeds maximum KB          | Compress without losing quality               |
| "File too small"         | Low resolution photo              | Re-scan or re-photograph at higher resolution |
| "Invalid format"         | Wrong file type (PNG, PDF)        | Convert to JPEG                               |
| "Face not detected"      | Poor lighting or face too small   | Re-take with proper framing                   |
| "Background not uniform" | Shadows or gradient in background | Use solid color backdrop                      |

### Tips for Digital Photos

1. **Scan physical photos** at 300+ DPI if you do not have the digital file
2. **Use a dedicated photo scanner** rather than a phone camera to scan prints
3. **Check the file size** before uploading - adjust compression if needed
4. **Maintain aspect ratio** - do not stretch or squish the image
5. **Test upload** before the final submission to catch errors early

## Biometric Enrollment at Immigration Office

Beyond photos, KITAS applicants undergo biometric enrollment at the immigration office. This is a mandatory in-person process.

### What Happens During Biometric Enrollment

| Step | Procedure                                      | Duration     |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| 1    | Identity verification (passport + application) | 5 minutes    |
| 2    | Facial photo capture (immigration camera)      | 2 minutes    |
| 3    | Fingerprint scanning (all 10 fingers)          | 5-10 minutes |
| 4    | Signature capture (digital pad)                | 1 minute     |
| 5    | Verification and confirmation                  | 2 minutes    |

**Total time:** 15-25 minutes per person

### Biometric Enrollment Tips

- **Fingers must be clean and dry** - wash hands and dry thoroughly before arriving
- **No cuts or bandages on fingertips** - these can prevent accurate scanning
- **Remove rings** before fingerprint scanning
- **Follow the officer's instructions** precisely for finger placement
- **If a finger does not scan** (scar, dry skin), the officer may use moisturizer or retry multiple times

### When Biometric Enrollment Is Required

| Situation                       | Biometric Enrollment Required? |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| New KITAS application           | Yes (full enrollment)          |
| KITAS renewal/extension         | Yes (new photo + fingerprints) |
| KITAS transfer (change sponsor) | Yes                            |
| KITAS conversion (change type)  | Yes                            |
| Address change only             | No (system update only)        |
| Lost KITAS card replacement     | Yes                            |

## Re-Photo Requirements on KITAS Renewal

Every time you renew your KITAS, fresh photos are required. Here is what changes at renewal:

### New Photos Needed

- Fresh set of 4x6 cm photos (red background)
- Fresh set of 3x4 cm photos
- Photos must be taken within 6 months of the renewal application
- New digital file for online submission (if applicable)

### New Biometric Capture

- Fresh facial photo at the immigration office
- Fresh fingerprint scan (all 10 fingers)
- This happens during the biometric enrollment appointment

### Why Fresh Photos Are Required

1. **Appearance changes** - Your appearance may change over 1-2 years
2. **Biometric database update** - Immigration updates their facial recognition database
3. **Fraud prevention** - Fresh photos prevent the use of outdated or altered images
4. **Card production** - The new KITAS card is printed with the new photo

## Common Photo Rejection Reasons

Based on frequent immigration office feedback, these are the most common reasons photos are rejected:

### Top 10 Rejection Reasons

| Rank | Reason                                             | Frequency   |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 1    | Wearing glasses                                    | Very common |
| 2    | Wrong background color                             | Common      |
| 3    | Photo too old (more than 6 months)                 | Common      |
| 4    | Smile or expression change                         | Common      |
| 5    | Face too small in frame                            | Moderate    |
| 6    | Shadows on face                                    | Moderate    |
| 7    | Head tilted or turned                              | Moderate    |
| 8    | Hair covering eyes or face                         | Occasional  |
| 9    | Poor print quality (inkjet instead of photo paper) | Occasional  |
| 10   | Wrong size (3x4 instead of 4x6 or vice versa)      | Occasional  |

### How to Avoid Rejections

1. **Go to a professional photo studio** - Not a phone selfie or home printer
2. **Tell the photographer "foto untuk imigrasi"** (photo for immigration) - They know the specs
3. **Remove glasses before the shoot** - Even clear prescription glasses
4. **Bring a collared shirt** if wearing a casual top
5. **Request both red and blue backgrounds** in one session
6. **Ask for digital files** in addition to prints
7. **Order extra prints** (at least 20 of each size)
8. **Check the photos yourself** before leaving the studio

## Storing and Managing Your Immigration Photos

### Physical Storage

- Keep photos in a clean, dry envelope
- Store away from heat and humidity (important in Bali's climate)
- Label the envelope with the date taken
- Do not fold, staple, or paper-clip the photos

### Digital Storage

- Save digital files in a dedicated folder
- Back up to cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox)
- Name files clearly: `KITAS_photo_red_4x6_2026-02.jpg`
- Keep multiple copies across devices
- These digital files can be reprinted if needed
