## The Facts

Indonesia's government has officially confirmed that Eid al-Fitr 1446H — known locally as Lebaran — will fall on March 21, 2026. The announcement follows the standard hilal (crescent moon) sighting process coordinated by the Ministry of Religious Affairs, with formal confirmation issued following the isbat session, a government-convened panel of religious scholars and astronomers.
