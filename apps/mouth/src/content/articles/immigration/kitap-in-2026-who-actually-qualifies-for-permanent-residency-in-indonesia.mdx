## What the Law Actually Says

Permanent residency in Indonesia is formalised through the **KITAP** — Kartu Izin Tinggal Tetap. It is issued by the Direktorat Jenderal Imigrasi (Imigrasi) under the authority of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights (Kemenkumham).
