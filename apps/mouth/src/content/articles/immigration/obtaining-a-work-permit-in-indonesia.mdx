## The Facts

Obtaining a work permit in Indonesia is a mandatory legal requirement for all foreign nationals intending to engage in paid employment within the country. The primary instrument is the KITAS (Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas), a limited stay permit linked to a work authorization (IMTA/RPTKA). Employers bear primary responsibility for sponsoring and processing work permits on behalf of foreign employees.
