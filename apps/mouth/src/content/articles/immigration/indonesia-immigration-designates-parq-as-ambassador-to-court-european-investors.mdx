## The Facts

Indonesian Immigration authorities have formally appointed Parq as a Duta Layanan Keimigrasian — an Immigration Service Ambassador — in a ceremony that underscores the government's strategic intent to attract European business capital to Indonesia. The designation is part of a broader push by the Directorate General of Immigration to expand its outreach to foreign investor communities, positioning trusted intermediary institutions as official bridges between immigration services and the international business world.

The ambassador designation system allows vetted organizations to represent and promote formal immigration services, guiding foreign nationals through the Indonesian immigration ecosystem. In this capacity, Parq is expected to serve as a formal conduit for European businesspeople seeking to understand and access Indonesia's investor immigration options — from short-term business visit visas to long-term investor stay permits.

The regulatory framework underpinning this push is well-established at the national level. Permenkumham No. 22 Tahun 2023, as amended by Permenkumham No. 11 Tahun 2024, governs the modern visa index system designed to facilitate foreign business activity and investment. Under this framework, European investors can access Business Visit Visas — such as the C2 index for meetings and audits — as well as Investor KITAS products including the E28A standard investor permit and the premium E28B and E28C Golden Visa categories offering five- to ten-year residency.

Further modernization has come through UU No. 63 Tahun 2024, which amended the foundational Immigration Law to automatically integrate Multiple Exit Re-Entry Permits (MERP) into all KITAS and KITAP documents at the point of issuance. This eliminates a historically burdensome administrative step for investors who travel internationally on a frequent basis — a particularly relevant development for European executives managing cross-border portfolios.

Cost transparency for immigration services is governed by PP No. 45 Tahun 2024, which standardizes Non-Tax State Revenue (PNBP) tariffs across all immigration service categories nationwide. Additionally, Permenimipas No. 5 Tahun 2025 — issued under the newly restructured Ministry of Immigration and Penal Correction — provides specific guidance on immigration guarantors, allowing qualifying foreign investors to sponsor themselves using financial deposits or proof of investment, without requiring an Indonesian corporate entity as a sponsor.
