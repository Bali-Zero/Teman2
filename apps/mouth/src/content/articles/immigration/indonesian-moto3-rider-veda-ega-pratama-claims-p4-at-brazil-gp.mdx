Riders compete on 250cc single-cylinder machines, with grids regularly featuring over 30 competitors from across Europe, Asia, and the Americas. A fourth-place qualifying result places a rider at the very front of a fiercely contested field.

Veda Ega Pratama has been part of Indonesia's broader effort to establish itself as a serious player in international motorsport. Indonesia has invested heavily in motorsport infrastructure and talent development in recent years, partly driven by the country's enormous domestic appetite for motorcycle culture — Indonesia is one of the world's largest motorcycle markets, with tens of millions of registered two-wheelers.
