# B211 Social-Cultural Visit Visa 2026: Indonesia's Sponsored Visa for Family, NGO Work & Cultural Exchange

Planning to visit Indonesia for **family visits**, **volunteer work with NGOs**, or **cultural exchange programs**? The **B211 Social-Cultural Visit Visa** is Indonesia's sponsored visa designed for non-business social and cultural activities. Unlike tourist visas, B211 requires a **sponsor letter** from an Indonesian citizen or registered organization, but rewards you with **180 days maximum stay** (60 days + 4 extensions).

This comprehensive 2026 guide covers everything you need to know: B211 requirements, sponsor criteria, application process at Indonesian embassies, extension procedures, costs, and how it compares to B211A business visa and E-VOA tourist visa.

---

## What is B211 Social-Cultural Visit Visa?

**B211 Social-Cultural Visit Visa** (Visa Kunjungan Sosial Budaya) is a single-entry visa for foreign nationals visiting Indonesia for **non-commercial social and cultural purposes**. It's designed for:

- **Family visits** (visiting Indonesian relatives or friends)
- **NGO volunteer work** (teaching, community service, humanitarian work)
- **Cultural exchange** (cultural programs, traditional arts learning)
- **Religious activities** (attending religious ceremonies, retreats)
- **Social research** (academic research requiring local organization support)

### Key Features

- **Duration:** 60 days initial stay
- **Extendable:** Yes, 4 times (30 days each) = **180 days maximum**
- **Cost:** IDR 3,600,000 - 8,500,000 (through Bali Zero, includes sponsor)
- **Entry:** Single entry only (must leave Indonesia after 180 days)
- **Application:** Indonesian embassy/consulate in your home country
- **Sponsor required:** Indonesian citizen OR registered Indonesian organization
- **Work restrictions:** **No employment allowed** (including remote work for Indonesian clients)

### B211 vs B211A: What's the Difference?

Indonesia has **two types** of B211 visas that are often confused:

| Feature              | B211 Social-Cultural                                | B211A Business/Government                           |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Purpose**          | Family visits, NGO work, cultural exchange          | Business meetings, conferences, government visits   |
| **Sponsor type**     | Indonesian citizen OR organization                  | Indonesian company, government agency               |
| **Work allowed**     | No                                                  | No (but business meetings OK)                       |
| **Duration**         | 60 days + 4x30 = 180 days max                       | 60 days + 4x60 = 180 days max (some sources)        |
| **Cost (Bali Zero)** | IDR 8,500,000 (full service)                        | IDR 13,900,000 (C2 business)                        |
| **Who needs it**     | Tourists with Indonesian family/friends, volunteers | Business travelers, investors, conference attendees |

**This guide focuses on B211 Social-Cultural.** For business purposes, see [B211A Business Visa Guide](extended-visitor-visa).

## Who Needs B211 Social-Cultural Visa?

### You Should Apply for B211 If:

**1. Visiting Family or Friends in Indonesia**

- Your spouse is Indonesian (not yet married = need sponsor letter from them)
- Your children/parents/relatives live in Indonesia
- Your Indonesian friend invites you for extended visit

**Example:** Sarah from Australia wants to visit her Indonesian boyfriend's family in Bali for 4 months. She needs B211 with her boyfriend as sponsor.

**2. NGO Volunteer Work**

- Teaching English at Indonesian schools (unpaid)
- Volunteering with environmental NGOs in Bali
- Humanitarian work with disaster relief organizations
- Community development projects

**Example:** Marco from Italy volunteers with a marine conservation NGO in Gili Islands. The NGO sponsors his B211 visa for 6 months.

**3. Cultural Exchange Programs**

- Learning traditional Balinese dance/gamelan
- Participating in cultural festivals or ceremonies
- Artist residencies (non-commercial)
- Academic cultural research

**Example:** Emma from Canada studies Balinese traditional painting. Her art school in Ubud sponsors her B211 visa.

**4. Religious Activities**

- Attending spiritual retreats (yoga, meditation)
- Religious ceremonies or pilgrimages
- Studying with religious teachers
- Volunteer work at temples/mosques/churches

**Example:** David from USA attends a 3-month silent meditation retreat in Bali. The retreat center sponsors his B211 visa.

### You Should NOT Use B211 If:

**Wrong visa type:**

- **Business meetings/conferences** → Use B211A business visa instead
- **Tourism only, no sponsor** → Use E-VOA (60 days extendable to 120 days, no sponsor needed)
- **Remote work for foreign companies** → Technically need E33G (though many use tourist visas)
- **Working for Indonesian companies** → Need KITAS work visa
- **Starting a business** → Need investor KITAS (E28A) or director KITAS (E25B)

## B211 Sponsor Requirements: Who Can Sponsor You?

The **sponsor** is the most critical part of B211 application. Immigration requires proof that your visit has legitimate social/cultural purpose and local support.

### Option 1: Indonesian Citizen as Sponsor

**Who qualifies:**

- Indonesian citizens (WNI) with valid KTP (national ID)
- Can be: family member, spouse, friend, colleague
- **Does NOT need to be blood relative**

**Sponsor must provide:**

1. **Sponsor letter** (Surat Jaminan/Surat Sponsor) stating:
   - Their full name, KTP number, address
   - Your full name, passport number, nationality
   - Purpose of your visit (e.g., "family visit", "cultural exchange")
   - Duration of stay
   - Statement accepting responsibility for you during stay
   - Signature + date
2. **KTP copy** (Indonesian national ID card)
3. **NPWP copy** (Indonesian tax number) - sometimes required
4. **Proof of relationship** (if applicable):
   - Marriage certificate (if spouse)
   - Birth certificate (if parent/child)
   - Photos together (if friend)

**Example sponsor letter:**

```
SURAT SPONSOR / SPONSOR LETTER

Yang bertanda tangan di bawah ini:
Nama: Wayan Suryadi
KTP: 5171051234567890
Alamat: Jl. Raya Ubud No. 123, Gianyar, Bali 80571
Telepon: +62 812 3456 7890

Dengan ini menyatakan bertanggung jawab penuh atas kunjungan:
Nama: Sarah Johnson
Paspor: AB1234567
Kewarganegaraan: Australia
Tujuan Kunjungan: Kunjungan keluarga (family visit)
Durasi: 180 hari (180 days)

Selama berada di Indonesia, saya menjamin bahwa orang tersebut akan:
- Mematuhi hukum dan peraturan Indonesia
- Tidak bekerja atau melakukan aktivitas komersial
- Meninggalkan Indonesia sebelum visa berakhir

Ubud, 15 Februari 2026
Tanda Tangan: _______________
```

   - At least 2 blank pages

   - Recent, 4x6cm, white or red background
   - 3-6 copies (depends on embassy)
3. **Visa application form**
   - Download from Indonesian embassy website
   - Or Bali Zero provides filled form
4. **Sponsor letter + sponsor documents** (see section above)

   - Minimum balance varies by embassy (~USD 2,000+)
   - Shows you can support yourself
8. **Travel insurance** (recommended, some embassies require)
   - Coverage for Indonesia
   - Minimum USD 50,000 medical coverage

**Additional documents (depending on purpose):**

- **NGO volunteers:** Letter of acceptance from NGO, program description
- **Cultural exchange:** Letter from cultural organization, program details
- **Religious activities:** Letter from religious organization

#### Step 3: Apply at Indonesian Embassy/Consulate

**Where to apply:**

- Indonesian embassy in your **home country or country of legal residence**
- Cannot apply from Indonesia (must exit first)

**Submission methods:**

- **In-person:** Walk-in or appointment (check embassy website)
- **By mail/courier:** Some embassies accept (rare)

**Embassy fees:**

- Varies by nationality: USD 50-150 (paid at embassy)
- Separate from agent service fee

#### Step 4: Wait for Processing (5-10 Business Days)

**Normal processing:** 5-7 business days (embassy to immigration to embassy)

**Some embassies offer:**

- **Express service:** 3 days (extra fee)
- **Same day:** Rare, only for urgent cases (medical emergencies, etc.)

**Tracking:**

- Most embassies don't provide tracking
- Bali Zero monitors application status if using our service

#### Step 5: Collect Visa Sticker

**What you receive:**

- **B211 visa sticker** in your passport (full-page sticker)
- Valid for **90 days from issuance** (you must enter Indonesia within 90 days)
- Shows "Single Entry" and "60 days stay"

**Check visa details:**

- Your name spelled correctly
- Passport number matches
- Validity dates correct
- Purpose: "Sosial Budaya" (Social-Cultural)

#### Step 6: Enter Indonesia

**At Indonesian immigration:**

1. Present passport with B211 visa sticker
2. Immigration officer stamps passport with entry date
3. You're granted **60 days stay from entry date**
4. Keep entry stamp safe (needed for extensions)

**Important:** B211 is **single entry**. If you leave Indonesia, your visa is canceled and you must apply for a new one.

## B211 Extension: From 60 to 180 Days

### Extension Policy

B211 can be extended **4 times**, each for **30 days**:

- **Initial B211:** 60 days
- **Extension 1:** +30 days = 90 days total
- **Extension 2:** +30 days = 120 days total
- **Extension 3:** +30 days = 150 days total
- **Extension 4:** +30 days = **180 days total (maximum)**

**After 180 days:** You must leave Indonesia. No further extensions possible (except extreme circumstances like medical emergency).

### How to Extend B211

#### Option 1: DIY at Immigration Office

**Steps:**

1. **Visit immigration office** 7-14 days before current visa expires
   - **Bali:** Kantor Imigrasi Kelas I Ngurah Rai (Jimbaran)
   - **Jakarta:** District immigration offices
   - **Other cities:** Check imigrasi.go.id for locations

2. **Bring documents:**
   - Passport (original)
   - Sponsor letter (renewal - ask sponsor to write new letter stating continued support)
   - Accommodation proof (hotel booking or sponsor address)
   - Passport photos (4x6cm, 2 copies)
   - Entry stamp (in passport, will be checked)

3. **Submit application at extension desk**
   - Fill out extension form (provided at immigration)
   - Pay government fee: **IDR 500,000** per extension

4. **Wait 3-7 days** (passport may be held during processing)
   - Some immigration offices allow you to keep passport, some hold it
   - You'll receive receipt with pickup date

5. **Return to collect passport** with extension stamp
   - New expiration date stamped in passport

**DIY Challenges:**

- Language barrier (immigration officers may not speak English)
- Long queues (arrive early, 7-8 AM recommended)
- Unclear requirements (may ask for additional documents)
- Passport held (can't travel domestically while processing)

## Common B211 Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

### 1. Wrong Sponsor Type

**WRONG:**

- Using your company as sponsor for B211 social-cultural
- Using business colleague as sponsor for family visit visa

**RIGHT:**

- **B211 social-cultural:** Indonesian citizen (friend/family) OR social organization (NGO, foundation)
- **B211A business:** Indonesian company OR government agency
- Match sponsor type to visa purpose

### 2. Visa Validity vs Stay Duration Confusion

**WRONG:**

- "My B211 is valid 90 days, so I can stay 90 days without extension"

**RIGHT:**

- **Visa validity:** 90 days from issuance (deadline to ENTER Indonesia)
- **Stay duration:** 60 days from ENTRY date (initial stay, before extensions)
- Example: Visa issued Feb 1 → Valid until May 1 → You enter April 25 → You can stay until June 24 (60 days from entry)

### 3. Single Entry Misunderstanding

**WRONG:**

- "I have B211, I can leave Indonesia for a weekend trip and come back"

**RIGHT:**

- B211 is **SINGLE ENTRY**
- If you leave Indonesia (even for 1 day), your B211 is CANCELED
- To return, you must apply for a NEW B211 (or different visa)
- **Exception:** If you need to travel, exit and re-enter on E-VOA (but lose your B211 extensions)

### 4. Working on B211

**WRONG:**

- "I'm volunteering teaching English, so I'll get paid a small stipend"
- "I'm on B211, I can do freelance web design for Indonesian clients"

**RIGHT:**

- B211 is strictly **NO WORK** (paid or unpaid employment)
- **Allowed:** Genuine volunteer work (no payment, no salary)
- **Not allowed:** Any form of employment, even if "volunteer" title
- **Gray area:** Remote work for foreign companies (not officially legal, but...)

**Consequences:** Working on B211 = visa revocation + deportation + 5-year ban

### 5. Missing Extension Deadlines

**WRONG:**

- "I'll apply for extension tomorrow" (visa expires today)
- "I'm only overstaying 2 days, immigration won't care"

**RIGHT:**

- Apply **7-14 days before** expiration (immigration needs processing time)
- **DO NOT overstay** - penalties are strict:
  - IDR 1,000,000 per day (up to 60 days)
  - After 60 days: Deportation + 5-year ban

**Set reminders:** Phone calendar, WhatsApp reminders, alarms

### 6. Insufficient Sponsor Documentation

**WRONG:**

- Sponsor letter with no KTP/NPWP
- Sponsor letter signed but not dated
- Generic sponsor letter ("To whom it may concern")

**RIGHT:**

- **Must include:**
  - Sponsor's full details (name, KTP number, address, phone)
  - Your full details (name, passport number, nationality)
  - Specific purpose (e.g., "family visit to attend wedding")
  - Duration (e.g., "180 days")
  - Dated and signed by sponsor
- **Attach:** KTP copy + NPWP copy (clear, readable)

## B211 FAQ

### Can I apply for B211 if I'm already in Indonesia on E-VOA?

**No.** B211 must be applied at an Indonesian embassy/consulate **outside Indonesia**.

**If you're in Indonesia on E-VOA and want B211:**

1. Exit Indonesia (e.g., fly to Singapore, Kuala Lumpur)
2. Apply for B211 at Indonesian embassy in that country
3. Wait for approval (5-10 days)
4. Re-enter Indonesia with B211 visa

**Alternatively:** Just extend your E-VOA twice (max 120 days) and plan better for next visit.

### Can I convert B211 to KITAS while in Indonesia?

**No.** B211 cannot be converted to KITAS (work visa, investor visa, etc.) from inside Indonesia.

**If you want KITAS:**

1. Complete your B211 stay (or exit early)
2. Leave Indonesia
3. Apply for KITAS (E25B, E28A, E33G, etc.) from abroad
4. Re-enter with KITAS

### What if my sponsor dies or becomes unavailable during my stay?

**For extensions:** You can change sponsors.

**Steps:**

1. Find new sponsor (another Indonesian friend/family OR use Bali Zero)
2. Get new sponsor letter + documents
3. Apply for extension with new sponsor

**Immigration will ask why sponsor changed** - be honest (e.g., "original sponsor moved abroad").

### Can I do a border run to reset my B211?

**Yes, but...** you'll lose your current B211 extensions.

**How it works:**

1. Your B211 (with 3 extensions remaining) expires in 10 days
2. You exit Indonesia (e.g., fly to Singapore)
3. Your B211 is **canceled** upon exit
4. You apply for **new B211** at Indonesian embassy in Singapore
5. Re-enter with fresh 60-day B211 (starts from scratch)

**Cheaper option:** Just extend your current B211 (saves visa application cost + flight).
