# Golden Visa Indonesia 2026: Complete Investment Visa Guide

Indonesia's Golden Visa program (officially called ITAP - Izin Tinggal Tetap untuk Investor) offers **5 or 10-year residence permits** to foreign investors without the traditional 5-year KITAS waiting period required for KITAP. Launched in 2024, this program targets high-net-worth individuals and significant investors seeking long-term Indonesian residence.

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="2026 Update (Source: imigrasi.go.id)">
**Golden Visa Program Status:**

As of February 2026, Indonesia's Golden Visa program is active with the following confirmed requirements:

- **Individual Investors:** IDR 350,000,000 minimum for 5-year, IDR 700,000,000 for 10-year
- **Corporate Investors:** IDR 2.5 billion minimum for 5-year, IDR 5 billion for 10-year
- **Investment Vehicles:** Government bonds, Indonesian property, public company stocks, bank deposits at state banks
- **Processing Time:** 4-8 weeks from application submission to approval

**Important:** This is NOT the same as the E28B/E28C/E28D investor visa categories. Golden Visa (ITAP) grants permanent residence, while E28 visas are temporary stay permits.

</InfoCard>

## What is the Golden Visa?

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Golden Visa (ITAP) Overview">
The Golden Visa is Indonesia's answer to residence-by-investment programs offered by countries like Portugal, Greece, and the UAE. Key features:

- **5-year or 10-year validity** - No annual renewals
- **No prior KITAS required** - Skip the traditional 5-year waiting period
- **Investment-based** - Must maintain qualified investment
- **Family inclusion** - Spouse and dependents can be sponsored
- **Work rights** - Depends on investment vehicle (see below)

**Legal Basis:** Peraturan Menteri Hukum dan HAM RI tentang Izin Tinggal Tetap bagi Investor (ITAP regulation, 2024)

</InfoCard>

### 1. Government Bonds (Surat Berharga Negara - SBN)

**Eligible Bonds:**

- SBN (Surat Berharga Negara)
- ORI (Obligasi Republik Indonesia)
- Sukuk (Islamic bonds)
- SUN (Surat Utang Negara)

**Requirements:**

- Must be held at Indonesian securities account
- Minimum holding period: Match visa validity (5 or 10 years)
- Must provide proof of purchase and custody
- Bonds must be in your name (not company)

**Work Rights:** ❌ No work authorization (residence only)

**Pros:**

- Liquid investment (can sell after visa period)
- Regular interest payments
- Government-backed security
- Clear exit strategy

**Cons:**

- Market risk (bond value fluctuates)
- Currency risk (IDR vs your home currency)
- Interest rates may be lower than alternatives

---

### 2. Indonesian Property

**Eligible Properties:**

- Residential property (houses, apartments, villas)
- Commercial property (offices, retail spaces)
- Land with development plans
- Must be located in Indonesia

**Requirements:**

- Property value must meet investment threshold
- Must provide proof of ownership (certificate of title)
- Property appraisal from certified appraiser
- Foreigners limited to leasehold or Hak Pakai (see note below)

**Work Rights:** ❌ No work authorization (residence only)

**Pros:**

- Tangible asset you can use/live in
- Potential appreciation
- Rental income possible
- No need to liquidate after visa period

**Cons:**

- Illiquid (hard to sell quickly)
- Foreigners cannot own freehold (Hak Milik)
- Property maintenance costs
- Market fluctuations

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="Foreign Property Ownership Limitations">
**Important:** Foreigners cannot directly own freehold (Hak Milik) land in Indonesia. For Golden Visa property investment:

- **Hak Pakai (Right to Use):** 25-80 years, renewable, allowed for foreigners
- **Leasehold:** 25-30 years, renewable, common in Bali
- **Through PT PMA:** Company ownership (different visa category)

Always consult a property lawyer before purchasing for Golden Visa purposes.

</InfoCard>

### 3. Public Company Stocks

**Eligible Investments:**

- Shares of Indonesian public companies listed on IDX (Indonesia Stock Exchange)
- Portfolio must be maintained at Indonesian securities firm
- Combined portfolio value must meet threshold

**Requirements:**

- Securities account at Indonesian broker
- Proof of stock ownership and value
- Portfolio valuation every 6 months
- Must rebalance if value drops below threshold

**Work Rights:** ❌ No work authorization (residence only)

**Pros:**

- Highly liquid (can sell anytime)
- Dividend income
- Potential capital appreciation
- Diversified investment

**Cons:**

- High market volatility risk
- Must maintain minimum value (market risk)
- Transaction fees and taxes
- Requires active monitoring

### 4. Bank Deposit (State Bank)

**Eligible Banks:**

- Bank Negara Indonesia (BNI)
- Bank Rakyat Indonesia (BRI)
- Bank Mandiri
- Bank Tabungan Negara (BTN)

**Requirements:**

- Deposit must be at Indonesian state bank
- Time deposit or savings account in your name
- Minimum holding period: Match visa validity
- Certificate of deposit from bank

**Work Rights:** ❌ No work authorization (residence only)

**Pros:**

- Safest option (government-backed)
- Guaranteed principal
- Interest income
- Easy to prove and maintain

**Cons:**

- Lowest returns (deposit interest rates)
- Currency risk (IDR depreciation)
- Funds locked for visa period
- Inflation may erode value

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Bank Deposit Interest Rates (2026)">
As of February 2026, Indonesian state bank deposit rates for large deposits:

- **1-year:** 3.5-4.5% per annum
- **5-year:** 4.5-5.5% per annum
- **10-year:** Not commonly offered (5-year renewed)

**Example:** IDR 350M at 5% = IDR 17.5M annual interest (~USD 1,166/month)

</InfoCard>

### 5. PT PMA Business Investment

**Eligible Investments:**

- Establishing or investing in PT PMA (foreign investment company)
- Must be registered with BKPM
- Business must be operational (not just on paper)

**Requirements:**

- Company registered and operational
- Investment must be in paid-up capital
- Business plan and financial statements
- Proof of capital deposit

**Work Rights:** ✅ Yes, if you hold director position with proper RPTKA

**Pros:**

- Can work in your own business
- Active investment with business control
- Potential for high returns
- Family members can be employed

**Cons:**

- Most complex option
- Business operational risks
- Requires active management
- Regulatory compliance burden

<InfoCard variant="highlight" title="PT PMA Golden Visa vs Investor KITAS (E28A)">
**Different programs:**

- **Golden Visa (ITAP):** Permanent residence, 5-10 year validity, IDR 350M+ investment
- **Investor KITAS (E28A):** Temporary stay permit, 2-year validity, IDR 10B+ shareholding, renewable annually

If you're establishing a PT PMA, evaluate both options. Golden Visa may be better if you want long-term residence without annual renewals.

</InfoCard>

### Choose Golden Visa If:

- You have significant liquid capital (IDR 350M+)
- You want Indonesian residence immediately
- You don't want to wait 5 years
- You prefer passive investment (bonds, property, stocks)
- You can afford to lock investment for 5-10 years

### Choose KITAP Path If:

- You don't have large upfront capital
- You're already working in Indonesia (have KITAS)
- You want to work before getting permanent residence
- You prefer to build toward residence over time
- Your company sponsors your KITAS

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Can You Have Both?">
**Yes, eventually.** If you get Golden Visa (ITAP) first, you can later apply for KITAP after meeting its requirements. However, this is uncommon since Golden Visa already grants permanent residence.

More common: Get KITAS → work 5 years → apply for KITAP (no investment needed).

</InfoCard>

## Rights and Limitations

### What You CAN Do with Golden Visa

✅ **Live in Indonesia indefinitely** - For full 5 or 10 year validity
✅ **Multiple entry/exit** - Travel freely, no re-entry permit needed
✅ **Open bank accounts** - As Indonesian resident
✅ **Own vehicles** - Cars, motorcycles in your name
✅ **Sponsor family** - Spouse, dependent children
✅ **Receive dividends** - From Indonesian investments
✅ **Buy property** - Under Hak Pakai (foreigner rules apply)
✅ **Start PT PMA** - Establish business (if separate from Golden Visa investment)

### What You CANNOT Do (Generally)

❌ **Work without authorization** - Golden Visa is residence, not automatic work permit
❌ **Access BPJS** - National health insurance (may change in future)
❌ **Vote** - Permanent residence is not citizenship
❌ **Own freehold land** - Hak Milik still restricted to Indonesian citizens
❌ **Withdraw investment early** - Must maintain for visa validity period

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="Work Rights Clarification">
**Golden Visa alone does NOT grant work authorization.** However:

- **PT PMA investment:** If you invest via your own PT PMA and hold a director position, you can work under that company with proper RPTKA work authorization
- **Passive investments (bonds, property, stocks, deposits):** No work rights, residence only
- **Separate work permit:** You can apply for a working KITAS while holding Golden Visa if you find employment

**Best practice:** Clarify work authorization needs before choosing investment vehicle.

</InfoCard>

## Family Inclusion

### Spouse Sponsorship

**Eligible:** Legal spouse (marriage certificate required)

**Process:**

1. Apply for your Golden Visa first
2. After approval, apply for spouse's dependent ITAP
3. Spouse gets same validity period as principal holder

**Requirements for Spouse:**

- Marriage certificate (apostilled/legalized)
- Passport (valid 24+ months)
- Medical fitness certificate
- Police clearance
- Passport photos

**Cost:** Additional ~IDR 10-15M (government + service fees)

### Dependent Children

**Eligible:** Unmarried children under 21 years old

**Process:**

- Apply together with principal Golden Visa application, OR
- Add children after principal approval

**Requirements per Child:**

- Birth certificate (apostilled/legalized)
- Passport (valid 24+ months)
- Medical fitness certificate
- Passport photos
- Proof of dependency (student enrollment, financial support)

**Cost:** Additional ~IDR 8-12M per child

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Children Over 21">
If your children are over 21, they **cannot** be sponsored as dependents on your Golden Visa. They would need to:

- Apply for their own Golden Visa (if they have qualifying investment), OR
- Apply for appropriate visa (student visa if studying, work visa if employed), OR
- Remain on tourist visa and do visa runs

This is a common challenge for families with adult children.

</InfoCard>

## Maintenance and Renewal

### During Validity Period (5 or 10 Years)

**Annual Requirements:**

- Maintain investment at minimum threshold
- Valid health insurance
- No criminal violations
- Tax compliance (if earning Indonesian income)

**Investment Monitoring:**

- **Bonds/Stocks:** Must maintain minimum value (market fluctuations monitored every 6 months)
- **Property:** Annual proof of ownership (PBB tax receipts)
- **Deposit:** Bank certificate confirming balance
- **PT PMA:** Annual financial statements, operational proof

**Reporting:**

- Immigration may request proof of continued investment annually or biennially
- Keep all investment statements organized

### Renewal After 5 or 10 Years

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="Golden Visa Renewal Process">
Unlike KITAP which renews for another 5 years, **Golden Visa requires full re-application** at the end of its validity period.

**This means:**

- New application process (similar to initial application)
- Re-prove investment and financial capacity
- Update all documents
- Pay fees again

**Strategy:** Many holders choose 10-year option to minimize renewal frequency.

</InfoCard>

**Renewal Requirements:**

- Investment still meets threshold (adjusted for inflation?)
- No criminal record during validity period
- Continued financial capacity
- Updated medical fitness
- All documents re-submitted

**Renewal Costs:** Similar to initial application (~IDR 25-40M total)

## Who Should Consider Golden Visa?

### Ideal Candidates

✅ **High-net-worth individuals** - Have IDR 350M+ liquid capital
✅ **Retirees with savings** - Want long-term residence without annual renewals
✅ **Property investors** - Already own or plan to buy Indonesian property
✅ **Business owners** - Establishing PT PMA and want long-term stability
✅ **Digital nomads with capital** - Remote workers who want Indonesian base
✅ **Family relocation** - Want to sponsor spouse and children long-term

### Not Ideal For

❌ **Budget travelers** - IDR 350M is too high for typical digital nomads
❌ **Short-term stays** - Tourist visa or B211A cheaper for 1-2 year stays
❌ **Those needing work rights** - Golden Visa alone doesn't grant work permit (unless PT PMA route)
❌ **Risk-averse investors** - Investment must stay locked for visa period
❌ **Those without capital** - Cannot borrow or lease investment (must be owned)

### What if my investment loses value?

**Stock/Bond portfolios:** If market fluctuations drop your portfolio below the minimum threshold (IDR 350M or 700M), immigration may require you to:

1. Top up investment to meet minimum, OR
2. Convert to different investment vehicle, OR
3. Risk Golden Visa revocation

**Recommendation:** Invest 10-20% above minimum as buffer for market volatility.

**Property:** Appraisal is typically done once at application. Unless major market crash, unlikely to cause issues during validity period.

**Bank deposit:** No risk (principal guaranteed).

---

### Can I change investment vehicles mid-validity?

**Generally, no.** Your Golden Visa is tied to the investment you declared at application. To change:

1. Must notify immigration in advance
2. New investment must meet same criteria
3. May require partial re-application
4. Immigration discretion to approve

**Example:** If you invested in bonds and want to switch to property, you cannot simply sell bonds and buy property without immigration approval.

---

### What happens if I don't renew after 5/10 years?

If you choose not to renew Golden Visa at expiration:

1. You lose Indonesian residence status
2. You revert to tourist visa or must obtain new visa
3. Your investment can be withdrawn/sold (no penalty)
4. No adverse immigration record (not an overstay)

**Note:** Unlike KITAP, letting Golden Visa expire is not an "offense" - it's simply choosing not to renew.

---

### Can Golden Visa lead to Indonesian citizenship?

**Not directly.** Indonesia does not have an "investment citizenship" program.

To become an Indonesian citizen, you must:

1. Hold permanent residence (KITAP or Golden Visa) for minimum duration
2. Meet citizenship requirements (language, integration, etc.)
3. Apply for naturalization
4. Renounce previous citizenship (Indonesia generally doesn't allow dual citizenship)

**Timeline:** Even with Golden Visa, citizenship takes 5-10+ years and is not guaranteed.

## Action Steps: How to Get Started

### Step 1: Financial Assessment

Before proceeding:

1. Verify you have minimum investment amount (IDR 350M or 700M)
2. Ensure funds are liquid and transferable to Indonesia
3. Budget additional ~IDR 20-30M for fees
4. Consider family sponsorship costs if applicable

### Step 2: Choose Investment Vehicle

Match your situation:

- **Passive/Safe:** Bank deposit (guaranteed principal)
- **Income-focused:** Government bonds (interest income)
- **Existing asset:** Property you already own/plan to buy
- **Risk-tolerant:** Stocks (highest potential returns)
- **Business owner:** PT PMA (work rights included)

### Step 3: Consult Immigration Expert

**Recommended:** Engage immigration lawyer or licensed agent (like Bali Zero) BEFORE making investment.

**Why?**

- Verify investment vehicle qualifies
- Ensure documentation is correct
- Avoid costly mistakes
- Streamline application process

### Step 4: Execute Investment

Once strategy confirmed:

1. Open necessary accounts (bank, securities, etc.)
2. Transfer funds to Indonesia
3. Execute investment transaction
4. Obtain proof documents (certificates, statements)

### Step 5: Apply for Golden Visa

With investment completed:

1. Gather all documents (see checklist above)
2. Legalize/apostille foreign documents
3. Submit application via immigration lawyer
4. Attend biometrics appointment
5. Receive Golden Visa approval

### Step 6: Maintain Compliance

During visa validity:

- Keep investment at minimum threshold
- File annual reports if required
- Maintain health insurance
- Keep all documentation organized for renewal
