# Golden Visa Indonesia 2026: Complete Investment Visa Guide

Indonesia's Golden Visa is not one permit but a family of investor visas listed by Imigrasi under the E28 codes. Each is a **limited-stay visa (ITAS)** for **5 or 10 years**, extendable, convertible into another limited-stay permit, and **needs no sponsor**. It is **not** permanent residence (not ITAP or KITAP). Bali Zero handles E28B, E28C, E28D and E28F at **price on consultation**.

## The Golden Visa Routes

| Code | Who it is for | 5 years | 10 years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E28B | An individual investor who will establish a company in Indonesia | Commitment to establish a company with issued capital or investment of at least USD 2,500,000 | Commitment to establish a company with issued capital or investment of at least USD 5,000,000 |
| E28C | An individual investor in Indonesian government bonds, shares of a listed company in Indonesia or mutual funds | Proof of ownership of at least USD 350,000 in Indonesian government bonds, shares of a listed company in Indonesia or mutual funds | Proof of ownership of at least USD 700,000 in Indonesian government bonds, shares of a listed company in Indonesia or mutual funds, or of an apartment worth at least USD 1,000,000 |
| E28D | A person who will serve as director or commissioner of a company to be established in Indonesia as a branch or subsidiary of a foreign company | Commitment by the foreign company to invest at least USD 25,000,000 | Commitment by the foreign company to invest at least USD 50,000,000 |
| E28F | The same, for a company to be established in the new capital Ibu Kota Nusantara (IKN) | Commitment by the foreign company to invest at least USD 5,000,000 | Commitment by the foreign company to invest at least USD 10,000,000 |
| E28E | Special Economic Zone investor | Listed by Imigrasi with no published details yet | Listed by Imigrasi with no published details yet |

For E28B, E28D and E28F the statement of commitment goes with the application, and the investment must be fulfilled within 90 days after entry into Indonesia.

## What Each Route Requires

- **E28B:** the commitment above, plus proof of your shares in a company outside Indonesia and proof of that company's turnover. Both must appear in financial statements audited to international standards, the shares in the audited report of the parent company from an international public accounting firm (the Imigrasi list includes Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Grant Thornton and BDO).
- **E28C:** proof of ownership of the bonds, shares, mutual funds or apartment.
- **E28D and E28F:** you must be appointed as a director or commissioner of the company. The foreign company, not you, is the party that commits to the investment, and it must fulfil it within 90 days after your entry.

## What the Golden Visa Lets You Do

- **All four routes:** invest and carry out business and investment activities; bring your family; leave and re-enter while the re-entry permit is valid; tourism, buying goods, and visiting friends or family; receive returns or benefits from your investment; extend the stay; convert it into another limited-stay permit.
- **E28B, E28D and E28F also:** carry out company-establishment activities; act as a member of the board of directors or the board of commissioners of the company you invested in; supervise its production, including at its office, factory or similar site; receive remuneration for the post you hold.
- **E28D and E28F also:** discuss, negotiate and sign business agreements.
- **E28B also:** take part in education through the dual-activity mechanism (Rangkap Kegiatan Izin Tinggal).

Prohibited on all four: staying beyond the permit, work that does not match the permit, and activities outside the rights granted unless you have applied for a change or a dual activity.

## How to Apply

1. Create an account at evisa.imigrasi.go.id and log in.
2. Upload your passport (valid at least 6 months before it expires), a bank statement for the last 3 months in the name of the foreigner or the sponsor showing at least USD 2,000 or the equivalent, a recent colour photo (taken within the last year), your CV and your itinerary, plus the proof for your route above.
3. A stateless person, or the holder of a travel document that is not a national passport (such as a temporary or emergency passport, titre de voyage, certificate of identity or laissez passer), must also attach a re-entry permit to the country where the visa is applied for and a return or onward ticket, except crew.
4. The visa stage takes five working days after the visa fee is paid. The visa is valid for 90 days from issuance for entry. The ITAS and the re-entry permit are issued automatically when the officer admits you at the immigration checkpoint.

## Golden Visa Is Not Permanent Residence

The Golden Visa is a limited-stay visa (ITAS). It is not ITAP or KITAP and it does not replace KITAP.
