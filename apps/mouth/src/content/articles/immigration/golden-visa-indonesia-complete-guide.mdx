# Golden Visa Indonesia 2026: Complete Investment Visa Guide

Indonesia's Golden Visa is not one permit but a family of investor routes listed by Imigrasi under the E28 codes. Each route grants a **limited-stay permit (ITAS) for 5 or 10 years**, extendable, with **no sponsor needed**. It is **not** permanent residence (not ITAP or KITAP). Bali Zero handles the Golden Visa routes at **price on consultation**.

## The Golden Visa Routes

| Route | Who it is for                                                    | 5 years                                                                              | 10 years                                                                                       |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| E28B  | Company establishment in Indonesia                               | USD 2.5M, committed within 90 days of entry                                          | USD 5M, committed within 90 days of entry                                                      |
| E28C  | Capital-market investors                                         | USD 350,000 in government bonds, listed shares or mutual funds                       | USD 700,000 in government bonds, listed shares or mutual funds, or an apartment of at least USD 1,000,000 |
| E28D  | Director or commissioner of a branch or subsidiary of a foreign company | Foreign company commits USD 25M within 90 days of entry                       | Foreign company commits USD 50M within 90 days of entry                                        |
| E28F  | The same as E28D, in the new capital IKN                         | USD 5M within 90 days of entry                                                       | USD 10M within 90 days of entry                                                                |
| E28E  | Special Economic Zone investor                                   | Listed by Imigrasi with no published details yet                                     | Listed by Imigrasi with no published details yet                                               |

Details by route:

- **E28B:** besides the commitment, you provide audited proof of your shares in a foreign company and of its turnover (international-standard audit).
- **E28C:** you provide proof of ownership of the bonds, listed shares, mutual funds or apartment. There is no 90-day commitment.
- **E28D and E28F:** the commitment is made by the foreign company, not by you personally.

## What the Golden Visa Lets You Do

- Invest and carry out business, investment and company-establishment activities (E28D and E28F also cover negotiating and signing business agreements)
- On E28B, E28D and E28F: act as director or commissioner of the company you invested in and supervise its production
- Bring your family and travel in and out while the re-entry permit is valid
- Receive compensation or benefits from your investment or the post you hold

Not allowed: overstaying, work that does not match the permit, and activities outside the rights granted unless you have applied for a change.

## How to Apply

1. Create an account at evisa.imigrasi.go.id.
2. Upload your passport (valid at least 6 months), a bank statement for the last 3 months showing at least USD 2,000, a recent photo, your CV and an itinerary, plus the route-specific proof above.
3. The official visa stage takes five working days after the visa fee is paid. The visa is valid 90 days from issuance for entry, and the ITAS is issued automatically when the officer admits you at the immigration checkpoint.

## Golden Visa vs E28A Investor KITAS

| Feature     | Golden Visa (E28B/C/D/F)               | E28A Investor KITAS                                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Permit      | ITAS, 5 or 10 years, extendable        | ITAS, sold for 2 years, extendable                         |
| Sponsor     | None                                   | Required - the PT PMA you invested in                      |
| Threshold   | USD figures per route (table above)    | Own shareholding of at least IDR 10 billion in the PT PMA  |
| Price       | On consultation                        | All-inclusive: IDR 17,000,000 offshore, IDR 19,000,000 onshore, IDR 18,000,000 extension |

See the [E28A Investor KITAS guide](/immigration/e28a-investor-kitas-guide) for the E28A route.

## Golden Visa vs KITAP

The Golden Visa is a limited-stay permit, so it does not replace KITAP, the separate permanent residence permit.
