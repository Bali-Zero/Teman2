# E28A Investor KITAS 2026: Requirements, Rights and Price

The E28A is Indonesia's investor visa for a foreigner who holds shares in a PT PMA (foreign-investment company). It is a limited-stay visa (ITAS) that can be extended and converted into another limited-stay permit. It needs a sponsor: the PT PMA you invested in. Bali Zero sells the E28A for 2 years.

## Who Qualifies

- **Own shareholding of at least IDR 10 billion** (or the equivalent) in the sponsoring company, which must be registered with the Ministry of Investment/BKPM.
- E28A admits passive shareholders with sufficient own shareholding: holding a post in the company is not required.
- If your shares are worth less than IDR 10 billion and you also hold a post in the company (director or commissioner), you must apply for a work visa matching that post instead.
- The PT PMA's own paid-up capital of IDR 2.5 billion is the company's threshold. It is not the test of your holding.

## What the E28A Lets You Do

- Invest, and carry out business, investment and company-establishment activities
- Act as a member of the board of directors or the board of commissioners of the company you invested in
- Supervise the production of goods or services at the company you established, including at its office, factory or similar site
- Bring your family to live in Indonesia
- Leave and re-enter Indonesia while the re-entry permit is valid
- Take part in education through the dual-activity mechanism (Rangkap Kegiatan Izin Tinggal)
- Carry out investment activities, travel as a tourist, and visit friends or family

## What the E28A Does Not Allow

- Staying in Indonesia beyond the period of your stay permit
- Work that does not match the stay permit
- Selling goods or services, unless the work requires it

## Documents

You apply online at evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Create an account first, then upload these electronic files:

1. Visa application letter
2. National passport valid for at least 6 months before it expires
3. Proof of living expenses in Indonesia: a bank statement for the last 3 months, in the name of the foreigner or the sponsor, showing at least USD 2,000 or the equivalent
4. Recent colour photo (taken within the last year)
5. Curriculum vitae (CV)
6. Itinerary
7. Proof of shareholding of at least IDR 10,000,000,000 or the equivalent in the sponsoring company registered with the Ministry of Investment/BKPM
8. The Ministry of Law decision approving the establishment of the company
9. The company's current-account (giro) statements for the last 2 months. If the company was established less than 2 months ago, they can be submitted to the immigration office up to 90 days after the ITAS is issued.

## The Journey

1. The visa stage at the Directorate General of Immigration takes five working days after the visa fee is paid. Bali Zero typically takes 7-10 working days (indicative).
2. The visa is valid for 90 days from issuance. If you do not use it within that time you must apply for a new visa to enter Indonesia.
3. The ITAS and the re-entry permit are issued automatically when the officer admits you at the immigration checkpoint.
4. The stay counts from the date of arrival.
5. The sponsor extends the stay online through its account at evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Imigrasi does not state the length of an extension.
