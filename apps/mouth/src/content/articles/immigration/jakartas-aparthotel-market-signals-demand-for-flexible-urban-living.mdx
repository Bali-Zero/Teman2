## The Facts

Citadines Gatot Subroto is a serviced residence located on one of Jakarta's principal arterial roads, Jalan Gatot Subroto, a corridor that runs through the heart of the city's business and diplomatic district. The property belongs to The Ascott Limited's Citadines brand, a Singapore-headquartered hospitality group with a significant footprint across Southeast Asia.

Serviced residences of this category occupy a specific market segment: they are neither standard hotels nor conventional rental apartments. Guests pay for the flexibility of short to extended stays — from a few days to several months — while accessing amenities typically associated with four-star hotels, including housekeeping, concierge services, fitness facilities, and food and beverage outlets. This model appeals particularly to corporate relocatees and expatriates who arrive in a city before securing permanent housing.

Gatot Subroto's location places the property within easy reach of the Sudirman Central Business District, Kuningan's embassy row, and the broader Golden Triangle — Jakarta's premium commercial zone. Proximity to major employers, government ministries, and foreign missions makes the corridor a natural draw for foreign nationals on assignment or in the process of establishing a legal presence in Indonesia.

The serviced residence segment in Indonesia has expanded steadily over the past decade, driven by the country's growing attractiveness to multinational companies setting up regional offices and by progressive reforms to the foreign investment framework that have encouraged more expatriate-level hires across sectors including finance, technology, and extractive industries.

For foreign nationals, the practical appeal of a serviced residence extends beyond comfort. Properties operating in this segment typically maintain the compliance infrastructure — including registration systems aligned with the civil registry requirements imposed on all foreign visitors staying more than a defined period — that individual landlords in the private rental market may not always provide consistently.
