Indonesia presents distinct regulatory frameworks for cryptocurrency and forex trading, both relevant to expatriates managing international finances. Crypto assets are classified as commodities under OJK (Financial Services Authority) and Bappebti oversight, not as legal tender, making them speculative investment vehicles.
