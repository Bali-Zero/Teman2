## The Facts

The Westin Resort Nusa Dua, Bali has launched a curated holistic wellness program timed around Nyepi, the Balinese Hindu Day of Silence (Hari Raya Nyepi). This annual observance, observed island-wide on the Saka New Year, enforces a 24-hour period of silence, fasting, darkness, and no travel (Catur Brata Penyepian).
