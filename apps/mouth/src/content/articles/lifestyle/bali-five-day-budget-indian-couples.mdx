## The Facts

A low package price can be a useful starting point for a Bali trip. It becomes a useful budget only when the traveller can see which costs sit outside it.

A guide published by Solve Your Trip on 24 September 2026 illustrates the difference. Using two sample itineraries for an Indian couple, it estimates the land package at approximately INR 53,000–59,000. Adding return flights, visa costs and Bali's tourist levy brings its indicative total to roughly INR 120,000–165,000, before meals. Its separate food allowance is approximately INR 13,500. These are dated examples from a commercial travel business, not an island-wide price survey or a bookable quotation for every departure date. [Source: Solve Your Trip](https://www.solveyourtrip.com/blog/bali-4-nights-5-days-package-price-for-couple).

## Separate official charges from travel estimates

Indonesian Immigration lists the B1 visa fee as IDR 500,000 for a 30-day stay. Eligibility and the appropriate visa must be checked for each traveller. Bali's provincial government separately states a foreign tourist levy of IDR 150,000 per person. Travellers should check applicable exemptions and payment instructions through the official services. The visa fee and provincial levy are different charges. [Immigration](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia) · [Bali government](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/faq).

## Ask for a quotation you can compare

Our recommendation is to request one dated breakdown showing the number of travellers, room category, travel dates, baggage, transfers, included meals, activities and cancellation terms. Ask the seller to identify any tax collected in India separately and explain its treatment on the invoice.

Compare two quotations using the same dates and inclusions. A cheaper headline can reflect a different hotel, excluded flights or fewer activities rather than better value.

For Bali accommodation and tour operators, the practical opportunity is clearer communication. Show guests what they will pay to your business, what they must arrange separately, and which amounts may change before booking.

A transparent quotation helps a visitor make a decision without turning one sample itinerary into a promise about the cost of every Bali holiday.
