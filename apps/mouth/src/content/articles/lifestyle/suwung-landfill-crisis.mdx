# The Waste Crisis Nobody's Talking About

Suwung, Bali's primary landfill serving the entire southern region, is reaching capacity. The closure timeline is accelerating, and there's no clear replacement ready.

If you run a hotel, villa, restaurant, or event venue — this will affect you.

---

## The 30-Second Brief

<InfoCard variant="error" title="Critical Facts">
- **Suwung landfill** serves Denpasar, Badung, Gianyar, and Tabanan
- **Capacity**: Nearly exhausted
- **Replacement**: Temporary Waste Recovery Center (not yet operational)
- **Impact**: Hospitality businesses face potential waste collection disruptions
- **Timeline**: 2026 is the critical year
</InfoCard>

---

## What's Actually Happening

The Suwung landfill has been operating beyond capacity for years. The provincial government has announced plans for a Waste Recovery Center, but construction and operational timelines remain unclear.

For businesses, this means:
- **Collection schedules** may become irregular
- **Disposal costs** will likely increase
- **Compliance requirements** are tightening
- **Sustainability certifications** are becoming mandatory for some permits

---

## Who's Most Affected

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="High-Impact Sectors">
1. **Hotels and villas** — daily waste volumes
2. **Restaurants and cafes** — organic waste compliance
3. **Event venues** — large-scale waste from weddings/conferences
4. **Construction** — debris disposal already problematic
5. **Retail** — packaging waste regulations incoming
</InfoCard>

---

## The Bali Zero Take

This crisis has been building for a decade. The difference now is that it's becoming impossible to ignore.

**Smart operators are:**
- Partnering with private waste management companies
- Implementing composting programs (especially for organic waste)
- Reducing single-use items proactively
- Building waste management into event pricing
- Documenting compliance for permit renewals

---

## Your Next Steps

1. **Audit your current waste management** — know your volumes and costs
2. **Identify backup providers** — don't rely on a single collector
3. **Start composting** if you haven't — it's becoming mandatory
4. **Budget for increases** — waste costs will rise 20-40% minimum
5. **Document everything** — compliance records matter for permits
