# Bali's Perfect Storm

2026 is shaping up to be the most challenging year for Bali in a decade. Five major crises are converging simultaneously, and if you're living, working, or investing here, you need a new playbook.

This isn't fear-mongering. It's preparation.

---

## The 30-Second Brief

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="What's Happening">
  - **Suwung Landfill** closing with no clear replacement - **Dengue cases** up
  636 this year alone - **Maritime blockades** disrupting travel routes -
  **40-75% entertainment tax** hitting hospitality hard - **Green zone permits**
  frozen across Canggu and Ubud
</InfoCard>

These aren't isolated problems. They're interconnected challenges that will affect everything from daily life to investment returns.

---

## Why This Matters

If you're in Bali for the long haul, these five factors will directly impact:

1. **Property values** in affected zones
2. **Business costs** in hospitality and entertainment
3. **Health insurance** premiums and coverage
4. **Travel planning** for inter-island trips
5. **Construction timelines** for any development projects

## The Bali Zero Take

<InfoCard variant="info" title="Our Position">
Bali has weathered storms before. What's different now is the **simultaneity** of these challenges.

The expats and investors who thrive will be those who:

- Stay informed on policy changes
- Build buffer time into all plans
- Diversify investments across zones
- Maintain comprehensive health coverage
- Work with local partners who understand the landscape
  </InfoCard>
