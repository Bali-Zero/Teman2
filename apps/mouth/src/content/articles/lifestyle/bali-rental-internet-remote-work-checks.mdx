## The Facts

A rental listing that includes Wi-Fi leaves an important question unanswered: will the connection support your working day in the room you plan to use?

Livuma's guide of 26 September 2026 recommends checking internet at the specific property before making a long-term commitment. Its checklist covers the installed provider, shared access, performance at the desk and responsibility for resolving faults. It is useful practical guidance from a property platform, rather than an independent survey of every Bali neighbourhood. [Livuma guide](https://livuma.com/blog/internet-options-speeds-remote-work-bali).

## A provider's offer is a starting point

CBN's Bali service page illustrates the range of advertised fibre products and describes upload and download specifications. An advertised product is not evidence that a particular rental subscribes to it, or that the equipment inside the property delivers the experience you need. Confirm the actual service with the owner and provider. [CBN Bali](https://www.cbn.id/en/branch/bali-1).

## Turn a viewing into a practical check

Our suggested approach is to bring the device you use for work and test an ordinary task: a video meeting, file transfer or connection to your company's services. Record the date, location and result so you can compare properties on the same basis.

Ask whether other units share the connection and who handles service interruptions. If you plan to rely on a mobile backup, test it inside the building. A reassuring answer about the area should be followed by a check of the address.

Before paying, put the agreed internet provision and support responsibilities in writing. If reliable connectivity is essential to your decision, discuss what happens if the agreed service is not available at handover.

Internet is one part of choosing a place to live and work. Review it alongside your housing terms and the separate immigration requirements for your planned activities.

The purpose of the check is straightforward: make the rental decision with evidence from your actual workspace, while there is still time to choose another property.
