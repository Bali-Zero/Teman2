## The Legal Framework, as It Stands

Indonesia's **Undang-Undang Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP)**, Law No. 27 of 2022, officially entered its enforcement phase in **October 2024** after a mandatory two-year transition period. The law is Indonesia's first comprehensive personal data protection legislation and applies to any entity — domestic or foreign — that processes the personal data of Indonesian citizens, regardless of where that entity is headquartered.
