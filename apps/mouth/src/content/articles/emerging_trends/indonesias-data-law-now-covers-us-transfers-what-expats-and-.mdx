## What the Minister Said

Indonesia's minister — reports suggest it was the Minister of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi) — publicly confirmed that cross-border personal data transfers from Indonesia to the United States are subject to UU PDP, Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022). The statement was reported by ANTARA News Mataram, though at time of writing, a full transcript or official press release has not been independently verified. Bali Zero
