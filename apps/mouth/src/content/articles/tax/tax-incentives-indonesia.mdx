# Indonesia Tax Incentives 2026: Tax Holiday, Allowance & Pioneer Industries

Indonesia's tax incentive system is one of the most aggressive in Southeast
Asia — and one of the least understood by foreign investors. The flagship
program, the Tax Holiday, offers 100% corporate income tax exemption for
up to 20 years for qualifying pioneer industries with investments above
IDR 100 billion. Below that threshold, the Tax Allowance provides a 30%
additional deduction over six years. For R&D-intensive businesses, the
Super Deduction goes further: a 300% deduction on qualifying research
expenditure. This guide covers every major incentive, who qualifies, how
long the benefit lasts, and how to apply — with the 2026 pioneer industry
list and investment thresholds verified against current BKPM regulations.

<KeyTakeaway>
  **TL;DR:** Indonesia offers 4 main tax incentives for investors. Tax Holiday:
  100% CIT exemption 5–20 years (pioneer industries, IDR 100B+). Tax Allowance:
  30% deduction over 6 years (wider sectors). Super Deduction: 300% R&D / 200%
  vocational training. SME rate: 0.5% of revenue if under IDR 4.8B/year. Apply
  via OSS.
</KeyTakeaway>

## Tax Holiday

### What Is It?

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Full CIT Exemption">
**Tax Holiday provides:**

- 100% Corporate Income Tax exemption
- For 5-20 years depending on investment
- 50% reduction for 2 years after
- For pioneer industries

This is the most generous incentive available.

</InfoCard>

### Eligibility

| Requirement        | Criteria                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Pioneer industry   | Specific list of sectors        |
| Minimum investment | IDR 100 billion+ (some sectors) |
| New investment     | Not expansion of existing       |
| Legal entity       | PT PMA or PT                    |
| Plans              | Business plan approved          |

### Duration Based on Investment

| Investment (IDR) | Holiday Period |
| ---------------- | -------------- |
| 100B - 500B      | 5 years        |
| 500B - 1T        | 7 years        |
| 1T - 5T          | 10 years       |
| 5T - 15T         | 15 years       |
| Over 15T         | 20 years       |

### Pioneer Industries Include

- Basic metals
- Oil refining
- Petrochemicals
- Industrial machinery
- Telecommunications
- Digital economy infrastructure
- Certain others

## Tax Allowance

### What Is It?

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Investment Deduction">
**Tax Allowance provides:**

- 30% additional deduction on investment
- Over 6 years (5% per year)
- Accelerated depreciation
- Loss carry forward up to 10 years
- Reduced withholding on dividends

More accessible than Tax Holiday for smaller investments.

</InfoCard>

### Eligible Sectors

| Category               | Examples             |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Manufacturing          | Various industries   |
| Agriculture            | Plantations, farming |
| Tourism                | Hotels, attractions  |
| Infrastructure         | Power, transport     |
| Special economic zones | Location-based       |
