## The Shift from DJP Online to CoreTax

For over a decade, DJP Online was the primary digital interface between Indonesian taxpayers and the Direktorat Jenderal Pajak (DJP). It served as the portal for e-Filing (tax return submission), e-Billing (tax payment codes), and basic taxpayer account management. Alongside DJP Online, separate desktop applications handled e-Faktur (VAT invoices) and e-Bupot (withholding tax certificates).

In 2025, the Indonesian government launched CoreTax Administration System (officially "Sistem Inti Administrasi Perpajakan"), consolidating all these separate tools into a single web-based platform. This was one of the largest IT modernization projects in Indonesian government history, developed with assistance from the World Bank and international technology partners.

For foreign business owners operating PT PMA companies, this transition means relearning your entire tax workflow.

## What Improved in CoreTax

### 1. Unified Platform

The biggest improvement is integration. Previously, a PT PMA accountant juggled between:

- DJP Online for e-Filing
- e-Faktur desktop app for VAT invoices
- e-Bupot web module for withholding certificates
- Separate e-Billing module for payment codes

Now, everything is accessible from a single CoreTax login. This eliminates the need to maintain separate applications and credentials.

### 2. Real-Time Validation

DJP Online accepted data and validated it after submission, sometimes rejecting filings days later. CoreTax validates data in real-time as you enter it. This means:

- NPWP numbers are checked against the database immediately
- Mathematical calculations are verified on-the-fly
- Duplicate invoices are detected before submission
- Missing fields are flagged before you can submit

**Practical impact:** Fewer rejected filings and faster processing.

### 3. Automatic Cross-Checking

CoreTax automatically cross-references data between different filing types:

- VAT invoices (e-Faktur) are matched against income reported in SPT
- Withholding certificates (e-Bupot) are matched against expenses claimed
- Employee income reported in PPh 21 is matched against individual SPT filings

This reduces opportunities for errors and discrepancies, but it also means you need to be more precise with your data.

### 4. Enhanced Reporting and Analytics

CoreTax provides a dashboard with:

- Tax payment history
- Filing status overview
- Upcoming deadlines
- Compliance score
- Historical data trends

This is a significant upgrade from DJP Online, which offered only basic PDF downloads of filed documents.

### 5. Digital Certificate Management

The digital certificate system has been streamlined:

- Request, renew, and download certificates directly in CoreTax
- Certificate status is visible on your dashboard
- Expiry notifications are sent proactively
- One certificate works across all functions (previously e-Faktur had its own)

### 6. Multi-Branch Consolidation

For companies with multiple branches (each having a separate NPWP), CoreTax allows:

- Viewing all branch data from the parent company login
- Consolidated reporting across branches
- Centralized certificate management
- Unified filing dashboard

## What Got Worse (or Broke) in CoreTax

### 1. Server Stability

CoreTax's launch in 2025 was plagued by server crashes, slow loading times, and random logouts. While stability has improved significantly by 2026, peak periods (especially around filing deadlines) still experience:

- Slow page loads (30+ seconds)
- Timeout errors
- Session drops
- Failed submissions requiring retry

**Tip:** File early in the month and during off-peak hours (before 8 AM or after 8 PM WIB).

### 2. Steeper Learning Curve

DJP Online was simple to a fault. CoreTax is powerful but complex:

- The interface has more layers and menus
- Input forms have more required fields
- Error messages are often technical and unclear
- Help documentation is primarily in Indonesian

For foreigners who are not fluent in Indonesian, navigating CoreTax can be particularly challenging.

### 3. Stricter Data Requirements

CoreTax is less forgiving about data quality:

- Names must match exactly between systems (passport, KITAS, NPWP)
- Addresses must follow the Dukcapil format
- Phone numbers must be Indonesian (+62)
- Email addresses must be verified
- Historical data discrepancies trigger flags

### 4. e-Faktur Desktop Transition

The move from the e-Faktur desktop application to web-based e-Faktur in CoreTax has been problematic:

- Some advanced features are not yet available in the web version
- Upload limits for bulk invoices are lower
- The desktop app is still needed for certain legacy functions
- Data migration from desktop to web was not always clean

### 5. Internet Dependency

DJP Online's e-Faktur worked offline (you could create invoices locally and upload later). CoreTax is entirely web-based, meaning:

- No internet = no invoicing
- Slow internet = slow invoicing
- Server down = no invoicing at all

For businesses in areas with unreliable internet (parts of Bali included), this is a real operational concern.

## Step-by-Step: How to Start Using CoreTax

If you are transitioning from DJP Online to CoreTax, follow these steps:

### Phase 1: Account Setup (Day 1)

1. **Determine your 16-digit NPWP:**
   - For foreigners and entities: "0" + old 15-digit NPWP
   - For Indonesian individuals: Your NIK
2. **Navigate to** [coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id](https://coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id)
3. **Reset your password** using your EFIN
4. **Log in** with your 16-digit NPWP and new password
5. **Complete 2FA setup** (SMS or email verification)

### Phase 2: Profile Verification (Day 1-3)

1. **Review your taxpayer profile** - Check name, address, contact details
2. **Verify your digital certificate** - Check expiry date, renew if needed
3. **Set up authorized users** - If multiple people access your tax account
4. **Explore the dashboard** - Familiarize yourself with the menu structure

### Phase 3: Test Transaction (Day 3-7)

1. **Create a test e-Faktur** (if PKP) - Verify your invoice workflow works
2. **Generate a test e-Billing** - Ensure payment code generation works
3. **Review historical data** - Check that your past filings are visible
4. **Download important documents** - Save copies of recent SPT filings

### Phase 4: Full Transition (Day 7+)

1. **Switch all tax operations to CoreTax**
2. **Stop using DJP Online** for new filings
3. **Update your workflow documentation**
4. **Train staff** on the new system
5. **Set up calendar reminders** for filing deadlines

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DJP Online still available in 2026?

DJP Online remains accessible for viewing historical tax records, but all new filings, payments, and document issuance must be done through CoreTax. The government has not announced a final shutdown date for DJP Online, but functionality is being progressively removed.

### Do I need to re-register for CoreTax if I had a DJP Online account?

No re-registration is needed. Your taxpayer data was migrated automatically. However, you must reset your password (old DJP Online passwords do not work) and activate your CoreTax account using your EFIN.

### Is CoreTax better than DJP Online?

CoreTax is more powerful with real-time validation, integrated services, and better reporting. However, it has a steeper learning curve, occasional server instability, and stricter data requirements. The adjustment period is worth the long-term efficiency gains.

### Can my accountant access CoreTax on my behalf?

Yes. CoreTax supports authorized representative access. Your accountant or tax consultant can be granted access through the "Kuasa Wajib Pajak" (taxpayer representative) feature. This requires a power of attorney letter and registration at KPP.

### What if I have issues with CoreTax that my accountant cannot resolve?

For persistent technical issues, you can: (1) Contact Kring Pajak at 1500-200, (2) Visit your registered KPP helpdesk, (3) Submit a ticket through the official complaint portal at pengaduan.pajak.go.id, or (4) Engage a tax consultant with direct DJP liaison capability.
