## Understanding CoreTax Login Errors in 2026

Indonesia's Direktorat Jenderal Pajak (DJP) launched the CoreTax Administration System in 2025, replacing the legacy DJP Online platform. While the new system promises streamlined tax administration, the transition has created a wave of login problems that frustrate both Indonesian citizens and foreign taxpayers alike.

If you are a foreign business owner or expat operating a PT PMA in Indonesia, CoreTax login issues can delay your tax filing, block invoice generation, and create compliance headaches. This guide covers every common error, explains why it happens, and gives you a clear fix.

## The Most Common CoreTax Login Errors

Before diving into specific error codes, here is a quick reference table of the most frequently reported CoreTax login problems and their root causes:

| Error / Symptom                   | Root Cause                                | Difficulty to Fix |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| "Invalid credentials"             | NPWP not migrated to 16-digit format      | Easy              |
| ETAX-40001                        | Connection failure to DJP server          | Medium            |
| ETAX-20001                        | Digital certificate expired or corrupted  | Medium            |
| "NIK tidak ditemukan"             | NIK-NPWP mismatch in Dukcapil database    | Hard              |
| Blank page after login            | Browser incompatibility or cache issue    | Easy              |
| "Akun belum diaktivasi"           | Account not activated after migration     | Easy              |
| Timeout / 502 error               | DJP server overload (peak filing periods) | Wait              |
| Certificate import failed         | Wrong file format or browser restrictions | Medium            |
| Two-factor authentication failure | Phone number not updated in profile       | Medium            |
| "Session expired" loop            | Cookie/cache conflict                     | Easy              |

## Error 1: Invalid Credentials After Migration

**What you see:** You enter your NPWP and password, but CoreTax returns "Login gagal" or "Invalid credentials."

**Why it happens:** When DJP migrated accounts from the old system to CoreTax, passwords were not carried over automatically for all users. Additionally, if you are still trying to log in with your old 15-digit NPWP, the system may not recognize it.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. Go to [coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id](https://coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id)
2. Click "Lupa Password" (Forgot Password)
3. Enter your 16-digit NPWP (for entities) or your NIK (for individual Indonesian taxpayers)
4. For foreigners: use your 16-digit NPWP that starts with "0" followed by your old 15-digit number
5. Check your registered email for the reset link
6. Set a new password meeting the requirements: minimum 8 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character
7. Try logging in again with the new password

**If the reset email does not arrive:** Your registered email may be outdated. Visit your local KPP (Kantor Pelayanan Pajak) with your passport, KITAS/KITAP, and NPWP card to update your email address.

## Error 2: ETAX-40001 Connection Failure

**What you see:** When opening e-Faktur or attempting to upload VAT invoices, you get "ETAX-40001: Tidak dapat menghubungi E-TaxInvoice Server."

**Why it happens:** This error means your e-Faktur desktop application cannot reach the DJP server. Common causes include ISP-level blocking of DJP ports, expired digital certificates, or server maintenance.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Check DJP server status** - Visit [pajak.go.id](https://pajak.go.id) to see if there are any maintenance announcements
2. **Test your internet** - Open other websites to confirm your connection works
3. **Check your digital certificate:**
   - Open e-Faktur > Referensi > Sertifikat Digital
   - If the certificate shows as expired, download a new one from CoreTax
4. **Clear e-Faktur cache:**
   - Close e-Faktur completely
   - Navigate to your e-Faktur installation folder
   - Delete the `db` folder (back it up first)
   - Restart e-Faktur and re-import your database
5. **Try a VPN** - Some Indonesian ISPs (notably Telkomsel and Indihome) occasionally have routing issues to DJP servers. A VPN can bypass this.
6. **Check your firewall** - Ensure ports 80 and 443 are open for outbound traffic to DJP domains

## Error 3: ETAX-20001 Digital Certificate Issues

**What you see:** "ETAX-20001: File sertifikat digital tidak ditemukan" or "Sertifikat digital expired."

**Why it happens:** Digital certificates issued by DJP have a validity period (typically 2 years). When they expire, all functions requiring certificate authentication stop working, including e-Faktur generation and e-Bupot signing.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Check certificate expiry:**
   - Log in to CoreTax
   - Navigate to Profil > Sertifikat Digital
   - Note the expiry date
2. **Request a new certificate:**
   - In CoreTax, go to Layanan > Permintaan Sertifikat Digital
   - Fill in the application form
   - For PT PMA: the director or authorized representative must submit
   - Upload required documents (NPWP, company deed, director's passport/KTP)
3. **Download and install:**
   - Once approved (usually 1-3 working days), download the new .p12 file
   - Import it into your e-Faktur application: Referensi > Sertifikat Digital > Browse
   - Enter the passphrase provided by DJP
4. **Update in browser:**
   - For CoreTax web access, import the certificate into your browser
   - Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Security > Manage Certificates
   - Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Certificates > View Certificates

**Important:** Keep your certificate file (.p12) backed up securely. If you lose it, you will need to request a new one from scratch.

## Error 4: NIK Tidak Ditemukan (NIK Not Found)

**What you see:** "NIK tidak ditemukan di database Dukcapil" when trying to log in or validate your identity.

**Why it happens:** This is one of the most frustrating errors for foreigners. The CoreTax system validates taxpayer identity against the Dukcapil (civil registry) database. For foreigners with KITAS or KITAP, your data may not have been properly synchronized between immigration (DGIM), Dukcapil, and DJP databases.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Verify your NPWP format:**
   - Foreigners should use their 16-digit NPWP, not their passport number
   - The 16-digit format is: "0" + your old 15-digit NPWP
2. **Check Dukcapil registration:**
   - If you hold a KITAS/KITAP, you should have been registered with Dukcapil
   - Contact your immigration agent to verify your Dukcapil status
3. **Visit KPP directly:**
   - Bring your passport, KITAS/KITAP, NPWP card, and company documents
   - Request a "validasi NIK-NPWP" at the helpdesk
   - The KPP officer will manually verify and sync your data
4. **File a complaint through Kring Pajak:**
   - Call 1500-200 (DJP helpline)
   - Or submit a ticket through [pengaduan.pajak.go.id](https://pengaduan.pajak.go.id)
   - Reference your NPWP and describe the NIK mismatch

**Timeline:** NIK-NPWP synchronization issues typically take 5-14 working days to resolve once escalated through KPP.

## Error 5: Blank Page or Infinite Loading After Login

**What you see:** You successfully enter your credentials, but CoreTax shows a blank page, an infinite loading spinner, or immediately logs you out.

**Why it happens:** CoreTax has specific browser requirements and is sensitive to cached data from old DJP Online sessions.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Use a supported browser:**
   - Google Chrome (version 90+) - recommended
   - Mozilla Firefox (version 95+)
   - Microsoft Edge (version 90+)
   - Safari is NOT officially supported
2. **Clear browser data:**
   - Clear cookies specifically for `*.pajak.go.id` domains
   - Clear cached images and files
   - In Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data > select "Cookies" and "Cached images"
3. **Disable browser extensions:**
   - Ad blockers (uBlock Origin, AdBlock Plus) can interfere with CoreTax
   - Temporarily disable all extensions and try again
4. **Try incognito/private mode:**
   - This eliminates all extension and cache issues at once
5. **Check JavaScript:**
   - CoreTax requires JavaScript enabled
   - Ensure your browser is not blocking scripts from pajak.go.id

## Error 6: Account Not Activated

**What you see:** "Akun belum diaktivasi" or "Account not yet activated."

**Why it happens:** After NPWP registration or migration to CoreTax, you must activate your account through the verification email sent by DJP.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. Check your registered email (including spam/junk folder) for an activation email from DJP
2. Click the activation link within the email
3. If the link has expired (valid for 24 hours), go to CoreTax login page and click "Kirim Ulang Aktivasi" (Resend Activation)
4. If you never received the email, visit your local KPP with identification documents
5. The KPP can manually activate your account on the spot

## Error 7: Server Timeouts and 502 Errors

**What you see:** "502 Bad Gateway," "Server Error," or the page simply times out.

**Why it happens:** CoreTax servers experience heavy load during peak filing periods, particularly:

| Peak Period         | Deadline                        | Expected Load |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------- |
| January 1-31        | December monthly tax payments   | High          |
| March 1-31          | Individual SPT Tahunan deadline | Very High     |
| April 1-30          | Corporate SPT Tahunan deadline  | Extreme       |
| 25th of every month | Monthly SPT Masa deadline       | Moderate-High |

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Try off-peak hours:** Access CoreTax early morning (6-8 AM WIB) or late evening (after 8 PM WIB)
2. **Do not repeatedly refresh:** This adds to server load and may trigger rate limiting
3. **Save your work frequently:** CoreTax sessions can drop during high load
4. **Use e-Faktur desktop application** instead of web-based filing when possible
5. **File early:** Do not wait until the last week of a deadline period

## Error 8: Two-Factor Authentication Failures

**What you see:** CoreTax sends a verification code, but you never receive it, or the code is rejected.

**Why it happens:** CoreTax uses SMS or email-based two-factor authentication. If your phone number or email is outdated, you will not receive the code.

**Step-by-step fix:**

1. **Check your registered phone number:**
   - If you changed your Indonesian SIM card, your old number may be associated with your NPWP
   - For foreigners: ensure an Indonesian phone number (+62) is registered, not your foreign number
2. **Update contact details:**
   - This must be done at KPP in person (for security reasons, contact details cannot be changed online)
   - Bring: passport, KITAS/KITAP, NPWP card, and the new phone SIM card
3. **Use email verification instead:**
   - If available, select "Kirim ke Email" instead of SMS
   - This is often more reliable for foreigners

## What is an EFIN and Why Does It Matter?

Your EFIN (Electronic Filing Identification Number) is a 10-digit number issued by DJP that is required for:

- Resetting your CoreTax password
- Activating your account
- Filing SPT online

**How to get your EFIN:**

1. **First time:** Visit your registered KPP with identification documents. EFIN is issued on the spot.
2. **Forgot your EFIN:** Call Kring Pajak at 1500-200 with your NPWP ready. They can look it up after identity verification.
3. **For PT PMA:** The company EFIN is separate from the director's personal EFIN. Both are needed.

## Common CoreTax Errors Specific to Foreigners

Foreign taxpayers face unique challenges in CoreTax. Here are the most common foreigner-specific issues:

### Passport Number Rejection

CoreTax may reject passport numbers containing special characters or exceeding the expected length. If your passport number is not accepted:

- Enter only alphanumeric characters (remove hyphens or spaces)
- Some older passport formats are not recognized; bring your physical passport to KPP for manual entry
- If you renewed your passport, the old number in DJP records must be updated

### KITAS/KITAP Number Not Recognized

When CoreTax asks for your stay permit number and rejects it:

- Use the format shown on your physical ITAS/ITAP card
- Do not include the prefix "2C" or "2D" unless specifically shown on the card
- If recently issued, allow 2-4 weeks for immigration data to sync with DJP

### Foreign Address Issues

CoreTax requires an Indonesian address for tax correspondence. If your address was entered incorrectly during NPWP registration:

- Visit KPP to update your address
- Provide a domicile letter (Surat Keterangan Domisili) from your local RT/RW or your office landlord
- Ensure the address matches what is on your KITAS/KITAP

## Step-by-Step: How to Reset Your CoreTax Password

This is the single most common fix for login issues. Follow these exact steps:

1. Open [coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id](https://coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id)
2. Click "Lupa Password" below the login form
3. Enter your 16-digit NPWP (for entities) or NIK (for Indonesian individuals)
4. Enter your EFIN
5. A verification email will be sent to your registered address
6. Open the email and click the reset link (valid for 24 hours)
7. Create a new password:
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - At least 1 uppercase letter
   - At least 1 number
   - At least 1 special character (!@#$%^&\*)
8. Log in with your new password
9. If two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the code sent to your phone/email

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does CoreTax keep saying my login credentials are invalid?

Invalid credentials usually mean your NPWP has not been migrated to the 16-digit NIK format, your password was reset during migration, or your digital certificate has expired. Try resetting your password at coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id and ensure you are using your 16-digit NPWP (NIK for individuals).

### How do I fix the ETAX-40001 error in CoreTax?

ETAX-40001 indicates a connection failure between your e-Faktur application and the DJP server. Check your internet connection, ensure your digital certificate is valid, verify the DJP server is not under maintenance, and try using a VPN if your ISP blocks DJP ports.

### Can I still use DJP Online instead of CoreTax?

DJP Online remains partially available for certain legacy functions, but all taxpayers are required to migrate to CoreTax. New registrations, e-Faktur, e-Bupot, and most filing functions are now exclusively on CoreTax as of 2025.

### What should I do if none of the fixes work?

If you have exhausted all troubleshooting steps, file a formal complaint through Kring Pajak (1500-200) or visit your registered KPP in person. Bring all identification documents and screenshots of the error. For PT PMA companies, consider engaging a tax consultant who has direct liaison channels with DJP.

### Is there a deadline to migrate to CoreTax?

All taxpayers were required to migrate to CoreTax by the end of 2025. If you have not yet migrated, your old DJP Online account may have limited functionality. Contact your KPP or a tax advisor to initiate migration immediately.
