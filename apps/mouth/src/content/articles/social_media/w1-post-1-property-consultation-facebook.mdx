Many foreigners look at Bali property and immediately ask:

“Can I legally use this land?”
“Is this area actually allowed for my activity?”
“Can this create problems later?”

The difficult part is not finding property.

The difficult part is understanding the rules behind it.

Before making decisions, it helps to understand:
- zoning compatibility
- business activity alignment
- ownership structure
- permit implications
