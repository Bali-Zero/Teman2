## The Framework: What the Law Actually Requires

Foreign nationals who want to operate a business in Indonesia cannot simply register a sole proprietorship. The legal vehicle for foreign investment is the **PT PMA** (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing) — a foreign-owned limited liability company regulated under Investment Law No. 25/2007 and the Company Law No. 40/2007, with subsequent amendments under the Omnibus Law (Cipta Kerja, 2020).
