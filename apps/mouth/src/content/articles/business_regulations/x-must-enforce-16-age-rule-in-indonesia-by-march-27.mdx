## The Facts

Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi) has issued a directive requiring X to enforce a minimum age threshold of 16 years for all account holders operating within Indonesia, with the compliance deadline set for March 27, 2026. The order is part of Indonesia's broader digital governance framework aimed at aligning foreign social media platforms with national child protection and digital safety standards.

The regulation falls under Indonesia's Electronic Information and Transactions Law (ITE Law) and the Government Regulation on the Implementation of Electronic Systems and Transactions (PP 71/2019), which grant Komdigi broad authority to impose operational conditions on digital platforms classified as Private Electronic System Providers (PSE). Platforms that fail to comply risk access restrictions or outright blocking within Indonesian territory, a measure the government has previously applied to platforms including Tumblr and, temporarily, PayPal.

X joins a growing list of platforms that have faced specific regulatory mandates from Jakarta. Indonesia, with approximately 24 million active X users as of early 2026, represents one of the platform's largest user bases in Southeast Asia, giving Komdigi significant leverage in negotiations. The age restriction mirrors similar requirements already in force for platforms such as TikTok and Instagram under their own compliance agreements with Indonesian authorities.

The enforcement mechanism remains a point of scrutiny. Indonesia does not operate a centralized national digital identity verification system that social media platforms can query directly, which means platforms typically rely on self-declaration by users during registration or account recovery. Independent verification remains technically and logistically challenging at scale, and critics have noted that such requirements are often difficult to enforce in practice without more robust identity infrastructure.

This directive is consistent with a pattern of increased regulatory assertiveness from Jakarta toward global tech companies. Since the mandatory PSE registration regime came into effect in 2022, Komdigi has progressively expanded its scope of intervention, addressing content moderation obligations, data localization considerations, and now user eligibility standards. Observers expect further directives targeting algorithmic transparency and advertising standards to follow in 2026.
