## Setting Up a Company in Indonesia: The Regulatory Framework

Foreign investors who want to operate a business in Indonesia legally must do so through a **PT PMA** (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing), the Indonesian foreign direct investment company structure. There is no equivalent of a sole proprietorship or LLC open to foreigners — if you want to own equity and actively direct a business, PT PMA is the vehicle.
