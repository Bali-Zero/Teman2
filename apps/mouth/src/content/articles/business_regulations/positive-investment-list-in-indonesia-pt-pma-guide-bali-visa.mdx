## The Facts

Indonesia's Positive Investment List (PIL), established under Government Regulation No. 5/2021, defines which business sectors are open to foreign direct investment through PT PMA (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing) structures. The PIL replaced the previous Negative Investment List, shifting Indonesia's approach to a more open, priority-based framework aligned with the Omnibus Law (UU Cipta Kerja No. 11/2020).
