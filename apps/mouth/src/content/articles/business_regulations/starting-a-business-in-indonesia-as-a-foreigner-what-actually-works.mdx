## The Facts

## The Legal Framework

Foreigners cannot own shares in a standard Indonesian limited liability company (PT). The vehicle available to them is the **PT PMA** — Penanaman Modal Asing, or Foreign-Owned Limited Liability Company. This structure is regulated by the Investment Coordinating Board (**BKPM**, now rebranded as BKPM under the Ministry of Investment) and requires registration through the **OSS** (Online Single Submission) platform at oss.go.id.
