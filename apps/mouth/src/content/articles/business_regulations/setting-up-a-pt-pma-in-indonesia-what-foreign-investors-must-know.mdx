## Company Registration in Indonesia for Foreign Investors: The Facts

Foreign investors who want to legally operate a business in Indonesia must establish a **PT PMA** (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing), the only corporate structure that permits foreign equity ownership. Branch offices and representative offices exist but carry significant limitations — they cannot generate local revenue in most cases.
