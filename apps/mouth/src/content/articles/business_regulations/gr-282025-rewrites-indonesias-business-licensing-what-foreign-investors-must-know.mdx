## The Facts

Indonesia's business licensing framework underwent a significant structural update in 2025 through Government Regulation 28/2025 (GR 28/2025), which revised key provisions of the country's risk-based licensing regime first introduced under the Omnibus Law (Law No. 11/2020). Multiple legal and advisory sources — including Mori Hamada & Tsurumoto, KPMG Indonesia's September 2025 guide, and China Briefing — have documented the changes.
