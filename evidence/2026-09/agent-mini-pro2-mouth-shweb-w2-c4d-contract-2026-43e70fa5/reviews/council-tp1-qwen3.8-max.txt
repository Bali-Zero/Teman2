**Claim:** The recursive walk produces route strings that include nested route-group directory names (e.g. `(admin)/settings`), but the prerender manifest keys strip all parenthesised segments (e.g. `/settings`). **Why:** This is the same failure class C3 exists to close — a prerendered payload under `.next/server/app` that the guard cannot see.
