### (a) The Reachability Guard and the ENGINE Decision
* **Can the guard pass vacuously or stay green while the class it catches is present?** No. The guard derives the universe of `SUPPORT`-bearing products dynamically using `compiled.rules_for(compiled_product, effective_at=_AS_OF)`. This ensures that even GLOBAL-scope support rules (which apply to all products but might lack explicit version mapping) are fully expanded. If an active product gains a support path, it immediately enters the denominator. The test will turn red unless a walk is provided or an explicit ruling excuse is filed.
* **Is the ENGINE decision the right subject?** Yes. The guard tests whether the *interview tree* collects the necessary facts to fire a product's support rules. The public layer enforces external policy (like the `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` privacy hold for minors). Grading this through the public funnel would falsely flag perfectly reachable engine products as unreachable, confusing policy interventions with tree completeness.

### (b) UNREACHABLE_BY_RULING, the AND-Spine Proof, and Guilt Tests
* **Is it a hole to silence real gaps?** No. `test_unreachable_by_ruling_holds_only_the_bridging_row` explicitly asserts that the table contains exactly `{"BRIDGING"}`. A future engineer cannot silently slip a new product into the allowlist; they must explicitly modify the guard rail test itself, leaving a clear audit trail.
* **Is the AND-spine proof SOUND?** Yes. The `_proves_it_cannot_fire_without` function evaluates conditions under Kleene three-valued semantics. An `all` (AND) condition cannot evaluate to `TRUE` if any of its children evaluate to `FALSE` or `UNKNOWN`. Leaf predicates on `UNKNOWN` facts cleanly resolve to `UNKNOWN` or `FALSE` (except for `unknown(fact)`, which is explicitly caught and negated properly). By only returning `True` when a dependency is structurally proven, it remains conservative (sound). 
* **Do the guilt tests genuinely fail if broken?** Yes. `test_guilt_a_rule_that_only_mentions_the_forbidden_fact_is_not_a_dependency` directly proves that the checker uses structural semantics rather than purely syntactic `required_facts` membership by feeding it an `any` bypass condition that *mentions* the fact but can still evaluate to true on another branch.
