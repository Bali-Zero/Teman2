RESIDUAL RISK: The single most important residual risk for the B2 inversion is that the sufficient/insufficient contrast is confounded with a fixed lexical template: every `relevant_insufficient` context in the spec pairs (and most validation cases) signals the missing fact with one of two stock deferral formulas ("…depends on X and is not stated in this summary" / "…varies case by case"), while every `sufficient` context states one of four frozen facts (IDR 20,000,000, IDR 2.5 billion, 3–4 weeks, plus the LKPM-style validation facts), all inside a single topic domain (PT PMA setup). A scorer or gate can therefore achieve a perfect benchmark score — and a perfect B2 inversion — by detecting the deferral template (or the frozen figures) rather than by assessing whether the requested fact is present; the inversion would then certify template sensitivity, not evidence sufficiency, and the result would not transfer to real corpus chunks that defer, hedge, or partially answer in non-template language. Mitigation for B2: add insufficient members that defer without the stock phrases (and sufficient members phrased without the frozen figures) before treating inversion results as evidence about the gate.
